Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

C57Bl6/J mice heterozygous for NDRG1-null allele were bred and separated next day (E0.5), and give ad libitum access to standard chow and water with 12h light:dark cycle. Pregnant dams were sacrificed at E15.5 by cervical dislocation following isoflurane anesthesia. Following sacrifice, embryos and placentas were collected, NDRG1 genotype and sex were determined by PCR of fetal tissue, and RNA was extracted from placental tissue following removal of membranes.
