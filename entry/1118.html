Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Mouse tracheal epithelial cells grown at air-liquid interface for seven days from either wildtype or E2f7 knockout mice were analyzed using scRNAseq.

Extraction protocol

To generate single-cell suspensions for mTEC scRNA-seq, mTECs derived from E2f7-/- and littermate control wild-type mice were cultured and differentiated. E2f7+/+ and E2f7-/- mTECs were collected on day seven at air/liquid interface. Collection involved washing mTECs with PBS three times both apically and basally, incubating in 0.05% Trypsin-EDTA in Cell Dissociation Buffer (Gibco, Cat #13151014) added both apically and basally at 37°C for 15 min, gentle trituration, and inactivating trypsin in mTEC Basic with 2% Nu-Serum (Corning, Cat #355100). Additional 0.05% Trypsin-EDTA in Cell Dissociation Buffer was added to the apical surface and incubated at 37°C for 5 min. Cells were washed and resuspended in 0.04% BSA in PBS at 1000 cells/uL. Library was performed according to the manufacter’s instructions (single cell 3’ v3.1 protocol, 10x Genomics). Briefly, 16,000 mTECs were resuspended in the master mix and loaded together with partitioning oil and gel beads into the chip to generate the gel bead-in-emulsion (GEM). The poly-A RNA from the cell lysate contained in every single GEM was retrotranscripted to cDNA, which contains an Ilumina R1 primer sequence, Unique Molecular Identifier (UMI) and the 10x Barcode. The pooled barcoded cDNA was then cleaned up with Silane DynaBeads, amplified by PCR and the apropiated sized fragments were selected with SPRIselect reagent for subsequent library construction. During the library construction Ilumina R2 primer sequence, paired-end constructs with P5 and P7 sequences and a sample index were added.
