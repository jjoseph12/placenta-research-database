Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

19 tissues and primary cell types were examined.

Extraction protocol

RNA samples from tissues and primary cells were extracted from Trizol according to protocol (Invitrogen). polyA+RNA was purified with the Dynabeads mRNA purification kit (Invitrogen). The mRNA libraries were prepared for strand-specific sequencing as described previously in Parkhomchuk, D. et al. (2009).
