Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of RNA expression in mouse placenta at E16.5. Samples were same-sex litter mates from n=5 pregnancies.

Extraction protocol

Placentas were removed, sonicated in Trizol reagent and stored at -80. Illumina TruSeq Standed mRNA was used with 1 ug of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries.

Main topic
This study investigates how the placenta grows under normal conditions through hyperplasia, polyploidy, and cell hypertrophy, and how a low protein diet during pregnancy alters this normal program, influencing fetal growth and placental structure in a sexually dimorphic manner in mice.
