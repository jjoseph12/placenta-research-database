Methods text

Assay description

To determine the overall impact of VGLL1 expression on the transcriptomic profiles of the different cancer cells, RNAseq data from PANC10.05, BT20, and Bewo cells treated with siScramble versus siVGLL1 was compared

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated from the cells after the 48hour knockdown using RNAeasy Plus mini kit (Qiagen, cat#74134). 500ng of total RNA was used for construction of sequencing library RNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using Illumina’s TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library kit (Cat# 20020594)

Main topic
This study evaluates the cellular function and downstream genomic targets of VGLL1 in placental, pancreatic, and breast cancer cells. The findings expand the mechanistic understanding of VGLL1 function in tumor cells and provide a rationale for developing VGLL1-targeted therapies for cancer patients.
