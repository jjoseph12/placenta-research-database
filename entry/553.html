Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA-seq of placental RNA from non-hypertensive control and severe preeclampsia.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using mirVana RNA Isolation Kit (ThermoFisher). RNA concentration was measured using Qubit RNA BR assay kit (ThermoFisher). RNA integrity was checked using RNA 6000 Nano chip read by Aligent 2100 bioanalyzer. All samples had a RIN above 7.0. RNAseq libraries were prepared using the TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Sample preparation kit with Ribo-Zero Gold (Illumina).

Main topic
This study combines clinical, pathologic, immunohistochemical, and transcriptomic profiling of placentas to develop integrated signatures for multiple subclasses of preeclampsia (PE), revealing novel trophoblast defects and the association of maternal vascular malperfusion (MVM) with preterm PE and small-for-gestational-age neonates.
