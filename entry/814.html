Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNASeq of mouse placenta exposed to NP Serum, PE Serum, PE + Trehalose, PE + Lactotrehalose

Extraction protocol

IL10 -/- At gd17 mice were sacrificed, and placenta was collected, put into RNAlater, and save in -20 degree. RNA was isolated using RNeasy Plus Mini kit, RNA quality was check and Only samples with RIN > 6.0 and 28S/18S > 2.0 go for sequence. Library was prepared following BGIs DNBSEQ Eukaryotic Stranded Transcriptome library preparation pipeline. mRNA was enriched with Oligo dT selection, framentation and first strand cDNA synthesis using random N6-primed reverse transcription and dUTP for second strand synethesis. Ends were repaired, 3' adenylated. and adapters ligated. cDNA was PCR amplified, denatured by heat and cyclized by splint oligo and DNA ligase. Sequenxing of library was done on the DNBSEQ platform.

Main topic
This paper investigates the potential of trehalose and its lacto analog lactotrehalose as therapeutic options for preeclampsia (PE) by targeting impaired autophagy and proteinopathy in the placenta. The study demonstrates that these disaccharides can restore autophagy, inhibit protein aggregation, and normalize the PE-associated transcriptome profile in a humanized mouse model.
