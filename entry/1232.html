Methods text

Assay description

Vdr heterozygous males and females were mated to generate Vdr knockout, heterozygous and wild-type offspring. At day 18.5pc, mouse placentae were collected and RNA was extracted and assayed on Affymetrix MoGene 2.1 ST arrays to measure the effect of Vdr on global gene expression.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of vitamin D receptor (VDR)-mediated activity in placental function by using heterozygous Vdr mice to assess placental morphology, function, and pregnancy outcomes.
