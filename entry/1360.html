Methods text

Assay description

cDNA from Mac-T cells induced in vitro into lactation for 36h (addition of prolactin) was directly hybridize in the microarray chip with cDNA from mammary tissue at -30 or 60 day in milk from 3 cows.

Extraction protocol

Mammary tissue was obtained by percutaneous biopsy. After biopsy tissue was weighted (~0.5-1.0 g) and immediately immerged in ice-cold TRIZol. Mac-T cells were harvested by addition of 1 mL TRIZol and immediately frozen at -80C. After RNA extraction genomic DNA was removed from RNA with DNAse (Qiagen) using RNeasy Mini Kit columns (Qiagen). Label Cy3,Cy5 Label protocol Labelled with Cy3 and Cy5 during cDNA synthesis from total RNA using Superscript III Kit (Invitrogen) as manufacturers instructions.
