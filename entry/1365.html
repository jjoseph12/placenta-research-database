Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular mechanisms underlying intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP) by characterizing the whole-genome expression profiles of placentas from healthy women and women diagnosed with ICP, revealing involvement of multiple molecular pathways in blood vessel formation and inflammation.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Characterizing the whole-genome expression profiles of placentas from healthy women and women with mild or severe intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP) to identify molecular mechanisms associated with pathogenesis.
Source excerpt
To reveal the underlying molecular mechanisms for the association of maternal serum bile-acid level and fetal outcome in ICP patients, DNA microarray was applied to characterize the whole-genome expression profiles of placentas from healthy women and women diagnosed with ICP.
