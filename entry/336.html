Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Primary human feto-placental endothelial cells form arteries (AEC) and veins (VEC) were isolated after healthy pregnancy (AEC, VEC) and after a pregnancy complicated by Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (dAEC, dVEC). Total RNA was isolated and subjected to microarray analysis.

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) on the DNA methylation and gene expression in fetoplacental arterial and venous endothelial cells, revealing cell-specific changes in morphology and barrier function.
