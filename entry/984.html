Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from 50 mg of fetal frontal cortex tissue at GD20 using the Qiagen RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen). RNA quality and integrity was confirmed on the 2100 Bioanalyzer. Libraries were prepared using the TruSeq Stranded mRNA Linrary Preparation Kit from Illumina.

Main topic
This study investigates whether gestational hypoxia promotes damaging secretions from the placenta that affect fetal development and whether a mitochondria-targeted antioxidant MitoQ might prevent this, finding that gestational hypoxia caused low birth-weight and changes in young adult offspring brain, mimicking those in human neuropsychiatric disease.
