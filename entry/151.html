Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from mESC, 2C::GFP+, 2C::GFP+/SGC and 2C::GFP+/SA cells using the Trizol kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific, catalog. no. 15596018) following the manufacturer’s instructions. PolyA(+) RNA was isolated for library construction. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols. For each ChIP, 5×10^6 cells were used. Cells were fixed in 1% formalin/mESC medium (v/v) for 10 min with gentle agitation on a rotator at room temperature. Fixation was stopped by the addition of glycine (125 mM) and agitation for 5 minutes at room temperature. Fixed cells were washed twice in ice-cold PBS, re-suspended in LB1 (50 mM HEPES-KOH, pH 7.5, 140 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA, 10% glycerol, 0.5%NP-40, 0.25% Triton X-100 and protease inhibitors). After 10 min of incubation, cells were pelleted by centrifugation and resuspended in LB2 (10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0, 200 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA, 0.5 mM EGTA and protease inhibitors). After 10 min of incubation, cells were pelleted and resuspended in 0.25ml of freshly prepared LB3 (10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0,100 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA, 0.5 mM EGTA, 0.1% Na-deoxycholate, 0.5%N-lauroylsarcosine and protease inhibitors) and sonicated to about 100 - 500 bp fragments using a Bioruptor (Diagenode). Anti-H3K79me2 and anti-H3K79me3 antibodies were diluted into 5 mg/ml BSA (PBS/BSA) and incubated with preblocked protein G magnetic beads (Invitrogen) at 4°C with gentle agitation on a rotator for 4 hours. Antibody-bead complexes were rotated overnight with sonicated chromatin solution at 4°C. Immune chromatin-bead precipitates were collected by the magnetic device (Invitrogen). Precipitates were washed sequentially with high-salt wash buffer (20 mMTris·HCl, pH 8.0, 500 mM NaCl, 2 mM EDTA, 0.1% SDS, and 1% Triton X-100), then with LiCl wash buffer (10 mM Tris·HCl, pH 8.0, 250 mM LiCl, 1 mM EDTA, and 1% Nonidet P-40), and then with Tris-EDTA (TE)-NaCl buffer (1 mM EDTA, 10 mM Tris·HCl, pH 8.0, and 50 mM NaCl). Immunoprecipitated DNA was eluted by incubating the beads with 100 µl elution buffer (50 mM Tris·HCl, pH 8.0, 10 mM EDTA, and 1% SDS), shaking at room temperature at a setting of 1300 rpm for one hour. To reverse crosslinking, sample was treated with proteinase K and incubated at 65°C for 4 hours. Add 100 µl TE buffer to each sample. The DNA was purified using phenol-chloroform extraction, and suspended in 40 µl TE buffer. ChIP-seq library was constructed with the Accel-NGS 2S Plus DNA Library kit (Swift Biosciences, catalog. no. 21096) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. The libraries were sequenced on an Illumina Hiseq platform and 150 bp paired-end reads were generated. A hyperactive pA-Tn5 transposon for the Illumina platform was first generated. Cells were harvested and washed twice. Then the nuclei were isolated by ice-cold NE1 buffer and re-suspended in wash buffer. 10 μl of activated concanavalin A beads were added per sample and incubated at room temperature for 10 minutes. Primary antibody (1:50 dilution of anti-IgG, anti-H3K4me1, H34me3, H3K27me3 and H3K27ac) incubation was performed on a rotator at 4°C overnight. To increase the number of protein A binding sites, a secondary antibody (such as guinea pig anti-Rabbit IgG antibody for a rabbit primary antibody, and rabbit anti-mouse IgG for a mouse primary antibody) was diluted 1:100 in wash buffer and incubated at RT for 1h. After removing unbound antibodies, cells were mixed with 1:100 dilution of pA-Tn5 (0.04 µM) and incubated at room temperature for 1 hour on rotator. Next, cells were re-suspended in 300 µl tagmentation buffer and incubated at 37 °C for 1 h. To stop tagmentation, 10 µl 0.5 M EDTA, 3µl 10% SDS and 2.5 µl of 20 mg/mL Proteinase K was added to sample, which was incubated overnight at 37 °C. DNA was extracted by PCI and dissolved in 30 μl TE buffer. To amplify libraries, we added P5 primer, P7 primer (Vazyme, catalog. no. TD202), TruePrep Amplify Enzyme (Vazyme catalog. no. TD601) and performed PCR with the conditions below: 72°C for 3 minutes; 98°C for 30 seconds; 18 cycles of 98°C for 15 seconds, 63°C for 15 seconds; 72°C for 5 minutes. Post-PCR clean-up was performed by adding 1.2 volume of DNA clean beads (Vazyme, catalog. no. N411) and libraries were eluted in 20 μl nuclease-free water. A total of 50,000 cells were washed once with 50 µl of cold PBS and resuspended in 50 µl lysis buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl2, 0.1% (v/v) NP-40). To perform transposition reaction, the collected nuclei by centrifuged for 10 minutes at 500g at 4°C was mixed with the transposition reaction mix (10 µl TTBL, 5 µl TTE Mix V50 and nuclease-free H2O up to 50 µl in total) of TruePrep DNA Library Prep Kit V2 for Illumina (Vazyme, catalog. no. TD501-01) for 10 minutes at 55 °C. Samples were then PCR amplified. Post-PCR clean-up was performed by adding 1.2 volume of DNA clean beads (Vazyme, catalog. no. N411-02). Library concentration was measured with Qubit. Library integrity was checked by the Agilent bioanalyzer. Finally, the ATAC library was sequenced on an Illumina Hiseq X10 platform and 150 bp paired-end reads were generated.
