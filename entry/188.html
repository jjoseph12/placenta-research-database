Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RRBS and RNA-seq were performed on fetal side of placentae in women with PCOS and corresponding controls (n=3 in each group). RNA-seq was performed on hippocampus and hypothalamus in femal rats born to PCOS rats (n=3 in each group).

Extraction protocol

Isolation of the total RNA was performed using Trizol Reagent (Invitrogen, USA). Total genomic DNA was extracted by TIANamp Genomic DNA Kit (Tiangen Biotech, China). The libraries for RNA-seq were obtained by TruSeq RNA LT Sample Prep Kit v2 (Illumina, USA). RRBS libraries of genomic DNA were prepared using TruSeq DNA LT Sample Prep Kit v2 (Illumina, USA). The libraries were quantified with Quant-iT PicoGreen dsDNA Assay Kit (Life technologies, USA).
