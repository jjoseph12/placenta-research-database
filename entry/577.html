Assay description

Total RNA obtained from H1 hESC cultured in E6 medium supplemented with 20 ng/ml BMP4, 100ng/ml FGF2, 5 μM GF109203X, 3 mM 2DG, 100 nM LDN193189, 5 μM CHIR99021, 50 nM TPA, 1 μM PD0325901, 10 μM SB431542 and 5 μM DAPT. Samples were collected after 6 days.
