Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Our study redefined the polarization state and physiological characteristic of dMΦ in early normal and RSA pregnancy

Extraction protocol

dMCs were washed in complete medium. Cells were then counted and distributed at approximately 1×106 cells/mL in 200 μL FACS wash (FW, 1×PBS, 2% FCS). Cells were stained with CD14 and CD45 antibody for 15 min at 4 °C. Viability was assessed using DAPI staining incubated for 5 min at 4 °C. Cells were then analyzed on a LSR Fortessa (BD Biosciences). Macrophages were gated on as live CD14+CD45+ lymphocytes.
