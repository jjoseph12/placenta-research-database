Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq comparing mouse blastocyst-stage embryos generated by in vitro fertilization (IVF) and somatic cell nuclear transfer

Extraction protocol

Six IVF or SCNT embryos of the early blastocyst stage (96 hours after fertilization or activation) were directly lysed and used for cDNA synthesis using the SMARTer Ultra Low Input RNA cDNA preparation kit (Clontech, 634936). After amplification, the cDNA samples were fragmented using a Covaris M220 sonicator (Covaris). Sequencing libraries were made with the fragmented cDNA using NEBNext Ultra DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina according to manufacturer’s instructions (New England Biolabs, E7370).

Main topic
The study investigates the low live birth rate in animal cloning achieved through somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) and identifies H3K9me3 in donor cells and abnormal Xist expression as reprogramming barriers.
