Assay description

Ten preeclampsia patients (mean age 33.1 ± 1.84 years) and ten control patients (mean age 30.4 ± 1.36 years) who received a caesarean section were included in this study. Preeclampsia was clinically diagnosed before caesarean. The main criteria for PE diagnosis were: SBP ≥ 140 or DBP ≥ 90 on two occasions at least 4 h apart; and proteinuria >0.3 g/24h. The control pregnancy was defined as: no medical complications and proteinuria, maternal blood pressure < 140/90 mm Hg. Of the ten pairs of placenta samples, four pairs were randomly selected for lncRNA, circRNA and mRNA microarray analysis, and the other samples were used for the following verification.

Main topic
This study aimed to delineate a regulatory network in preeclampsia (PE) comprised of messenger RNAs (mRNAs), circular RNAs (circRNAs), long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs), and microRNAs (miRNAs) via ceRNA profiles from human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs) to further reveal the pathogenesis of PE and potential biomarkers.
