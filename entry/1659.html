Assay description

Comparison of EBV lgene expression among EBV infectyed cells.For a dual color analysis of EBV gene expression, the cDNA obtained from EBV-negative cells was labeled with Cy3 as a reference, and cDNA obtained from EBV-positive cells was labeled with Cy5. The EBV gene expression was then compared among various types of EBV infected cells.

Main topic
This study investigates the virus–host interaction in EBV-associated NK/T-cell lymphoproliferative disorders (LPD) by comparing EBV expression patterns and host cellular gene expression in cell lines established from NK/T-cell LPD and chronic active EBV infection.
