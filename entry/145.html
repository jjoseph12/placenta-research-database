Extraction protocol

Briefly, after cells were collected by flow cytometry as described before, 100,000 cells were centrifuged at 500 rcf for 5 min in a 4 °C pre-chilled fixed-angle centrifuge and washed by ice-cold PBS. After centrifugation, supernatant was removed with two pipetting steps to avoid the cell pellet. Cell pellets were then resuspended in 50 μl of ATAC-seq RSB containing 0.1% IGEPAL-630, 0.1% Tween-20, and 0.01% digitonin and incubated on ice for 10 min. After lysis, 1 ml of ATAC-seq RSB containing 0.1% Tween-20 (without NP40 or digitonin) was added, and the tubes were inverted for six times to mix. Nuclei were then centrifuged for 10 min at 500 rcf. in a 4 °C pre-chilled fixed-angle centrifuge. Supernatant was removed and nuclei were then incubated with the Tn5 transposome and tagmentation buffer at 37 °C for 30 min (Novaprotein, N248). After the tagmentation, the stop buffer was added directly into the reaction to end the tagmentation. Reactions were cleaned up with 2.2x VAHTS DNA Clean Beads (Vazyme, N411). PCR was performed to amplify the library for 8 cycles by KAPA HiFi PCR Kit (Kapa Biosystems, kk2102) using the following PCR conditions: 72 °C for 5 min; 98 °C for 3mins; and thermocycling at 98 °C for 20 s, 63 °C for 30 s and 72 °C for 3 min. After the PCR reaction, libraries were purified with the 1.8x VAHTS DNA Clean Beads. Total RNAs were extracted using TRIzol reagent. mRNA-seq libraries were generated using NEBNext® UltraTM RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (#E7530L, NEB, USA) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. Total RNAs were extracted using TRIzol reagent. mRNA-seq libraries were generated using KAPA stranded mRNA library preparation kit (KAPA Biosystems, KK8421) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. RNA was extracted according to the manufacture’s manual for 10X Genomic’s scRNA-seq . About 10,000 cells were used to generate 10X Genomic’s scRNA-seq library using single cell 3’ library and Gel Bead kit V2 according to the manufacture’s manual. 10X Genomics scRNA-seq Genomic DNAs were extracted using DNeasy Blood and Tissue Kits (QIAGEN) and digested by MspI (NEB). The digested DNAs were filled in and tailed. Then, bisulfate conversion was performed using MethyCode bisulfite conversion kit (Invitrogen). After amplification and purification，all libraries were constructed and subjected to 150 bp paired-ends Illumina Novaseq 6000 platform.
