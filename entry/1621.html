Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Mouse placentas were obtained from timed pregnant female mice at each timepoint, and fetal tissues were used to confirm embryo staging. Fetal placenta and maternal decidual tissues were dissected and pooled separately for each litter prior to RNA extraction and hybridization on Affymetrix microarrays.

Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular and genomic changes underlying placentation in mice and humans, revealing that ancient genes were co-opted for early embryogenesis and recently duplicated genes are utilized later to meet metabolic needs.

Reviewed answer Investigation of the evolutionary genomics of the placenta across development, showing that the developing placenta co-opts ancient genes for growth, whereas the mature placenta utilizes recently duplicated, species-specific genes.

By using a timecourse microarray analysis including the entire lifetime of the placenta, we uncover molecular and genomic changes that underlie placentation and find that two distinct evolutionary mechanisms were utilized during placental evolution in mice and human.
