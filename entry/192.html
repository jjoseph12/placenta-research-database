Methods text

Assay description

mRNA extraction of maternal-fetal interface with HiSeq sequencing

Extraction protocol

At 10.5 days post conception,whole maternal fetal interfaces were collected by excising the decidua and placenta from the embryo/myometrium. Samples were stored at -80 °C until homogenization and RNA extraction. Care was taken to reduce excess myometrial, perimetrium, amniotic sac, and umbilicus tissue. Tissues were lysed using the QIAzol lysis reagent (79306, Qiagen) in conjunction with the Tissuelyser II homogenization system (85300, Qiagen) as per the manufacturer’sinstructions. RNA-seq libraries were constructed with Illumina Truseq stranded mRNA library prep kit and sequenced on an Illumina HiSeq 2000 instrument.

Main topic
This paper elucidates the molecular function of N6-methyladenine (N6-mA) in DNA and its interaction with SATB1, a chromatin organizer, during early development. The study demonstrates that N6-mA antagonizes SATB1 binding and affects trophoblast differentiation.
