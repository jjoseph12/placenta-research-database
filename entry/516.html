Methods text

Assay description

Expression profiling by high throughput sequencing, Genome binding/occupancy profiling by high throughput sequencing, Elf5 promoter methylation profiling by CRISPR sequencing.

Extraction protocol

cells were washed in cold D-PBS and resuspended in 50 µl of cold lysis buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl2, and 0.1% IGEPAL CA-630). Samples were spun at 500 g for 10 min, 4oC. Each cell pellet was resuspended in 50 µl of transposition reaction mix containing 25 µl of 2X TD buffer (Illumina, #FC-121-1030), 2.5 µl Tn5 transposase (Illumina, #FC-121-1030) and 22.5 µl nuclear free H2O. Tagmentation by Tn5 was performed at 37oC, 30 min. Transposed DNA fragments were then purified with MinElute Kit (QIAGEN) and amplified by PCR with barcoded Nextera PCR primers. Amplified DNA products were then purified by MinElute Kit, and eluted in elution buffer (10 mM Tris Buffer, pH 8). Libraries were subject to quality control test on Bioanalyzer, and sequencing on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 instrument with paired-end reads at the University of Michigan core facility. Cells were sorted and resuspended in the wash buffer (20 mM HEPES pH7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 0.5 mM spermidine, 1X protease inhibitor cocktail). 10 µl of Concanavalin A-coated beads (Bangs Laboratories, Inc., #BP531) were added into the cell suspension with rotation for 10 min. Bead-bound cells were then incubated overnight at 4oC with Rabbit anti-H3K4me3 (Millipore, #07-473) or Rabbit anti-H3K27me3 (Millipore, #07-449) antibody diluted in the antibody buffer (EDTA/Digitonin/wash buffer) (1 µg antibody in 500 µl antibody buffer). The beads were then washed three times with the wash buffer containing Digitonin (EMD Millipore, #300410), followed by an incubation with 200 µl pA-MNase (0.7 ng/µl) for 1 hour at 4oC. Next, the beads were washed three times with the wash buffer containing Digitonin before 2 mM of CaCl2 were added to active the pA-MNase for 30 min on ice. The reaction was quenched by 2X stop buffer (340 mM NaCl, 20 mM EDTA, 4 mM EGTA, 0.02% Digitonin, 50 µg/ml RNase A (QIAGEN, #19101), 50 µg/ml glycogen (Roche, #10901393001) and 2 pg/ml Drosophila spike-in DNA) at 37oC for 10 min. The digested target chromatin was released and purified by phenol, chloroform, and ethanol. Libraries were prepared with the NEBNext UltraTM II DNA library Prep kit (NEB, #E7645L) and 10 cycles PCR. Total RNAs were extracted by the RNeasy mini kit (QIAGEN). Samples were prepared using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (NEB, #E7760L), NEBNext rRNA Depletion Dit (Human/Mouse/Rat) (NEB, #E6310X), NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (NEB, #E6440L) and ERCC RNA Spike-In control Mix (Thermo Fisher Scientific, #4456740). Genomic DNA was extracted using the phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1 ratio) method. Genomic DNA was subjected to DNA bisulfite conversion using the Methylation-GoldTM Kit (ZYMO RESEARCH, #D5005). DNA were then used for PCR amplification using the NEB Taq DNA polymerase (NEB, #M0273S). The PCR products were then purified by the QIAquick PCR purification kit (Qiagen, #28104). Paired-end Tru-Seq compatible Illumina libraries were prepared by the Center for Computational and Integrative Biology (CCIB) DNA Core at Massachusetts General Hospital. Briefly, partial Illumina compatible adapters with unique barcodes were ligated onto each sample during library construction. Subsequently, a low-cycle PCR amplification step completeed the addition of the full-length adapter sequences.

Main topic
The paper investigates the molecular underpinning and developmental potential of quiescent embryonic stem cells (qESCs), revealing that qESCs possess an expanded cell fate, capable of generating both embryonic and extraembryonic cell types, and exhibit a divergent metabolic landscape compared to cycling ESCs.
