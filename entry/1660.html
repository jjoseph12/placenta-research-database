Assay description

3 sets of pregnant C57/BL mice were treated with dexamethasone (0.5 mg/kg) on gestational day 15, 16, 17. 3 sets of pregant mice were injected with saline and served as controls.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of antenatal dexamethasone (DEX) treatment on placental gene expression in pregnant mice, examining its potential impact on fetal growth.
