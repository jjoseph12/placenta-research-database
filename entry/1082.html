Methods text

Assay description

The primary goal of this study was to identify differentially expressed transcripts and illuminate molecular mechanisms distinguishing IAI-associated PTBs from spontaneous PTBs in the absence of IAI. We further compared iPTB specimens to term specimens to determine genes differentially regulated with advancing gestational age and following spontaneous PTB without IAI. Finally, we determined transcripts selectively expressed in either the villous trophoblast or decidua basalis in each clinical context. Raw data for this study are not available because consent forms do not allow for public access to raw data.

Extraction protocol

Individual paired placental specimens (basal plate decidua basalis and villous trophoblast biopsies) were flash frozen in liquid nitrogen in the Operating Room and stored at -80°C for RNA studies. Total RNA was isolated using TRIzol reagent (Life Technologies, Grand Island, NY, USA) per the manufacturer's recommendations. Total RNA integrity was initially assessed by agarose gel electrophoresis with ethidium bromide labeling in the presence of Protector RNase Inhibitor (Roche) to visualize intact 28S and 18S rRNA bands. The RNA was further purified using an RNA Clean-Up and Concentration Kit (Norgen Biotek, Thorold, Ontario, Canada) prior to sequencing. Long total RNA-Seq libraries were constructed using the TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Sample Prep Kit with Ribo-Zero Gold (Illumina, San Diego, CA). The Ribo-Zero kit was utilized to remove contaminating ribosomal RNA and mitochondrial RNA while retaining both coding and non-coding long RNAs in the subsequent libraries. A Qubit 2.0 fluorometer (Life Technologies) and BioAnalyzer 2100 system (Agilent, Santa Clara, CA) were used to select transcripts of appropriate mass and insert size. The Illumina HiSeq 2500 system and HiSeq version 3 sequencing reagents were used to generate 50 bp single-end reads. Automated clonal amplification was accomplished using the cBOT system (Illumina) using Illumina HiSeq version 3 cluster generation reagents to achieve a target density of approximately 800,000/mm^2 in a single flow cell channel. Image analysis, base calling, and error estimation was performed using Illumina Analysis Pipeline in HiSeq Control Software version 2.2.38.

Main topic
This study performed RNA sequencing to identify key placental villous trophoblast (VT) and decidua basalis (DB) transcripts differentially expressed in intra-amniotic infection (IAI)-induced preterm birth (PTB). The study identifies a transcriptional signature consistent with acute inflammation in the villous trophoblast associated with IAI.
