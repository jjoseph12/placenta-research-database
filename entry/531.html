Methods text

Assay description

To verify the effect of rsPIBF on the development of hHO, on day 8 (one day after hHO induction), cells were cultured in the hHO medium (control group), or hHO medium containing 200 ng/ml sPIBF (sPIBF group), with the medium replaced every two days until day 19-21.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from EBs (day 3), control or sPIBF-treated hHOs (day 20) using Trizol Reagent kit (15596026, Invitrogen). RNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using TruSeq RNA Library Prep Kit v2 following manufacturer's protocols.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of progesterone immunomodulatory binding factor 1 (PIBF1) in trophoblast syncytialization and its impact on cardiovascular development, revealing that PIBF1 is crucial for trophoblast differentiation and vascular network development in the placenta, which in turn affects early embryonic cardiovascular system development.
