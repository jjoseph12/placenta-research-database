Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-Seq was performed in triplicates from naïve and primed female H9 human pluripotent embryonic stem cells (hESC). EZH2 was inhibited with EPZ-6438 for 7 days.

Extraction protocol

Flash frozen hESCs were thawed and spiked in with 5e4 Drosophila cells per sample and then total RNA was purified using the RNeasy Plus Mini Kit ( Qiagen; 74136) according to the manufacturer's protocol. Purified RNA quantities were estimated using the Qubit RNA HS assay kit (Life technologies; Q32852) and the samples were subsequently flash frozen. RNA-seq libraries were generated and sequence through BGI service for strand specific RNA-seq with PolyA selection (DNBseq Eukaryotic Transcriptome De novo Sequencing).

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of polycomb repressive complex 2 (PRC2) in maintaining naïve pluripotency and restricting differentiation to trophectoderm and mesoderm lineages in human embryonic stem cells. The study finds that PRC2-mediated repression provides a mechanism to restrict lineage potential during early human development.
