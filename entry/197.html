Main topic
This study profiles messenger RNA, long non-coding RNA, and circular RNA (circRNA) expression in placentas from normal and severe pre-eclampsia (SPE) pregnancies to identify potential novel blood biomarkers for early pre-eclampsia, focusing on the role of hsa_circ_0036877.
