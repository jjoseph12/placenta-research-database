Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Fetal placental tissue of both sexes in GR+/+ vs. GR+/- c57/bl6 mice was analyzed genome-wide by capture sequencing

Extraction protocol

During a caesarean section protocol, the placenta was removed, flash frozen on dry ice, the marked fetal part was cut in slices in the cryostat at -20 degrees and ?? Kit was used to harvest the DNA. DNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols capture sequencing

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of prenatal stress on offspring phenotypes through epigenetic programming, focusing on the role of the glucocorticoid receptor (Nr3c1) and sex-dependent effects on DNA methylation and behavior in mice.

Reviewed answer The study investigates how glucocorticoid receptor (Nr3c1) deficiency affects fetal placental DNA methylation in a sex-dependent manner, programming long-term phenotypes such as anxiety-like behavior in adult offspring.

We hypothesized that the glucocorticoid receptor (Gr, Nr3c1) deficiency in the placenta might lead to sex-dependent epigenetic alterations in placenta and might program behavior of the offspring in adulthood.
