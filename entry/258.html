Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq of mouse EBs differnitated in standard base media or media conditioned by mTSCs for 4 or 8 days (N=3 for each condition at each timepoint). Every sample was sequenced in two lanes.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from Ebs at day 4 or 8 of differentiation with the mirVana™ miRNA Isolation Kit according to the manufacturer's protocol. Libraries were constructed with the Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep, using poly-A capture for mRNA enrichment.

Main topic
This paper establishes a differentiation protocol of mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) into cardiomyocyte-containing embryoid bodies to test the impact of trophoblast on this key developmental process, demonstrating that trophoblast helps prolong a pluripotent state in embryonic cells and delays early differentiative processes.
