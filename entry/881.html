Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Villous fragments from eight fresh term human placenta were treated for 8 hours with 20µM 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25(OH)D) and processed for RNA-seq together with controls.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from 30 mg placental samples using the miRNeasy mini kit with the RNase-free DNase Set (Qiagen, UK) according to manufacturer’s instructions. RNA samples (450 ng) were converted into cDNA libraries using the Illumina TruSeq Stranded mRNA sample preparation kit.

Main topic
The study investigates the role of the placenta in regulating the relationships between maternal 25(OH)D and fetal physiology, demonstrating active placental uptake of 25(OH)D and its impact on the placental transcriptome and proteome.
