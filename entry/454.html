Assay description

RNAseq of BeWo cells constantly expressing shControl or shGPX4 following ferrostatin-1 withdrawal

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated with Qiagen RNeasy mini kit RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols at Novogene Corporation The samples were sequenced using PE150 sequencing on a Novaseq 6000 with S4 flowcell.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of ferroptosis in placental dysfunction and identifies PLA2G6 as a key player in mitigating trophoblast and placental ferroptosis, suggesting potential therapeutic strategies for pregnancy complications.
