Methods text

Assay description

Three different first-trimester placentas (8-8.5 weeks gestation) were dissected into 6 explants each and cultured either with antiphospholipid antibodies (25ug/mL) or untreated media for 16h. RNA was extracted from treated placental explants and Affymetrix HGU133 Plus 2 arrays were conducted to investigate changes in mRNA expression. Untreated: 1UNT, 2UNT, 3UNT Antiphospholipid antibody treatment: 1APL, 2APL, 3APL Biological replicates: 1, 2, 3
