Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq analysis was performed in 3 ovine whole embryos in vitro-produced and cultured until D14, 3 E11 and 3 E12.5 in vivo-derived embryos.

Extraction protocol

Embryos were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and total RNA was extracted using MagMAXTM mirVanaTM Total RNA Isolation kit according to the manufacturer´s protocol. cDNA was synthetized with SMART-Seq™ v4 Ultra™ Low Input RNA Kit (Clontech) and amplified ds-cDNA was purified with AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter) and quantified with Qubit (Life Technologies). Libraries were prepared using Covaris shearing system and a size selection of 200 bp was performed. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper introduces an in vitro culture system to support ungulate embryo development beyond hatching, focusing on the proliferation and maturation of extra-embryonic membranes and the formation of a flat embryonic disc, which are critical processes for embryo survival and often lead to pregnancy losses.
