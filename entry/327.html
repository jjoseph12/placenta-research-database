Main topic
This study investigates the effects of polybrominated diphenyl ether BDE-47 on human placental cytotrophoblasts using an in vitro transcriptomics approach to understand the potential consequences of PBDE exposures on human placental development.
Source evidence 31 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Evaluating the toxicological effects of BDE-47 on human primary villous cytotrophoblasts (CTBs) in vitro, including impacts on cell viability, migration, invasion, the transcriptome, and the methylome.
Source excerpt
Thus, to study the potential consequences of PBDE exposures on human placental development, we used an in vitro model: primary villous cytotrophoblasts (CTBs). Following exposures, the endpoints that were evaluated included cytotoxicity, function (migration, invasion), the transcriptome, and the methylome.
