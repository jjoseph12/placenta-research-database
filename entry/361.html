Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of the impact of EED deletion in oocytes on oocyte epigenetic programming and offspring development

Extraction protocol

Oocytes: Cumulus-Oocyte Complexes (COCs) were collected from eight-twelve week old female mice and transferred to M2 media. Oocytes were denuded mechanically with a narrow-bore glass pipette and incubated with M2 media containing 5 μg/mL Hoechst 33342 for 10 minutes at 37°C. GV oocytes were then scored as either surrounded nucleolus (SN), or non-surrounded nucleolus (NSN) based on Hoechst staining. SN oocytes were then collected, frozen on dry ice and stored at -80°C until RNA extraction. Ten to fifteen oocytes isolated from each female were pooled and total RNA isolated using the Agencourt RNAdvance Cell V2 extraction kit. High quality RNA (RIN >7.5) was used for library preparation (>1.2ng total RNA) using the Nugen Trio Library protocol, MU01440V2; 2017. 75bp single end sequencing was carried out on 4-6 libraries / genotype using Illumina NextSeq500 High output mode and v2.5 chemistry (Illumina Protocol 15046563 v02, Mar 2016) to collect >25M reads per sample. Bone growth plate tissue was isolated at E17.5 and snap frozen as described in the associated manuscript (Jarred et al. In prep). Femoral growth plates were isolated from E17.5 offspring and growth plates were extracted from the distal end on both the left and right femurs, washed in PBS, digested in 3mg/ml collagenase for 15 min at 37°C in a 5% CO2 incubator. Growth plates were then washed in PBS, agitated in 0.5mg/ml collagenase in PBS to remove soft tissue, washed in PBS, frozen on dry ice and stored at -80°C. Placentas were isolated at E17.5, weighed and bisected. Half of the placenta was rinsed in PBS, snap-frozen on dry ice and stored at -80°C for RNA analysis. Growth plate and placental RNA was extracted from 4-5 samples / genotype using NucleoSpin RNA XS (bone growth plate) or NucleoSpin RNA Plus columns (Placenta). RNA quality was assessed on an Agilent Bioanalyzer and samples with RIN >7.5 used for library preparation and sequencing either on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 S1 collecting >25M 150b PE reads (bone growth plates; Ramaciotti Centre for Genomics) or on the BGI Genomics platform (placentae, BGI Genomics, Hong Kong). Oocyte libraries were constructed using Nugen Trio Library prep using protocol M01440v2; 2017 followed by 75bp SR sequencing using Illumina NextSeq500 High output mode and v2.5 chemistry. Illumina Protocol 15046563 v02, Mar 2016. Bone Growth Plate libraries were constructed using Illumina RNAseq chemistry followed by sequencing on an Illumina NovaSeq 6000 S1 2x150bp flowcell (Ramaciotti Centre for Genomics). Placental libraries were constructed using the BGI DNBseq protocol followed by 150bp PE sequencing on the BGI DNBSeq Eukaryotic Transcriptome Resequencing platform (Beijing Genomics)
