Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Transcriptomic analysis of organoids derived from fetal and pediatric gastric samples infected by SARS-CoV-2 virus at MOI 1, and paired negative controls.

Extraction protocol

Pelletted organoids were extracted in RLT buffer cDNA was synthesized using the SMART-Seq v4 Ultra Low Input RNA Kit for Sequencing, following manufacturer suggested protocol. cDNA generated with SMART-Seq v4 Kit were used for preparation of library with NEXTERA XT DNA Library Preparation kit, following the suggested protocol.

Main topic
This paper investigates the susceptibility of the stomach mucosa to SARS-CoV-2 infection by developing an in vitro model that reproduces the gastric microenvironment, finding that pediatric and late fetal gastric organoids are more susceptible to infection.
