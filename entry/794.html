Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Bisulphite converted DNA from the 12 placental samples and 12 umbilical cord samples of mothers with or without obesity before pregnancy and mothers with and without gestational obesity were hybridised to the Infinium Human DNA Methylation EPIC 850K.

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted and purified from umbilical cord and placenta samples using Qiagen Puregene Kit according to standard instructions and then bisulfited with EZ DNA methylation Gold kit, according to manufacturers instructions.
