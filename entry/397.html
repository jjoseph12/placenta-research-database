Main topic
This study investigates immunological alterations in early- and late-onset preeclampsia placentas using a targeted expression profile approach, finding that the placental innate immune system is altered in early-onset preeclampsia compared to uncomplicated pregnancies, with distinct immunological profiles between the two subtypes.
