Methods text

Assay description

HTR8/Svneo cells which were stably over expressed with PLAC8-EGFP and EGFP were established.Then trancriptional sequencing experiment was performed to explore the effect of PLAC8 on trophoblast cells.

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent and extracted with chloroform. RNA concentration and quality were tested by Agilent 2100. 0.2 ug of total RNA was utilized for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard dUTP strand-specific RNA-seq library protocols

Main topic
This paper reports that placenta-specific protein 8 (PLAC8) is specifically expressed in the interstitial extravillous trophoblast cells (iEVTs) on the fetomaternal interface and promotes invasion and migration of iEVTs. It also shows that PLAC8 is significantly upregulated under hypoxia and expression of PLAC8 is higher in iEVTs from preeclamptic placentas when compared with those from the normal control placentas.
