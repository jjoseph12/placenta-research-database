Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Transcriptome analyses of mRNAs from trophoblast cells upon YTHDF3 knockdown

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from HTR8 cell lines infected with shControl or shYTHDF3 using Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 1 ug of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of YTHDF3, an m1A reader protein, in regulating trophoblast activity and its potential impact on pregnancy. The study finds that YTHDF3 selectively binds to m1A-modified RNAs and inhibits trophoblast activity by targeting IGF1R expression, suggesting a role in maintaining pregnancy.
