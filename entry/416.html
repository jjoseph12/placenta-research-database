Methods text

Assay description

Illumina MethylationEPIC analysis performed on 96 human placentas collected at term.

Extraction protocol

DNA, RNA, and miRNA were simultaneously extracted from placenta, powdered under liquid N2, using the AllPrep miRNA kit (Qiagen) and the QIAcube for automated nucleic acid extraction (Qiagen, USA).

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of vitamin C supplementation on placental DNA methylation changes related to maternal smoking during pregnancy, and its association with gene expression and respiratory outcomes in offspring.

Reviewed answer The publication investigates the impact of vitamin C supplementation on maternal smoking-induced changes in placental DNA methylation and gene expression, and their relationship with offspring respiratory outcomes.
Source excerpt
Vitamin C supplementation to pregnant smokers ameliorates changes associated with maternal smoking in placental DNA methylation and gene expression in pathways potentially linked to improved placental function and offspring respiratory health.
