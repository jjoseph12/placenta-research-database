Main topic
This study investigates the impact of Zika virus (ZIKV) infection during the earliest stages of pregnancy, specifically at pre- and peri-implantation, demonstrating that trophectoderm cells of pre-implantation human and mouse embryos can be infected with ZIKV, leading to impaired fetal development and miscarriage.
