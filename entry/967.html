Assay description

Human myeloid cells from the normal human term placenta were sorted to high purity for RNA extraction and hybridization on Affymetrix microarrays. This series contains re-analyzed samples from GSE78896: GSM2080659 - GSM2080662.

Main topic
This study identifies and characterizes two distinct subsets of myeloid cells in the human placenta at term, which differ in their pro-inflammatory/regulatory capacity, suggesting a mechanism for maintaining tolerance during pregnancy.
