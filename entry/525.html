Methods text

Assay description

Transcriptome profiling of human trophoblast stem cells (TSC) and EVT differentiating cells (multiple time-points) was conducted to monitor dynamics in the gene expression program during EVT differentiation. To investigate the requirements of the defined TFs (DLX5, DLX6, ASCL2, ZNF439, NRIP1, and TFAP2C) in proper EVT differentiation, we knocked down (KD) the individual TF in TSC and differentiated the cells to EVT. RNA-seq was performed on the individual key TF-KD EVT and non-targeting shRNA-expressing EVT (control). We also conducted RNA-seq of EVT overexpressing TFAP2C during days 2 to 8 of differentiation to monitor the effects of sustained expression of TFAP2C on EVT differentiation. The non-DOX-treated EVT was used as a control.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (74136) following the manufacturer’s instructions. mRNA was enriched from total RNA using NEBNext Poly(A) mRNA Magnetic Isolation Module (E7490). RNA-seq libraries were generated with 300 ng of total RNA using NEBNext Ultra II RNA Library Prep with (E7775) and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (E7600S) following the manufacturer’s protocol.

Main topic
This paper defines stage-specific transcription factors and their inter-connected regulatory mechanisms modulating extravillous trophoblast differentiation, providing a framework for understanding early human placentation and placenta-related complications. The study analyzes dynamic changes in transcriptome and enhancer profile during human trophoblast stem cell to extravillous trophoblast differentiation.
