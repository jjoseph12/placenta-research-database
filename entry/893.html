Methods text

Assay description

mRNA expression profiles of 5 cynomolgus monkey trophoblastic cell lines in different culture conditions, using Illumina HiSeq 2500.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from culture cells and placenta using TRIzol. TruSeq Stranded mRNA LT Sample Prep Kit was used for the construction of sequencing libraries of CyTRF#1, #2, fibroblast, placenta and ESC. TruSeq Stranded mRNA Sample Prep Kit was used for the construction of sequencing libraries of CyTRF#3-5. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using TruSeq Stranded mRNA Sample Preparation Guide Rev.E protocols
