Main topic
This paper validates the regulated expression of a large number of non-coding RNAs (ncRNAs) from the mouse genome, demonstrating that these ncRNAs are genuine transcripts with tissue-specific expression patterns and dynamic changes in expression following lipopolysaccharide stimulation in macrophages.

Reviewed answer This publication focuses on the experimental validation and characterization of non-protein-coding RNAs (ncRNAs) in the mouse genome, demonstrating that their expression is tissue-specific, dynamically regulated, and not the result of genomic contamination.

Here we show, using reverse transcriptase-dependent PCR, microarray, and Northern blot analyses, that many of these clones were derived from genuine transcripts of unknown function whose expression appears to be regulated.
