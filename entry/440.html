Methods text

Assay description

We used animals and semen of Angus (Bos taurus taurus) and Brahman (Bos taurus indicus) cattle, subsequently referred as Bt and Bi respectively. Pure Bt and Bi females of approximately 16-20 months of age were maintained on pasture supplemented with silage. Fetuses produced by artificial insemination with semen of pure bred Bt or Bi sires. Cows were pregnancy tested by ultrasound scanning. Cows and fetuses were humanely sacrificed by stunning and exsanguination at day 153+/- 1 of gestation. Fetuses were dissected, tissues snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80oC study as previously described (Xiang et al., 2013). Five of the tissues were used in this study: Brain, Liver, Lung, Muscle and Placenta. Three male and three female fetus from each of the 4 genetic types (Bt x Bt; Bi x Bt; Bt x Bi; Bi x Bi; paternal genome listed first) were used in this study for a total of 24 samples per tissue.

Extraction protocol

The total RNA was isolated from the tissues using the illumina kit, in accordance with the manufacturer’s recommendations (Illumina). RNA libraries were prepared with with a KAPA Stranded RNA-Seq Library Preparation Kit following the Illumina paired-end library preparation protocol.Sequence reads were produced on an Illumina Next-Seq platform Illumina

Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptome of the placenta and four somatic tissues at mid-gestation from two cattle breeds (Angus and Brahman) and their reciprocal crosses to identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs) that may be involved in establishing phenotypic differences between the cattle subspecies.
