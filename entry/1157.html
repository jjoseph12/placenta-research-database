Methods text

Assay description

(1) Study of gene expression changes from mRNA of mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) after the overexpression of the transcription factor C/EBPa. (2) Gene expression profile of mouse trophoblast stem cells (TSCs).

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from ESCs with a miRNeasy mini kit (217004, Qiagen), quantified with a NanoDrop spectrophotometer and its quality examined in a fragment Bioanalyzer (Aligent 2100 Bioanalyzer DNA 7500 assay). For RNA-sequencing, libraries were prepared with a TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library Preparation Kit (Illumina) followed by single-end sequencing (50 bp) on an HiSeq2500 instrument (Illumina), obtaining at least 40 million reads per sample.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of IL-6 signaling in somatic cell reprogramming, specifically focusing on the C/EBPα-enhanced reprogramming of B cells into induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) and demonstrates a C/EBPα-IL-6 regulatory axis in the trophectoderm during blastocyst development.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication investigates the role and regulation of the IL-6 signaling pathway during somatic cell reprogramming and pre-implantation embryo development, showing that C/EBPα regulates IL-6 signaling and that the trophectoderm acts as a niche for the inner cell mass via IL-6.
Source excerpt
The observed requirement of C/EBPa-regulated IL-6 signaling for pluripotency during somatic cell reprogramming thus recapitulates a physiologic mechanism in which the trophectoderm acts as niche for the ICM through the secretion of IL-6.
