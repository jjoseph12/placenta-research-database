Methods text

Assay description

RNA was isolated from whole tissue decidua, myometrium and mesometrial of B6 control (n=5) and IL-33 KO (5) mice on gestation day 18.5, one day prior to parturition.One IL-33 KO decidua and one B6 mesometrial triangle were excluded due to failed QC.Next generation sequencing was performed using Illumina HiSeq 4000

Extraction protocol

Implantation site from gestation day 18.5 were dissected to separate decidua, myometrium and mesometrial triangle. Each layer was washed in cold PBS and homogenized in TRIzol. RNA was subsequently purified by phenol chloroform extraction. RNA libraries were prepared according to Illumona's instructions, using Nugen Universal Plus mRNA

Main topic
This study investigates uterus-intrinsic pathways that trigger parturition in mice, focusing on the role of innate type 2 immunity, IL-33, uterine group 2 innate lymphoid cells, and eosinophils in controlling the timing of labor onset.
