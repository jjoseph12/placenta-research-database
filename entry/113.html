Methods text

Assay description

placental genomic DNA from 22 early-onset preeclampsia patients and 20 normal controls was bisulfate converted in 450k platform and measured with Illumina HiScan.

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted with a QIAGEN DNAeasy blood & tissue kit (Hilden, Germany) from the placental tissue.
