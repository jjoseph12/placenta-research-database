Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Single cell mRNA-seq of placenta from two term healthy pregnancies.

Extraction protocol

C1 Single-Cell Auto Prep Integrated Fluidic Chip for mRNA-seq (17-25 micron cell size selection). Nextera XT DNA Library Preparation Kit - manufacturer's recommendation. A total of 125 pg cDNA was tagmented at 55 °C for 20 min. Libraries were pooled and purified on AMPure bead-based magnetic separation before a final quality control using Qubit dsDNA HS Assay Kit (Life Technologies) and Agilent High Sensitivity DNA Kit.

Main topic
This study investigates the cell-cell interactome of fetal placental trophoblast cells and maternal endometrial stromal cells using single-cell transcriptomics, revealing a highly cell-type-specific expression of G-protein-coupled receptors and identifying uterine decidual cells as a cell-cell interaction hub.
