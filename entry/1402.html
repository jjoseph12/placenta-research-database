Main topic
The paper discusses the role of atypical E2F repressors and activators in coordinating placental development.
Source evidence 31 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication investigates how atypical E2F repressors (E2F7 and E2F8) and activating E2Fs (such as E2F3a) coordinately regulate cell cycle-dependent gene expression, placental development, and embryonic survival.
Source excerpt
In this study we demonstrate that the function of atypical repressors in extra-embryonic tissues is essential for repressing a broad spectrum of E2F targets that control trophoblast proliferation, placental development and embryonic viability.
