Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Holtzman Sprague-Dawley rats were housed in an environmentally controlled facility at the University of Kansas Medical Center (KUMC) with lights on from 0600 to 2000 h and allowed free access to food and water. Female rats (8-10 weeks of age) were mated with adult male rats (>3 months of age). Mating was assessed by inspection of vaginal lavages. ­The presence of sperm in the vagina was designated gd 0.5. A single dose of either streptozotocin (STZ; 45 mg/kg of body weight) or vehicle control solution (0.1 M citrate buffer, pH 4.2) was administered via tail vein injection on gd 6.5. Placentation sites were dissected on gestation day 13.5 and junctional zones were isolated. Dissected junctional zone tissues were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80 degrees celsius until processing for RNA isolation.

Extraction protocol

Placentation sites were dissected on gestation day 18.5 and junctional zones were isolated. Dissected junctional zone tissues were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80°C until processing for RNA isolation. Total RNA was isolated from tissues using TRIzolÒ reagent (Thermo Fisher Scientific, catalog No. 15596018). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with a HiSeq2000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility.

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of poorly controlled diabetes on placental formation and fetal development in pregnant rats, using streptozotocin to induce hyperglycemia and examining the effects on trophoblast lineage development and placental plasticity.
