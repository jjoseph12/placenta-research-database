Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

E14T ESCs were transiently transfected with plasmids expressing CITED1 or empty vector pPy. 8 hours later, ESCs were selected by puromycin at the final concentration of 2 ug/mL, and then collected for RNA isolation. In total, 15 RNA samples were prepared from three independent biological triplicates. Microarray was carried out with the Affymetrix GeneChip® Mouse Genome 430 2.0 Arrays. The procedures including RNA extraction, cDNA synthesis, labeling, hybridization, washing, and scanning were conducted according to the standard Affymetrix protocol by the Shanghai Biotechnology Corporation.

Main topic
The study investigates the role of Cited1, a transcription coactivator, in inducing a trophoblast-like state from mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) through the activation of BMP signaling, revealing its importance in early trophoblast development.
