Assay description

Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data for human placental tissue relative to retinopathy of prematurity clinical outcomes.

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvested using Qiagen ALL PREP Universal Whole transcriptome expression analysis was performed on fetal surface placental tissues using the Illumina Stranded Total RNA Kit with Ribo-Zero Plus per the standard protocol

Main topic
This study investigates the correlation between placental inflammation and the risk of retinopathy of prematurity (ROP), a blinding condition affecting preterm infants.
Source evidence 31 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer This publication explores the protective association between acute placental inflammation and retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) in preterm infants, investigating underlying candidate proteins and transcriptional/epigenetic alterations in the placenta.
Source excerpt
The current study identified a protective association between acute placental inflammation and preterm infant ROP development using logistic regression, with the most significant association found for infants without gestational exposure to maternal preeclampsia and those with earlier preterm birth.
