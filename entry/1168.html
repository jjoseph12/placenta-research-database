Methods text

Assay description

In the first batch, in-situ placental, and decidual, and in-vitro-derived TB-ORG-EVTs from first trimester tissue (7 - 8th weeks of gestation) were isolated and generated, respectively, from the same single donors. In the second batch TB-ORG-EVTs were differentiated under three conditions: a) DIFF1: conventional, transforming growth factor beta (TGFb)-inhibiting condition, b) DIFF2: without TGFb inhibition; c) DIFF3: without TGFb inhibition and recombinant TGFb1

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using AllPrep DNA/RNA/miRNA Universal Kit (Qiagen). Illumina RNA-Seq Library Preparation stranded RNA with poly-A enrichment (NEB Next Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina) 2x75bp Paired End Read; >50million read pairs per sample
