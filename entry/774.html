Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was isolated from E13.5 placentas and subjected to directional RNAseq to identify sex-based transciptome differences.

Extraction protocol

Placentas from E13.5 embryos were isolated and total RNA extracted using an Ezna total RNA extraction kit (Omega) Diretional RNA-seq libraries were prepared from 1 µg total RNA using the NEBNext Directional Ultra II RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (New England Biolabs), with initial polyA+ isolation.

Main topic
This paper investigates the mechanisms underlying female-biased embryonic lethality resulting from genomic instability (GIN) in mice, focusing on the role of inflammation and the protective effect of testosterone.
