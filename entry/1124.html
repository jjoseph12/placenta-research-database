Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Pregnant rats were randomized to receive ultrasound-guided intra-amniotic injections of placebo (PBS) or LPS (E. coli O55:B5, 10 micrograms) on gestational day 18. Tissue samples were collected 24 hours after injection.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from tissue homogenates with QIAGEN Rneasy kit per manufacturer's intructions After polyA selection, paired-end RNA-sequencing with 150bp was done with a depth of 30M reads.Library was generated at BGI Genomics.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of c-Myc in placental inflammation and bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) induced by intra-amniotic inflammation (IAI) using a rat model, finding that c-Myc dysregulation modulates inflammation in the placenta, fetal membranes, and neonatal lungs, suggesting c-Myc as a potential therapeutic target for IAI-induced BPD.
