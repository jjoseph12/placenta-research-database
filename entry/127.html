Main topic
This study profiled the transcriptome of single cells in the mature bovine placenta and identified trophoblast cell types and candidate regulatory genes, factors and pathways involved in trophoblast cell fate and differentiation. Single-nuclei RNA-seq analysis found substantial differences in cell type composition and transcriptional profiles between the two distinct regions of the placenta.
