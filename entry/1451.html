Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was extracted from human term non-labour myometrium explants treated with prokineticin for 6 and 24 hours compared to vehicle-treated explants. Six biological replicates were analyzed for each treatment and time point. Two of the 6-hour vehicle samples failed a quality-control analysis and were substituted with a 4-hour vehicle treatment from the same tissue sample in each case.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of prokineticin 1 (PROK1) in inducing an inflammatory response in human myometrium, suggesting its potential involvement in initiating both term and preterm parturition. The study demonstrates that PROK1 up-regulates inflammatory mediators in the myometrium and placenta during labor.
