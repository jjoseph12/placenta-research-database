Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Bovine trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) and bovine and extended pluripotent stem cells (EPSCs), were aggregated in tFACL+PD and collected at day 4.Single-cell RNA-Seq was performed using the 10x Genomics Chromium Chip and sequenced by Illumina NextSeq 500/550 sequencing systems.

Extraction protocol

Bovine blastoids were single cell dissociated and strained cells were prepared as stated adobe. Cells were washed in PBS containing 0.04% BSA and centrifuged at 90° x500g for 5 min. Cell were resuspended in PBS containing 0.04% BSA at a single cell suspension of 1,000 cells/µL. Cells were loaded into a 10x Genomics Chromium Chip following manufacturer instruction (10x Genomics, Pleasanton, CA, Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3.1) and sequenced by Illumina NextSeq 500/550 sequencing systems (Illumina).

Main topic
This paper describes an efficient method to generate bovine blastocyst-like structures (termed blastoids) by assembling bovine trophoblast stem cells and expanded potential stem cells, aiming to improve farm animal reproduction.
