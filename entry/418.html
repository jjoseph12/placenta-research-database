Main topic
This paper investigates the role of first-trimester placenta-derived exosomes (pEXOs) in inducing maternal systemic immune tolerance by reprogramming circulating monocytes, revealing that pEXOs contribute to immunosuppression by altering monocyte phenotype and function through the microRNA miR-29a-3p.
