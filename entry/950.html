Main topic
This paper investigates the role of type I interferons (IFNs) in Zika virus (ZIKV) infection during pregnancy, focusing on how IFN signaling impacts fetal outcomes such as microcephaly, growth restriction, and fetal demise using type I IFN receptor knockout mice.
