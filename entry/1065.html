Main topic
This study investigates the molecular mechanisms underlying differential pregnancy outcomes in non-obese diabetic (NOD) mice and wild-type (WT) mice in response to CpG oligodeoxynucleotide (CpG ODN) stimulation, which mimics intrauterine infection, by analyzing differentially expressed genes (DEGs) in the placenta and decidua basalis using RNA-Seq.
