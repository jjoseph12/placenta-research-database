Methods text

Assay description

cell-free DNA deconvolution analysis from plasma of pregnant women with normal pregnancies or with adverse pregnancy outcomes (APOs) such as gestational diabetes, preeclampsia or gestational hypertension. The samples have been collected at 1st, 2nd and 3rd trimester during pregnancy and again at delivery. The cord blood has been collected as well. We analyzed the cell-free WGBS content of 102 maternal, 25 cord plasma samples and 7 non pregnant women as control.

Extraction protocol

Peripheral venous blood was collected into EDTA-coated tubes. Blood was then centrifuged at 2000 X g for 7 minutes at 4°C, in order to collect only the plasma fraction. Plasma was centrifuged at 16000 X g for 10 min at 4°C to remove all the residual cells, aliquoted and stored at −80°C. Cell-free DNA was extracted from 200 μL of plasma using the QIAmp MiniElute Virus Spin kit (Qiagen). Total DNA was quantified using the Qubit dsDNA HS Assay kit (Thermofisher Scientific). Whole genome bisulfite libraries were prepared using the pico methyl-Seq library prep kit (Zymo Research) according to manufacturer’s instructions. Libraries were sequenced using 100 bp single-end reads on the Hiseq4000 System (Illumina)

Main topic
This study investigates cell-free DNA and RNA content in maternal and cord plasma to detect adverse pregnancy outcomes (APOs) based on placental function, finding that placenta-specific nucleic acids during early gestation can predict APOs before clinical features emerge.
