Main topic
This study investigates the dose-dependent effects of maternal exposure to carbon black nanoparticles (CB-NP) on astrocyte activation and reactive astrogliosis in the brains of mouse offspring, finding a relationship between maternal NP exposure and phenotypic changes in the offspring's central nervous system.
