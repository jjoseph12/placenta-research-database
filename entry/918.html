Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Using CRISPR/Cas9 technology, we knocked out both Suv39h2 and Prdm2 genes. Mouse extraembryonic ectoderm was collected at E6.5 and E7.5 stages for total RNA-seq sequencing, and three replicates of sequencing data were obtained before and after the gene knockout.

Extraction protocol

Pre-implantation embryos were obtained by in vitro fertilization (IVF). MII-stage oocytes were collected from 7-week-old superovulated C57BL/6N female mice by intraperitoneally injecting with 6 IU of pregnant mare’s serum gonadotropin (PMSG) and 6 IU of human chorionic gonadotrophin (hCG) (San-Sheng Pharmaceutical). MII oocytes were then transferred into G-IVF PLUS medium (Vitrolife) and inseminated with activated spermatozoa obtained from the caudal epididymides of adult PWK/PhJ male mice. The zona pellucida of each MII oocyte was punched to make a small hole using a Piezo-drill micromanipulator, and the spermatozoa capacitation was attained by 20-30 minutes incubation in G-IVF PLUS medium. At 4 hours post-insemination (hpi), fertilized oocytes were transferred and cultured in G-1 PLUS medium (Vitrolife) at 37°C under 5% CO2. The morula and blastocyst stage IVF embryos were harvested at 78 and 96 hpi, respectively. To obtain post-implantation embryos, female mice at 6.5 days post-coitum (dpc) were executed and the uteri were dissected and transferred into Dulbecco's phosphate-buffered saline (DPBS). Next, each decidua was carefully freed from the uterine muscle layers using properly sharpened forceps. 6.5 dpc embryos were carefully separated from deciduas. Reichart's membrane and the ectoplacental cone were also removed from the embryos. Embryos with zona pellucida and polar bodies removed were disrupted in TRIzol Reagent (Invitrogen) and total RNAs were isolated by chloroform extraction, coupled with isopropanol precipitation with 1/10 volume of 3M NaAc and 1 μL glycogen added to the aqueous phase of each sample. RNAs were washed twice with 75% ethanol before they were eluted with nuclease-free water. Purified RNAs were then subjected to library generation using SMARTer Stranded Total RNA-Seq Kit (Takara) following the manufacturer’s instructions. Briefly, random primers were used for reverse transcription, and the amplified cDNA was then subjected to ribosomal RNA depletion. Prepared RNA-seq libraries were sequenced on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 platform with paired ends and 150-bp read lengths at Novogene Co., Ltd.
