Methods text

Assay description

Here we isolated bone marrow of 3 mice per group (Lyz2cre and Lyz2cre:CITED2Fl/Fl) and differentiated into macrophages by culturing them in M-CSF containing media. These cells were stimulated with 100ng/ml LPS for 4hours, 10ng/ml IFNg for 6 hours or 20ng/ml IL4 for 18 hours. Total RNA was isolated and subjected to RNAseq analyses.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from indicated samples using the High Pure RNA Isolation Kit. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
