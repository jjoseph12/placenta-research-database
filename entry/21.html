Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNAseq profiling on single cells, generated after induction of hemogenic reprogramming in human dermal fibroblasts (HDFs, 2 donors), day4 and day6 cells after induction in HDFs with MD-GATA2 or MDmut-GATA2, in combination with GFI1B and FOS. Single cell RNAseq profiling of HDFs (2 donors) was used as controls.

Extraction protocol

Single cells were isolated using 10x Chromium by 10x Genomics. scRNA-seq libraries were prepared with Dual Index Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ Kit v3.1 (Dual Index) (10x Genomics) according to manufacturer’s instructions. Library size and quality check was performed using High Sensitivity D1000 ScreenTape with High Sensitivity D1000 Reagents (Agilent) in a 4200 TapeStation System (Agilent), according to the manufacturer’s protocol.

Main topic
This paper investigates the mitotic retention of hemogenic factors, specifically GATA2, during haematopoietic specification, demonstrating that GATA2 remains bound to chromatin throughout mitosis and is critical for definitive haematopoiesis.
