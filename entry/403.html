Main topic
This paper investigates the role of VE-cadherin in trophoblast migration and endovascular invasion during placental development in mice, finding that VE-cadherin deficiency leads to impaired spiral artery remodeling, reduced maternal blood flow, fetal growth restriction, and embryonic death.
