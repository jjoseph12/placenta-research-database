Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Animal work was approved by the North Carolina State University Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee, under protocols 16-045-B and 19-049-B. All mice were on a 14-hour/10-hour light/dark cycle at 30-70 % humidity, 22°C ± 4°C. 5-week-old C57Bl/6J (B) female mice were exposed for 5 weeks to 0, 1, or 50 ppm CdCl2 through drinking water (Sigma-Aldrich, 202908). At 10 weeks B mice were mated with unexposed CAST/EiJ (C) males. Cd exposure continued throughout mating and gestation. Embryos and placentae were dissected at e18.5; collected tissues were weighed, snap-frozen immediately upon dissection and stored at -80°C. Genomic DNA was isolated from four female 0 ppm and four female 50 ppm exposed placentae using the AllPrep DNA/RNA/miRNA kit (Qiagen). 100 ng of gDNA was digested with TaqaI (NEB) at 65⁰C for 2 hours followed by MspI (NEB) at 37⁰C overnight. Following enzymatic digestion, samples were used for library generation using the Ovation RRBS Methyl-Seq System (Tecan) following the manufacturer’s instructions. In brief, digested DNA was randomly ligated, and, following fragment end repair, bisulfite converted using the EpiTect Fast DNA Bisulfite Kit (Qiagen) following the Qiagen protocol. After conversion and clean-up, samples were amplified resuming the Ovation RRBS Methyl-Seq System protocol for library amplification and purification. Libraries were measured using the Agilent 2200 TapeStation System and quantified using the KAPA Library Quant Kit ABI Prism qPCR Mix (Roche). Libraries were sequenced on a NextSeq 550 using single end 75 bp sequencing.

Main topic
This paper investigates the effects of in utero cadmium exposure on the expression of the imprinted gene Cdkn1c in the murine placenta, finding that cadmium exposure leads to increased Cdkn1c expression and fetal growth restriction without altering DNA methylation at imprinting control regions.
