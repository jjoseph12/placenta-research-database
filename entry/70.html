Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The goal of this study is to investigate the transcriptomes of placentas from different types of hypertensive pregnancies. We identified pregnant people in the University of Michigan Biorepository for Understanding Maternal and Pediatric Health with hypertensive disorders affecting singleton, euploid gestations (N=36) along with non-hypertensive control subjects (N=12). Subjects were grouped as: (1) normotensive (N=12), (2) chronic hypertensive (N=13), (3) preterm preeclampsia with severe features (N=5), (4) term preeclampsia with severe features (N=11), (5) preterm superimposed preeclampsia (N=3), or (6) term superimposed preeclampsia (N=4). Bulk RNA sequencing of paraffin-embedded placental tissue was performed.

Extraction protocol

Placenta samples were formalin-fixed and paraffin-embedded; five 20-micron sections of each placenta sample were subjected to RNA extraction using the RNAstormTM kit (Cell Data Sciences, Fremont, CA, USA) as per the manufacturer’s instructions. Total RNA was processed for high-throughput sequencing using the QuantSeq 3′ mRNA-Seq Library Prep Kit FWD for Illumina (Lexogen, Greenland, NH, USA). Following mRNA generation, fragmentation, and cDNA synthesis, adaptor barcodes were ligated and final cDNA libraries were amplified by PCR and assessed for quality and concentration.

Main topic
This study analyzes placental transcriptomes in pregnancies complicated by preeclampsia (PreE) and superimposed preeclampsia on chronic hypertension (SI) to identify distinct gene expression profiles and refine the classification of SI versus PreE in the absence of chronic hypertension.
