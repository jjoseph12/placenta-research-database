Main topic
This paper investigates the role of Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor γ (PPARγ) in human placental development by analyzing the transcriptome of PPARγ target genes and revealing the involvement of lysyl oxidase (LOX) in human placental cytotrophoblast invasion.
