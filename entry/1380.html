Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The liver biopsy was performed after 14 days of treatment in 8 Holstein dairy cows in a 2 x 2 factorial arrangment with 5 days washout between treatments. A dye-swap reference design (reference = mixture of RNA from several bovine tissues) was used.

Extraction protocol

Biopsy tissue was weighted (~0.5-1.0 g) and immediately subjected to RNA extraction using ice-cold TRIZol. Genomic DNA was removed from RNA with DNAse (Qiagen) using RNeasy Mini Kit columns (Qiagen). Label Cy3,Cy5 Label protocol Labelled with Cy3 and Cy5 during cDNA synthesis from total RNA using Superscript III Kit (Invitrogen) as manufacturers instructions.
