Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Endothelial cells were isolated from placentas from pregnant wildtype (C57BL/6) and hepcidin-1 knockout (on the C57BL/6 background) dams after treatment with water (baseline) or 0.5ug/g lipopolysaccharide (LPS) on E15.5 for 6h. For each condition, we pooled endothelial cells from 5 placentas/pregnant mouse, from 4 different mice per group. For each group we have 4 biological replicates. The total number of samples is 16. Total RNA from placental endothelial cells was isolated and subjected to RNA-Seq.

Extraction protocol

Five placentas per pregnant dam were pooled and endothelial cells were isolated by magnetic separation using anti-CD31 magnetic beads on the MidiMACS system following the manufacturer's instructions (Miltenyi #130-095-927, #130-097-418). Total RNA was extracted using a RNeasy Micro kit (Qiagen #74004) following the manufacturer's instructions. Strand-specific RNA-Seq libraries were generated using Nugen Universal plus mRNA-Seq Kit. The workflow consisted of poly(A) RNA selection, RNA fragmentation and double-stranded cDNA generation using a mixture of random and oligo(dT) priming, followed by end repair to generate blunt ends, adaptor ligation, strand selection, and PCR amplification to produce the final library.

Main topic
This study uses mouse models to investigate the effects of maternal iron loading, inflammation, and their interaction on pregnancy outcomes, finding that the combination of iron excess and inflammation leads to embryo malformations and demise due to TNFα-mediated apoptosis.
