Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA from placental tissues obtained from 5 placenta accreta and 5 control patients at Caesarean section were extracted and performed RNA-seq.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using TRIzol solution according to the manufacturer’s instructions (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA) with strict quality control at each step. First, rRNA is removed from the extracted total RNA with a biotin-labeled specific probe (Ribo-Zero ™ rRNA Removal Kit). After purification, the RNA is fragmented under a certain temperature and ionic environment. Then use the random primers and reverse transcriptase to synthesize the first strand of cDNA with TruSeq® Stranded kit , and then use DNA polymerase I and RNaseH to synthesize the double-stranded cDNA. During the second-strand cDNA synthesis, the RNA template is removed and dTTP is replaced by dUTP. The double-stranded cDNA product was subsequently ligated with an "A" base and a linker. The ligation product will be amplified and the final cDNA library will be obtained after purification.
