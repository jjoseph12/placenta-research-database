Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

A total of 20 cytotrophoblast samples were divided into four groups according to the age (8-10 gestational week vs. 12-14 gestational week) and sex (male vs. female). One microgram of total RNA from each sample preparation was used for the construction of sequencing libraries,

Extraction protocol

Total RNA extration was performed using RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (Qiagen) following manufacturer's instructions Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 800 ng of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper explores the changes in mRNA levels in human placentas during the first trimester (8-10 weeks and 12-14 weeks of gestation) to understand the adaptative responses to increased oxygen levels during this period. The study identifies key genes and pathways involved in placental development and sexual dimorphism.
