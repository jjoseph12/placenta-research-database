Methods text

Assay description

Total RNAs in mTSCs from two independent experiments were extracted by TRIzol and subjected to RNA-Seq by MGISEQ 2000. The expression of level of each gene is normalized to RPKM.

Extraction protocol

mTSCs were harvested and extracted using Trizol reagent. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of PR-SET7, a methyltransferase, in trophoblasts and its relevance to recurrent miscarriage (RM), demonstrating that PR-SET7-mediated histone methylation is essential for repressing transcription of endogenous retroviruses, preventing double-stranded RNA stress and subsequent interferon response and necroptosis in trophoblasts.
