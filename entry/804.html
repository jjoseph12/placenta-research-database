Main topic
This paper focuses on the isolation and characterization of fetal stem cells from amniotic fluid (AF) to derive primary organoids, specifically targeting gastrointestinal, renal, and pulmonary origins, and demonstrates their potential for modeling prenatal diseases like congenital diaphragmatic hernia.
