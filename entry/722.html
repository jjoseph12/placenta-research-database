Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using the mirVana miRNA isolation kit (Ambion). RNA concentration was measured by Qubit RNA BR assay kit (ThermoFisher) and the quality of isolated RNA was checked using a bioanalyzer (Agilent). All samples were found to have a RIN above 7.5. RNA-seq libraries were prepared using the TruSeq Stranded mRNA sample preparation kit (Illumina) at the IGM Genomics Center at UC San Diego.

Main topic
This paper profiles the genomic organization and transcriptome of first trimester and term extravillous trophoblast (EVT) to define pathways and gene regulatory networks involved in the differentiation and maturation of this trophoblast lineage at the maternal–fetal interface, suggesting that term EVT undergoes senescence and endoreduplication, is primarily tetraploid, and lacks high rates of copy number variations.

Here, we extend these reports by performing comprehensive profiling of both the genomic organization and transcriptome of first trimester and term EVT.
