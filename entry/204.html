Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of transcriptomes in two cell lines, two samples in each cell line.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from cells using the RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen) followed by on-column DNase I treatment. RNA-seq libraries were prepared using the SureSelect Strand-Specific RNA Library Prep for Illumina Multiplexed Sequencing kit (Agilent).

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of tripartite-motif family-like 2 (TRIML2), a eutherian-specific gene, in the evolution of highly invasive placentation and extended pregnancy in eutherian mammals by comparative genomic studies and gene expression profiling.
