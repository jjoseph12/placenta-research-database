Assay description

Primary cultured cytotrophoblasts (CTBs) obtained from term placental samples were subjected to RNA extraction, quantification, and evulation, followed by hybridization on Illumina beadchip. Three biological replicates were used (N=12 total samples).

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of IL-6 and IL-11 on trophoblast differentiation and invasion in preeclampsia (PE) by examining the role of interferon-stimulated gene 15 (ISG15) in primary trophoblast cultures using whole genome microarray analysis.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication investigates the expression and role of interferon-stimulated gene 15 (ISG15) in preeclampsia, demonstrating that reduced decidual extravillous trophoblast ISG15 expression is associated with impaired differentiation, invasion, and increased inflammation.
Source excerpt
The current study focused on the impact of separate vs. combined IL-6 and IL-11 treatments in primary trophoblast cultures using whole genome microarray analysis. This initial microarray analysis identified down-regulation of interferon-stimulated gene 15 (ISG15)
