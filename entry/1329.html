Main topic
This paper describes a new strategy to study allele-specific DNA methylation in the human placenta for the discovery of novel imprinted genes, confirming known differentially methylated regions (DMRs) and identifying two imprinted genes (DNMT1, AIM1). The study indicates that while many genomic regions have allele-specific methylation in the placenta, only a small subset are associated with allele-specific transcription.
