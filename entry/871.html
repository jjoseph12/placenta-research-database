Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

36 samples were run on the Illumina Epic methylation array. The 36 samples include three day 0 samples from each of the 6 patients, for a total of 18 samples. Each patient has one day +4 2% sample and day +4 21% sample from the same iPSC clone for a total of 12 samples. The remaining six samples are the MSC patient samples from which the iPSCs were derived.

Extraction protocol

DNA was isolated using DNeasy kit (Qiagen) and quantified using Qubit dsDNA BR assay kit (ThermoFisher).

Main topic
This paper models preeclampsia (PE) using human induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSC) derived from placentas of pregnancies with and without PE, finding that PE-iPSC-derived trophoblast showed a defect in syncytialization and a blunted response to hypoxia, recapitulating multiple defects associated with placental dysfunction.
