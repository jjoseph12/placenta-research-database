Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

10X single-cell RNA-seq measured on two human naïve stem-cell populations that were maintained in either RCL medium (RPMI based medium supplemented with CHIR99021 and LIF and B27 WITHOUT Insulin) to induce PrE/ExEM cells, or in BAP(J) medium, to induce TE lineage.

Extraction protocol

RNA extracted according to 10x Genomics Chromium v3.1 Dual Index system kit instructions 10x Genomics Chromium v3.1 Dual Index system (5000 cell target cell recovery)

Main topic
This paper describes the creation of stem-cell-derived embryo models (SEMs) from human naive embryonic stem cells that recapitulate post-implantation human embryos up to 13–14 days after fertilization, providing a platform to study early human development.
