Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Human trophoblast stem cells were cultured as cytotrophoblast cells and also differentiated into syncytiotrophoblast using a differentiation medium. Cells were mock-infected or infected with T. gondii (RH-YFP strain) with a multiplicity of infection (MOI) of 5. RNA-seq was performed in order to evaluate the differences in transcriptome between both cells and the changes after infection. The experiment was designed with 3 biological replicates for each condition.

Extraction protocol

Qiagen Rneasy RNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit following manufacturer's protocols.

Main topic
This study investigates the interaction between the placenta and pathogens, specifically Toxoplasma gondii and Listeria monocytogenes, using human trophoblast stem cells to understand the molecular mechanisms of placental resistance to congenital infections.
