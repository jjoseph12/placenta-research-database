Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA samples were extracted placentas collected from 7 female mice fed a zinc deficient diet (10 mg/kg zinc) and 10 female mice fed a zinc replete diet (40 mg/kg zinc). For each female mouse, except for 3 in which only one placenta was available, 2 placentas, one from a male offspring and one from a female offspring were selected. Overall, gene expression in 31 placentas were analysed.

Main topic
This study investigates the physiological mechanisms by which zinc is important to placental morphogenesis and the maternal blood pressure changes during pregnancy using a mouse model of moderate zinc deficiency, finding that adequate zinc status is required for correct placental morphogenesis and appropriate maternal blood pressure adaptations to pregnancy.
