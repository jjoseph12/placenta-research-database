Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

For SCNT embryos or injected SCNT embryos 3-8 replicates were performed for each stage . As the control, 3-6 replicates were performed for each stage of wild type samples

Extraction protocol

Harvested cells were washed three times in 0.5% BSA-PBS solution and transferred into the lysis buffer. cDNA were synthesized using primers with 24-nt poly(dT),then a universal tail was added to the second strand cDNAs. After 18-20 cyclesof PCR, the PCR product is purified by QIAquick PCR purification kit(28104). The prepared cDNA are sheared using Covaris S220. Fragment ends are repaired using NEBNext End Repair Module(E6050), then a NEBNext dA-Tailing Module(E6053) was used to yield a protruding 3- 'A' base for ligation of Illumina's adapters.After adapter ligation DNA was PCR amplified with Illumina primers for 8-12 cycles. library fragments of 200-500bp were band isolated from an agarose gel.

Main topic
This paper developed a somatic cell nuclear transfer embryo biopsy system and used single-cell transcriptome sequencing to identify that inactivation of Kdm4b and Kdm5b function as barriers for two-cell and four-cell arrest of cloned embryos, respectively, and that co-injection of these factors can improve blastocyst development and the production of cloned mice.
