Methods text

Assay description

Pregnant mice were infected with GBS and placentas were collected at time points post infection for analysis. Results were validated in human placental macrophages

Extraction protocol

human placental macrophages were isolated by selection of CD14+ expression by magnetic beads from single cell suspension 10x single cell RAN seq V3 mouse placental cells were dissociated and enzymatically digest, red blood cells were lysed and remaining cells were washed and run for scRNA-Seq 10x single cell RAN seq V3

Main topic
This paper investigates the placental response to Group B Streptococcus (GBS) infection in mice, focusing on the role of the bacterial toxin β‐hemolysin/cytolysin (β‐h/c) in modulating the function of innate immune cells and its impact on placental inflammation and infection outcomes.
