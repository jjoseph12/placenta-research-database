Methods text

Assay description

RNA was harvested at 7 time points from TS cells cultured without Fgf4 over two weeks for transcriptome analysis. RNA was collected from undifferentiated TS cells at T=0hr, and differentiated TS cells cultured without Fgf4 for 24-hrs, 48-hrs, 72-hrs, 6 days, 10 days, and 14 days. Mouse TS cells were ChIPed with antibodies specific to Ap-2g (Tcfap2c), Brg1 (Smarca4), Eomes, Ets2, and Gata3 vs. Input DNA from TS cells

Extraction protocol

ChIP-chip experiments were performed as previously described with minor modifications (Kidder, Palmer and Knott 2008). Mouse TS cells (TSC-BK12) were grown on feeder-free dishes for 2 passages to remove iMEFs, and chemically crosslinked with 1% formaldehyde (Sigma) for 20 hours at 4 deg C and subsequently sonicated. Sonicated cell extracts equivalent to 2 X 10^6 cells were used for immunoprecipitation assays. 5 micrograms purified ChIP-enriched DNA (Tcfap2c-chip, Smarca4-chip, Eomes-chip, Ets2-chip, Gata3-chip, and Input) was amplified, DNase treated, labeled, and hybridized to Affymetrix mouse promoter 1.0R tiling arrays. At least three biological replicates were used for each ChIP-chip analysis. Peak detection and gene annotation were performed as previously described (Kidder, Palmer and Knott 2008; Kidder, Yang and Palmer 2008). Briefly, peak detection was performed using the TileMap (Ji and Wong 2005) application in CisGenome (Ji, Jiang, Ma, Johnson, Myers and Wong 2008). Enrichment peaks were annotated with the closest gene using build 36 of the mouse genome. Main topic
This paper investigates the transcriptional networks in trophoblast stem cells (TS cells) by examining the targets of transcription factors (TCFAP2C, EOMES, ETS2, and GATA3) and a chromatin remodeling factor (SMARCA4) using ChIP-chip analysis, transcriptome analysis, and genome-wide analysis of histone H3 acetylation.
