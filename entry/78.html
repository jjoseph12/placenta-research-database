Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We differentiated primed PSC into TE-like cells by treatment with BMP4/IWP2 and collected cells at 12h, 24h and 4 days (96h) of treatment. We also collected primed H9 hESC-derived TSC. Single cell libraries were created using the 10x Genomics technology with a target cell number of 1000/cells per sample, pooled and submitted for sc RNA-seq.

Extraction protocol

Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3' reagent kit v3.3 from 10X Genomics was used to extract RNA from single cells. Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3' reagent kit v3.3 from 10X Genomics was used to rpepare the libraries from single cells

Main topic
This paper focuses on deriving functional trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) from primed human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) using a modified two-step protocol involving BMP4 and a newly developed TSC medium, demonstrating their similarity to placenta-derived and naive hPSC-derived TSCs in terms of transcriptome and function, offering a new platform for pregnancy research.
