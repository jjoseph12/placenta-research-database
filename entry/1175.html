Main topic
This paper investigates the functional antagonism between ΔNp63α and GCM1 in regulating human trophoblast stemness and differentiation, demonstrating how manipulating this antagonism facilitates the derivation of trophoblast stem cells from term placenta.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that the stemness and differentiation of human trophoblasts are regulated by the functional antagonism between GCM1 and ΔNp63α, and manipulating this antagonism under hypoxia allows the derivation of trophoblast stem cells from term placentas.
Source excerpt
Here we demonstrate that the induction efficiency of cytotrophoblast is determined by functional antagonism of the placental transcription factor GCM1 and the stemness regulator ΔNp63α.
