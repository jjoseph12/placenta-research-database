Main topic
This paper examines the role of NRF2 in respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) susceptibility after neonate lung injury, and whether maternal supplementation of sulforaphane (SFN) protects newborn mice against hyperoxic lung injury. The study investigates the impact of hyperoxia-induced lung injury in developing murine lungs on later-life RSV susceptibility and the protective effects of maternal SFN supplementation against hyperoxic lung injury in newborns.
