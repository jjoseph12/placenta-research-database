Main topic
This study investigates the role of myeloid-derived suppressor cells (MDSC), particularly polymorphonuclear MDSC (PMN-MDSC), in unexplained recurrent pregnancy loss (URPL). The findings indicate that increased TRAIL expression and reduced DcR2 in decidual PMN-MDSC contribute to excessive apoptosis of PMN-MDSC in URPL, potentially impairing the viability of decidual PMN-MDSC.
