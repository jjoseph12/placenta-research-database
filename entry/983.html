Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

mRNA profiles of three cell lines at five stages and two different conditions were generated by deep sequencing, using HiSeq 2500.

Extraction protocol

The placenta tissue were extracted and immediately immersed in an RNA stabilization reagent (RNase later sample and assay technology; Qiagen, Valencia, CA).The placentas were then homogenized in Qiazol (Qiagen) with a pestle homogenizer, and total RNA was extracted from the homogenized mixture with a RNeasy mini kit (Qiagen). The total RNA of cells were prepared using the TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer's recommendations. To eliminate potential contamination with genomic DNA, the samples were subjected to additional treatment with recombinant DNaseⅠ(Qiagen) for 30min at 370C. The resulting 8 individual samples (4 from the severely preeclampsia group and 4 from the control group) and cells were then assessed with a spectrophotometer to determine their quality and total RNA concentration (NanoDrop 2000c, Thermo Scientific) RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
