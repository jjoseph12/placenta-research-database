Main topic
This paper investigates the role of mechanistic Target of Rapamycin Complex 1 (mTORC1) in regulating the expression of genes encoding Electron Transport Chain (ETC) proteins and stimulating oxidative phosphorylation in primary human trophoblast cells, and examines the relationship between mTOR signaling and ETC protein expression in placentas of infants with intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR). The study finds that mTORC1 positively regulates mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation in trophoblast cells and that ETC protein expression is decreased in IUGR placentas.

We hypothesized that mechanistic Target of Rapamycin Complex 1 (mTORC1) is a positive regulator of key genes encoding Electron Transport Chain (ETC) proteins and stimulates oxidative phosphorylation in trophoblast and that ETC protein expression is down-regulated in placentas of infants with intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR).
