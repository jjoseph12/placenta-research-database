Pregnant rats treated with TCDD and Oil at gestational day (gd) 6.5 and uterine-placental interface tissue was collected at gd 13.5.

Extraction protocol

Single cells were captured into droplets using 10x chromium device followed by preparation of single-cell cDNA libraries with the 10x protocol.

Main topic
This study examines the effects of TCDD exposure on pregnancy and placentation in a rat model, evaluating the roles of AHR and CYP1A1 in TCDD action, and finds that TCDD-mediated placental adaptations depend on maternal AHR signaling affecting uterine and trophoblast cell dynamics.
