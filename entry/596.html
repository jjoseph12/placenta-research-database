Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNAseq of chorioamnion-decidua in a Rhesus model of intrauterine infection/inflammation (3 ctrl, 3 LPS 48h; 3 E coli, 2 E coli +Abx)

Extraction protocol

chorioamnion decidua samples were collected right after delivery, sna-frozen, and RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the relationship between the intensity of the immune response to intrauterine infection (IUI) and the induction of preterm labor (PTL) in rhesus macaques, using lipopolysaccharide (LPS) or live Escherichia coli to induce IUI.
