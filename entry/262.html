Methods text

Assay description

The single cells from hTSC-organoids and hTSC-organoids infected with ZIKV at MOI 1 and 10 for scRNA-seq

Extraction protocol

The Day 8 hTSC-organoids were removed from Matrigel using Cell Recovery Solution at 4℃ for 30 minutes (min). The free hTSC-organoids were further digested using TrypLE Express Enzyme in a shaker at 37℃ for 20 min, and pipetted every 5 min. The cells were collected by centrifugation and filtered through a 40 μm Cell Strainer. The cell viability was detected using trypan blue staining. The construction of transcriptome libraries was performed following the standard protocol of 10x Genomics (v3). Briefly, the cDNA was purified after GEM generation, barcode addition, GEM-RT reaction and bead cleanup. The GEX libraries were generated following the protocol of 10x Genomics Chromium Single Cell 3’ Reagent Kits (v3). The concentration of each library was accurately detected for 10x Genomics sequencing.

Main topic
This study investigates the infectivity and pathological effects of Zika virus (ZIKV) on placental trophoblast progenitor cells in early human embryos, demonstrating that human trophoblast stem cells (hTSCs) are permissive to ZIKV infection and that resistance to ZIKV increases with hTSC differentiation.
