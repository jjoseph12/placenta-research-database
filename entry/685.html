Methods text

Assay description

Comparative gene expression profiling analysis based on RNA-seq data for the placenta, heart, brain, and face of E10.5 embryos from young vs. aged C57BL/6 females

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from tissues using the Invitrogen™ mirVana™ miRNA Isolation Kit RNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep kit.

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of advanced maternal age (AMA) on the transcriptomes of placentas, brains, hearts, and facial prominences from mid-gestation mouse conceptuses, revealing that AMA increases transcriptional heterogeneity, particularly in the fetal brain, and that defects in placental development likely induce these brain alterations.
