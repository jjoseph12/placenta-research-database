Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We employed QuantSeq method to analyzed the global expression profile of the cytotrophoblast organoids (4 replicates, CTB-ORG 1-4) and compared to the profile of the tissue of origin i.e. villous cytotrophoblast (3 replicates, vCTB 1-3) as well as to in vitro differentiated syncytiotrophoblast (3 replicates, STB1-3) and placental fibroblasts (2 replicates, FIB 1-2).

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using the RNeasy kit (Qiagen) with the DNaseI (Qiagen) digest performed on the column according to manufacturer's protocol Libraries were constructed using the QuantSeq 3′ mRNA-Seq Library Prep Kit FWD for Illumina (Lexogen), according to manufacturer's protocol and multiplexed.

Main topic
This paper focuses on generating human trophoblast organoids from villous cytotrophoblasts (vCTBs) to model the early human placenta development and study pregnancy complications like intrauterine growth restriction and preeclampsia.
