Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Bisulfite converted DNA from 5 1st trimester trisomy 16 placentas, 5 chromosomally normal 1st trimester placentas, 10 third trimester placentas from confined placental mosaicism placentas and 10 chromosomally normal 3rd trimester placentas

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was purified using the Dneasy Blood and Tissue kit (Qiagen) followed by bisufite conversion using the EZ DNA methylation kit (Zymo) following manufacturer's protocols.
