Assay description

Analysis of similarities and differences in the transcriptomes among mouse embryos and stem cell derived synthetic embryos, generated with the standard protocol (Sozen et al., 2018) and the new protocol established in this study

Extraction protocol

Samples were dissociated to single cells by cutting with needles and then incubation in TrypLE Express dissociation solution for 15 minutes followed by reaction inactivation with media containing 15% fetal bovine serum, filtration and 2 washes in PBST (0.02% Tween-20. inDrops v3

Main topic
The paper describes the generation of induced ETX embryos (iETX embryos) by replacing XEN cells with ESCs transiently expressing transcription factor Gata4, and combining them with ESCs and TS cells. iETX embryos establish a robust anterior signaling center that migrates unilaterally to break embryo symmetry, and gastrulate generating embryonic and extra-embryonic mesoderm and definitive endoderm.
