Main topic
This study investigates the role of pigment epithelium-derived factor (PEDF) in regulating feto-placental angiogenesis during pregnancy, finding that trophoblast-derived PEDF limits angiogenesis in late pregnancy by modulating VEGF signaling.

Reviewed answer The study investigates the role of pigment epithelium-derived factor (PEDF) as a novel trophoblast-derived anti-angiogenic factor that restricts feto-placental angiogenesis in late pregnancy, dependent on and modulating VEGF signaling.

Among the proteins secreted by trophoblast in late pregnancy, pigment epithelium-derived factor (PEDF) was identified as a novel trophoblast-derived suppressor of feto-placental angiogenesis.
