Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Suv39h2 was knocked down in rat trophoblast stem cells using lentiviral delivery of shRNA. Following selection by puromycin, cells were differentiated. Cells treated with either control shRNA or Suv39h2-specific shRNA were collected and RNA was isolated.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from cells and tissues using TRIzol reagent (cat no. 15596018, Thermo Fisher, Waltham, MA). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with a HiSeq2000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility. Reads from *.fastq files were mapped to the rat reference genome (Ensembl Rnor_5.0.78) using CLC Genomics Workbench 12.0 (Qiagen, Redwood City, CA). mRNA abundance was expressed in reads per kb of exon per million reads mapped. A false discovery rate of 0.05 was used as a cutoff for significant differential expression (euglycemia vs hyperglycemia).
