Methods text

Assay description

RNA-Seq of undifferentiated and differentiated murine shLuc and Kdm5b TS cells

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvest using RNeasy and miRNeasy Qiagen kits. Double-stranded cDNA was sonicated and end-repaired using the End-It DNA End-Repair kit (Epicentre), followed by addition of a single A nucleotide, and ligation of PE adapters (Illumina) or custom indexed adapters. PCR was performed using Phusion High Fidelity PCR master mix

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of the H3K4 demethylase, KDM5B, in regulating the H3K4 methylome and expression landscapes of trophoblast stem (TS) cells, demonstrating its involvement in TS cell self-renewal and differentiation by modulating H3K4 methylation.
