Extraction protocol

Harvested cells were washed three times in 0.5% BSA-PBS solution and transferred into the lysis buffer. cDNA were synthesized using primers with 24-nt poly(dT),then a universal tail was added to the second strand cDNAs. After 18-20 cyclesof PCR, the PCR product is purified by QIAquick PCR purification kit(28104). The prepared cDNA are sheared using Covaris S220. Fragment ends are repaired using NEBNext End Repair Module(E6050), then a NEBNext dA-Tailing Module(E6053) was used to yield a protruding 3- 'A' base for ligation of Illumina's adapters.After adapter ligation DNA was PCR amplified with Illumina primers for 8-12 cycles. library fragments of 200-500bp were band isolated from an agarose gel. 500-1000 cells were washed three times in 0.5% BSA-PBS solution. Lysates were clarified from micrococcal nuclease treated nuclei and histone-DNA complexes were isolated with antibody. Libraries were prepared according to illumina TruSeq DNA LT Sample Prep Kit( FC-121-2001, FC-121-2002) Harvested cells were washed three times in 0.5% BSA-PBS solution and transferred into the lysis buffer. After lysed for 3h at 50°C, naked genomic DNA were relesed. The released naked DNA was digested by MspI(Fermentas,ER0541),The digested DNA was then end-repaired and tailed with an extra A to the 3'blunted ends.Illumina standard premethylated indexed adaptors were ligated with the dA-tailed DNA fragments. Bisulfite conversion was then performed using the MethyCode bisulfite conversion kit (Invitrogen,MECOV-50) followed by two rounds of PCR enrichment.After PCR enrichment, DNA fragments between 200 and 500bp were size-selected by a 12% native polyacrylamide TBE gel.

Main topic
This paper developed a somatic cell nuclear transfer embryo biopsy system and used single-cell transcriptome sequencing to identify that inactivation of Kdm4b and Kdm5b function as barriers for two-cell and four-cell arrest of cloned embryos, respectively, and that co-injection of these factors can improve blastocyst development and the production of cloned mice.
