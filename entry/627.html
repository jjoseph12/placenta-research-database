Extraction protocol

Placenta tissues were removed from the mice after they were sacrificed at gestational day 15.5 and were immediately flash frozen in liquid nitrogen. RNA were extracted from the tissues using Trizol following the manufacturer's protocol. RNA-seq libraries were prepared from 1 ug total RNA using the NEBNext Directional Ultra RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (New England Biolabs), with initial polyA+ isolation. Main topic
This study explores the effects of maternal choline supplementation (MCS) on various epigenetic markers in the placenta of pregnant mice, specifically examining placental imprinted gene expression, DNA methylation patterns, and microRNA (miRNA) abundance.
