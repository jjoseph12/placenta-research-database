Methods text

Assay description

FGR was defined as an SGA infant (birthweight <10th percentile for gestational age and sex based on a Canadian growth reference) with signs of placental insufficiency, and 20 normotensive suspected FGR placentas were purchased from a biobank. Genome-wide mRNA expression was assessed by Affymetrix Human Gene 1.0 ST Array chips and aggregated with samples from GSE75010. Unsupervised clustering analysis was applied to the combined dataset (N=97), followed by pathway enrichment analysis and correlative analysis with clinical information and histological lesions in these placentas.
