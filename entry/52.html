Methods text

Assay description

Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data for human placental and fetal membrane tissues and cells from term not in labor women

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated from placental and FM tissues and cell lines using RNeasy Mini Kit (QIAGEN, Germantown, MD) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Using RNA extracted from placental and FM tissues (n=5 per tissue) and cells (n=3 per cell type), a cDNA library was prepared according to the Smart-3SEQ protocol
