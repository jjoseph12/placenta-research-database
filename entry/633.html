Methods text

Assay description

Bisulfite-converted DNA from 48 placentas belonging to transcriptional clusters 1, 2, 3, and 5 were hybridized to Illumina Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChips. Methylation data was analyzed separately, as well as integrated with matched gene expression information.

Extraction protocol

To control for batch affect, samples were randomized into groups of 20 for processing. A 100 mg aliquot was used to prepare an mRNA extract using Trizol followed by RNAeasy spin columns. RNA purity and integrity were determined using 28S to 18S rRNA ratio (Agilent Bioanalyzer).

Main topic
This paper investigates DNA methylation alterations underlying transcriptional development of preeclampsia (PE) subtypes, specifically canonical PE in cluster 2 and immunological PE in cluster 3, using placental samples.
