Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Placental mRNA profiles of e18.5 fetuses of Sham- and SG-operated dams.

Extraction protocol

Placentas were harvested at e18.5, flash frozen in liquid nitrogen, and RNA was extracted using TRI reagent (Sigma-Aldrich, Cat. No. T9424). RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of sleeve gastrectomy (SG) in lean female mice on pregnancy outcomes and offspring metabolic health. The findings suggest that elevated Glp1 signaling, rather than weight loss, contributes to the increased prevalence of small-for-gestational age (SGA) births following bariatric surgery, with metabolic consequences for the offspring.
