Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was obtained from placentas taken from patients in the following groups: 1) preterm preeclampsia (n=6); 2) preterm preeclampsia with HELLP syndrome (n=6); and 3) preterm controls (n=5).

Main topic
This study uses a systems biology approach to investigate maternal and placental compartments in preeclampsia, aiming to identify novel pathways involved in the disease and its various aspects, including placental disease, fetal and maternal outcomes, and environmental factors.
