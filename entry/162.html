Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA was prepared from whole tissue myometrium and decidua of control (n=4) and Ezh2 cKO (n=3) and E7.5 of pregnancy. One control myometrial sample was excluded due to failed QC. Decidua was the tissue of primary interest, with myometrium serving as reference tissue. Next generation sequencing was performed using Illumina HiSeq 4000.

Extraction protocol

Implantation sites at E7.5 were dissected to separate the decidua from overlying myometrium and each layer was washed in cold PBS and homogenized in TRIzol (Thermo Fisher Scientific), for a total of n=4 wild-typecontrol and n=3 Ezh2 cKO isolates. RNA was subsequently purified by phenol chloroform extraction. DNAse digestion and RNA cleanup was performed using the RNase-Free DNase set (QIAGEN) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. RNA libraries were prepared according to Illumina's instructions, using a TruSeq mRNA kit.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of EZH2, a histone methyltransferase, in suppressing wound-healing responses in the decidua during early pregnancy in mice, finding that EZH2 deficiency leads to pregnancy failure due to ectopic myofibroblast formation and altered gene expression.
