Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from uterine tissue using TriReagent according to the manufacturer's recommendations. Libraries were prepared from poly-A containing RNAs according to protocols provided by the TruSeq RNA sample preparation kits (Illumina). Single cells were captured into droplets using 10x chromium device followed by preparation of single-cell cDNA libraries with the 10x protocol.

Main topic
This study examines the effects of TCDD exposure on pregnancy and placentation in a rat model, evaluating the roles of AHR and CYP1A1 in TCDD action, and finds that TCDD-mediated placental adaptations depend on maternal AHR signaling affecting uterine and trophoblast cell dynamics.
