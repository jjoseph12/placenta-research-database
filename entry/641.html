Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To investigate the impact of placental SLC2A3 deficiency, we performed a transcriptomic analysis on fetal pancreases at mid-gestation.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using RNeasy Mini Kit (Ref# 74104; QIAGEN) Assessment of the RNA samples integrity and quality, library generation and RNA-Seq (2x150 cycles, 80 million paired-end reads/sample; NovSeq6000, Illumina, San Diego, CA, USA) was conducted by the Genomics Shared Resource Core Facility, Uni-versity of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus (Aurora, CO)

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of impaired placental SLC2A3 glucose transport on fetal pancreas development in sheep, focusing on transcriptomic analysis of fetal pancreases from SLC2A3-deficient fetuses to identify altered pathways involved in growth and metabolism.
