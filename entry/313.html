Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using a RNeasy kit with on-column DNase digestion (Qiagen) and 0.5 ug used for library preparation with a TruSeq RNA Sample Preparation Kit v2 (Illumina). The libraries were multiplexed and then sequenced on a HiSeq2500 (Illumina) to generate 30 million paired end 75 bp reads. Extraction of RNAs was performed according to the eCLIP protocol (Van Nostrand et al. 2016 Nat Methods.) Libraries were sequenced 50 bp single end on HiSeq 4000 (Illumina).

This paper studies the role of PEG10, a retrotransposon-derived gene, in mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) and trophoblast stem cells (TSCs), focusing on its interaction with the deubiquitinating enzyme USP9X and its function in TSC differentiation.
