Main topic
This paper analyzes microarray expression data from 55 mouse tissues to demonstrate that quantitative transcriptional co-expression is a powerful predictor of gene function, challenging the traditional view that tissue-specific expression alone is indicative of gene function in mammals.

Reviewed answer Generation and analysis of quantitative microarray expression data across 55 mouse tissues to show that co-expression is a powerful predictor of gene function.
Source excerpt
We generated microarray expression data for nearly 40,000 known and predicted mRNAs in 55 mouse tissues, using custom-built oligonucleotide arrays. We show that quantitative transcriptional co-expression is a powerful predictor of gene function.
