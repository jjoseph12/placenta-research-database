Methods text

Assay description

Time-mated guinea pigs were either infected with 2E5 PFU of GPCMV or injected with PBS. After animals were euthanized at 21 days post-infection, samples of placenta were stored in RNAlater until RNA extraction and next-generation sequencing.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using the Rneasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) with the on-column DNase digestion Dual indexed TruSeq stranded mRNA libraries (Illumina) were prepared and sequenced by Illumina RNA-Seq (NextSeq 550 high-output 75-bp single-end run).

Main topic
This study investigates the placental response to guinea pig cytomegalovirus (GPCMV) infection at different gestational times, finding that infection at 35 days gestation (dGA) leads to significant changes in placental gene expression compared to infection at 21 dGA.
