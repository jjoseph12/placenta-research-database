Assay description

25 samples were analyzed. Developmental stages e8.5, e9.5 and e11.5 (embryos from all 3, placenta for only e9.5 and e11.5). 5 biological replicates for each.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using TriZOL (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Main topic
This paper presents a genome-wide analysis of alternative isoform expression in mouse embryos and placenta during early development (E8.5, E9.5 and E11.5) using a splicing-sensitive exon microarray, revealing that alternative splicing is frequent and involved in important developmental processes.
