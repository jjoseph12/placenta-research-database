Extraction protocol

Placentae were isolated at e18.5, weighed, snap-frozen immediately and stored at −80°C. Frozen tissues were homogenized in lysis buffer and RNA was isolated via AllPrep DNA/RNA/miRNA kit (Qiagen). cDNA libraries for Illumina sequencing were constructed via the NEBNext Ultra Directional RNA Library Prep Kit (NEB) and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (NEB) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. NovaSeq S4, utilizing a 150x2 bp paired end S4 sequencing RNA from Jeg-3 cells was isolated via NucleoSpin RNA kit (Macherey-Nagel). cDNA libraries for Illumina sequencing were constructed via the NEBNext Ultra Directional RNA Library Prep Kit (NEB) and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (NEB) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. Main topic
This study investigates the role of the lncRNA Tuna in cadmium-induced placental dysfunction and fetal growth restriction, focusing on its impact on the NRF2-mediated oxidative stress response pathway.
