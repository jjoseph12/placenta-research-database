Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of gene expression patterns of cells was performed with the single-cell transcriptome analysis following 96h of culture. Single-cell transcriptome analysis was performed using an inDrops v3 barcoding scheme, and the data preprocessing was performed according to the Github inDrops analysis pipeline in order to generate count matrices (https://github.com/indrops/indrops). The transcriptomic analysis comprises three replicates of SB-EP, two replicates of SB-2iLif and one replicate of natural mouse embryo cells at E4.5.

Extraction protocol

Blastoids (n=100 per group) were exposed to Tryple Express Select X10 (Thermofisher A1217701) for 15 mins in 37°C, and subsequently dissected with glass capillaries of different diameters. For ESC-derived blastoid group; total of 1,154 cells including 752 ESCs, 350 TSCs 52 PE-like cells extracted. For EPSCderived blastoid group; total of 2,350 cells including 1,087 EPSC, 1,140 TSCs 123 PE-like cells extracted. Indrop v3

Main topic
The paper describes the generation of induced ETX embryos (iETX embryos) by replacing XEN cells with ESCs transiently expressing transcription factor Gata4, and combining them with ESCs and TS cells. iETX embryos establish a robust anterior signaling center that migrates unilaterally to break embryo symmetry, and gastrulate generating embryonic and extra-embryonic mesoderm and definitive endoderm.
