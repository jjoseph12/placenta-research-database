Main topic
This study investigates the impact of maternal urinary tract infection (UTI) on perinatal outcomes in mice, demonstrating that localized cystitis can lead to intrauterine growth restriction and elevated proinflammatory cytokines in the maternal serum, suggesting a link between UTI and adverse pregnancy outcomes.
