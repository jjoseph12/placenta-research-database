Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq on human trophoblast stem cells treated with either a JNK inhibitor (SP600125) or DMSO control.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted with QIAGEN's AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit, and treated with Ambion's TURBO DNA-free Kit. 10-100 ng total RNA was treated with the NEBNext rRNA Depletion Kit. Library was generated with the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina.
