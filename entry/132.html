Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of monosomy X on an in vitro model for early human placenta using X-monosomic human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs), revealing impaired secretion of placental growth factor and chorionic gonadotropin in X-monosomic trophoblast-like cells.
