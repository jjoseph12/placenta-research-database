Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptional, physiological, and phenotypic differences of the immune disorder between severe combined immunodeficient (SCID) mouse and pig models, focusing on the recombination activating gene-2 (RAG-2). The research aims to determine if SCID pigs can serve as a useful model for studying phenotypes associated with late-onset or complex diseases.
