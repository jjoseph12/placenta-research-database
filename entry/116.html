Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq data compares gene expression in mouse trophoblast stem cells upon modulation of the Airn and Kcnq1ot1 lncRNAs or upon knockdown of HNRNPK.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using Trizol (Invitrogen). RNA-Seq libraries were prepared using Stranded mRNA-Seq Kits (Kapa Biosciences) and RNA HyperPrep Kits with RiboErase (Kapa Biosciences).

Main topic
This study investigates the relationships between long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs) and Polycomb Repressive Complexes (PRCs) in mouse trophoblast stem cells, finding that genome architecture, lncRNA abundance, and CpG island DNA play important roles in PRC-induced chromatin modifications within lncRNA target domains.
