Methods text

Assay description

The placental junctional zone was dissected from 8 Control and 8 UE litters and stored at -80ºC. Single nucleus suspensions were made, pooling together 2x 4 junctional zones from Control litters (offspring sex: 2M, 2F) and 2x 4 junctional zones from UE litters (offspring sex: 2M, 2F), totalling 4 different samples. Suspensions were FACS sorted for DRAQ5 positive events. Single nucleus RNA sequencing libraries were generated using the 10X Chromium single cell 3' reagents.

Extraction protocol

Females were time-mated and on day 16 of gestation, pregnant females were killed by cervical dislocation and uteri removed. Each fetus and its corresponding placenta was identified and placentas were then dissected and the endocrine region of the placenta (junctional zone) immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen. Samples were stored at -80ºC until further analysis. Samples were pooled to create 2 x control samples and 2 x UE samples (4 placenta per sample). Samples were thawed immediately before nucleus dissociation. Placenta were homogenized using a dounce homogenizer in ice cold homogenate buffer containing DRAQ5. These were then centrifuged at 900xg for 10 minutes and pellets were resuspended in 25% Optiprep solution. Suspensions were layered on top of 29% optiprep and centrifuged for 20 minutes at 13,500xg. Nuclei pellets were then removed from the optiprep and resuspended in wash buffer and passed through a 40um cell strainer prior to FACS sorting. Saples were FACS sorted with gating set according to size, grnaularity and DRAQ5+ events to capture singlets and remove debris. Each sample was sorted into their own tubes and sequencing libraries were generated from these samples. Library construction was perfomed using 10X Genomics Chromium Single Cell 3’ Reagent kits (Version 3.1) according to the standardised protocol. Single nucleus RNA sequencing
