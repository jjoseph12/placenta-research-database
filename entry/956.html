Assay description

Rhesus Monkey placenta RNA-seq data. Illumina Hiseq2000 were used for sequencing.

Extraction protocol

RNA was prepared from Rhesus monkey placenta using the RNeasy Plus Mini kit (Qiagen). RNA-seq libraries were constructed according to Illumima protocol and sequenced with the Illumina Hiseq 2000.
