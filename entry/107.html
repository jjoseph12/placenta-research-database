Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Cord blood and placenta samples were collected from 48 pregnancies that met the enrolment criteria. Samples with low quality DNA were removed. The methylation profile was determined for 40 whole blood samples and 41 placenta samples.

Extraction protocol

A fetal blood sample (0.2-2 ml) was collected from the umbilical cord into two EDTA-containing tubes after the delivery of the baby and before placental removal. The placental sampled area was taken from the midway between the point of cord insertion into the placenta and the placental circumference. DNA extraction was performed according to standard protocols(Qiagen kit). RRBS libraries were prepared as described (Boyle, P. et al. 2012)
