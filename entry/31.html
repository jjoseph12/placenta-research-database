Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from cells using TRIzol reagent (cat no. 15596018, Thermo Fisher, Waltham, MA). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with a HiSeq2000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility. Reads from *.fastq files were mapped to the human reference genome (GRCh37) using CLC Genomics Workbench 20.0.4 (Qiagen, Redwood City, CA). mRNA abundance was expressed in reads per kb of exon per million reads mapped. A false discovery rate of 0.05 was used as a cutoff for significant differential expression (scr versus KD). RACE Total RNA was isolated from cells and tissues using TRIzol reagent (cat no. 15596018, Thermo Fisher, Waltham, MA). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with a HiSeq2000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility. Reads from *.fastq files were mapped to the rat reference genome (Rnor_6.0) using CLC Genomics Workbench 20.0.4 (Qiagen, Redwood City, CA). mRNA abundance was expressed in reads per kb of exon per million reads mapped. A false discovery rate of 0.05 was used as a cutoff for significant differential expression (null versus wild type). Main topic
This paper examines the involvement of CBP/p300-interacting transactivator with glutamic acid/aspartic acid-rich carboxyl-terminal domain 2 (CITED2) in rat and human trophoblast cell development, highlighting its role as a conserved regulator of transcriptional events essential for establishing the uterine–placental interface.
