Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examine allelic expression from four individual samples of invasive trophoblast tissue of the chorionic girdle from gestation day 33 conceptuses of horse, donkey, mule and hinny.

Extraction protocol

The mRNA-seq libraries were made with 2.5 ug of starting total RNA samples using the mRNA-Seq 8-Sample Prep Kit (Illumina Inc., CA), following the Illumina protocol for mRNA sequencing sample preparation.

Main topic
This study uses RNA sequencing of trophoblast tissue from horse and donkey reciprocal hybrids to identify a core group of 15 ancient imprinted genes, with a majority showing paternal expression bias in the placenta.
