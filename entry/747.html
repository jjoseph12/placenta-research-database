Methods text

Assay description

Autopsy samples from patients deceased due to SARS-Cov2 infection were collected for total RNA-seq analysis to assess viral load and immune response.

Extraction protocol

RNA extraction from FFPE slides was done using the FormaPure Total nucleic acid extraction kit (C16675, Beckman Coulter) according to manufacturer instructions. Three 5 um thin tissue sections from areas devoid of acute inflammation were used per sample The Smarter Stranded Total RNA-Seq kit v2 (634413, Illumina) was used with 10 ng RNA input, according to the manufacturer instructions to generate libraries. Dual-indexed pooled libraries were sequenced on the Illumina NextSeq 500 platform using a 150 cycles kit with paired end read mode.

Main topic
This paper analyzes autopsy specimens from 24 patients who died from SARS-CoV-2 infection, using RNA and protein analysis to characterize the heterogeneity of pulmonary virus infection and host immune response, finding correlations between viral load, disease duration, interferon pathway activation, and macrophage infiltration.
