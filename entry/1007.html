Methods text

Assay description

Two datasets were produced for this study, an inter-specific dataset and intra-specific dataset. Inter-specific samples are whole placental samples, originated from three species, and are composed of reciprocal hybrid crosses between strains in each species. Intra-specific samples are purified methylomes of the junctional and labyrinthine zones with equal numbers of samples from male and female mice and embryonic days 15 and 18.

Extraction protocol

Using a Qiagen DNA extraction kit, DNA was extracted and purified for all whole placental, labyrinthine, and junctional zone samples. Samples were sent to the Beijing Genomics Institute for bisulfite conversion and sequencing. BGI library construction protocols for WGBS samples

Main topic
This paper studies placental DNA methylation at high resolution in multiple strains and closely related species of house mice to understand the evolution and development of the placental epigenome and its link to diseases like intrauterine growth restriction and preeclampsia.
