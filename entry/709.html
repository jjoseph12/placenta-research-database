Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from cells and tissues using TRIzol reagent (cat no. 15596018, Thermo Fisher, Waltham, MA). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with a HiSeq2000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of tissue factor pathway inhibitor (TFPI) in trophoblast cell development and its impact on hemochorial placentation, demonstrating that TFPI facilitates trophoblast differentiation and intrauterine invasion, which are crucial for uterine spiral artery remodeling at the maternal-fetal interface.
