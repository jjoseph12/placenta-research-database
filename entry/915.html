Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq was performed with embryonic brain at day 15 (E15), postnatal brain day 7 (P7) and adult brain at week 8 (W8) from control and knockout mice (three each time point).

Extraction protocol

AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit (Qiagen, Cat No./ID: 80204) was used to isolate total RNA following the manufacturer’s instruction. Stranded bulk RNA-Seq libraries using a poly A enrichment method.
