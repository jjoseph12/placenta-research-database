Extraction protocol

The zona pellucidae of the cleavage-stage embryos were removed with 0.5% pronase E and the embryos were then incubated in Ca2+-free Chatot-Ziomek-Bavister (CZB) medium for 10-20 minutes. Polar bodies were removed by gentle pipetting using a fire-polished glass needle. Single blastomeres were separated and manually picked before subjected to uliCUT&RUN, uliNChIP and total RNA library preparation and sequencing. For ICM and TE isolation (Liu et al., 2016), the zona pellucidae of blastocysts were punched, followed by removal with 0.5% pronase E. The embryos were then incubated in Ca2+-free CZB for 20-30 minutes to disrupt the cell-cell junctions. ICM cells and TE cells were then distinguished and collected according to their sizes and shapes with the aid of a piezo-driven micromanipulator. For epiblast and extraembryonic ectoderm isolation (Wang et al., 2018), embryos were cut at the embryonic/extraembryonic boundary using properly sharpened forceps. A fire-polished glass needle with an inner diameter that is slightly smaller than the width of the embryonic/extraembryonic fragment was used for separating epiblast or extraembryonic ectoderm from the visceral endoderm, and the separated epiblast or extraembryonic ectoderm fragment was then incubated in Ca2+-free CZB medium for 10 minutes. uliNChIP-seq was performed as previously described (Brind'Amour et al., 2015) to capture the H3K4me3 and H3K27ac status in embryos. Briefly, the harvested blastomeres or cells were subjected to nuclei extraction buffer. The MNase Master mix was then added and the mixture was incubated at 25°C for 10 minutes to allow MNase digestion. The reaction was stopped by 10 mM EDTA, and 0.1% Triton X-100 together with 0.1% DOC was added to lyse the nuclear membrane. The released chromatin was then diluted with ChIP buffer. After 1/20 volume of the reaction was saved as the input, the rest was incubated with H3K4me3 or H3K27ac antibody-coated Dynabeads Protein A/G for overnight at 4°C. The ChIP samples were washed twice with low-salt wash buffer and twice with high-salt wash buffer. The washed beads were then incubated in hot elution buffer for 2 hours at 65°C with shaking. The eluted DNAs were further purified and subjected to sequencing library generation as described above. Paired-end 150-bp sequencing was also performed on ChIP libraries. Briefly, all isolated fresh blastomeres or cells were washed three times with CUT&RUN wash buffer to avoid possible contamination and then transferred into a 1.5 mL low-binding PCR tube (Eppendorf) containing 600 µL of the wash buffer. The samples were then bound to Concanavalin A-coated magnetic beads, which had been activated and resuspended in CUT&RUN binding buffer. After cell immobilization, bead-bound samples were successively incubated with appropriate amount of primary antibodies against protein-of-interest in 250 µL of CUT&RUN antibody buffer and a secondary antibody if necessary in 250 µL of CUT&RUN digitonin buffer for overnight at 4°C. Cell membranes were permeabilized with 0.01% digitonin to allow the specific antibody to find its target. After unbound antibodies were washed away, 700 ng/mL protein A-MNase (pA-MN; a gift from Steven Henikoff lab or purchased from Vazyme Biotech) was added and incubated for 1 hour at 4°C. After washing, CaCl2 was added to a final concentration of 2 mM to activate pA-MN, and the digestion reaction was carried out for 30 min at 0°C and then stopped by adding 150 μL 2× CUT&RUN stop buffer. The protein–DNA complex fragments were then released by 20 min incubation at 37°C. After transferring the supernatant to a new tube, 3 µL of 10% SDS and 2.5 µL of Proteinase K (Thermo) were added and incubated for 30 min at 55°C. DNA was then precipitated by phenol/chloroform/isoamylalcohol followed by ethanol precipitation with glycogen and then dissolved in nuclease-free water. Sequencing libraries were prepared using KAPA Hyper Prep Kit following the manufacturer’s instructions with slight modifications. Briefly, end repair was conducted for 30 min at 20°C followed by end-repair/A-tailing for 30 min at 50°C. After adaptor ligation for 30 min at 20°C, the DNA fragments were purified by 1.4× volume of AMPure beads followed by 18 cycles of PCR amplification with 2× KAPA HiFi HotStart Ready Mix. The final libraries were cleaned up with 1.2× volume of AMPure beads, and all CUT&RUN libraries were sequenced on a NovaSeq6000 (Illumina) platform at Novogene Co., Ltd. with paired-ended 150-bp reads. Embryos with zona pellucida and polar bodies removed were disrupted in TRIzol Reagent (Thermo) and total RNAs were isolated by chloroform extraction, coupled with isopropanol precipitation with 1/10 volume of 3 M NaAc and 1 μL glycogen added to the aqueous phase of each sample. RNAs were washed twice with 75% ethanol before they were eluted with nuclease-free water. Purified RNAs were then subjected to library generation using SMARTer Stranded Total RNA-Seq Kit v2 following the manufacturer’s instructions. Briefly, random primers were used for reverse transcription, and the amplified cDNA was then subjected to ribosomal RNA depletion. Prepared RNA-seq libraries were sequenced on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 platform with paired ends and 150-bp read lengths at Novogene Co., Ltd. The HiCuT experiments were performed by Wuhan Frasergen Bioinformatics Co., Ltd. according to previous studies (7-9) with minor modifications. Briefly, placenta samples were cross-linked with 1% formaldehyde for 10 minutes at room temperature and quenched with 0.125M final concentration glycine for 5 minutes. Formaldehyde-fixed cells were subjected to additional cross-linking with 3 mM EGS, freshly prepared for 40 minutes and then quenched with 0.4 M glycine for 10 minutes at room temperature. Formaldehyde and ethylene glycol bis (succinimidylsuccinate) dual cross-linked cells were washed once with PBS supplemented with 0.05% BSA and were subsequently permeated with 0.05% digitonin for minutes at room temperature followed by 0.1% SDS at 65℃ for minutes. SDS were quenched by 1.8% Triton X-100 at 37℃ for minutes. Then chromatin DNA were digested by 50U MboI + 50U DdeI (NEB) at 37℃ for 2 hours, and fill-in with dNTP (Invitrogen) using 15U Klenow (NEB) at 37℃ for 1 hour. Spatial proximal DNA were ligated by 50U T4 DNA ligase (NEB) at 16℃ for 4 hours. Then cells were washed twice in Wash Buffer (20mM HEPES pH 7.5; 150mM NaCl; 0.5mM Spermidine; 1× Protease inhibitor cocktail) by gentle pipetting. Activated concanavalin A-coated bead-bound cells were resuspended in Dig-wash Buffer (Wash Buffer containing 0.05% Digitonin) containing 2mM EDTA and a 1:50 dilution of the primary antibody (H3K27ac antibody: CST, #8173). Primary antibody incubation was performed on a rotating platform at 4℃ overnight. Then the liquid was removed on the magnet stand. Secondary antibody (goat anti-rabbit IgG: Proteintech, B900210) was diluted 1:50 in Dig-Wash buffer and cells were incubated at room temperature for 1 hour. Cells were washed twice in Dig-Wash buffer to remove unbound antibodies. A 1:200 dilution of pG-Tn5 adapter complex (~0.04 μM) was prepared in Dig-300 Buffer (0.01% Digitonin, 20mM HEPES, pH 7.5, 300mM NaCl, 0.5mM Spermidine, 1× Protease inhibitor cocktail). Cells was incubated with pG-Tn5 at room temperature for 1 hour, then were washed twice in Dig-300 Buffer to remove unbound pG-Tn5 protein. Next, cells were resuspended in Tagmentation buffer (10mM MgCl2 in Dig-300 Buffer) and incubated at 37 °C for 1 hour. Tagmentation were stopped by incubating at 55 °C for 1hour containing 16mM EDTA, 0.1% SDS and 0.16 mg/mL Proteinase K. Then DNA was purified using phenol–chloroform–isoamyl alcohol and ethanol, and amplified by PCR reaction as follows: 3 min at 72 °C and 30 s at 98 °C followed by 16 cycles of 15 s at 98 °C and 30 s at 60 °C and 30 s at 72 °C, with a final extension at 72 °C for 3 min. Finally, the amplified DNA was purified using Ampure XP beads. Libraries were sequenced on Illumina NovaSeq platform. After cell immobilization, bead-bound samples were successively incubated with appropriate amount of primary antibodies against protein-of-interest (TFAP2C antibody: Santa Cruz, sc-12762; TEAD4 antibody: Santa Cruz, sc-101184; KLF5 antibody: Proteintech, 21017-1-AP) in 250µL of CUT&RUN antibody buffer and a secondary antibody if necessary (rabbit anti-mouse IgG: Abcam, ab46540) in 250µL of CUT&RUN digitonin buffer for overnight at 4°C. Sequencing libraries were prepared using KAPA Hyper Prep Kit (KAPA biosystems) following the manufacturer’s instructions with slight modifications. Briefly, end repair was conducted for 30 min at 20°C followed by end-repair/A-tailing for 30 min at 50°C. After adaptor ligation for 30 min at 20°C, the DNA fragments were purified by 1.4× volume of AMPure beads (Beckman Coulter) followed by 18 cycles of PCR amplification with 2× KAPA HiFi HotStart Ready Mix. The final libraries were cleaned up with 1.2× volume of AMPure beads, and all CUT&RUN libraries were sequenced on a NovaSeq6000 (Illumina) platform at Novogene Co., Ltd. with paired-ended 150-bp reads. The released genomic DNA, together with 1% unmethylated lambda DNA (Thermo Scientific), were sheared into 150 bp to 350 bp by Covaris S2 before subjecting to bisulfite conversion using a MethylCode Bisulfite Conversion Kit (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. The bisulfite-converted templates were then used to prepare the sequencing library through EpiArt DNA Methylation Library Kit following the manufacturer’s instructions. The PBAT libraries were also sequenced on Illumina NovaSeq with paired ends and 150-bp read lengths at Nanjing Jiangbei New Area Biophamaceutical Public Service Platform Co., Ltd.
