Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

mRNA from the trophoblast-like (TBL) samples was sequenced on a NovaSeq (Illumina) in 100bp paired-end reads.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using the PureLink RNA Minit Kit (Thermo Fisher). TBL mRNA libraries were prepared using the Illumina Strand mRNA Kit. For the TBLs, 150bp paired-end reads were sequenced on the NovaSeq.

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of monosomy X on an in vitro model for early human placenta using X-monosomic human induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs), revealing impaired secretion of placental growth factor and chorionic gonadotropin in X-monosomic trophoblast-like cells.
