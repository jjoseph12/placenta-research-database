Assay description

183 total RNA samples from 8 tissue types collected from 35 women grouped into six categories of pregnancy outcome. One microarray replicate per sample. Other Contributors: Radek Bukowski, Sam Parry and the NICHD Genomic and Proteomic Network for Preterm Birth Research

Source excerpt
Patients admitted prior to delivery whose gestational age at admission was between 24 weeks 0 days and 34 weeks 0 days or between 39 weeks 0 days and 41 weeks 6 days were eligible for the study. Because this study required the collection of multiple uterine tissues specimens at the time of delivery, only patients delivering by cesarean delivery were eligible.
