Methods text

Assay description

Examination of 5-mC and 5-hmC pattern in 4 control cases' tissue and 4 severely Preeclamptic Placentas cases' tissue.

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA of severely preeclamptic placentas and normal tissues was purified using the Qiagen Blood & Cell Culture DNA Mini Kit (Cat. No: 13323) and sonicated. Illumina barcode adapters were ligated before hMeDIP. Adaptor-ligated DNA (5 μg) was denatured and incubated with 3 μl of 5-hmC antibody (Active Motif) or 5 μg of 5-mC antibody (Eurogentec) at 4°C overnight. Antibody-DNA complexes were captured by protein A/G beads. The immunoprecipitated DNA was purified and sequenced following standard Illumina protocols. Libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
