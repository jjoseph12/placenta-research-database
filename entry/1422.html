Assay description

Cells were grown without feeders and harvested. Comparisons were made to provide evidence of unique gene expression between the cell lines. Specifically, pre-implantation (ICM, epiblast and primitive endoderm, and trophoblast), as well as pre-implantation and post-implantation epiblasts.

Main topic
This paper provides a large-scale proteomic resource of cell-surface proteins for four embryo-derived stem cell lines (ES, EpiSC, TS, and XEN), and validates antibodies against lineage-specific cell-surface markers, enabling investigation of specific cell populations during stem cell differentiation/reprogramming and prospective isolation of viable lineage progenitors from blastocysts.
