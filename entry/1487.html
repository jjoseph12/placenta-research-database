Methods text

Assay description

Total RNAs from Junctional zone tissues of gestation day18.5 HSD and BN rat strains were subjected to microarray analyses. Three biological replicates of each strains were analyzed.

Main topic
This study investigates the genetic factors influencing placentation in rats by examining strain-specific differences in trophoblast invasion and junctional zone characteristics using chromosome-substituted rat strains.
