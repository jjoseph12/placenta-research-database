Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Bisulphite converted DNA from 12 human placenta samples (two pairs of first trimester CVS and samples at term, two pairs of opposite sides biopsies from the same term placentas, one pair of dyzigotic twin term placentas and one trio of triplet placenta samples) were hybridised to the Illumina Infinium HumanMethylationEPIC and HumanMethylation450 BeadChips.

Extraction protocol

genomic DNA was extracted from placenta samples using standard phenol/chloroform extraction protocol

Main topic
This study investigates the role of aberrant DNA methylation and gene expression of imprinted genes in the placenta in relation to intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR). The study identifies that DNA methylation is less stable at placenta-specific imprinted differentially methylated regions (DMRs) compared to ubiquitous DMRs, and IUGR-associated expression differences were identified for several imprinted transcripts independent of allelic methylation.
