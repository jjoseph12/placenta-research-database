Main topic
This study examines temporal transcriptomic changes in the uterus, uterine implantation sites, and placentae of Dahl salt-sensitive rats, a model of superimposed preeclampsia, to identify genetic factors contributing to the preeclamptic phenotype and uncover potential biomarkers and therapeutic targets.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study examines temporal transcriptomic changes in the uterus, uterine implantation sites, and placentae of a rat model of spontaneous superimposed preeclampsia (Dahl salt-sensitive rat) compared with healthy Sprague Dawley rats to identify relevant molecular pathways.
Source excerpt
To identify molecular pathways involved in PE, we examined temporal transcriptomic changes occurring within the uterus, uterine implantation sites, and placentae from the Dahl salt-sensitive (Dahl S) rat model of superimposed PE compared with Sprague Dawley (SD) rats.
