Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

C57/BL6 mice were exposed to 50ppm benzene for 5 hours a day from E0.5 to E17.5. Tissues were collected on E17.5

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvested using Rneasy mini plus kit (Qiagen). 1.3 ug of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries for Placental RNA-seq were prepared using NEBNext Ultra II RNA Library Prep Kit following manufacturer's protocols. RNA libraries for Decidua RNA-seq were prepared using Lexogen QuantSeq 3' mRNA-Seq FWD with UDI 12 nt (Unique Dual Indexing) kit according to manufacturer's protocols.

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of benzene exposure during pregnancy on maternal immune activation and fetal development, demonstrating a sexual dimorphic response in placentas and suggesting intrinsic differences between male and female placentas influence responses to environmental factors.
