Main topic
This study investigates the role of Notch1 in decidualization, a critical event for successful pregnancy, in mice. The findings indicate that Notch1 is essential for normal decidualization in the mouse uterus during the first pregnancy but not in subsequent ones, and its deletion leads to impaired decidualization and initial pregnancy loss.
