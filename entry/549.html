Main topic
This study investigates the impact of chronic prenatal delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) exposure on placental function and development in a rhesus macaque model, revealing adverse effects on amniotic fluid volume, placental perfusion, and fetal oxygen availability, along with placental histological and transcriptomic changes indicative of placental insufficiency.
