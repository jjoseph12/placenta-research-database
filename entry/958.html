Methods text

Assay description

hESC cell line H9 was differentiated towards trophoblast lineage using treatment with BMP4 for 3 days. Undifferentiated hESC and cells stained with anti-APA antibody were sorted into 384-well plates at 1 cell per well (1 plate per condition) and subjected to SCRB-seq

Extraction protocol

Single-cells were FACS-sorted directly into 384-well plates containing 5µl lysis buffer (1/500 dilution of Phusion HF buffer) Libraries were constructed using the SCRB-seq protocol (Soumillon et al., 2014) as previously described (Ziegenhain et al., 2017)

Main topic
This study provides a mechanistic explanation for the differentiation of trophoblasts from human pluripotent stem cells, a process relying on BMP morphogens, and identifies a network of transcription factors (GATA2, GATA3, TFAP2A, and TFAP2C) that regulates early trophoblast progenitor specification by activating placental genes and inhibiting the pluripotency gene OCT4.
