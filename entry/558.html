Main topic
This paper depicts a high-resolution cell roadmap of the primed-to-naïve pluripotency transition using a dual fluorescent reporter system, providing insights for embryo modeling-related studies. The study identifies the appearance of primitive endoderm and trophectoderm signatures in transitioning subpopulations, revealing cell fate dynamics during human naïve pluripotency establishment.
