Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

C57BL/6J mice were fed either a standard control diet, RM1 [7% simple carbohydrates/3% lipids (w/w)] or an energy-rich obesogenic diet [10% simple carbohydrates/20% lipids (w/w)] plus sweetened condensed milk [55% simple carbohydrates, 8% lipids, 8% protein (w/w), Nestle (Nestle, UK), supplemented with mineral and vitamin mix AIN93G]. Female mice were mated, and Pregnant mice were housed individually and maintained on their respective diets until sacrafice. A subset of the mice on the Obesegenic diet were exercised until E17 of pregnancy. Placenta tissue was collected at embryonic day 19.

Extraction protocol

Placentas were dissected, weighed, snap frozen and stored at −80 °C. Total liver RNA was extracted using a miRNeasy mini kit (Qiagen, Hilden, Germany) including a DNAse digestion protocol. Four high quality samples from each group were randomly selected and subjected to a cDNA library preparation using TruSeq RNALibrary Preparation kit, following the manufacturer’s protocol.

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of maternal obesity on the placental transcriptome and morphology associated with fetal growth restriction (FGR) in mice, revealing abnormal placental development and impaired vascularization that may mediate FGR through reduced nutrient transfer.
