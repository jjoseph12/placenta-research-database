Methods text

Assay description

We generated embryos from 3 different sources natural fertilization (NF), somatic nuclear transfer (NT), and somatic nuclear transfer with HDACi Scriptaid treatment (SNT) and chosed 5-time points during the TSC maintaining, E3.5 blastocyst trophectoderm (TE3.5), E4.5 blastocyst trophectoderm (TE4.5), outgrowths at half-time progression (outgrowth), passage 1 TSCs (TS_P1), and passage 3-4 TSCs (TS_Pn) to perform RNA-seq and RRBS. For each sample, we performed several independent replicates.

Extraction protocol

For the RRBS library, we digested nuclear proteins and extracted the DNA from the nuclei of samples. Then, we add unmethylated lambda DNA (Fermentas) and took a one-tube reaction, treating the DNA with MspI digestion (Fermentas, USA), end repair, dA tailing, adaptor ligation, and bisulfite conversion using MethylCode Bisulfite Conversion Kit (Invitrogen, USA, MECOV-50). We purified the converted libraries by Agencourt AMPure XP beads (Beckman A63881, USA) and amplified using two-round PCR enrichment. Only 200–500 bp DNA fragments were reserved for sequencing.

Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptomes and methylomes of trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) derived from naturally fertilized (NF) embryos, somatic cell nuclear transfer (NT) embryos, and Scriptaid-treated NT (SNT) embryos to understand the regulatory mechanisms underlying TSC derivation.
