Assay description

To investigate the DNA methylation differences in the mouse placenta at late gestation between males and females, we conducted SureSelect Methyl-Seq (Capture).

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted using QIAamp DNA Micro Kit (Qiagen) following manufacturer's recommendations Libraries were performed using SureSelectXT Methyl-Seq mouse Target Enrichment System (Agilent) following manufacturer's protocol

Main topic
This study investigates sex-based disparities in DNA methylation and gene expression in late-gestation mouse placentas, revealing significant differences in genes and regions associated with placental functions and embryonic development, highlighting the importance of including sex-specific analyses in epigenetic and transcription studies.
