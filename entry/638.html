Methods text

Assay description

Holstein heifers (N = 6/treatment) were estrous synchronized and received intrauterine infusions of one of the following treatments: 200 μg/mL bovine serum albumin (BSA; vehicle) from day 14 to 16 of the estrous cycle (BSA), vehicle + 10 μg/mL of IFNT from day 14 to 16 (IFNT3), vehicle + 10 μg/mL of IFNT from day 14 to 19 (IFNT6), and vehicle + 10 μg/mL of IFNT from day 14 to 16 followed by vehicle + 10 μg/mL of IFNT + 5 μg/mL of PAG from day 17 to 19 (IFNT+PAG). RNA was extracted from endometrium (N = 4/treatment) and transcriptomic data were obtained through BGI (Shenzhen, China) services.

Extraction protocol

Tissue samples were collected at the end of the treatment schedule, 12 hours after last infusion (8:30 am of day 17 or day 20). Animals received caudal epidural anesthesia and a sterilized custom-made mini-Tischler biopsy forceps with a 22 inch shaft length, 4 mm shaft diameter, and 4x8 mm bite size (Aries surgical, Davis CA) was passed through the cervix for collection of 1 endometrial biopsy. The biopsies were immediately placed into sterilized cryotubes and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen. RNA was isolated from one frozen endometrial biopsy (~50-60 mg) following homogenization in 800 μL of TRIzol reagent (Life Technologies). Total RNA was purified using RNA Clean and Concentrator kit (Zymo Research) to eliminate contaminating DNA and reagents. Endometrial RNA samples were shipped on dry ice to BGI laboratory (Beijing Genomics Institute, Shenzhen, China) where RNA quality and quantity were assessed using Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer (Agilent Technologies). All samples met the quality criteria for RNA sequencing, showing RNA integrity number (RIN) between 8 and 9.9 values. Preparation of mRNA library and sequencing were performed following BGI DNBSEQ technology platform with paired end 100 bp (PE100).

Main topic
This study investigates the short and long-term effects of IFNT and PAG on the transcriptome of endometrium and blood leukocytes in dairy cattle, finding that IFNT primarily regulates endometrial gene expression while PAG predominantly affects the transcriptome of circulating immune cells during early pregnancy.
