Methods text

Assay description

Allele-specific RNA-seq analysis of the trophoblast isolated from E7.5 embryos. Embryos were obtained from JF1 females crossed with B6 males carrying respective Xist mutations as well as B6 wild-type males.

Extraction protocol

Trophoblasts were isolated from E7.5 embryos. RNA was prepared using Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#15027078) was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
