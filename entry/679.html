Methods text

Assay description

Two TEAD4 knockout human embryonic stem cell clones and the wild type cells were induced towards trophoblast differentiation under BAP treatment. Cells were collected on day9 after transition for the transcriptome analysis.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted by using TRIZOL Reagent (Life Technologies) following the manufacturer's instructions. 1~2 μg total RNA were used for establishing sequencing libraries with KAPA Stranded RNA-Seq Library Prep Kit (Illumina).
