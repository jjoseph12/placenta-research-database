Assay description

Gene expression was measured in placenta of 18.5 dpc fetuses sired by either a folate sufficient male (n=4 placentas from different litters and different fathers) or a folate deficient (FD) male (n=4 placentas from different litters and different fathers).

Main topic
This paper investigates the impact of paternal dietary folate deficiency on the sperm epigenome in mice, and its association with adverse pregnancy outcomes and increased birth defects in offspring, suggesting that adequate paternal dietary folate is essential for offspring health.
