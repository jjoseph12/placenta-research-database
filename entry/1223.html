Main topic
This study analyzes placental tissue from preeclamptic cases and normotensive controls for genome-wide differential CpG methylation and concomitant changes in gene expression, identifying functional epimutations associated with preeclampsia disease status and potential novel biomarkers of the disease, particularly those involved in the transforming growth factor beta (TGF-β) signaling pathway.
