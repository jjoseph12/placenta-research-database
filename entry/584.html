Methods text

Assay description

bulk RNA-seq from 16 matched male and 19 matched female human fetal brain and placenta samples (72-82 days post conception). Triglyceride accumulation in maternal placenta (decidua) was used as a proxy for maternal dietary fat consumption, given no available patient characteristics. Correlations were performed to understand which genes change in response to increasing maternal fat intake.

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated using TRIzol RNA extraction reagent chloroform extraction RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using an Illumina TrueSeq RNA Exome kit and standard Illumina protocol

Main topic
This study investigates the sex-specific mechanisms by which a maternal high-fat diet (mHFD) impacts offspring susceptibility to neuropsychiatric disorders, revealing a microglia-dependent pathway involving endotoxin accumulation, perinatal inflammation, and altered serotonin bioavailability in the fetal brain.
