Methods text

Assay description

small RNA profiling of mouse trophoblast (TS) stem cells, embryonic stem cells (ES wild-type, Dnmt1 KO, Setdb1 inducible KO), and mouse embryonic fibroblasts, transfected HeLa cells, 3-5 replicates (a/b technical rep., integer biological rep.); H3K9m3 native ChIPseq of trophoblast stem cells (TS), 4 replicates; transposon-specific RACE of uncapped, 5' RNA ends (2x2 conditions), 2 replicates per condition; sRNA libraries of transfected HeLa cells, 3 conditions, 3 replicates each

Extraction protocol

Trizol, TBE urea PAGE size selection 14-38 nt Illumina sRNA library prep H3K9me3 native ChIP as described in Karimi et al. 2012 Truseq adapters, https://ethanomics.wordpress.com/chip-seq-library-construction-using-the-illumina-TruSeq-adapters Trizol ligation of 5' RACE adapter (5'-GCUGAUGGCGAUGAAUGAACACUGCGUUUGCUGGCUUUGAUGAAA), RT using SuperScriptII and random hexamers, nested amplification with ERVK-ETn-specific primer (5'-GACTGGAGTTCCTTGGCACCCGAGAATTCCAGCGTTGGGTTAGCACTGATT) and Truseq-linker (5'-TTCAGAGTTCTACAGTCCGACGATCGCTGATGGCGATGAATGAACACT), then Illumina Truseq RNA PCR primers.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of tRNA-derived small RNAs (tRFs) in controlling LTR-retrotransposon activity in mouse cells, finding that tRFs target the tRNA primer binding site (PBS) of endogenous retroviruses (ERVs) to inhibit reverse transcription and retrotransposition.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that 18 nt and 22 nt tRNA-derived small RNAs (tRFs) target the tRNA primer binding site of active LTR-retrotransposons to inhibit their replication and retrotransposition in mouse preimplantation stem cells.
Source excerpt
We found abundant, 18 nt tRNA-derived small RNA (tRF) in these cells, and ubiquitously expressed 22 nt tRFs, that include the 3′ terminal CCA of mature tRNAs, and target the tRNA primer binding site (PBS) essential for ERV reverse transcription.
