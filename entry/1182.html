Methods text

Assay description

RNA sequencing analysis was performed on a total of 48 samples, including undifferentiated human aESCs and bi-parental ESCs, as well as human aESCs, pESCs and bi-parental ESCs differentiated into teratomas, trophoblast cells and hepatic cells.

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated using RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) or NucleoSpin RNA Plus Kit (Macherey-Nagel). For RNA sequencing analysis, poly(A) pull-down was used to enrich mRNAs from total RNA samples (RIN > 8) Libraries were prepared using Illumina TruSeq RNA Library Prep Kit
