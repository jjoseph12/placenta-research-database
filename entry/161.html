Methods text

Assay description

Decidual stromal cells (DSCs) were isolated from control or Ezh2 cKO decidual tissue at E7.5 of pregnancy. Each biological isolate was split into two replicates, one of which was cultured without treatment, and one of which was cultured with 2 ng/mL of TGF-beta. Samples were cultured for 24 hours. RNA was subsequently prepared. Next generation sequencing was performed using Illumina HiSeq 4000.

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated with QIAGEN Mini reagents and kit according to manufacturer's instructions. DNAse digestion and RNA cleanup was performed using the RNase-Free DNase set (QIAGEN) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. RNA libraries were prepared according to Illumina's instructions, using a Smartseq/NexteraXT kit.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of EZH2, a histone methyltransferase, in suppressing wound-healing responses in the decidua during early pregnancy in mice, finding that EZH2 deficiency leads to pregnancy failure due to ectopic myofibroblast formation and altered gene expression.
