Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

A selective breeding strategy in which wildtype C57BL/6J dams were mated with Rgs2-deficient (Rgs20KO) sires or with wildtype male littermates of these Rgs2-KO sires. These matings resulted in pregnant wildtype C57BL/6J dams carrying feto-placental units that were either heterozygous for Rgs2 or wildtype, respectively. Total RNA from whole placenta at GD 12.5 was isolated, and transcriptomes were assessed by RNAsequencing.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA from placentas of pregnant GD12.5 mice was isolated using mirVanaTM RNA isolation kit (ThemoFisher Scientific: AM1560) per manufacturer’s instructions. Indexed sequencing libraries were generated from total RNA according to the manufacturer’s (Illumina) suggested protocol.
