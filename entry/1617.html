Study details Samples, assay, platform, and submission

Sample characteristics

  • Collection of human placentae was approved by the Institutional Review Board of the University of Washington. Subjects were excluded if they had any chronic disease, were on chronic medication, were smokers, abused alcohol or drugs. Term human placenta samples were obtained from scheduled uncomplicated C-sections performed at the University of Washington Medical Center, Seattle, WA. The 1st (45-59 days) and 2nd (109-115 days) trimester placentae, from uncomplicated elective termination, were provided by Birth Defects Laboratory of the University of Washington. After collection, the tissues were kept on ice until the placental villus could be dissected and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80C.

Main topic
This paper identifies major functional categories of genes whose expression depends on gestational age by comparing gene expression profiles in the villous tissues of first and third trimester placentas using microarray analysis.
