Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Analyzing transcriptional profiles in naive PSCs, primed PSCs, naive PSCs-derived TE (nTE), nTE-derived CT (nCT), nTE-derived ST (nST), nTE-derived EVT (nEVT), primed PSCs-derived cells (pBAP), placenta-derived CT (CT30), and in vivo CT.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using miRNeasy Mini Kit (QIAGEN). To generate RNA-seq libraries, TruSeq Stranded mRNA LT sample prep kit (Illumina) was used according to the supplier’s instruction.
