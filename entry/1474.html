Methods text

Assay description

A total of 18 samples for each tissue type (liver & placenta) was analyzed (8 biological replicates for in vivo-produced fetuses and 10 biological replicates for in vitro ones). Two technical replicates were done for each sample.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA from dissected tissues was extracted using the RNeasy mini/midi kit according to manufacture's instructions (Qiagen, Valencia, CA) Label Cy5 Label protocol Ten µg of total RNA was mixed with 2 µl random hexamers (3 µg/µl, Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA), heated at 70°C for 5 min, snap-cooled on ice, and added to the following reaction mix: 6 µl 5X first strand buffer (Invitrogen), 3 µl 0.1 M DTT, 0.6 µl 25 mM dNTP (except for dTTP: 15 mM), 0.12 µl 50 mM aminoallyl-dUTP, 2 µl SuperScript III reverse transcriptase (200 U/µl, Invitrogen) and 0.5 µl RNase inhibitor (20 U/µl, Ambion, Austin, TX). The 30 µl reaction was then incubated at 25 °C for 10 min, followed by further incubation at 46 °C overnight.
