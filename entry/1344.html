Main topic
This paper examines the role of proinflammatory cytokines in the pathogenesis of inflammation-associated premature delivery, focusing on the effects of the organic solvent N,N-dimethylformamide (DMF) on cytokine release from human amniotic epithelial cells (hAECs). It investigates how DMF exposure can contribute to preterm birth.

Reviewed answer The study investigates the effect of N,N-dimethylacetamide (DMA) on preventing lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced preterm birth and associated inflammatory responses in a mouse model and in vitro.
