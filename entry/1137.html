Extraction protocol

Cells (3x10^6) were cross-linked with 2% formaldehyde, lysed in the lysis buffer (10mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10mM NaCl, 0.2% NP40), digested with HindIII, treated with Klenow fragment and dNTPs to fill in the HindIII digested overhangs, and ligated under a dilute condition. After reversing crosslinks, DNA was purified as a 3C library. The 3C library was digested wth MspI, ligated, and amplified by PCR tp generate a 4C library. 4C library was amplified by PCR using two sets of PCR primers. The fist set of primers, Tead4_4C-Hind3F(+) "5'-TCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCTGCCTTCACTATTCCCCAAAA-3'" and Tead4_4C-Msp1R(-) "5'-GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCTGAACCACTGCTGGGACCTAA-3'", contain a sequence specific to the mouse Tead4 promoter region at the 3' end and a partial sequence of illumina TruSeq adaptor at the 5' end. The second primers are "5-AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACTCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCT-3'" (TruSeq Universal Adapter sequence) and "CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[6bp_index]GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCT-3'" (TruSeq Index Adapter sequence). Trypsinised feeder-free ES and TS cells (1×10^6) were collected and fixed with 1% formaldehyde at room temperature. Crosslinking reaction was quenched with 125 mM glycine for 5 min. The cells were resuspended in SDS lysis buffer (ChIP Reagent, Nippon Gene Co., Ltd.), and the lysate was sonicated to fragment chromatin using a Covaris S220 (Covaris, Woburn, MA, USA). The sonicated lysate was subjected to immunoprecipitation with a monoclonal antibody against TEDA4, KLF5, H3K4me1, or H3K27ac. The immunoprecipitated and reverse-crosslinked DNA was purified using AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter, Inc., Pasadena, CA, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. ChIP or input DNA (0.1 ~ 1 ng) was subjected to end repair, dA-tailing, and adaptor-ligation, and amplified by 6-13 cycles of PCR. Trypsinised feeder-free cells (1×10^6) were collected and fixed with 1% formaldehyde at room temperature. Crosslinking reaction was quenched with 125 mM glycine for 5 min. The cells were resuspended in SDS lysis buffer (ChIP Reagent, Nippon Gene Co., Ltd.), and the lysate was sonicated to fragment chromatin using a Covaris S220 (Covaris, Woburn, MA, USA). DNA was purified from sonicated lysate using phenol-chloroform (Sigma #P3803 phenol, chloroform, and isoamyl alcohol 25:24:1 saturated with 10 mM Tris at pH 8.0, 1 mM EDTA), and ethanol-precipitated. Sequencing libraries were prepared using the NEBNext ChIP-Seq Library Prep Master Mix Set and Multiplex Oligos from Illumina (New England BioLabs Inc., Ipswich, MA, USA) according to the manufacturer's instructions. FAIRE DNA (1 ng) was subjected to end repair, dA-tailing, and adaptor-ligation, and amplified by 9 cycles of PCR. The formaldehyde-assisted isolation of regulatory elements combined with the high-throughput sequencing (FAIRE-seq) In vivo-developed blastocyst-stage embryos were collected at 88 h after hCG, then incubated in M2 medium microdrops. Each blastocysts was picked up from the microdrops, and RNA from each blastocyst was extracted using Allprep DNA/RNA Micro kit (QIAGEN). RNA libraries were prepared usig Ovation SoLo RNA-Seq Systems (NuGEN) for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols Total RNA was extracted using Allprep DNA/RNA Mini kit (QIAGEN). RNA-seq libraries were prepared using SureSelect strand-specific RNA library preparation kit (G9691A, Agilent). One microgram of total RNA was used as a starting material.
