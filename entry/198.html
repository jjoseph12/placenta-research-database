Assay description

Gab3-KO vs. WT

Extraction protocol

For gd8.5 uNK cells, total RNA isolated from 1 X 104 cells was amplified with the Ovation RNA-Seq System v2 (NuGEN) and Libraries were sequenced with Illumina HiSeq2500 (DNA sequencing core, University of Cincinnati). RNA-seq libraries were generated from 300 ng total RNA with an RNA Sample Prep Kit (Illumina).
