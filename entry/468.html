Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

This was a cross-sectional analysis of placentas collected at mid-gestation(n=4). Laser microdissection enabled the isolation of samples that were enriched for syncytiotrophoblasts, villus cytotrophoblasts or endovascular cytotrophoblasts. A microarray approach was used for global transcriptional profiling.

Main topic
This paper uses laser capture to isolate syncytiotrophoblasts and two subpopulations of invasive cytotrophoblasts from cell columns and the endovascular compartment of spiral arteries, followed by transcriptional profiling to reveal genes with previously unknown placental or trophoblast expression, including neurotensin and C4ORF36.
