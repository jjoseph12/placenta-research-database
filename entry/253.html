Methods text

Assay description

The study included 6 groups: 1) Early-preeclampsia mothers (>32 weeks) N=10; 2) Children from early-preeclampsia mothers N=10; 3) Late-preeclampsia mothers (>37 weeks) N=9; 4) Children from late-preeclampsia mothers N=9; 5) Normal pregnancy mothers N=10; 6) Children from normal pregnancy mothers N=9

Extraction protocol

Oragene OC-175 ORAcollect for pediatric samples and Oragene DNA Purification kit (Genotek, OGR-500) for adult samples Reduced Representation Bisulfite Sequencing libraries were generated as described in Gu et al. 2011. Bruefly, between 100-200 ng of genomic DNA was digested for 2 hours with MspI restriction enzyme (New England Biolabs) which cuts the DNA at every occurrence of the CCGG sequence, resulting in fragments starting with a CpG site. Libraries were prepared with the NEBNext Ultra II Modules (New England Biolabs) and the NEBNext Methylated Adaptor (New England Biolabs). The ligated DNA was size-selected using Ampure XP magnetic beads (Beckman Coulter) to produce a final library size of approximately 200 bp. Bisulfite conversion was performed with the EZ DNA Methylation-Gold Kit for single use (D5005, Zymo Research), followed by PCR amplification with the NEBNext Q5U polymerase and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (New England Biolabs) to barcode each library.
