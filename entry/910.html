Methods text

Assay description

Bovine placentomes were obtained from three pregnant cows from days 118 to 130 of gestation. UTCs and TGCs were isolated by an optimized fluorescence activated cell sorting (FACS) procedure. RNA was isolated and subjected to mRNA microarray analysis using Bovine Gene 1.0 ST Arrays (Affymetrix, St. Clara, CA, USA).

Main topic
This paper analyzes gene expression patterns in bovine uninucleate trophoblast cells (UTCs) and trophoblast giant cells (TGCs) to identify differentially expressed genes (DEGs) and understand the molecular functions and biological processes regulated during trophoblast cell differentiation in the bovine placenta.
