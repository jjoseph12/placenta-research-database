Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA sequencing of E6.5 wild type (WT) and Tgfbr1 Pgr-Cre knockout mouse decidual

Extraction protocol

Decidual tissues from E6.5 were prepared by clearing away maternal myometrial and embryonic materials under a dissecting microscope. Samples were stored at -80 °C for subsequent RNA and/or protein isolation. Total RNA was isolated from mouse decidual tissues using RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) based on manufacturer’s protocol. Briefly, mouse decidual tissues were collected and homogenized in lysis buffer (Qiagen) that contains β-mercaptoethanol prior to RNA extraction. Total RNA was eluted with nuclease-free water and concentration measured using a NanoDrop Spectrophotometer. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
