Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Gene expression patterns in placenta and intervillus macrophages in CIUE placenta (4 cases) are compared to normal placenta (4 cases) and granulomatous lymhpadenitis macrophages (4 cases) controls respectively using GeoMX DSP and nCounter sequencing.

Extraction protocol

A 5 micron formalin fixed paraffin embedded tissue microarray section is labeled with fluorescently labeled cytokeratin and CD68 antibodies to highlight the trophoblast, stroma, and macrophage components. GeoMX human whole transciptome atlas is hydridized and bound probe barcode oligomers cleaved and collected for further analysis using the GeoMX digital spatial profiler. NanoString's GeoMX Human Whole Transcriptome Atlas

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study compares placental DNA methylation profiles of early pregnancy losses affected by chronic intervillositis of unknown etiology (CIUE) with non-CIUE losses to investigate disease-specific epigenetic alterations.
Source excerpt
This study compares the DNA methylation profiles of placental tissue (whole chorionic villi) from early pregnancy losses with CIUE, to placental tissue from non-inflamed early pregnancy losses, to determine if CIUE has a unique DNA methylation profile and whether there is aberrant methylation of genes involved in the IFNγ signaling pathway.
