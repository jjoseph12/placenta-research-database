Methods text

Assay description

[RRBS] DNA methylation comparison in human placenta between 6 first trimester and 5 third trimester samples [RNA-Seq] Comparison of RNA expression in human placenta between 5 first/second trimester and 4 third trimester samples

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted using QIAamp DNA Mini Kit (QIAGEN GmbH, Germany). Library was constructed using modified Illumina RRBS Sample Preparation Protocol. Illumina's RRBS for Methylation Analysis Total RNA was extracted using TRIZOL protocol (Life Technologies). Sequencing library was constructed using Illumina mRNA-Seq Sample Preparation protocol. Illumina's mRNA-Seq for Gene Expression Analysis

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of DNA methylation changes on gene expression in human placenta tissues from the first and third trimesters, revealing a complex, non-linear relationship between DNA methylation and gene expression during placental development.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer This publication investigates the complex relationship between DNA methylation changes and gene expression in the human placenta at early and late gestational ages (first and third trimesters).
Source excerpt
In this study, we systematically analysed the transcriptomes and the DNA methylomes of human placenta samples derived from different gestational ages. Furthermore, we studied the dynamic correlations between gene expression and DNA methylation at different gestational ages and genomic locations.
