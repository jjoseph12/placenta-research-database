Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

HTR-8/SVneo cells transfected with MIR193BHG overexpression plasmids and mock plasmids were collected and lysed with Trizol reagent. Next, 1μg RNA was used for library preparation with TruSeq Stranded Total RNA with Ribo-Zero Gold kit (Illumina). The sequenced reads were then aligned to the human reference genome (GRCh38.91) using Hisat2. Differential gene expression was performed using EdgeR. The threshold used in this study to screen up- or downregulated mRNAs was logFC > 0.5 and P < 0.05.

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent and extracted with chloroform. 1ug of total RNA was utilized for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using SMARTER mRNA-Seq Library Prep Kit following manufacturer's protocols.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of lncRNA MIR193BHG in preeclampsia (PE) by assessing its effects on trophoblast function and exploring the underlying molecular mechanisms, finding that MIR193BHG inhibits trophoblast proliferation, migration, and invasion by upregulating p53.
