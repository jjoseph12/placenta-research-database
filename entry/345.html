Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq assays were performed using BirA-expressing ES cell lines- expressing BirA only or BirA and biotin-tagged Fosl1.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using RNeasy® Plus Mini Kit (Qiagen). 1μg of RNA was used to prepare libraries for RNA-sequencing using NEBNext® Poly(A) mRNA Magnetic Isolation Module (New England BioLabs).

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of Fosl1, a member of the AP-1 complex, in trophoblast development by ectopically expressing it in embryonic stem (ES) cells to induce trophoblast lineage-like cells, revealing that Fosl1 directly activates trophoblast-specific gene expression independently of ES cell core factor changes.
