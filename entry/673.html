Methods text

Assay description

Human trophoblast cell lines (HTR8/Svneo) were grown up and plated into 6-well plates. Upon reaching 70% confluency, cells were treated with media containing vehicle or 65µM coumestrol for 24 hours. At the time of harvest, cells were washed with sterile PBS and then RNA was isolated for downstream microarray analysis.
