Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

gene expression profiling of placental tissue from 5 controls and 5 PEs were assessed using microarray.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from placental tissues with using Trizol reagent (Life technologies, Gaithersburg, MD). RNA quality was evaluated using formaldehyde agarose gel electrophoresis and was quantified via spectrophotometry (Nano drop, Wilmington, DE). Label Cy5 Label protocol RNA was amplified and labelled according to a previous protocol. Briefly, total RNA was used to synthesize the double strand cDNA. RNA was amplified by in vitro transcription using Ambion’s MessageAmp™ II aRNA Amplification Kits (Life Technologies, Austin, TX, USA). Then, aRNA was reverse transcribed into cDNA and further labelled with Klenow enzyme. cDNA from placental tissue of PE was labelled with Cy5-dCTP and cDNA from control placental tissue was labelled with Cy3-dCTP. Samples of approximately 0.2–0.5 cm3 were excised from central cotyledons close to the umbilical cord in order to reduce the possible bias due to regional differences in gene expression. After extensive washing in saline solution to remove excessive blood, the samples were stored in RNALater reagent. Growth protocol Placental samples were collected from women complicated by severe preeclampsia (n=5) and women with normal pregnancies (n=5) at Peking Union Medical College Hospital. Severe preeclampsia was defined as blood pressure ≥160/100mmHg, measured at least 6 h apart, in combination with proteinuria ≥3g/24 hours or ≥2+ (dipstick), developing after 20 weeks of pregnancy. Women who had a history of cardiovascular, renal, and other hypertension-associated diseases were excluded, but family history of PE was not recorded.
