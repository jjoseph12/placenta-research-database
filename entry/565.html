Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq analysis of multiple cohorts of placenta tissues

Extraction protocol

Placental villous tissue was collected from women who were undergoing elective pregnancy terminations (6-23+6 weeks’ gestation) immersed in RNAlater solution (Invitrogen) within minutes of termination and placed at -4°C for 24 h prior to being stored at -80°C. RNA was extracted from each placental villous sample using the Qiagen RNeasy kit following the manufacturer’s protocols. Sequencing libraries were prepared using Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Sample Preparation kits and all the ribosomal RNA was depleted using Ribo-Zero Gold.

Main topic
This study investigates transcript variability in the human placenta across 6–23 weeks’ gestation, identifying differential transcript expression and usage between early and mid gestation and revealing that differential expression patterns of individual transcripts are often masked at the gene-level.
