Main topic
This study investigates the impact of hyperglycemia on the metabolic function of BeWo trophoblast cells, focusing on mitochondrial respiratory activity and nutrient storage, using multi-omics and functional metabolic analysis to understand the effects of gestational diabetes on placental cells.
Workbook excerpt Source evidence 30 evidence notes linked 0 supplement repair findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates the impact of independent hyperglycemia on metabolic function, mitochondrial respiration, and nutrient storage in cytotrophoblast-like and syncytiotrophoblast-like BeWo cells.
Source excerpt
The current study examined metabolic and mitochondrial function as well as nutrient storage in both undifferentiated cytotrophoblast and differentiated syncytiotrophoblast BeWo cells cultured under hyperglycemia conditions (25 mM glucose) for 72 hours to further characterize the direct impacts of placental hyperglycemic exposure.
