Main topic
This study performs a systematic micro-array analysis of GPCR expression in primary mouse macrophages to identify family members that are either enriched in macrophages compared to other cell types or are regulated by the bacterial product lipopolysaccharide (LPS). The study identifies several P2RY family members with striking expression patterns in macrophages.
