Extraction protocol

Multiple pools of freshly dissected decidua (E6.5) from mice expressing an endogenous N-terminal EGFP tagged Blimp1 protein (Mitani et al., 2017) and WT mice were minced using a razor blade in a drop of culture media (RPMI-1640, 10% FCS, 0.0004% β-mercaptoethanol, 1x Pen/Strep, 2mM glutamine) and cross-linked in 1% formaldehyde in culture media for 20 min at ambient temperature. After quenching in 0.125 M glycine for 5 min, cells were washed in PBS and processed for ChIP using 6 micrograms of rabbit anti-GFP antibody (Ab290, Abcam). Triplicate test (GFP ChIP of Prdm1NEG/NEG) and duplicate control (GFP ChIP of WT) decidual samples, and their respective input samples were analysed. DNA was multiplexed and paired end sequencing was performed over two lanes of Illumina HiSeq4000 platform. Total RNA was extracted using an RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen Cat#74104) with on column DNase treatment according manufacturer’s protocol. RNA was normalized to 800ng per sample followed by depletion of cytoplasmic and mitochondrial ribosomal RNA sequences (Ribo-Zero Gold rRNA Removal Kit (H/M/R), Cat: #MRZG12324). Subsequent library preparation was completed using the Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Library Prep kit (H/M/R) (Cat: #20020597). Followed by sequencing on an Illumina HiSeq4000 (75bp paired end).
