Methods text

Assay description

Reduced representation bisulfite sequencing of E11.5 embryonic tissue (whole embryo and placenta) obtained from naturally mating, or ART matings wildtype or heterozygote for Dnmt1o

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted from embryos and placentas using DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit (Qiagen) as per manufacturer protocols Reduced represenation bisulfite sequencing (RRBS) libraries were prepared according to previously published prototols using gel-free technique (Boyle, P. Genome Biol., 2012). Briefly, DNA was digested with MspI restriction enzyme, followed by size selection, gap filling and 3'-A-tailing. Ligation of NEBNext methylated adaptors was performed, followed by bisulfite conversion and index PCR amplificaton.
