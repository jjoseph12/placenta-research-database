Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was purified by using RNeasy Mini kit (QIAGEN). Microarray targets from 200 ng total RNA were synthesized and labelled using the Low RNA Input Linear Amp Kit (Agilent) and hybridized to Mouse 4x44K Ver.2.0 arrays. Arrays were scanned on an Agilent Technologies Microarray scanner and signal intensities were calculated in Agilent Feature Extraction 10.7.3.1 software.

Main topic
This paper reports the establishment of defined culture conditions for deriving and maintaining undifferentiated trophoblast stem (TS) cells from mouse blastocysts and early postimplantation extraembryonic ectoderm using FGF2, activin A, XAV939, and Y27632.

Reviewed answer The establishment and derivation of mouse trophoblast stem (TS) cells under chemically defined culture conditions from pre-implantation blastocysts and early postimplantation extraembryonic ectoderm.
Source excerpt
Here, we report that addition of FGF2, activin A, XAV939, and Y27632 are necessary and sufficient for derivation of TS cells from both of E3.5 blastocysts and E6.5 early postimplantation extraembryonic ectoderm. Moreover, the undifferentiated TS cell state can be stably maintained in chemically defined culture conditions.
