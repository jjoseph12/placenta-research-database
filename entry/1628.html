Main topic
This paper examines the expression stability of ribosomal protein genes across different mouse tissues to assess their potential as reference genes for normalizing gene expression data. The study reveals that ribosomal protein genes exhibit significant tissue-dependent variation in mRNA expression, questioning their suitability as true reference genes despite their housekeeping status.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication evaluates the expression stability of ribosomal protein genes across various tissues, showing they exhibit tissue-dependent variation and cautioning against their use as universal reference genes.
Source excerpt
Here we provide statistical and biological evidence that ribosomal protein genes exhibit important tissue-dependent variation in mRNA expression. Though these genes are most stably expressed of all investigated genes in a meta-analysis they cannot be considered true reference genes.
