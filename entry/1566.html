Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

A set of 9 different NIH-approved hESC lines were treated with conditions to promote either maintenance of an undifferentiated state (mouse embryonic fibroblast- conditioned media) or undirected differentiation of the hESCs into various cell lineages (DMEM + 20% FBS). RNA was harvested from the cells and subjected to both mRNA and miRNA expression analysis on Affymetrix and Agilent microarray platforms, respectively. An embryonal carcinoma cell line, NTera-2, which shows growth characteristics similar to those of hESCs, was used an additional comparative sample. Human placental RNA samples were used as internal controls for the miRNA microarray slides (Agilent Technologies). The data were used to determine differential mRNA and miRNA expression in each specific cell line, as well as to make large-scale comparisons of the undifferentiated and differentiated hESC lines.

Main topic
This paper analyzes the expression of microRNAs (miRNAs) and mRNAs in human embryonic stem cell (hESC) lines during early commitment and examines the expression of key ESC-enriched miRNAs in earlier developmental states across several species, suggesting miRNAs could serve as diagnostic indicators in defining the developmental state of embryonic cells.
