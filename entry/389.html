Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Analyses of the effects of gestational exposure to pesticides (groups: corn oil control, chlorpyrifos or deltamethrin on the gene co-expression networks of fetal brain and placenta tissue of mouse pups at gestational day 17. C57BL/6J Dams were exposed via oral gavage every 3 days beginning 2 weeks prior to breeding continuing until gestational day 17 (GD17) and were euthanized on GD 17.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. 1 ug was used for RNA-seq libraries) Libraries were prepared with the RNAse H protocol for rRNA depletion (New England Biolabs (NEB), Ipswich, MA, USA).

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of prenatal exposure to organophosphate (chlorpyrifos) and pyrethroid (deltamethrin) pesticides on the fetal brain and placenta transcriptomes in a rodent model, identifying disruptions in gene co-expression modules and signaling pathways.
