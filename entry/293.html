Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq analysis of ESCs, TSCs, control TSCs, P150 knockdown TSCs, IVF 2-cell embryos, TSC-cloned 2-cell embryos, control TSC-cloned 2-cell embryos, and P150 knockdown TSC-cloned 2-cell embryos.

Extraction protocol

RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen #74134) was used for RNA extraction according to manufactures instruction. RNA-seq library preparation and sequencing were performed at Macrogen Japan Corporation. Briefly, libraries were prepared using TruSeq stranded mRNA Library (Illumina #20020594), then sequenced by NovaSeq6000 for paired-end 100 bp. SMART-Seq v4 PLUS Kit (TAKARA #R400752) was used to construct RNA-seq libraries from late 2 cell embryos. We picked up 5 embryos per reaction and subjected to construction of libraries according to manufactures instructions. Next-seq was used for sequencing with 75 bp single-end mode.

Main topic
This paper analyzes the epigenomic features of mouse trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) and identifies large-scale heterochromatin architectures with high H3.1/3.2–H3K9me3 accumulation, termed TSC-defined highly heterochromatinized domains (THDs), which protect placental lineage cells from fate reprogramming.
