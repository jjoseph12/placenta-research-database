Main topic
This study investigates the DNA methylation profiles in placentas affected by early-onset pre-eclampsia (EOPET) compared to gestational age-matched controls, late-onset pre-eclampsia (LOPET), and normotensive intrauterine growth restriction (nIUGR) to identify epigenetic changes associated with EOPET.
Source evidence 34 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Investigation of extensive DNA methylation alterations in placentas from pregnancies complicated by early-onset pre-eclampsia (EOPET) compared to gestational age-matched controls, late-onset pre-eclampsia (LOPET), and normotensive intrauterine growth restriction (nIUGR).
Source excerpt
This case–control study focused on DNA methylation and gene expression of whole chorionic villi samples from 20 EOPET placentas and 20 gestational age-matched controls from pre-term births. DNA methylation was also assessed in placentas affected by late-onset pre-eclampsia (LOPET) and normotensive intrauterine growth restriction (nIUGR).
