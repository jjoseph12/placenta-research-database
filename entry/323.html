Methods text

Assay description

Placenta from ART smaples are compared to control samples, of note that all samples were collected from one ART center and over the same period of time. Controls and ART samples were matched for new born sex, term gestation and matcernal age. Furthermore, an analysis comparing ART subtypes (In-vio versus in-vitro) was also performed.

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted from placenta samples using DNEasy (Qiagen) chemistry and columns. No modification was made to the manufacturer's specifications.

Main topic
This study examines the extent of epigenetic abnormalities in placentas from ART/infertility pregnancies compared to control singleton pregnancies to determine if the placental methylome can be a sensitive indicator of 'at risk' conceptuses.

Our goal was to use genome-wide profiling to examine the extent of epigenetic abnormalities in matched placentas from an ART/infertility group and control singleton pregnancies ( n = 44/group) from a human prospective longitudinal birth cohort, the Design, Develop, Discover (3D) Study.
