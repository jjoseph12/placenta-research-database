Methods text

Assay description

mRNA profiles of placental villous tissue at term delivery from control and SARS-CoV-2-positive mothers

Extraction protocol

Villi were dissected from fresh placental tissue and rinsed three times in PBS. Villous samples were homogenized and centrifuged before RNA isolation using the Qiagen RNAeasy Mini Kit. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing following rRNA depletion using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the susceptibility of the human placenta to SARS-CoV-2 infection and the placental response to maternal respiratory SARS-CoV-2 infection at term, finding that maternal infection is associated with immune activation at the maternal-fetal interface even without detectable virus in the placenta.
