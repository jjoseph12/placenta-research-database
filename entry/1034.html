Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

mRNA-seq of placenta from two term healthy pregnancies.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using TRIzol. All samples passed initial quality testing using Bioanalyzer 2100 (Agilent), assuring RIN values > 9. Libraries were prepared from one microgram of total RNA with TRUseq Stranded mRNA HT kit (Illumina Inc.). The libraries were subjected to paired-end 50 bp RNA-sequencing on a HiSeq 2500 (Illumina Inc.), at the depth of 30 Million reads per sample.

Main topic
This study investigates the cell-cell interactome of fetal placental trophoblast cells and maternal endometrial stromal cells using single-cell transcriptomics, revealing a highly cell-type-specific expression of G-protein-coupled receptors and identifying uterine decidual cells as a cell-cell interaction hub.
