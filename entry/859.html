Main topic
This study investigates the impact of maternal obesity on placental lipid metabolism in the first trimester of pregnancy, finding that obesity downregulates lipid metabolism genes in the placenta, particularly affecting placentas of male fetuses.

Reviewed answer The publication investigates the effect of maternal obesity on lipid metabolism in the first-trimester human placenta, finding downregulation of several lipid oxidation, transport, and synthesis pathways.

We hypothesized that placental FA metabolism is impaired in women with obesity from early pregnancy. We assessed expression of key regulators of FA metabolism in first trimester placentas of lean and obese women.
