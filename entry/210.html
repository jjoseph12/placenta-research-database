Main topic
This study reports reduced expression of miR-92a-1-5p in EGF treated HTR-8/SVneo trophoblastic cells, which controls HTR-8/SVneo cells invasion by targeting MAPK8 and FAS, which in turn increases MMP-2/MMP-9 expression.
