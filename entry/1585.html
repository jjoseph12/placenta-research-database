Methods text

Assay description

Experiments were performed in duplicate, using dye-swap method. We used linearly amplified RNA samples (T7-based protocol), derived from 56 normal or tumor tissues from 12 distincts body localization, and a pool of RNAs obtained from 15 distinct human cell lines as reference. cDNA was synthesized in the presence of aminoallyl-dUTP (Sigma-Aldrich) and labeled using Alexa Fluor 555 or Alexa Fluor 647 dyes (Invitrogen).

Main topic
This paper explores the use of no-match ORESTES (open reading frame EST sequences) from the Human Cancer Genome Project as potential tumor markers by constructing a cDNA microarray and hybridizing it against different normal or tumor tissues to identify differentially expressed sequences.
