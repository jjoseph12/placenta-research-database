Methods text

Assay description

Mouse uterine decidual masses were isolated from control and conditional knockout mice on the morning of day 8 of pregnancy. The decidual masses, from 3 animals/genotype, were pooled and total RNA was isolated and then hybridized to high density affymetrix microarrays according to the Affymetrix protocol (Mouse Genome 430A 2.0 Array)

Main topic
This study investigates the role of Rac1, a signaling molecule, in regulating endometrial secretory function and its impact on placental development during early pregnancy in mice, revealing its importance in maternal-fetal communication.
