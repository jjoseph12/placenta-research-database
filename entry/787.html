Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To understand the role of Isl1 in the inductive interactions between the allantois and chorion epithelium during chorioallantoic fusion and labyrinth formation. We generated Isl1 knock-out mice (Isl1 Cre+/-; Isletf/+) with Black Swiss background through crossed Isl1 Cre+/- females with Isl1f/+ males. Then we performed RNA-seq analysis of the feto-placentas and associated allantoises at E8.25 with Rosa-tdTomato background in order to trace Cre lineage.Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data for control and Isl1-null (mut) mice littermates of the feto-placentas and associated allantoises tissue.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of Isl1 in placenta development, specifically focusing on its expression in extraembryonic mesoderm (Ex-Mes) progenitors and its contribution to allantois and placenta morphogenesis and vasculature formation.
