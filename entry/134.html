Main topic
This paper investigates the interaction between extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs) and decidualized endometrial fibroblasts (ESFs) during placental invasion, focusing on how EVTs reduce the contractile forces of ESFs by secreting HB-EGF, which in turn modulates the SRF-MRTF transcriptional axis and cellular energetics.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs) reduce the mechanical contractility and energetic state of decidualized endometrial stromal fibroblasts (dESFs) through paracrine HB-EGF signaling, which rewires the SRF-MRTF-TCF transcriptional axis.
Source excerpt
Here, using traction force microscopy, we show that increased cellular contractility in decidualized ESFs is reversed after interaction with EVTs. ... EVT induced paracrine secretion of Heparin Binding Epidermal Growth Factor (HBEGF), a potent MAPK activator, which shifts the balance of SRF association away from MRTF based transcription, reducing decidual ESF contractility and mechanical resistance to placental invasion.
