Methods text

Assay description

TAZ expression was depleted in CT27 human TSC lines via RNA interference using shRNA. Global gene expressions were monitored by culturing control and TAZ-depleted cells in stem state culture condition.

Extraction protocol

For RNA extraction we used Qiagen RNeasy Kit (Catalog#74104) following manufacturer's protocol. For each experimental conditions, 1 million cells were used to isolate total RNA. For sequencing, libraries were prepared using the SMART-Seq v4 Ultra Low Input RNA Kit (Clontech) designed for transcriptome analysis from samples as small as a single cell. For library preparation, the mRNA fraction was enriched, fragmented, sized, reverse transcribed into cDNA and ligated with the appropriate adaptors. Finally, deep sequencing was performed using the Illumine NovaSeq 6000 sequencing system at the University of Kansas Genome Sequencing Facility to generate pair-end reads with read length 101 bp.

Main topic
This study discovers that WW domain containing transcription regulator 1 (WWTR1), a transcription cofactor and a component of the conserved Hippo signaling pathway, optimizes trophoblast progenitor self-renewal and is essential for its differentiation into the invasive extravillous trophoblast cell lineage. The findings establish WWTR1 as a critical regulator for success in human placentation and progression of a healthy pregnancy.
