Methods text

Assay description

ZHBTc4, ZHTc6, and EB5 ES cells were cultured without feeder cells in LIF-supplemented medium as described . For differentiation, ZHTc6 cells were cultured in the absence of Tet, which induced the overexpression of Oct3/4. ZHBTc4 cells were cultured in the presence of Tet, repressing Oct3/4 expression. Trophoblast stem (TS) cells and another ES cell line (R1) were cultured as previously described . Three independent samples were prepared for each time point. Microarray experiments were carried out as described using the NIA Mouse 22K Microarray v1.0.

Main topic
This paper focuses on identifying the downstream target genes of Oct3/4, a transcription factor required to maintain embryonic stem (ES) cells in an undifferentiated state, through global expression profiling of Oct3/4-manipulated ES cells combined with genome-wide chromatin-immunoprecipitation (ChIP) assays to delineate Oct3/4-regulated gene networks.
