Assay description

Four Day 33-35 chorionic girdle RNAs were compared to matching chorion RNAs, and fetal tissue from two of the conceptuses. Gene expression in resting lymphocytes was compared to gene expression in PWM treated lymphocytes.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of IL-22, a cytokine highly expressed in the invasive trophoblast cells of the equine placenta, in fetal-maternal communication and modulation of endometrial responses to trophoblast invasion during early pregnancy.
