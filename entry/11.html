Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from whole placenta using an AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit (Qiagen, Cat No./ID: 80204) following the manufacturer’s instruction. Novogene workflow of RNA-seq library construction Single nuclei from WT and Cav1-null placenta were isolated using Pure Prep Nuclei Isolation kit (Product No. NUC-201, Sigma, St. Louis, MO, USA) as per manufacturer’s instructions. The nuclei were used to prepare sequencing libraries using 10X Genomics Chromium Single Cell 3ʹ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3.1 at the University of Missouri Genomics Technology Core according to manufacturer’s specifications.

Main topic
This paper investigates the single-cell transcriptional response of the placenta to the ablation of Caveolin-1 in mice, aiming to understand the adaptive regulation of the brain–placental axis. The study compares gene expression in wild type and knockout mice placentas and fetal brains to identify key cell types and genes involved in this regulation.
