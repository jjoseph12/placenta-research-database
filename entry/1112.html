Main topic
This study investigates the physiological functions of BAHD1, a heterochromatinization factor, and demonstrates that its ablation in mice leads to placental and fetal growth defects due to deregulation of steroid metabolism genes, suggesting BAHD1 cooperates with MIER proteins to control metabolic genes involved in placental/fetal growth and cholesterol homeostasis.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates the physiological roles of the chromatin-repressive protein BAHD1, demonstrating its crucial role in placental morphogenesis, fetal growth, and lipid/steroid metabolism.
Source excerpt
Here, we demonstrate that ablation of the Bahd1 gene results in hypocholesterolemia, hypoglycemia and decreased body fat in mice. It also causes placental growth restriction with a drop of trophoblast glycogen cells, a reduction of fetal weight and a high neonatal mortality rate.
