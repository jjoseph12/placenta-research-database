Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA was harvested from ES cells and TS cells cultured in the presence of 0 nM, 10 nM, 50 nM, or 100 nM Trichostatin A (TSA) for 24 hours for transcriptome analysis.

Extraction protocol

ChIP-chip experiments were performed as previously described with minor modifications (Kidder and Palmer 2010). The polyclonal HDAC1 (ab7028) antibody was obtained from Abcam. Briefly, 1 X 10^8 mouse ES cells (R1) and TS cells (TSC-BK12) (all feeder-free) were harvested and chemically crosslinked with 1 % formaldehyde (Sigma) for 20 hours at 4oC and subsequently sonicated. Sonicated cell extracts equivalent to 2 X 10^6 cells were used for immunoprecipitation assays. 5 μg amplified ChIP-enriched DNA (ES cell HDAC1-ChIP and TS cell HDAC1-ChIP, and corresponding Input whole cell extracts for ES cells and TS cells) was DNase treated, labeled, and hybridized to Affymetrix mouse promoter 1.0R tiling arrays. Mouse promoter 1.0R DNA tiling arrays contain 4.6 million 25-mer oligonucleotide probes spanning a distance of -6kb to +2.5kb relative to the transcriptional start site for 28,000 mouse promoter regions, providing a resolution of 35 bp with 10 bp gaps between probes. At least three biological replicates were used for each ChIP-chip analysis. Quantile normalization, including probe intensity computation and log2 adjustment, was applied to the tiling array data using CisGenome. Peak detection and gene annotation were performed as previously described (Kidder et al. 2009). Briefly, peak detection was performed using the TileMap application in CisGenome. MA statistics was applied to analyze the tiling array data. Post filtering included discarding peaks if the total length was less than 100 bp, or if there were less than 3 continuous probes passing the cutoff, and merging two adjacent peaks if the gap between the two peaks was less than 300 bp and if there were less than 5 probes that did not pass the cutoff between the two peaks. Peaks with an FDR less than 0.10 were also discarded. Enrichment peaks were annotated with the closest gene using build 36 of the mouse genome. Main topic
This paper investigates the targets of histone deacetylase 1 (HDAC1) in embryonic stem (ES) and trophoblast stem (TS) cells using genome-wide chromatin immunoprecipitation, finding that HDAC1 occupies mainly active genes, including important regulators of ES and TS cells self-renewal.
