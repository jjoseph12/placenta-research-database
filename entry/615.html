Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Sfmbt2gt/gt embryos were generated by intercrossing heterozygous Sfmbt2gt males and females. Embryos were extracted at e7.5 and individually dissected into embryo and extraembryonic (i.e. ectoplacental cone and extraembryonic ectoderm) portions. The embryonic portion was used for genotyping, and total RNA was extracted from extraembryonic portions of both wild-type (+/+) and mutant (gt/gt) embryos using Trizol. Total RNA from pools of 12 embryos was extracted and processed by The Centre for Applied Genomics (TCAG) at The Hospital for Sick Children in Toronto. The RNA-seq library was generated using the Illumina TruSeq protocol and was subject to paired-end next-generation sequencing on the Illumina HiSeq 2500 platform. Two biological replicates of each were analyzed.

Extraction protocol

Extraembryonic tissues were dissected from e7.5 embryos. Embryonic portions were used for genotyping and RNA was extracted from extraembryonic tissues of homozygous wild-type and mutant embryos using Trizol. RNA libraries were prepared using the Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Library Preparation protocol with rRNA depletion performed using Ribo-Zero Gold rRNA removal kit.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of the trophoblast-specific Polycomb group (PcG) protein SFMBT2 in maintaining the chromatin environment of stem/progenitor cells, specifically focusing on its involvement in the higher-order organization of the trophoblast genome through interactions with repetitive elements and non-coding RNAs.
