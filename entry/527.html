Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptomic profiles of trophoblastic vesicles (TVs) in bovine embryos during the elongation process, comparing them to intact extra-embryonic tissues (EET) to understand the molecular mechanisms driving embryo development in the absence of embryonic inputs.
