Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

E17.5 placental mRNA profiles from Control (Con), EO and LO PE mice were generated by deep sequencing, in quadruplication, using Illumina GAIIx

Extraction protocol

Placetnas on pregnant 17.5 days were removed, flash frozen on dry ice, and RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 1 ug of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. mRNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
