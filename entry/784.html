Methods text

Assay description

The mesenchymal stem cells were isolated from human placenta and cultured in vitro to passage 5. The mesenchymal stem cells produced by two-dimensional adhesion culture and three-dimensional spherical culture were digested with trypsin and analyzed by single cell sequencing.

Extraction protocol

To obtain single cells, the 2D and 3D cell were digested with 0.05% trypsin containing 0.53 mM EDTA for 3-5 min. Single cells were suspended in calcium- and magnesium-free PBS containing 0.04% weight/volume BSA. Then Cells were added to each channel. After generation of GEMs, reverse transcription reactions were engaged barcoded full-length cDNA followed by the disruption of emulsions using the recovery agent and cDNA clean up with DynaBeads Myone Silane Beads (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Subsequently, the amplified cDNA was fragmented, end-repaired, A-tailed, and index adaptor ligated and library amplification. Library was performed according to the manufacture's instructions (Chromium Single Cell 3ʹ Reagent Kits v3.1. 10x Genomics). Briefly, MSCs were resuspended in the master mix loaded together with partitioning oil and gel beads into the chip to generate the gel bead-in-emulsion (GEM). The poly-A RNA from the cell lysate contained in every single GEM was retrotranscripted to cDNA which contains an Ilumina R1 primer sequence, Unique Molecular ldentifier (UMl) and the 10x Barcode. The pooled barcoded cDNA was then cleaned up with Silane DynaBeads. Barcoded, full-length cDNA is amplified via PCR to generate sufficient mass for library construction. Appropriated sized fragments were selected with SPRlselect reagent for subsequent library construction. P5 and P7 primers, and TruSeq Read2 (read2 primer sequence) are added via End Repair, A-tailing, Adaptor Ligation, and PCR. The final libraries contain the P5 and P7 primers used in Illumina bridge amplification.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of 3D spheroid culture on mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), finding that it synchronizes heterogeneous MSCs into a uniform cell population with enhanced immunosuppressive and anti-inflammatory properties, suggesting a novel therapeutic potential for inflammatory diseases.
