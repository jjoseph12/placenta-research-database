Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using TRIzol Reagent (15596018, Invitrogen) according to the instruction. mRNA was purified with Oligo(dT)-attached magnetic beads, followed by fragmentation.The first strand and second cDNA were generated by random hexamer-primed reverse transcription. The cDNA was amplified by PCR and purified by Ampure XP Beads. The quality control of the products was verified by Agilent Technologies 2100 bioanalyzer.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of adrenomedullin (ADM) in trophoblast differentiation and its potential as a therapeutic target for early-onset preeclampsia (EOPE). The study reveals that trophoblastic ADM regulates placental development and its deficiency contributes to the pathophysiology of EOPE, suggesting its potential clinical uses in early prediction and treatment of EOPE.
