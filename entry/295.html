Extraction protocol

Libraries for ATAC-seq were prepared according to previous studies (Corces et al., 2017). Collected cells were washed and subjected to tagmentation by Tn5 transposase from the Illumina Nextera DNA Library Preparation Kit (Illumina #15032354) for 30 min at 37 °C. Transposed DNA was purified by QIAGEN MinElute PCR Purification Kit (Qiagen #28204) and amplified by PCR using published primers (Buenrostro et al., 2015). ChIP were performed as described previously (Harada et al., 2012). Briefly, 1x107 ESCs or TSCs were collected by trypsinization, and fixed using truChIP Chromatin Shearing Kits (Covaris #520154). Totally 2x107 cells were suspended with 2 ml of ChIP buffer (10 mM Tris-HCL pH8.0, 200 mM KCL, 1 mM CaCL2, 0.5%NP40, protease inhibitor cocktail) and incubated on ice for 5 min. Cells were sonicated using Handy Sonic (Tomy Seiko #UR-20P) with 5 sec sonication (intensity 7) and 15 sec interval for three times, then incubated with micrococcal nuclease (NEB #M0247) for 25 min at 37℃. Reaction was stopped by EDTA, then lysates were centrifuged by 15,000 rpm for 15 min at 4°C. Aliquot of supernatants were kept for input. Antibodies were pre-bounded to Dynabeads Protein G or Protein A (Invitrogen #10001D and #10004D) for at least 2 hours at 4°C with rotation. Supernatants were incubated with beads complexes at 4°C with rotation, then the immunocomplexes were washed by using ChIP buffer for 2 times, wash buffer (10 mM Tris-HCL pH8.0, 500 mM KCL, 1 mM CaCL2, 0.5%NP40, protease inhibitor cocktail) for 2 times, and TE buffer for one time. These complexes were eluted by elution buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl, 1% SDS, 5 mM EDTA and 300 mM NaCl) at 65°C for 30 min. Crosslinking was reversed by incubation at 65°C for 6 h, after which RNAs and proteins were degraded by adding RNase (Ambion #AM2286) at 37°C for 30 min and Proteinase K (Zymo Research #D3001-2) at 55°C for 2 hours, respectively. ChIPed-DNAs were purified using QIAquick PCR purification kits (Qiagen #28106). ChIP-seq libraries were prepared using NEBNext Ultra II DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (NEB #E7645) according to manufactures instructions. Single-end 65 bp sequencing was performed on a Hiseq 2500. RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen #74134) was used for RNA extraction according to manufactures instruction. RNA-seq library preparation and sequencing were performed at Macrogen Japan Corporation. Briefly, libraries were prepared using TruSeq stranded mRNA Library (Illumina #20020594), then sequenced by NovaSeq6000 for paired-end 100 bp. SMART-Seq v4 PLUS Kit (TAKARA #R400752) was used to construct RNA-seq libraries from late 2 cell embryos. We picked up 5 embryos per reaction and subjected to construction of libraries according to manufactures instructions. Next-seq was used for sequencing with 75 bp single-end mode.

Main topic
This paper analyzes the epigenomic features of mouse trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) and identifies large-scale heterochromatin architectures with high H3.1/3.2–H3K9me3 accumulation, termed TSC-defined highly heterochromatinized domains (THDs), which protect placental lineage cells from fate reprogramming.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The paper characterizes the epigenomic properties of mouse trophoblast stem cells, showing they contain large-scale H3.1/H3.2-H3K9me3 heterochromatin domains that stably maintain cell fate and present a major barrier to nuclear transfer reprogramming.
Source excerpt
Our genome-wide, high-throughput analyses revealed that the TSC genome contains large-scale (>1-Mb) rigid heterochromatin architectures with a high degree of histone H3.1/3.2–H3K9me3 accumulation, which we termed TSC-defined highly heterochromatinized domains (THDs).
