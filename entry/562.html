Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

A prospective cohort study including patients from 2010-2020 was performed and human placentas from singleton pregnancies were obtained after: fresh IVF cycles and programmed frozen embryo transfer cycles

Extraction protocol

Placental genomic DNA was extracted using Invitrogen PureLinkTM Genomic DNA kit. The genomic DNA was converted to bisulfite DNA using Zymo EZ DNA methylation kit.

Main topic
This study examines preterm and term placentas from both unassisted conceptions and those conceived using in vitro fertilization to understand the etiology of preterm birth.
