Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

9 zygotes, 10 2-cell, and 5 4-cell mouse (C57BL/6) embryos were collected and multi-cell embryos were separated into blastomeres. 4 inner cell mass (ICM) and 3 trophectoderm (TE) samples are also extracted from mouse blastocysts. Transcriptome profiles for all samples are obtained via Smart-seq protocol.

Extraction protocol

The total RNA was extracted from ICM or TE cells with Trizol (Life Technologies) and the supplement of 1μg of Glycoblue (Invitrogen). The zygotes, blastomeres and the RNA extracted from ICM and TE were used for cDNA amplification using the SMARTer Universal Low Input RNA kit (Clontech). For all samples, the total amplified cDNA was used for library preparation using Nextera XT DNA Sample Prep Kit (Illumina).
