Methods text

Assay description

RNA sequencing was performed on RNA isolated form frontal cortices of gestational day 20 foetal rats. Dams had been exposed to chronic social stress during pregnancy, while control rats were left undisturbed. Stressed rats had been injected with nanoparticle-bound MitoQ (MitoQ-NP) or saline, while control rats had been injected with saline. For each of the 3 experimental conditions, samples were obtained from 4 independent foetuses (from different dams).

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from 50 mg of foetal frontal cortex using the RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen) and subjected on-column incubation with RNase-free DNase according to manufacturer’s protocol (Qiagen). RNA quality and integrity was measured on the 2100 Bioanalyzer (Agilent Technologies). Libraries were prepared using the TruSeq Stranded mRNA Linrary Preparation Kit from Illumina.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of placental oxidative stress in conveying the effects of maternal social stress to the fetus in a rat model, and the potential for treatment using a nanoparticle-bound antioxidant to prevent adverse outcomes in the offspring's brain and behavior.
