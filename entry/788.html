Methods text

Assay description

To understand the role of Isl1 in the inductive interactions between the allantois and chorion epithelium during chorioallantoic fusion and labyrinth formation. We generated Isl1 knock-out mice (Isl1 Cre+/-; Isletf/+) with Black Swiss background through crossed Isl1 Cre+/- females with Isl1f/+ males. Then we performed RNA-seq analysis of the feto-placentas and associated allantoises at E8.75 with Rosa-tdTomato background in order to trace Cre lineage.Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data for control and Isl1-null (mut) mice littermates of the feto-placentas and associated allantoises tissue.
