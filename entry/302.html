Methods text

Assay description

Single cells of human E5, E6 and E7 blastocysts and human blastocyst models by self-organization solely of naïve pluripotent stem cells mRNA profiles were generated using Smart-seq2.

Extraction protocol

Dissociated single cells were transferred immediately into Smart-Seq2 single-cell lysis buffer and immediately frozen. The Smart-Seq2 library was prepared as described in Picelli S et al. 2014.

Main topic
The paper describes the generation of a human embryo model (blastoid) from naive stem cells that self-organize into blastocyst-like structures composed of three tissue layers displaying exclusive lineage markers, mimicking the natural blastocyst.
