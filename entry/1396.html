Assay description

Human embryonic Stem Cells (hESCs) were treated under defined conditions (N2B27) with BMP4 (B), SB431542 (SB) (ACTIVIN/NODAL inhibitor), SU5402 (SU) (FGFR1 inhibitor), FGF2 (F) either alone or in various combinations as mentioned, followed by isolation of total RNA.

Main topic
This paper investigates how inhibiting Fibroblast Growth Factor (FGF) directs Bone Morphogenetic Protein 4 (BMP4)-mediated differentiation of human embryonic stem cells into syncytiotrophoblast.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that inhibiting the fibroblast growth factor (FGF) pathway directs the differentiation of human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) specifically to hormone-secreting, multinucleated syncytiotrophoblasts while preventing alternative lineages like mesendoderm and primitive endoderm.
Source excerpt
Here, we show that BMP activation, coupled with inhibition of both ACTIVIN/NODAL and FGF signaling, induces differentiation of hESCs, specifically to βhCG hormone-secreting multinucleated syncytiotrophoblast and does not support induction of embryonic and extraembryonic lineages, extravillous trophoblast, and primitive endoderm.
