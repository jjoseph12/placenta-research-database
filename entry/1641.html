Assay description

Placental samples from twenty first-time mothers were selected based on placental malaria (PM) status and RNA quality. Ten had active PM-episodes, seven of which had inflammatory cells on histology. Of the ten PM-negative women, five had histological evidence of a past PM-episode, including one with inflammatory cells.
