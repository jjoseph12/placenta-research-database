Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The bone marrow derived macrophges were obtained from Lyz2cre and Lyz2cre:CITED2Fl/Fl mice (n=3). Total RNA samples derived from these BMDMs were subjected to RNAseq analyses.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from indicated samples using the High Pure RNA Isolation Kit. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
