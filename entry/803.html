Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Human naïve PSC were plated at a density of 100000 cells per well onextracellular matrix (ECM; Geltrex™)-coated 6-well plates and cultured with TE medium. Cells were collected for sequencing on day 5. For human TSC cells, cells cultured in TS-1 medium for five or six days were plated on Collagen IV coated 6-well plates and cultured with TSC medium. Cells cultured in TSC medium became morphologically stable after 2-3 passages.

Extraction protocol

Bulk RNA-seq library preparation and sequencing were performed by BGI, China.

Main topic
This paper demonstrates that VGLL1 is a critical regulator of cell fate determination and self-renewal in human trophectoderm-like cells (TELCs) and trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) derived from naïve PSCs, partnering with TEAD4 to regulate chromatin accessibility and cooperating with GATA3 and TFAP2C.
