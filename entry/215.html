Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Comparison of male and female hESC transcriptome during trophectoderm differentiation.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated for all samples at the same time using TRIzol (Invitrogen). RNA quality was assessed using an Agilent BioAnalyzer. TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library Preparation Kit (Illumina). RNA-Sequencing (Illumina NextSeq 500), two runs.

Main topic
This study investigates sex-specific gene expression differences in human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) and during in vitro differentiation of human placental progenitor cells of the first-trimester placenta, revealing early origins for sexual dimorphism.
