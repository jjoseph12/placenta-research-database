Methods text

Assay description

Term (weeks 38 -40) placental micro explants from normal singleton pregnancies delivery by Elective Caesarean Sections were incubated in 8% (PL 8%) or 20% (PL 20%) oxygen for 6 days with (PL 8%-Treat or PL 20%-Treat) or without (PL 8%-Con or PL 20%-Con) histamine and aminoquanidine.

Reviewed answer The paper focuses on the establishment and characterization of 2D primary trophoblast cultures from first-trimester human placentas, evaluating their classical and non-classical HLA profiles (HLA-C and HLA-G) and their interactions with maternal immune cells.
Source excerpt
Our objective was to establish 2D cultures of primary trophoblasts derived from first-term placentas, to study their profile of classical HLA molecules and HLA-G, and to examine if they could be used to study the interaction with immune cells.
