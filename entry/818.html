Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Trophoblast organoids in the maintenance condition and the differentiation condition were digested into single cells by accutase and papain, respectively. The libraries were constructed on the 10X Genomics. Library samples were subjected to Illumina Nova-seq 6000 sequencing system with a sequencing depth of at least 50,000 reads per cell and 150-bp (PE150) paired-end reads.

Extraction protocol

Organoids in the maintenance condition were dissociated by accutase incubation for 30-45 min with gentle pipetting every 10 min. Organoids in the differentiation condition were dissociated by the Papain Dissociation System for 30-45 min with gentle shaking/mixing every 10 min. Cell suspensions were passaged through 40 μm cell strainers to remove cell clumps. Library was used the 10X Genomics with Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3.1 according to manufacturer’s instructions.
