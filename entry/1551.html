Sample, assay, and methods

Sample characteristics

Female worms were homogenized in TRIzol respectively. After centrifugation, the supernatant was extracted with an equal volume of chloroform. After centrifugation, supernatant was taken from the organic phase. The aqueous phase was then precipitated by an equal volume of isopropanol at 4°C, centrifuged to pellet the RNA and dissolved in Milli-Q H2O. | Male worms were homogenized in TRIzol respectively. After centrifugation, the supernatant was extracted with an equal volume of chloroform. After centrifugation, supernatant was taken from the organic phase. The aqueous phase was then precipitated by an equal volume of isopropanol at 4°C, centrifuged to pellet the RNA and dissolved in Milli-Q H2O. | description = The cDNA from female and male worms RNA was covalently coupled separately with Cy3 and Cy5 monoreactive fluorescein, respectively, 5 ug of oligo(dT) 18 was added and annealed to 50ug of total RNA by heating the reaction to 70°C for 10 min, and then chilling on ice. The final reaction buffer mixture contained dNTPs , 2uL of Superscript II reverse transcriptase, and 1 X reaction buffer. Reactions were carried out at 42°C for 2 hrs,.Then RNA was hydrolyzed by adding 4 uL of 2.5 M NaOH and incubating at 65°C for 10 min and then neutralized with 4uL of 2.5 M HCl. The RNA samples from male worms were labeled with Cy5-dCTP and those from females with Cy3-dCTP. The two color probes were then mixed and diluted to 500uL with TE, and concentrated using Microcon YM-30 filter(Millipore, Bedford, MA) to 10uL, The sample was then vacuum dried. | description = The probe was dissolved in 20 μl of Hybridization Solution [5X SSC (0.75M NaCl and 0.075M sodium citrate), 0.4% SDS, 50% formamide. Microarrays were pre-hybridized with Hybridization Solution containing 0.5 mg/ml denatured salmon sperm DNA at 42°Cfor 6hr. Fluorescent probe mixtures were denatured at 95°Cfor 5 minutes, and the denatured probe mixtures were applied onto the pre-hybridized chip under a cover glass. Chips were hybridized at 42°C for 15-17 hours. The hybridized chips were then washed at 60°C for 10 min each in solutions of 2X SSC and 0.2% SDS, 0.1X SSC and 0.2% SDS and 0.1X SSC, then dried at room temperature.
