Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA was extracted from individual E18.5 mouse placenta and fetal thymus pools following either control or influenza (H1N1) infection on E.7.5 and E12.5 or E7.5 of pregnancy; respectively and analyzed for RNA sequence (RNA-Seq).

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from two to three individual placentae from each litter and from pooled fetal thymuses from sham- and influenza-inoculated female (E3.5, E7.5 and E12.5) groups using TRIzol reagent (Ambion, Carlsbad, CA). RNA samples were further purified using Qiagen MiniElute columns according to the manufacturer’s instructions (Qiagen, USA). The RNA quantity and quality for RT-qPCR were assessed using a Nanodrop ND-1000 spectrophotometer (Thermoscientific). RNA concentrations for all samples had 260/280 ratios >2.03. RNA extracted from an individual placenta from each of 3 litters (E7.5 and E12.5) from Experiment 1 were selected at random for RNA sequencing (RNA seq) and analysis (provided by Ted Shade, University of Colorado Cancer Center-Genomics and Microarray Core, Aurora, CO.) in addition, RNA extracted from 3 control and 3 influenza-inoculated E7.5 litters pooled fetal thymuses were submitted for RNA seq. The quantity of RNA was determined using TECAN. The quality and integrity of the RNA samples was assessed using Agilent Tape Station 2200 or 4200 prior to library prep: RNA sequencing and data analysis were performed. Briefly, messenger RNA (mRNA) was purified, disrupted and a double-stranded cDNA library was synthesized using the TruSight mRNA kit (Illumina San Diego, CA). The cDNA library was sequenced using an Illumina HiSeq2500 platform and the quality of single-end (SE) reads using Real-Time Analysis (RTA) v2.7.3.

Main topic
This paper examines the effect of maternal influenza virus infection on placental and fetal growth in pregnant mice, finding that maternal influenza infection restricts fetal and placental growth and adversely affects the fetal thymic transcriptome.
