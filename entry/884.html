Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The deposited data are RNAsequencing data for studying human naïve stem cells to trophectoderm differentiation. The study comprises two human naïve pluripotency cell lines, HNES1 and cR-H9. The two cell lines were treated in four different medium, N2B27 basal medium or N2B27 basal mudium supplementated with PD0325902(PD03), A8301 or PD03+A8301(PDA83). RNA samples were collected in a time course.

Extraction protocol

Cell lysate and RNA are prepared using Trizol/Chloroform Sequencing libraries are prepared using TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit( Illumina), following ribosomal RNA depletion using Ribozero(Illumina)

Main topic
This paper investigates the lineage potential of human naive embryonic stem cells, demonstrating their ability to differentiate into trophectoderm, the founder tissue of the placenta, unlike mouse embryonic stem cells. The study also explores the developmental specification coupled to lineage restriction, showing that epiblast plasticity and the potential for blastocyst regeneration are retained until implantation in humans.
