Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We investigated the roles of various TSC-specific TFs (FOS, GATA2, MAFK, TEAD4, and TFAP2C) in human trophoblast stem (TS) cells by depleting the TFs using shRNAs. Total RNAs were extracted from the TS cells upon knockdown (KD) of the TFs, and we performed RNA-seq and investigate global gene expression changes upon KD of the TFs.

Extraction protocol

Total RNAs were extracted using the RNeasy plus mini kit following the manufacturer’s instructions. RNA-seq libraries were generated with 500 ng of total RNA using NEB Next Ultra II RNA Library prep kit (NEB, E7770) following the manufacturer’s protocol. mRNAs were enriched from total RNAs with Magnetic mRNA Isolation Kit (oligo(dT) beads) (NEB, E7490), and then cDNAs were synthesized using random primers. cDNAs were purified by NEBNext Sample Purification Beads (NEB, E7767). End prep reactions were performed followed by ligation with a sample-specific adaptor and index.
