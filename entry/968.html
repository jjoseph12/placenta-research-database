Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of DNA Cytosine methylation in DPSCs, EPI-NCSC, and LUHMES: DPSC_Control: 5 different DPSCs derived from patients with no diagnosed neurological disorders; DPSC_Dup15: 4 different DPSCs derived from patients diagnosed with Dup15q Syndrome; DPSC_Dup15: 1 DPSC derived from a patient diagnosed with Dup15q Syndrome that also had a duplication of part of chromosome 17; EPI-NCSC: 1 EPI-NCSC derived from a single patient; LUHMES: 2 biological replicates of the LUHMES cell line

Extraction protocol

Extracted and purified using the Qiagen Puregene kit WGBS libraries were prepared using 500 ng of DNA bisulfite converted using Zymo’s EZ DNA Methylation-Lightning kit according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Then, 100 ng of converted DNA was used to prepare the library using the EpiGnome/TruSeq DNA Methylation. The manufacturer’s instructions were followed except we performed 14 cycles of amplification instead of 10 in the last PCR step.

Main topic
This paper investigates dental pulp stem cells (DPSCs) as a model for early life epigenomic studies, demonstrating that DPSCs derived from baby teeth more closely mimic the DNA methylation patterns of the inner cell mass (ICM) and placenta compared to induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) and embryonic stem cells (ESCs). The study also explores the suitability of DPSCs in modeling epigenetic differences associated with diseases like Dup15q syndrome.
