Extraction protocol

Cells were sorted via flow cytometry, genomic DNA was extracted, and PCR for the sgRNA sequences was performed. Illumina adaptors and barcodes were incorporated into the PCR primers. PCR amplified libraries were generated with independent barcodes to allow multiplexing. Whole lungs or placentas were homogenized in PBS before being extracted via standard Trizol extraction protocol mRNA was prepped for sequencing using the NEBNext Poly(A) mRNA Magnetic Isolation Module (NEB #E7490), NEBNext Ultra II RNA Library Prep Kit (NEB #E7770), and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (NEB #E7335, #E7500).

Main topic
This paper reports that activation of the guanine nucleotide–binding protein–coupled estrogen receptor 1 (GPER1) during pregnancy is both necessary and sufficient to suppress IFN signaling and does so disproportionately in reproductive and fetal tissues, preserving fetal health during maternal inflammation.
