Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Intact RNA isolated from 6 human decidua tissues was fragmented, end repaired, adapter ligation and PCR amplified following illumina protocol. Libraries were sequencing by illumins Hiseq 2000.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted by RNeasy Kits (Qiagen, Hilden, NRW, Germany) and treated with DNase I (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Wilmington, DE, USA) according to standard protocols. RNA-sequencing was done in Genesky using TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library Preparation (Genesky, Shanghai, China). Main topic
This paper investigates the role of enhancer RNAs (eRNAs) in regulating trophoblast cells and unexplained recurrent pregnancy loss (URPL), identifying lnc-SLC4A1-1 as a key eRNA that promotes trophoblast cell migration and apoptosis by activating the CXCL8 and NF-kB pathway.
