Assay description

Characterization of each cell line by RNA-seq analysis and differential analysis among them to identify gene signatures

Extraction protocol

Main topic
This paper characterizes enhancer activity and 3D connectivity in embryo-derived stem cell lines representing early developmental fates, revealing extensive enhancer remodeling and fine-scale 3D chromatin rewiring among the three lineages, which strongly associate with transcriptional changes.
