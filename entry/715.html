Assay description

Examination of transcriptomes in 4 experimental conditions (SPTB males, SPTB females, Term males, and Term females)

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from frozen placenta using TRIzol® Reagent (Invitrogen), followed by Qiagen RNeasy® Mini Columns following manufacturer’s instructions. Multiplexed libraries were created using Agilent SureSelect strand-specific RNA library preparation kit following manufacturer’s instructions

Main topic
This paper investigates the placental transcriptome in male and female placentas from women with spontaneous preterm birth (SPTB) compared to normal pregnancies, focusing on Black women to assess alterations in gene expression profiles related to inflammation, energy metabolism, and fetal sex differences in SPTB.
