Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of the transcriptome of extraembryonic ectoderm with sikdm5b or water.

Extraction protocol

According to previous methods, approximately 10-20 cells were used to construct each library. The prepared cells were transferred to lysis buffer by mouth pipetting. Reverse transcription was performed directly using oligo (dT) primers in lysates containing complete cytoplasm. Second-strand cDNA was synthesized depending on template switching with a TSO. Fragment cDNA was prepared for sequence library construction using a Covaris sonicator. A Kapa Hyper Prep Kit was used to complete the sequence libraries.
