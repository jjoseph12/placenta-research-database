Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq of placental specimens from 24 human subjects

Extraction protocol

Briefly, placenta biopsies, free of maternal decidua, were excised from each placenta quadrant, snap frozen in liquid nitrogen for 24 hours and then stored at -80°C. RNA extraction was carried out by first grinding frozen tissue in a liquid nitrogen-cooled mortar. RNA was extracted with the Maxwell 16 automated DNA/RNA extraction equipment (Promega: Madison, WI) using the proprietary extraction kits following the manufacturer’s protocol. Ribosomal RNA was removed using Ribo-Zero Kit. mRNA was fragmented randomly by adding fragmentation buffer, then the cDNA was synthesized using mRNA template and random hexamer primers, after which a custom second-strand synthesis buffer (Illumina), dNTPs, RNase H and DNA polymerase I were added to initiate the second-strand synthesis. After a series of terminal repair, ligation and sequencing adaptor ligation, the double-stranded cDNA library was completed through size selection and PCR enrichment.

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of prenatal exposure to a climate-related disaster (Superstorm Sandy) on placental transcriptome and infant temperament, finding correlations between storm stress, placental gene expression, and temperament, particularly an association between altered placental transcriptome and 'Slow-to-Warm-up' temperament in infants.
