Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using Rneasy mini plus kit (Qiagen). 4 ug of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using KAPA Stranded mRNA-Seq Kit (Illumina Platforms) and KAPA Single-Indexed adapter kit following manufacturer's protocols.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of trophectoderm biopsy (TEBx) on placental and embryo development, as well as offspring metabolic outcomes in a mouse model, finding that TEBx impacts placental and embryonic health with lasting metabolic changes in adult offspring.
