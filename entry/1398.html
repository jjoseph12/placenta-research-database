Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Two-condition experiment, JEG-3 cells vs. valproic acid(VPA)-treated JEG-3 cells. Biological replicates: 3 control replicates, 3 VPA-treated replicates.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA extracted using Trizol, and was purified using an RNeasy mini kit (Qiagen, Valencia, CA), following manufacturer's instructions. Genomic DNA was removed using an RNase-free DNase set (Qiagen) during RNA purification. Label Cy5 Label protocol Labeling was performed using a FairPlay microarray labeling kit (Stratagene, Glenville, VA), followed by the coupling of Cy3 (controls) or Cy5 (treated samples) dye. Channel 2 Source name Control JEG-3 human placental choriocarcinoma cell line Organism Homo sapiens Characteristics cell line: JEG-3 (KCLB-30036) tissue: placenta Growth protocol The human placental choriocarcinoma cell line (JEG-3 cells) was purchased from Korean Cell Line Bank (KCLB No. 30036, Seoul, Korea) and was maintained under a humidified atmosphere of 5% CO2 and 95% air at 37°C. The culture medium was 90% Dulbecco’s modified Eagle’s medium (GIBCO BRL, Grand Island, NY) supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (GIBCO BRL), sodium bicarbonate (Sigma, St. Louis, MO), sodium pyruvate (GIBCO BRL) as well as penicillin and streptomycin (GIBCO BRL). The medium was replaced every 2 to 3 days. JEG-3 cells were seeded in a 100 mm dish at a density of 1.8 × 10^6 cells/ml. After incubation for 24 h at 37°C, the cells were treated with 7.38 mM valproic acid for 48 h.
