Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

DNA methylation at specific CpG sites was determined by MethylTarget sequencing (Genesky Biotechnologies Inc., Shanghai, China), using next-generation sequencing-based multiple targeted CpG methylation analysis.

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted from frozen samples using Genomic Tip-500 columns (Qiangen, Valencia, CA, USA) and from bisulfite-converted samples using an EZ DNA Methylation™-GOLD Kit (Zymo Research, CA, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Libraries from different samples were quantified and pooled together, followed by sequencing on the Illumina HiSeq platform according to manufacturer's protocols. Sequencing was performed with a 2x150bp paired-end mode. Targeted Bisulfite-Seq

Main topic
This study investigates the role of succinate metabolism in recurrent spontaneous abortion (RSA), finding that low embryonic villous succinate levels are associated with increased RSA risk, and increasing succinate levels can reduce the incidence rate in a mouse model.
