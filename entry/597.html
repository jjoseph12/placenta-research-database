Methods text

Assay description

12 samples from 4 different conditions with 3 replicates per sample. Each sample divided across 2 sequencing lanes. Conditions were compared pairwise

Extraction protocol

Cells were lysed in TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen). PureLink RNA extraction kit with on-column DNase treatment (Invitrogen) was used to isolate high quality RNA from Hofbauer cells. Total RNA-seq libraries were generated with NEBNext® Ultra™ II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (NEB #E7760L) and NEBNext® rRNA Depletion Kit with sample purification beads (NEB #E6350) with an input amount of 200 ng total RNA per sample. Libraries were pooled and sequenced on an Illumina NovaSeq SP flowcell in paired-end 150 bp format (Illumina, San Diego, CA) to a read yield between 70 – 80 million reads (equivalent to 35 – 40 million clusters).

Main topic
This paper investigates the interaction between Listeria monocytogenes and Hofbauer cells (HBCs) in the human placenta, finding that HBCs can exert antimicrobial activities but also spread L. monocytogenes, and that they can undergo pro-inflammatory reprogramming in response to L. monocytogenes infection.
