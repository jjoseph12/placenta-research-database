Methods text

Assay description

Whole genome-wide microarray was used to describe the gene expression profiles in the placenta tissues from patients with early-(n=7; <34 weeks), late-onset (n=8; >36 weeks) PE and their controls who delivered preterm(n=5;<34 weeks) or at term(n=5; >36 weeks). Pooled controls who delivered at term were labled with cy5.

Extraction protocol

We used RNAiso Plus (Takara, China) to isolate RNA from placental samples in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions. Label Cy5 Label protocol Sample total RNAs were reverse transcribed to synthesize cDNAs, then transcripted into antisense cRNAs, which were labeled with Cy3-CTP. Pooled controls were labled with cy5-CTP. Channel 2 Source name EOPE_placental tissue Organism Homo sapiens Characteristics subject status: patients with early-onset (<34 weeks) preeclampsia (EOPE) tissue: placental tissue Growth protocol Placental Tissue samples were obtained immediately after delivery. Pieces of villous tissue (0.5×0.5×0.5 cm), approximately 2 cm beside the umbilical cord insertion, from the middle layer of placenta midway between maternal and fetal surfaces from different lobes were excised, excluding sites of haemorrhage , infarction, and fibrin deposition. To avoid RNA degradation, tissues were immediately stored in 1.0ml RNAstore Reagent (CWbiotech Company, China), and then stored at -80℃ until use.
