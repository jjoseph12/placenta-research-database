Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

expression profiling of single bovine blastocysts using custon 7,872 cDNA microarray

Extraction protocol

RNA isolation was accomplished using Trizol. The RNA underwent two rounds of linear amplification (Baugh et. al. 2001) , the aRNA was reverse-transcribed into cDNA with Superscript III reverse transcriptase for 15h at 42°C in the presence of 0.5 mM dGTP, 0.5 mM dCTP, 0.5 mM dATP, 0.3 mM dTTP, 0.2 mM amino-allyl dUTP. After hydrolysis of RNA in 1M NaOH, neutralization with 1M HCl,Tris was removed from the reaction with a Qiagen PCR-column. Label Cy5 Label protocol The cDNA from sample and reference was covalently coupled separately with Cy5 and Cy3 monoreactive fluors, respectively, in 0.05 M sodium carbonate, pH 9.0. The Cy5 and Cy3 labelled cDNAs were purified separately with a QIAquick PCR purification column. Each labelled sample was analyzed using a spectrophotometer, after which sample and reference were speedvacced, and suspended/combined in 40 µl of ddH2O containing 20 µg of bovine Cot1 DNA, 40 µg oligo dT and 20 µg T7dT24. After denaturation for 2 min at 94°C, reannealing 10 min at 60°C, 2x Hybridization buffer (50% formamide,10x SSC, 0.2% SDS) is added and mixture is hybridized to the microarray. Main topic
This study compares global gene expression profiles of bovine nuclear transfer (NT) blastocysts with somatic donor cells and fertilized control embryos, demonstrating significant nuclear reprogramming in NT embryos by the blastocyst stage.
