Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of transcriptomic profiles of human MSCs maintained in culture and injected into mouse blastocyst

Extraction protocol

Bulk mRNA was extracted using RNeasy mini kit (QIAGEN). Smart-seq samples were lysed in lysis buffer containing Triton X-100, RNase inhibitor, dNTPs and miliQ H2O preparing for expansion. Single cell mRNA was captured in Nadia platform (Dolomite) using ChemGenes Barcoded beads for Drop-seq (Macosko-2011-10) RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper investigates the developmental potential of human embryonic stem cell (hESC)-derived extraembryonic (ExE) cells by examining their ability to chimerize with mouse blastocysts and contribute to placenta formation, revealing that ExE cells contain both trophoblasts and amniotic cells with mesodermal potential.
