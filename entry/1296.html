Main topic
This study investigates gene-specific alterations in DNA methylation in placental biopsies from normal pregnant women delivering at term, with term preeclampsia (PE), or with preterm PE, and demonstrates aberrant methylation correlating with disease severity in PE placentas, suggesting epigenetic modifications early in pregnancy can affect trophoblast function and contribute to PE.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates gene-specific alterations in DNA methylation in preeclamptic placentas, identifying differentially methylated genes in cell adhesion pathways and their correlation with altered mRNA expression in both preeclamptic placentas and first-trimester extravillous trophoblasts.
Source excerpt
Using the genome-wide Illumina Infinium Methylation 450 BeadChip array, we analyzed gene-specific alterations in DNA methylation in placental biopsies collected from normal pregnant women delivering at term (n = 14), with term PE (≥37 weeks; n = 19) or with preterm PE (<37 weeks, n = 12).
