Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We performed droplet-based scRNA-seq of over 40,000 cells to construct a molecular landscape and dissecting the heterogeneity of MSCs from perinatal and adult human tissues.

Extraction protocol

Each sample was calculated to an appropriate volume for a target capture of 5000 cells. Single cells were then encapsulated into emulsion droplets using Chromium Controller (10x Genomics). scRNA-seq libraries were prepared using the Chromium Single Cell 3’ Reagent Kits V2 (10x Genomics; Pleasanton, CA, USA) according to the manufacturer’s protocol.

Main topic
This study profiles single-cell transcriptomes of MSCs from different tissue sources and ages to understand how cell aging affects the immunosuppressive activity of MSCs, finding that cellular senescence influences immunosuppression by downregulating PD-L1 expression.

Reviewed answer The paper profiles the single-cell transcriptomes of human MSCs to demonstrate how cellular senescence impairs their immunomodulatory activity via downregulation of PD-L1, identifying GATA2 as a key regulator of this process.
Source excerpt
Our results demonstrate that MSCs undergo a progressive aging process and that the cellular senescence state influences their immunosuppressive activity by downregulating PD-L1 expression. Through integrated analysis of single-cell transcriptomic and proteomic data, we identify GATA2 as a regulator of MSC senescence and PD-L1 expression.
