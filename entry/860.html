Methods text

Assay description

SAMPLES A,B,C,D for the study of EPL. SAMPLES N1-5, E1-5 for study of endometriosis. 10x Genomics Chromium Single Cell sequencing is applied to study the cell-specific transcriptomics for EPL.

Extraction protocol

Human tissue was obtained from healthy donors and patients. Place a sterile RNase-free petri dish containing 1 ml Ca2+ and Mg2+ free Phosphate Buffer Saline (PBS) , the tissue in the protection solution (Miltenyi, Bergisch Gladbach, Germany, 130-100-008) was transferred into the petri dish and scraped the fluff with a scalpel. The tissue was transfered into a 2 ml centrifuge tube and digested at 37°C for 7 min in 0.25% (W/V) of trypsin. After Aspirated the trypsin solution, the cell suspension was washed twice with preheated PBS (Ca2+, Mg2+ free) and aspirated the cleaning solution. Digestion of the cell suspension was at 37°C for 20 minutes with 1mg/ml collagenase I, 1mg/ml dispase and 0.05mg/ml DNase I and observed under microscope to obtain cells and the cell suspension was filtered by passing through a 40 μm cell sieve and centrifuged at 300g for 5 min at 4°C after digestion. The cell precipitation was resuspended in PBS (Ca2+, Mg2+ free) and lysed the red blood cells by Lysing Buffer (Gibco ACK, A1049201). The suspension was centrifuged at 300g for 5 min at 4°C to pellet cells and removed dead cells and cell debris by washing twice with PBS (Ca2+, Mg2+ free). Microscopic examination was performed to confirm the overall cell viability.

Main topic
This study investigates cell development in chorionic villi and decidua of different forms of early pregnancy loss (EPL) using single-cell RNA sequencing, comparing them to normal first-trimester tissue to understand the pathogenesis associated with placental-decidual interface impairment.
