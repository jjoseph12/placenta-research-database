Assay description

Gene expressions in human maternal peripheral blood NK cells were measured at 1st-trimester, 3rd-trimester. Five independent experiments were performed at each term (1st-trimester or 3rd-trimester) using different donors for each experiment.

Main topic
This study investigates the roles of microRNAs (miRNAs) within gene regulatory networks of maternal peripheral blood natural killer (pNK) cells during pregnancy by performing comprehensive miRNA and gene expression profiling of maternal pNK cells and analyzing the differential expression levels between first- and third-trimester pNK cells.
