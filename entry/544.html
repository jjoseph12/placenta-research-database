Extraction protocol

Cell samples were fixed (hTSC, STB24h and STB48h) by 1% formaldehyde solution and ultrasonic using a BioRuptor sonicator (Diagenode). After ultrasonic, fragmented chromatin was centrifuged to remove debris, and 5% of the volume was used as an input control. The rest was incubated with anti-H3K27ac (abcam, ab4729) and anti-H3K4me3 (abcam, ab8580) bound to 20 μl protein A magnetic beads (Invitrogen) on a rotator at 4℃ overnight. DNA was purified and Illumina libraries were constructed using the KAPA DNA Hyper Prep Kit (KK8502) following the manufacturer’s instruction and sequenced on an Illumina Novaseq 6000. For the extraction of RNA, total RNA was isolated from three biological repeats with the TRI reagent (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Ribosome RNA was removed before the chain-specific library was constructed. Each sample was sequencing as 100 bp paired-end reads with MGISEQ-2000.
