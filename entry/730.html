Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-seq experiment in triplicate comparing control (NT-sgRNA) with Bap1- overexpressing mTSCs (sgRNA1) at two different time points, 0 days and 3 days of differentiation

Extraction protocol

Sample collection protocol: Cells were grown in stem cell conditions (0d) and after 3 days of differentiation (3d). Then, cells were washed twice with PBS and RNA was extracted with Trizol. Total RNA was extracted with Trizol followed by DNase treatment using TURBO DNA-free kit (Life Technologies AM1907) RNA-seq libraries were generated from 500 ng using TruSeq Stranded mRNA library prep (Illumina, 20020594). Indexed libraries were pooled and sequenced on an Illumina HiSeq2500 sequencer in 100 bp single-end mode

Main topic
This study investigates the role of BRCA1-associated protein 1 (BAP1) in regulating epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT) during trophoblast differentiation and invasion, demonstrating that BAP1 downregulation is essential for triggering EMT and that its function is dependent on the BAP1/ASXL complex in both mouse and human trophoblast stem cells.
