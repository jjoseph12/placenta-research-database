Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Whole-transcriptome sequencing was carried out to quantify the mRNAs, miRNAs, lncRNAs, and circRNAs expression levels in fetal side placental tissue of women with PCOS.

Extraction protocol

Isolation of the total RNA was performed using Trizol Reagent (Invitrogen, USA). Two libraries were constructed for the purpose of whole transcriptome sequencing. The TruSeq Small RNA Sample Prep Kits (Illumina, San Diego, USA) were employed to create the small RNA library. A 1 µg of total RNA was utilized in accordance with the provided protocol. Following the completion of library preparation, the created libraries underwent sequencing by employing the Illumina Hiseq2000 platform, employing a single-end configuration with a read length of 1×50 bp. For mRNA and circRNA library, first, ribosomal RNA was depleted, and the remaining RNA was fragmented. Following the purification process, the poly(A)– or poly(A)+ RNA fractions were subjected to fragmentation by means of divalent cations at an increased temperature. Additionally, the construction of the cDNA library was carried out utilizing the mRNA-Seq sample preparation kit (Illumina, San Diego, CA, USA).Finally, the paired-end sequencing was conducted on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 system with a 2 × 150 bp read length (LC Bio, China).
