Extraction protocol

ChIP and sequential ChIP assays. Chromatin immunoprecipitations were performed with the following modifications. Anti-H2A.Z antibodies (5 µg), Anti-H3 antibodies (5 µg), anti-CTCF antibodies (7 µg) were added to 40 µl of Dynabeads Protein A or Protein G (Dynal Biotech ASA, Oslo, Norway) and incubated for 1 hr at room temperature, followed by two washes with 200 µl PBS, 0.01% (v/v) Tween 20. 100 µg of chromatin was combined with 1ml of RIPA buffer (140mM NaCl, 10mM Tris-HCl -HCl pH 8.0, 1mM EDTA, 1% (v/v) Triton X100, 0.1% (w/v) SDS, 0.1% (w/v) sodium deoxycholate, 1mM PMSF), mixed with antibodies and incubated overnight at 4°C. Bound chromatin was subsequently washed (10 min per wash) three times with RIPA buffer, three times with RIPA buffer plus 0.36M NaCl, once with LiCl buffer (250mM LiCl, 10mM Tris-HCl pH 7.6, 1mM EDTA, 0.5% (v/v) NP-40, 0.5% (w/v) sodium deoxycholate, and finally twice with TE buffer (10mM Tris-HCl pH 7.6, 1mM EDTA). Washed beads were re-suspended in 90 µl of cold TE buffer and treated with 1 µl (0.5mg/ml) RNAse A (Roche) for 30 min and then with 0.5mg per ml Proteinase K (Invitrogen) for 4 hr at 65°C followed by 1 hr at 72ºC to reverse cross-linking. An aliquot of the original input chromatin was processed in parallel. DNA from the samples was recovered using AMPure XP beads (Agencourt) according to the manufacturer's protocol. Sequential H2A.Z ChIP-H2A reChIP was carried out as described previously with minor modifications 1; following the final ChIP wash, chromatin complexes were eluted twice in 100 μl of 10mM DTT at 37°C for 30 minutes and diluted 20 times with RIPA buffer. Eluates were re-immunoprecipitated with the second primary antibody against H2A (5 µg), as described above. Rference: 1. Soutoglou, E. & Talianidis, I. Coordination of PIC assembly and chromatin remodeling during differentiation-induced gene activation. Science 295, 1901-4 (2002) Chromatin preparation from TS cells arrested at the G1;S and M stages. Cells were grown in 150 mm dishes, containing 2-5 x 107 cells per dish. Cells were fixed for 15 min at room temperature in 10 ml of fresh DMEM medium with 10% (v/v) FBS in the presence of 1.5% (v/v) formaldehyde. Cross-linking was stopped by adding glycine solution to a final concentration of 0.125M. Fixed cells were washed 3 times with ice-cold PBS (20 ml). Cells were then scraped into ice-cold PBS (15 ml), containing 0.05% (v/v) Triton X–100. Cells were then pelleted at 900 g for 5 min at 40C. The cell pellet was re-suspended with 2 ml of Buffer I (25mM Hepes pH-7.6, 15mM NaCl, 10mM KCL, 2mM MgCl2, 0.2% (v/v) NP-40, 1mM EDTA, 0.5mM EGTA, 0.5mM DTT, 0.2mM PMSF, Roche EDTA-free protein inhibitor cocktail) and incubated on ice for 10 min. 2 ml of Buffer II (0.6M Sucrose, 15mM Hepes pH-7.6, 120mM KCL, 15mM NaCl, 2mM MgCl2, 0.2% (v/v) NP-40, 1mM EDTA, 0.5mM EGTA, 0.5mM DTT, 0.2mM PMSF, Roche EDTA-free protein inhibitor cocktail) was added and the suspension was incubated for a further 10 min. Nuclei were released by 20 strokes with a Dounce homogeniser type B and pelleted by centrifugation (40 min at 5000 g) through a 8 ml sucrose cushion containing Buffer III (1.2 M Sucrose, 60mM KCL, 15mM Hepes pH-7.6, 15mM NaCl, 2mM MgCl2, 1mM EDTA, 0.5mM EGTA, 0.5mM DTT, 0.2mM PMSF, Roche EDTA-free protein inhibitor cocktail). The supernatant was carefully removed and the nuclei pellet was re-suspended in 1ml of micrococcal digestion buffer (50mM Tris-HCl pH-7.6, 3mM CaCl2) and passed 10 times through a size 29-gauge needle. The nuclear extract was digested for 30 min at 37°C with micrococcal nuclease (New England Biolabs; 2 units per 330 μl of extract) to obtain chromatin comprising mostly of mononucleosomes. The reaction was stopped by adding 15 μl of 100mM EDTA. The digested nuclear extract was centrifuged at 10,000 g for 5 min. The supernatant containing mononucleosomes (S1) and the pellet (which was re-suspended in 500 μl of dialysis buffer (10mM Tris-HCl pH-7.6, 1mM EDTA, 0.5mM EGTA) were dialysed independently overnight at 40C. The dialysed re-suspended pellet was centrifuged at 10,000 g for 10 min and the supernatant (S2), containing short chromatin fragments primarily mono-nucleosomes, was mixed with S1. The combined S1 and S2 supernatants were dialysed against 10mM Tris-HCl pH-7.6, 1mM EDTA, 0.5mM EGTA, 4% (v/v) glycerol, aliquoted and stored at -80°C. Antibodies: anti-H2A (ab18255; Abcam) ; anti-H3 (ab1791; Abcam); anti-CTCF (ab70303; Abcam); for H2A.Z antibodies were produced in-house, please see Rangasamy, D., Berven, L., Ridgway, P. & Tremethick, D.J. Pericentric heterochromatin becomes enriched with H2A.Z during early mammalian development. EMBO J 22, 1599-607 (2003) Libraries for ChIP Sequencing of DNA were prepared using ChIP-Seq Sample Prep Kit (Illumina) according to the manufacturer's protocol. Quality and concentration of the libraries were assessed on Agilent Technologies 2100 Bioanalyzer and using quantative PCR with adaptor specific primers (ABI Prism 7900HT) according to Illumina's recommendations. DNA from input nucleosomes, H2A.Z ChIP and H2A.ZchIP-H2A reChIP was sequenced employing the Genome Analyser 2X (Illumina) using 75 base pair single end reads. For H3 ChIP-seq we used 100 bp single end reads on Illumina Hi-Seq 2000. For CTCF ChIP-seq we used 100 bp paired-end reads on Illumina Hi-Seq 2000.
