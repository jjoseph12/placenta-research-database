Main topic
This study investigates the role of the imprinted gene Krüppel-like factor 14 (Klf14) in placental function and fetal growth using a murine model, finding that loss of maternal Klf14 affects placental gene expression.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Investigation of the role of the imprinted gene Klf14 in murine placental growth and development, demonstrating that loss of maternal Klf14 results in late-gestation placental overgrowth and alters a specific transcriptional network.
Source excerpt
Our results demonstrate that Klf14 matKO placentas are larger than wild-type controls, and that KLF14 is a negative regulator of placental growth.
