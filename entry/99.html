Main topic
This study investigates the role of human leukocyte antigen (HLA) in villitis of unknown etiology (VUE), suggesting that VUE may resemble a rejection response between the mother and fetus.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study profiles human leukocyte antigen (HLA) classes I and II, gene expression, and tissue typing in placentas diagnosed with villitis of unknown etiology (VUE) compared to gestational age-matched controls, suggesting VUE resembles an immunologic allograft rejection response.
Source excerpt
Therefore, we profiled human leukocyte antigen (HLA), an important predictor of transplant rejection, in VUE using placental tissue from ten patients with VUE and ten gestational age matched controls... The data suggest that VUE resembles a rejection response between the mother and the fetus.
