Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of four human tissue samples by MethylC-seq, with one tissue (placenta) having a technical replicate (taken from same placenta) and two additional biological replicates (from different placentas)

Extraction protocol

Tissue DNA was purified using Qiagen's Puregene kit. The genomic DNA was sonicated to ~300 bp and methylated Illumina adapters were ligated to the ends. 500 ng of library was bisulfite converted using Zymo's EZ DNA Methylation-Direct kit according to the manufacturer's instructions. The library was then amplified using 2.5 U PfuTurboCx Hotstart DNA Polymerase (Stratagene) for 14 cycles using Illumina's standard amplification protocol.

Main topic
This paper investigates the human placenta methylome, demonstrating that the placenta is the first known normal tissue exhibiting large partially methylated domains (PMDs), which cover a significant portion of the placental genome and are associated with gene repression.
