Assay description

We have compared gene expression profiling of FCM-BMP4-treated hESC to a panel of fetal tissues and first trimester tissues. Moreover, we have analysed the expression profiles of cells after shRNA knock-down of p63 compared to Scramble upon BMP4 treatment. Difference in expression between CTB trimester 1 / trimester 3 and PSC were compared the difference between FCM-BMP4-treated hESC and PSC at each time point collected.

Main topic
This paper investigates BMP4-directed trophoblast differentiation of human embryonic stem cells, focusing on the early trophoblast differentiation in the human embryo and the role of ΔNp63 in this process.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The paper investigates the role of TP63 (specifically the ΔNp63α isoform) in mediating BMP4-directed trophoblast differentiation of human embryonic stem cells, demonstrating that the differentiation progresses through a cytotrophoblast stem cell state, mimicking in vivo development.
Source excerpt
Here, we show that BMP4-treated hESCs differentiate into bona fide CTB by direct comparison with primary human placental tissues and isolated CTB through gene expression profiling... our results establish that BMP4-treated hESCs are an excellent model of human trophoblast differentiation, closely mimicking the in vivo progression from p63 + CTB stem cells to terminally differentiated trophoblast subtypes.
