Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Ascl1 or Ngn2 overexpression in mouse embryonic stem cells over period of 6 days and sorted for Mapt-Venus positive (neurons) or negative cells (non-neurons)

Extraction protocol

Cells were washed with PBS, trypsinized and FACS sorted where indicated. Cells then were processed using the Qiagen RNeasy mini kit. RNA was additionally treated with TURBO DNA-free™ kit (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer’s protocols. Integrity of RNA was measured using Fragment Analyzer™. RNAseq-Libraries were prepared using QuantSeq 3` mRNA-Seq Library Prep Kit (Lexogen GmbH).

Main topic
This paper explores the mechanistic differences in how the expression of Ascl1 or Ngn2 transitions mouse embryonic stem cells between two identical states, showing that cell type conversion can occur through two distinct mechanistic paths, even when induced by closely related transcription factors.
