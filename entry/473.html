Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Matched decidua (4) and placenta (4) samples from first trimester singleton healthy pregnancies included 2 female conceptions and 2 male conceptions.

Extraction protocol

Tissue samples (5-15 mg) were stored with 250 μl RNAlater RNA Stabilization Reagent (QIAGEN, Hilden, Germany) at -80°C in the CSMC Prenatal Repository until further processing. Briefly, tissue was thawed on ice with 600 μl of RLT Plus lysis buffer (QIAGEN) and 1% β-mercaptoethanol added to each sample. Tissue was homogenized by passing through increasingly thinner single-use needles (22G, 25G, 27G) attached to a 1 mL sterile, RNase-free syringe. The homogenates were loaded onto AllPrep spin columns and the remainder of the protocol was performed following manufacturer instructions for the AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit (QIAGEN). The RNA elution was used for total RNA-sequencing. RNA-seq libraries were constructed using Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA LT with Ribo-zero kits (Illumina, San Diego).

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of fetal sex on maternal-fetal crosstalk by identifying sexually dimorphic receptor-ligand pairs across the maternal-fetal interface in the first trimester placenta and defining them in each cell type using single-cell RNA-Sequencing (scRNA-Seq). The study reveals sexually dimorphic signaling at early stages of placentation.
