Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

mRNA-seq was performed on uterus and placenta harvested at embryonic day 18.5 from nontransgenic mice, Tg(BAC1) mice, and Tg(CR1) mice.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted with the TRIzol reagent (ThermoFisher Scientific). All samples had RQN >7 measured on an Advanced Analytical Fragment Analyzer. PolyA stranded RNA-seq was performed on an Illumina HiSeq machine using a paired-end approach with 75 bp reads, generating approximately 30 million paired reads per sample.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of the endogenous retroviral long terminal repeat transposon-like human element 1B (THE1B) in selectively controlling placental expression of corticotropin-releasing hormone (CRH) in humans and other anthropoid primates, which is crucial for regulating gestational length.
