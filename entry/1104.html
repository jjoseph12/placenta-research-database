Methods text

Assay description

Three size fraction mRNA profiles of syncytial areas emerged at day 8 BAP treatment of hESC were generated by deep sequencing along with untreated hESC, in triplicate, using Illumina HiSeq 2500.

Extraction protocol

RNAs were extracted with Trizol (Thermo) and 1-bromo-3-chloro-propane (Sigma). Libraries were constructed following the Illumina’s protocol with reagents supplied in Illumina’s TruSeq mRNA stranded sample preparation kit.

Main topic
This paper compares syncytiotrophoblast (STB) derived from human embryonic stem cells (ESCs) with cytotrophoblasts isolated from term placentas, finding dissimilarities that suggest the ESC-derived STB represents syncytial tissue encountered at the initiation of placental development and may serve as an in vitro model for studying diseases of placentation.
