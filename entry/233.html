Assay description

All MSCs (BM, AT, UC and PL) and fibrpblats were cultured and processed under similar conditions. Platelet lysate was used instead of fetal bovine serum (FBS), as a serum supplement. Affymetrix® Microarray and Human Transcriptome Analysis 2 (HTA 2) chips were used for transcriptome profiling.
