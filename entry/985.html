Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq was performed on the following cells: primary hTSCs (CT27), undifferentiated hESCs (H9), undifferentiated hiPSCs, hTSLCs (TS-like cells) derived from hESCs (ES-TSLCs), hTSLCs derived from hiPSCs (iPS-TSLCs), EVT differentiated from hTSCs, EVT differentiated from ES-TSLCs (ES-EVT), EVT differentiated from iPS-TSLCs (iPS-EVT), ST differentiated from hTSCs, ST differentiated from ES-TSLCs (ES-ST), and ST differentiated from iPS-TSLCs (iPS-ST). Two biological replicates were used for each cell type.

Extraction protocol

Global gene expression profiles using RNA-seq were performed in two replicates of primary hTSCs (CT27), undifferentiated hESCs (H9), undifferentiated hiPSCs, hTSLCs (TS-like cells) derived from hESCs (ES-TSLCs), hTSLCs derived from hiPSCs (iPS-TSLCs), EVT differentiated from hTSCs, EVT differentiated from ES-TSLCs (ES-EVT), EVT differentiated from iPS-TSLCs (iPS-EVT), ST differentiated from hTSCs, ST differentiated from ES-TSLCs (ES-ST), and ST differentiated from iPS-TSLCs (iPS-ST). Briefly, total RNAs were isolated using the RNeasy plus minikit (Qiagen). mRNAs were enriched from total RNAs, fragmented, and primed. First strand cDNAs were synthesized following by second strand synthesis. The ends of purified double strand cDNAs were repaired, ligated with a barcoded adaptor and index, and amplified with PCR. Purified final PCR product was sequenced using Illumina Novaseq 6000 machine. RNA-seq library was constructed using NEBNext Ultra RNA Library prep kit (NEB, E7700L) according to manufacturer’s instruction.

Main topic
This study reports that trophoblast stem-like cells (TSLCs) can be generated from human primed PSCs by a short-term treatment of BMP4 without amnion lineage marker expression, and that these TSLCs are self-renewing in long-term culture and bipotent as they can differentiate into functional extravillous trophoblasts and syncytiotrophoblasts.
