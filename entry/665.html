Extraction protocol

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of factors secreted from placental cells on transcriptomic responses in hematopoietic stem cells and whether monoethylhexyl phthalate (MEHP) modifies these effects, aiming to understand the communication between placental cells and hematopoietic stem cells and the influence of toxicants on this interaction.
