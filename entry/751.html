Methods text

Assay description

One microgram of total RNA from each sample preparation was amplified using the MessageAmp RNA kit (Ambion), and 3 µg of amplified RNA (aRNA) was Cy-dye labeled using the CyScribe first-strand cDNA labeling kit (Amersham Biosciences). To compare the gene expression pattern obtained from each 24h rosiglitazone-treated cells (RT-VCT) with the corresponding not treated control cells (VCT), amplified RNA from RT-VCT was labeled with Cy5, while the aRNA from VCT was labeled with Cy3. A total of five (RT-VCT vs VCT) individual cDNA microarrays were performed in this condition.

Main topic
This paper investigates the effects of peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor γ (PPARγ) activation on gene expression in extravillous cytotrophoblasts (EVCTs) and villous cytotrophoblasts (VCTs) using microarray analysis, revealing widespread effects of PPARγ on trophoblast function and gestational diseases.
