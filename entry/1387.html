Main topic
This study investigates how CD158d agonists trigger a DNA damage response signaling pathway in natural killer (NK) cells, leading to cellular senescence and a senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP) that promotes vascular remodeling and angiogenesis, suggesting a positive function of senescence in healthy tissue to favor reproduction through the sustained activation of NK cells to remodel maternal vasculature in early pregnancy.
