Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examine changes in gene expression upon PLAGL1 knockdown. We performed RNA-seq in the human placental cell line, HTR-8/Svneo, with PLAGL1 siRNA and a negative control siRNA. We identified 4,003 differentially expressed genes, of which 1,964 were upregulated in the knockdown and 2,039 were downregulated in the knockdown. Genes downregulated along with PLAGL1 were associated with placenta vasculture development.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted 48 hours after transfection using the Qiagen Mini RNA kit Libraries were prepared using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional library prep kit, unique dual index plate, and poly(A) mRNA magnetic isolation module, following manufacturer’s protocol Samples were run on the HiSeq 3000, across three lanes using single-end 50bp reads.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of transcription factor PLAGL1 in placental development using RNA-seq and ChIP-seq data from mouse placenta at embryonic day 9.5, and experiments with a human trophoblast cell line, revealing its association with angiogenic gene expression.
