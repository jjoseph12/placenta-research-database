Main topic
This study investigates the effects of maternal cannabis use (mCB) on psychosocial and physiological measures in young children, along with the potential relevance of the in utero environment reflected in the placental transcriptome. The findings reveal a relationship between mCB and immune response gene networks in the placenta as a potential mediator of risk for anxiety-related problems in early childhood.
