Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To investigate the role of ELF3 and NLRP7 in regulating HLA-C expression in JEG-3 cells, we generated ELF3 and NLRP7 knockout JEG-3 cell lines. We then performed comparative gene expression analysis of WT and ELF3 or NLRP7 knockout cells

Extraction protocol

Cells grown at 60-90% confluency were harvested, and total RNA were extracted by RNeasy Mini Kit. RNA-seq library was prepared by NEBNext Ultra RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina

Main topic
This study investigates the regulatory roles of ELF3, NLRP2, and NLRP7 on HLA-C expression in extravillous trophoblasts, revealing a novel regulatory axis involved in immune tolerance during pregnancy and potential implications for choriocarcinoma.
