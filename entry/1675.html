Main topic
This study uses DNA microarrays to analyze gene expression patterns in 72 samples from 19 human placentas from successful full-term pregnancies, revealing differences in gene expression among placental tissues and interindividual variations in villus parenchyma, with some genes potentially associated with preeclampsia.
