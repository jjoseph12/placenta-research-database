Main topic
This paper compares porcine and bovine blastocyst stage embryos, as well as dissected inner cell mass (ICM) and trophoblast (TE) cell populations, regarding lipid droplet storage and expression of genes functionally annotated to selected lipid gene ontology terms using RNA-seq, revealing differences in lipid metabolism and gene expression.
