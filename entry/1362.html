Extraction protocol

Embryonic cells and purified TGCs from 29 implantation sites (replicate 1) were lysed in CHIP buffer (50mM HEPES pH. 7.5; 140mM NaCl; 1mM EDTA; 1% Triton-X100; 0.1% Na deoxycholate), sonicated, and treated with proteinase K and RNAse. DNA was isolated following standard phenol:chloroform extraction. Embryonic cells and purified TGCs from 14 implantation sites (replicate 2) were lysed in CHIP buffer (50mM HEPES pH. 7.5; 140mM NaCl; 1mM EDTA; 1% Triton-X100; 0.1% Na deoxycholate), sonicated, and treated with proteinase K and RNAse. DNA was isolated following standard phenol:chloroform extraction. Polyploid and diploid MKs were lysed in CHIP buffer (50mM HEPES pH. 7.5; 140mM NaCl; 1mM EDTA; 1% Triton-X100; 0.1% Na deoxycholate), sonicated, and treated with proteinase K and RNAse. DNA was isolated following standard phenol:chloroform extraction. Tissues were lysed by homogenization in TRIzol reagent and RNA was prepared as indicated in the manufacture's protocol. Ten micro grams of RNA were processed with an Illumina mRNA kit to generate cDNA libraries for mRNA-sequencing according to manufacture's instructions.Thirty-six base sequencing was performed on an Illumina Genome Analyzer II (GAII) sequencer, according to Illumina’s standard protocols. TGC tissues were lysed by homogenization in TRIzol reagent and RNA was prepared as indicated in the manufacture's protocol. TGCs miRNA sequencing samples were processed using an Illumina miRNA kit following manufacture's instructions.
