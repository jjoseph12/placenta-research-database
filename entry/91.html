Methods text

Assay description

3 samples each of human and bovine endometrial stromal trophoblasts were sequenced with and without co-culture with their respective trophoblasts

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated using RNeasy micro kit (QIAGEN, Germany) and resuspended in 10 μL water. SuperScript IV VILO Master Mix (ThermoFisher, USA) was used to synthesize cDNA from 1μg mRNA and ezDNase was used to remove DNA after reverse transcription. Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 was used to determine RNA quality and RIN number of over 8 was observed in the samples. TruSeq RNA Library from illumina was used to prepare mRNA library. These libraries were sequenced on illumina HiSeq to generate 30-40million reads per sample (Single end 75 bp reads) and a high Q score was observed (Q>30) for the sequenced data.

Main topic
This paper explores the evolutionary link between placental invasion and cancer metastasis, proposing that the permissiveness or resistance of uterine stromal cells to placental invasion is mechanistically linked to the vulnerability to cancer malignancy. The study uses comparative transcriptomics and in vitro assays to demonstrate that bovine stroma is more resistant to both trophoblast and melanoma invasion compared to human stroma, suggesting that differences in stromal gene expression contribute to species-specific differences in cancer susceptibility.
