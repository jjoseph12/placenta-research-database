Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Mouse ESCs were treated with 0.5 mM NaB for 2 days in ESC medium. Gene expression profiling using RNA-seq was performed on mESCs (GFP negative) and 2C-like cells (GFP positive). Gene expression of mouse TSCs (NaB induced or blastocyst-derived) were also profiled.

Extraction protocol

Cell RNA were extracted and purified by Total RNA Extraction Kit (LS1040, Promega). RNA-seq libraries were constructed according to the standard protocol of NEBNext® Ultra™ RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (NEB, 7530L).
