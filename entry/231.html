Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

WGBS comparing the methylation characteristics between mouse blastocyst-stage embryos generated by in vitro fertilization (IVF) and somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT).

Extraction protocol

IVF and SCNT embryos of the early blastocyst stage (96 hours after fertilization or activation) were directly subjected to bisulfite conversion using the EZ DNA Methylation-Direct Kit (Zymo Research, D5020). Thirty-nine and 36 embryos were used for preparing the IVF and SCNT samples, respectively. A small amount (0.01 ng) of unmethylated Lambda DNA (Promega, D152A) was added to each sample before bisulfite conversion to serve as spike-in controls for evaluating bisulfite conversion efficiency. Sequencing libraries were prepared using the EpiGnome Methyl-Seq kit (Epicenter, EGMK81312) following the manufacturer’s instructions. Libraries were only amplified for 12 cycles, and were then purified using Agencourt AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter, A63880). Final libraries were subjected to single-read (100 bp) sequencing on a HiSeq 2500 sequencer (Illumina) with PhiX spike-in control.
