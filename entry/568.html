Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Primary human trophoblasts were isolated from first-trimester placental villi. mRNA expression profiling was conducted on primary placental trophoblast cells from five first-trimester placentas (n=5) treated with vehicle control or 25 ng/ml BMP2 for 24 h.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA from human primary trophoblasts was extracted using the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) according to the manufacturer's instructions. Sample quality control was performed using the Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer. Qualifying samples were then prepped following the standard protocol for the NEBnext Ultra ii Stranded mRNA (New England Biolabs).

Main topic
This study analyzes publicly available microarray and RNA-seq datasets to reveal differences in expression of TGF-β superfamily members between gestational age-matched non-preeclamptic control and early-onset preeclampsia (EOPE) placentas, focusing on the role of BMP2 in regulating trophoblast function.
