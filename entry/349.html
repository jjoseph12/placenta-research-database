Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

mRNA profiles of male and female D14.5 murine fetal liver, fetal brain and placental tissues cultured in vitro with 2 mM βOHB versus control, or 0.8 mM AcAc + 2 mM βOHB versus control.

Extraction protocol

Livers, brains and placentas were removed, flash frozen in Liquid nitrogen. Tissues were lysed and total RNA was harvested using Qiagen RNeasy Mini kit (liver, placenta) or Qiagen RNeasy Micro Kit (brain). RNA was analysed on the Illumina NovaSeq 6000 system at the Australian Genome Research Facility (AGRF) with 1 ug of total RNA as input for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols.
