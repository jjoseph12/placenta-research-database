Methods text

Assay description

The CDi was recreated in a microfluidic device (CDi-on-chip) with an outer chamber of DECs and innate immune cells (70:30 ratio) and an inner chamber of wild-type (WT) or KO CTCs interconnected by microchannels. After device preparation and cell seeding, DECs were treated with LPS and pIC, respectively, for 48 hours. To mimic fetal (fOS) and maternal oxidative stress (mOS), CTCs and DECs were treated with CSE, respectively. Expression levels of inflammation and immunity genes were determined via targeted RNA sequencing.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using RNeasy Mini Plus kit (Qiagen). 30 ng of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. cDNA libraries for RNA-seq were prepared using QIAseq Targeted RNA Human Inflammation & Immunity Transcriptome Panel (RHS-005Z-96, Qiagen) according to manufacturer’s instructions.

Main topic
This study explores how chorion trophoblasts (CTCs) act as a barrier at the chorio-decidual interface (CDi) using a microphysiological model, focusing on the roles of human leukocyte antigen (HLA)-G and progesterone receptor membrane component 2 (PGRMC2) in mediating immune homeostasis in response to maternal stressors.
