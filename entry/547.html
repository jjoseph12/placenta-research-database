Methods text

Assay description

Mutant mouse lines were generated with EUCOMM/KOMP knockout first conditional-ready targeted ES cell resources ((http://www.mousephenotype.org/about-ikmc/eucomm-program/eucomm-targeting-strategies). Gene expression profiling was comapred at E14.5 between wild-type, and heterozygous and homozygous (KO) mutants (6-11 per genotype) in Atp11a, Smg9 and Ssr2 lines (Tm1a- non functional gene strcuture only)

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from E14.5 frozen placentas with the mirVana™ miRNA Isolation Kit according to the manufacturer's protocol. Libraries were constructed with the NEB Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep, using poly-A capture for mRNA enrichment.
