Methods text

Assay description

RNA-seq of sorted Hex low and high expressing ES cell populations cultured in serum/LIF or 2i/LIF as well as unsorted ES cells from 2i or 2i/LIF.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from ES cells using Qiagen RNAeasy kit. Approx. 10 µg of total RNA underwent 2 rounds of mRNA enrichment with Dynalbeads Oligo(dT)25 (61005, Life tech). Solid whole transcriptome libraries were made according to the Solid Total RNA-seq protocol with the exception of RNA fragmentation (4445374, Life tech). RNA was fragmented by chemical hydrolysis; heating to 95°C, 10 min in 1x RNAse III buffer (AM2290, Life tech) and snap cooled on ice. ATP (0.83mM, 11140965001, Roche) and 10 U of T4 PNK (M0201L, NEB) were added and incubated at 37°C, 30 min. RNA was purified using Purelink RNA Micro Kit (12183-016, Life tech). Equimolar pools of RNA-seq libraries were made following qPCR quantification using Kapa Library Quantification kit (KK4823, Kapa Biosystems). ePCR and templated bead enrichment was carried out with Solid EZ bead system according to manufacturer’s guidelines. Enriched beads were sequenced on an ABi SOLiD 4 analyser according to the manufacturer’s instructions to generate 50 bp reads in colour space.

Main topic
This paper investigates the totipotency of embryonic stem cells (ESCs) cultured in 2i conditions, demonstrating that they co-express embryonic and extraembryonic markers and can differentiate into both embryonic and extraembryonic lineages, supported by the cytokine LIF.
