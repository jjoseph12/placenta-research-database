Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Four stem cell culture samples (two Naïve and two primed) were sequenced using 10X Multiome

Extraction protocol

Cell nuclei were isolated using the multiomics protocol provided by 10X genomics (cat CG000365), aiming for ~6000 nuclei. 10X Genomic library preparation was performed via the protocol provided by 10X genomics (cat CG000338) and sequenced by 2 units of NovaSeq SP sequencing system (100 cycles). Single cell RNA-seq + ATAC-seq (Multiomics)

Main topic
This paper describes the creation of stem-cell-derived embryo models (SEMs) from human naive embryonic stem cells that recapitulate post-implantation human embryos up to 13–14 days after fertilization, providing a platform to study early human development.
