Methods text

Assay description

Placenta, fetal duodenum and jejunum mRNA profiles in the IUTN and IUHS groups (There are 18 samples in the study and three biological replicates per group in each tissue)

Extraction protocol

Placenta, fetal duodenum and jejunum were collected, flash frozen on dry ice, and RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of maternal heat stress (HS) on the placental immune response and the development of the fetal intestine and its innate immune system in late pregnant mice, finding that maternal HS increases placental immune response gene expression and inhibits fetal intestinal development and innate immunity.
