Extraction protocol

Organoids in the maintenance condition were dissociated by accutase incubation for 30-45 min with gentle pipetting every 10 min. Organoids in the differentiation condition were dissociated by the Papain Dissociation System for 30-45 min with gentle shaking/mixing every 10 min. Cell suspensions were passaged through 40 μm cell strainers to remove cell clumps. Library was used the 10X Genomics with Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3ʹ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3.1 according to manufacturer’s instructions. mTS were digested by Accutase and organoids were removed from Matrigel by Cell Recovery Solution, then DNAs were extracted from mTSCs or organoids for PCR amplification using the same primers for library validation. PCR products were treated with End Prep Enzyme Mix for end repairing, then index were added using PCR reaction. The final library product for sequencing were then purified by beads. The qualified libraries were sequenced pair end 150bp on illumina Novaseq System.
