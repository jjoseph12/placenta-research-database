Extraction protocol

Cell RNA were extracted and purified by Total RNA Extraction Kit (LS1040, Promega). RNA-seq libraries were constructed according to the standard protocol of NEBNext® Ultra™ RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (NEB, 7530L). Cells purfied by FACS were used for scRNA-seq library construction. scRNA-seq libraries were constructed according to the standard protocol of Chromium Next GEM Chip G Single Cell Kit (1000121, 10x Genomics) . Cell lysates were clarified from sonicated nuclei and histone-DNA complexes were isolated with antibody. ChIP-seq libraries were constructed according to the standard protocol of EZ-Magna ChIPTM A/G kit (Millipore-17-10086). Cells purfied by FACS were used for scRNA-seq library construction.scRNA-seq libraries were constructed according to the standard protocol of Chromium Next GEM Chip G Single Cell Kit (1000121, 10x Genomics) .
