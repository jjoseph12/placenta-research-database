Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Female Hoxa11 knockout, heterozygotes or wild type mice were given Hoxa11 knockout or wild type bone marrow transplants. After mating, uterine implantation sites were collected and total RNA was extracted on ED 5.5. 14 samples were sequenced by single-end next generation sequencing and differential transcriptome analysis.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using the TRIZOL-chloroform extraction method. RNA was cleaned using the QIAGEN Rneasy Cleanup kit. Poly-A RNA library construction was performed by the Yale Center for Genomic Analysis utilizing the TruSeq Library Prep Kit 2.0 per the manufacturers specifications.
