Assay description

Placental trophoblast stem cells were subjected to 0, 0.5, 2, 6, and 24 hours of hyperosmolar stress and then assayed by Affymetrix 430MOE V2 whole mouse genome arrays.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of hyperosmolar stress on placental trophoblast stem cells (TSC), finding that it induces both homeostatic responses and differentiation towards trophoblast giant cells, mimicking early post-implantation development.
