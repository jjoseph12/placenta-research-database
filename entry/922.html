Methods text

Assay description

For each developmental stage from female gonads RNA was extracted from at least a pool of 34 ovaries. Total RNA samples from different mouse somatic tissues were purchased via Ambion (liver, brain, thymus, heart, lung, spleen and kidney) and Zyagen (mammary gland, pancreas, placenta, salivary gland, skeletal muscle, skin, small intestine, spinal cord, tongue and uterus)

Extraction protocol

Strand specific RNA-Seq library preparation was performed with Ultra I Directional RNA Library Prep (New England Biolabs)
