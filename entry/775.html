Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted using QIAzol (QIAGEN). RNA-Seq libraries were generated from 400-600 ng of total RNA using the NEBNext rRNA depletion kit and NEBNext ultra II directional RNA library prep kit. Chromatin was extracted 8 days post-infection from cross-linked cells, and sonicated to 200-700 bp fragments. Immunoprecipitation was performed with 15 µg of chromatin and 2.5 µg of anti-H3K27ac antibody. DNA was purified using the GeneJET PCR purification kit (Thermo Fisher). Libraries were prepared from 1-5 ng of DNA using the NEBNext ultra II DNA library prep kit.

Main topic
This paper investigates the gene regulatory function of specific transposable element (TE) families in mouse embryonic (ESCs) and trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) using genetic and epigenetic editing approaches, comparing them with predictions from epigenomic and transcriptomic data, to determine the roles of TEs as enhancers in early mouse development.
