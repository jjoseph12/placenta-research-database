Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To delineate the trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) heterogenity, single cell RNA seq analysis was performed. After the removal of FGF4 and TGFβ1, TSCs were let to differentiate. Samples were collected at different time points (0, 4, 12, 24, 48 and 36 hours) and subsequentially sequenced. Library preparation was performed by following CEL-Seq2 protocol.
