Methods text

Assay description

RNA-sequencing of three independent collections of bovine blastocyst derived trophoblast colonies

Extraction protocol

Trophoblast colonies were collected and total RNA was extracted using RNAqueous micro kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific). After Poly-A mRNA isolation, Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit was used with for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA-seq libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study focuses on developing methods for culturing undifferentiated bovine blastocyst-derived trophoblasts and uses transcriptomics and proteomics to categorize and elucidate their functional characteristics, aiming to understand early events in bovine pregnancy towards initiation of implantation.
