Methods text

Assay description

To understand the difference in gene expression profiles between mesenchymal stem cells dervived from amniotic fluid vs chorionic villi

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from AF- and CV-MSCs at passage 2 (P2) using total RNA Purification Plus Kit (Norgen) and processed by the Functional Genomic Center (University of Verona, Italy) NA-seq library preparation was performed using the TruSeq stranded mRNA kit (Illumina) from 1μg of RNA per sample. Library size was assessed by capillary electrophoretic analysis with the Agilent 4200 Tape station. RNA libraries were analysed on an Illumina NextSeq 500 sequencer using 75nt single reads.

Main topic
This study investigates the anti-cancer effect of mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) derived from amniotic fluid (AF) and chorionic villi (CV) by analyzing their transcriptomic profile and secretome, revealing their potential to inhibit cancer cell growth and regulate immune pathways.
