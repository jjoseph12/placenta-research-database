Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

From gestational day (GD)15 to GD20, twelve pregnant mice received a daily subcutaneous injection of sodium chloride (NaCl 9‰) or alcohol (3g/kg, Fisher Scientific) diluted in NaCl (50%, v/v). At GD20, placentas and paired fetal cortices were collected, immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at −80 °C until use.

Main topic
This study establishes the first transcriptomic placenta–cortex signature of a developing mouse to investigate the effects of prenatal alcohol exposure (PAE) on fetal angiogenesis. The data show that PAE markedly unbalances this inter-organ signature, particularly ligands and/or receptors involved in the control of angiogenesis, and opens new research avenues regarding the impact of placental dysfunction on the neurovascular development of fetuses, which would present a clinical value for early diagnosis of brain defects in FASD.
