Methods text

Assay description

Comparison of gene expression level between EVT and CVT cells obtained from the first-trimester human placenta by RNA sequencing analysis (n=3).

Extraction protocol

The total RNA was extracted from each sample using RNAiso Plus (cat. no. 9109; Takara Bio, Shiga, Tokyo). TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Library Prep Kit (cat. no. RS-122-2301; Illumina) was used with 1 µg of total RNA for the preparations of mRNA/lncRNA-seq libraries according to the manufacturer’s instructions.

Main topic
This study aims to elucidate the gene expression profiles of both coding and non-coding transcripts (i.e., mRNAs, long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs), and microRNAs (miRNAs)) expressed in extravillous trophoblast (EVT) cells isolated from first-trimester placentae, focusing on the role of lncRNA H19 and miR-675-5p in EVT cell invasion.

Reviewed answer The study profiles mRNAs, lncRNAs, and miRNAs expressed in extravillous trophoblast (EVT) cells from first-trimester human placentae and investigates the role of the lncRNA H19-derived miR-675-5p/GATA2 pathway in regulating EVT cell invasion.
Source excerpt
To elucidate the gene expression profiles of both coding and non-coding transcripts (i.e., mRNAs, long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs), and microRNAs (miRNAs)) expressed in EVT cells, we performed RNA sequencing analysis of EVT cells isolated from first-trimester placentae.
