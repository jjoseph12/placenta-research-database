Extraction protocol

Cells were fixed with 1% formaldehyde for 12 mins, followed by chromatin extraction and sonication. Immunoprecipitation was performed with 10 µg of chromatin for 2.5 µg of anti-H3K27ac or H3K4me3 antibody. DNA was purified using the GeneJET PCR purification kit (Thermo Fisher). Libraries were prepared from 1-5 ng of DNA using the NEBNext ultra II DNA library prep kit. Cells were bound to BioMagPlus Concanavalin A coated magnetic beads and incubated overnight with primary antibodies, then washed and incubated with pA-Mnase followed by digestion (100mM CaCl2) and reaction arrest in digitonin/EGTA STOP buffer. DNA was extracted using phenol/chloroform. Libraries were constructed with the NEBNext Ultra II DNA Library Prep Kit. Cells were bound to BioMagPlus Concanavalin A coated magnetic beads and incubated overnight with primary antibodies. Samples were washed and incubated with secondary antibody (Guinea Pig anti-Rabbit IgG ABIN101961) for 1 hr, then washed and incubated with pA-Tn5 loaded with Illumina NEXTERA adapters, followed by tagmentation. Adapter-ligated DNA was released with proteinase K and phenol/chloroform extracted. NEXTERA-tagmented libraries were amplified using NEBNext HiFi 2x PCR Master mix. RNA was extracted with QIAGEN's AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit, and treated with Ambion's TURBO DNA-free Kit. 10-100 ng total RNA was treated with the NEBNext rRNA Depletion Kit. Library was generated with the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina.

Main topic
This paper investigates the contribution of transposable elements to regulation of gene expression in human trophoblasts, identifying endogenous retrovirus families with regulatory potential that lie close to genes with preferential expression in trophoblast and demonstrating that several elements act as transcriptional enhancers of important placental genes.
