Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Comparative RNA-seq profiling of effects of progesterone on cervical fibroblast in cervical insufficiency and normal pregnancy

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from cells and purified using an RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) Poly-A mRNA was extracted from the total RNA using oligo-dT beads in a NEBNext Poly (A) RNA Magnetic Isolation Module (New England Biolabs), after which, RNA-seq libraries were prepared using the RNA-Seq library preparation kit for Illumina (New England Biolabs), according to the manufacturer’s protocols.

Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular mechanisms of cervical insufficiency (CI), a major cause of preterm birth, by examining progesterone-induced molecular signaling in cultured cervical fibroblasts from patients with refractory CI. The study finds that progesterone response and receptor expression are impaired in CI, suggesting a novel insight into the molecular mechanism of preterm birth.
