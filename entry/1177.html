Methods text

Assay description

200 ng of RNA was processed to assess 20,000+ genes using the Clarion S Human microarray (Thermo Fisher) and GeneChip™ 3000 instrument system (Thermo Fisher), all carried out at the SickKids TCAG Microarray facility. There are four biological replicates per group, and two groups in total (3D vs. 2D). Normalization and output file generation was performed with Affymetrix Gene Expression Console (Thermo) using the Robust Multi-Chip Analysis (RMA) algorithm [RMA-Gene-full].

Main topic
This paper focuses on transcriptomic and functional analyses of 3D placental extravillous trophoblast spheroids, which are essential for establishing proper blood supply to the fetus during pregnancy, comparing them to traditional 2D monolayer cultures to identify differences in gene expression and invasive capabilities.
