Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

DNA from 6 chorionic villus samples from the 1st trimester (from 3 male and 3 female fetuses) as well as 3 maternal blood cell samples was extracted, hybridized to probes in the Agilent SureSelectXT Methyl-Seq Target Enrichment kit, targeting 84Mb of the genome and then bisulfite converted before sequencing.

Extraction protocol

CVS: Qiagen DNeasy Blood and Tissue Kit, MBC: Qiagen QIAmp DNA Blood Midi Kit Agilent SureSelectXT Methyl-Seq Target Enrichment Workflow
