Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

MEFs, ESCs and TSCs were used as controls, and cells in reprogramming by different factors were harvested at day 3 of reprogramming and subjected to ATAC-seq, RNA-seq and ChIP-seq. This series contains the re-analysis of GSM1576854, GSM1576855 and GSM1576856 from GSE64684.

Extraction protocol

ATAC-seq: Eight ATAC libraries were prepared: iPSCs, TSCs, MEFs, and MEFS at three days of reprogramming by OSKM, GETM, GTMS or GETMS. ATAC-seq: Library preparation was performed as previously described (Buenrostro et al., 2016). Libraries were subjected to paired-end sequencing using Illumina Next-Seq 500 platform. RNA-seq: RNA extraction was done using the RNesay kit (Qiagen) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Six RNA libraries were prepared: MEFs, and MEFS at three days of reprogramming by OSKM, GETM, GTMS or GETMS. ESC and TSC samples were re-analyzed from GSE64684. RNA-seq: Library preparation was done with QuantSeq 3′ mRNA-Seq Library Prep Kit by Lexogen. Libraries were subjected to single-end sequencing using Illumina Next-Seq 500 platform. Cells were homogenized and RNA was harvested using the RNeasy kit (Qiagen). 1 ug of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols ChIP-seq: Chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) assay was performed as previously described (Mendenhall et al., 2013) using H3K27ac antibody (Abcam, ab4729) and H3K4me2 antibody (Millipore, 07-030). Total RNA was isolated using the Qiagen RNeasy kit RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using the SENSE mRNA-seq library prep kit V2 (Lexogen), and pooled libraries were sequenced on an Illumina NextSeq 500 platform to generate 75-bp paired-end reads

Main topic
This paper identifies a combination of five transcription factors (Gata3, Eomes, Tfap2c, Myc, and Esrrb) that can reprogram fibroblasts into induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs), induced trophoblast stem cells (iTSCs), and induced extraembryonic endoderm stem cells (iXENs) concomitantly, providing insights into the molecular mechanisms underlying the reprogramming process.
