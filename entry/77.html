Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3' reagent kit v3.3 from 10X Genomics was used to extract RNA from single cells. Main topic
This paper focuses on deriving functional trophoblast stem cells (TSCs) from primed human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) using a modified two-step protocol involving BMP4 and a newly developed TSC medium, demonstrating their similarity to placenta-derived and naive hPSC-derived TSCs in terms of transcriptome and function, offering a new platform for pregnancy research.
