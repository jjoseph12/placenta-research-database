Methods text

Assay description

23 preeclamptic, and 37 control placentas included in this study were collected during the years of 2004-2008 and hybridized in two batches to microarrays. Samples were randomized across arrays to control for array and batch variability.

Main topic
This study describes the genome-wide gene expression profiles of human placentas to uncover gene expression patterns associated with preeclampsia, identifying changes in immune-associated signaling pathways and upregulation of sialic acid acetylesterase.
