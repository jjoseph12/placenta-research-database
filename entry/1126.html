Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Single-cell from single elongation embryos were isolated with trypLE, resuspended in 0.04% BSA and analyzed using scRNAseq.

Extraction protocol

After embryo collection, fresh embryos were washed with PBS and placed in a 3% FBS in ice cold PBS. Embryos were centrifuged for 5 minutes at 400xg at 4ºC. After supernatant aspiration, embryos were resuspended in 200mL of TrypLE and minced by scissors. 500mL of TrypLE were added and then embryos were incubated at 37°C in a shaker at 150rpm for 4-7 minutes depending on size of embryos. Dissociation was stopped with same volume of 3% FBS (700L), and the suspensions were pass through 70m cell strainer and centrifuged for 5 min at 400xg at 4ºC. The cell pellet was resuspended with 0.04% BSA (volume depended on the size of cell pellet). Cell suspensions were filtered through 40m cell strainer into a new 1.5mL Eppendorf tube. Cell viability and concentration were measured using a Countess Automated Cell Counter. Library was performed following manufacter's instructions (10x Chromium Next GEM Single Cel 3' Reagent Kit v3.1 Dual Index) and was target of 10,000 cells per sample.

Main topic
This paper presents single-cell transcriptomes of bovine peri-implantation embryo development at day 12, 14, 16, and 18 post-fertilization to define the cellular composition and gene expression of embryonic disc, hypoblast, and trophoblast lineages in bovine peri-implantation embryos, and identify markers and pathway signaling that represent distinct stages of bovine peri-implantation lineages.
