Methods text

Assay description

Examine allelic expression in E17.5 and E13.5 placenta tissues from two reciprocal crosses, AKR-PWD and B6-CAST.

Extraction protocol

mRNA-seq libraries were made for F1 female placenta total RNA from AKRxPWD E17.5, PWDxAKR E17.5, B6xCAST E17.5, CASTxB6 E17.5, B6xCAST E13.5 and CASTxB6 E13.5 (mother comes first) crosses, using Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Illumina Inc., CA) with 4 µg of total mRNA input . The six mRNA-seq libraries were pooled and sequenced for three 51bp lanes on Illumina HiSeq2000 machine (Illumina Inc., CA). Image analysis and base calling were performed by the Illumina instrument software (Illumina pipeline CASAVA v1.7).
