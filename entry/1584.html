Main topic
This study catalogs individual-to-individual variation and differences between homologous chromosomes within the same individual (allele-specific variation) in chromatin structure and transcription factor binding in lymphoblastoid cells derived from individuals of geographically diverse ancestry, finding that a significant proportion of active chromatin sites are individual-specific and allele-specific and heritable.
