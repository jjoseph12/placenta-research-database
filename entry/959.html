Extraction protocol

Histone-DNA complexes were immuoprecipitated from sonicated nuclei. Libraries were prepared using the Illumina TruSeq system and PCR amplification. RNeasy Mini Plus reagents (Qiagen) and QIAshredder columns (Qiagen). Poly(A)+ RNA was enriched using magnetic oligo(dT) containing beads (Invitrogen). cDNA was prepared and strand-specific libraries were constructed using the dUTP method.

Main topic
The paper investigates the role of H3K27me3 dynamics in regulating uterine states during pregnancy and parturition in mice, revealing its importance in maintaining uterine quiescence in early gestation and enabling decidual activation in late gestation. Immunofluorescence analysis of human specimens suggested that similar regulatory events might occur in the human decidua.
