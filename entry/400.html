Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The study included Nlrp3+/+ and Nlrp3-/- dams intra-amniotically injected with lipopolysaccharide (LPS) or PBS. Decidual and uterine macrophages and neutrophils were sorted by fluorescence-activated cell sorting. The number of samples analyzed were as follows: Macrophages, n = 3 per group; Neutrophils, n = 2 per group. Please note that C57BL/6 and B6.129S6-Nlrp3tm1Bhk/J strains were used for the study

Extraction protocol

Pregnant Nlrp3+/+ and Nlrp3-/- dams were intra-amniotically injected with LPS or PBS on 16.5 days post coitum. Sixteen hours after the injection, the decidual and uterine tissues were collected for the isolation of neutrophils and macrophages using fluorescence activated cell sorting. Total RNA was extracted from the sorted cells using the Pico Pure RNA isolation kit (Applied Biosystems) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. RNA concentrations, purity, and integrity were evaluated using the NanoDrop 1000 spectrophotometer and the Bioanalyzer 2100. Two to four biological replicates from each group were combined for the neutrophil samples to obtain sufficient RNA quantities for RNA-seq. Low-input RNA-seq libraries were constructed by Beijing Genomics Institute (BGI; Wuhan, China) using the Nextera DNA Library Preparation Kit (Illumina, San Diego, CA, USA). Library sequencing was performed by BGI.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of fetal and maternal NLRP3 signaling in preterm labor and birth, using a translationally relevant model of preterm birth in Nlrp3 deficient mice to demonstrate that Nlrp3 deficiency protects against intra-amniotic inflammation-induced preterm birth and neonatal mortality.
