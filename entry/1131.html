Methods text

Assay description

To investigate the function of SP6 in the regulation of trophoblast cell generation， we established GATA3-mCherry knock in （G3M），SP6 knock out (SP6 KO) and inducible exogenous SP6 overexpression（DOX/noDOX）EPSCs cell lines. These cells were differentiated to cytotrophoblast-like cells respectively.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from cultured cells using Rneasy mini kit (Qiagen). 1.5 ug of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. Library for RNA-seq were prepared using Illumina TruseqTM RNA sample prep Kit.
