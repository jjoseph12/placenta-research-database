Main topic
This paper investigates a novel population of macrophages expressing the β chain of the IL-15 receptor complex (CD122) that respond to IL-15 and are enriched in the uterus of mice and humans, suggesting their potential role in mediating IL-15 signals critical for optimal pregnancy outcomes.

Reviewed answer The paper identifies and details a novel population of CD122+ macrophages in the uterus of mice and humans that are driven by M-CSF and interferons and respond to IL-15.

We now show a novel population of macrophages, evident in several organs but enriched in the uterus of mice and humans, expressing the β chain of the IL-15 receptor complex (CD122) and responding to IL-15.
