Main topic
This paper investigates the imprinted expression in cystic embryoid bodies (EBs) differentiated from mouse embryonic stem cells, revealing that cystic EBs model embryonic, rather than extra-embryonic, imprinted expression and share more similarities with fetal liver than yolk sac endoderm.
