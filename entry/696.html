Main topic
This study investigates the role of subgenomic flaviviral RNA (sfRNA) in Zika virus (ZIKV) pathogenesis, demonstrating that sfRNA is required for transplacental virus dissemination and fetal brain infection in pregnant mice, promotes apoptosis of neural progenitor cells, and inhibits multiple antiviral pathways by reducing STAT1 phosphorylation through cooperation with the viral protein NS5.
