Assay description

Cultured amniocytes and fibroblasts cells were used for RNA extraction and hybridization on Affymetrix microarrays. We used two trisomy 13, two trisomy 18 and nine controls from amniocytes cells; and five trisomy 21 and five controls from chorionic villi (fibroblasts).
