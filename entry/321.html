Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The mRNA libraries of first trimester decidual macrophage subpopulations and monocytes were sequenced by illumina hiseq 2000 in triplicates. And SRA Study accession was SRP091948. BioProject accession was PRJNA350204.

Extraction protocol

When primary cells were isolated by FACS or MACS, cells were precipitated. RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent. RNA concentration and quality were tested by Agilent 2100. 0.2 ug of total RNA was utilized for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study identifies three decidual macrophage subsets in the uterus during early human pregnancy and suggests their potential roles in maintaining inflammatory balance and interacting with extravillous trophoblast cells.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The identification and characterization of three distinct decidual macrophage subsets (CCR2-CD11cLO, CCR2-CD11cHI, and CCR2+CD11cHI) at the human maternal-fetal interface during the first trimester of pregnancy.
Source excerpt
In the present study, we identified three decidual macrophage subsets, CCR2−CD11cLO (CD11c low, ~80%), CCR2−CD11cHI (CD11c high, ~5%), and CCR2+CD11cHI (CD11c high, 10–15%), during the first trimester of human pregnancy by flow cytometry analysis.
