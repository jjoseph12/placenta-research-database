Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Single Cell RNA sequencing of early trophoblast revealed the mystery of human implantation, highlighting the critical role of interferon signaling. In original datasets, S, L and EVT represent CTB, STB and MTB trophoblasts sub-lineages, respectively.

Extraction protocol

The RNA-seq libraries were generated from individually cells using Smart-seq2 with minor modification. Briefly, individually cells were lysed and mRNA was captured and amplified by using Smart-seq2 v4 kit (Clontech). After pre-amplification and AMPure XP beads purification, amplified RNAs were quality checked using Agilent High Sensitivity D5000 kit (Agilent Technologies). High quality of amplified RNAs were subject to library preparation using Nextera XT DNA Library Preparation Kit (Illumina) and multiplexed by Nextera XT Indexes (Illumina). The quality and quantity of sequencing libraries were determined using Qubit 3.0 assay (Life Technologies) and Agilent High Sensitivity kit at Tapestation 4200 system (Agilent), respectively. Pooled indexed libraries were then sequenced on Illumina HiSeq X platform with 125 bp pair-end reads.

Main topic
This study uses single-cell RNA sequencing of cells from cultured human blastocysts to define the transcriptomic landscape of placental trophoblast during the peri-implantation period, revealing the emergence of different cell lineages and their roles in placental development, hormone production, and interaction with the maternal immune system.
