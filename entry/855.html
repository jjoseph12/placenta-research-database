Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA was extracted from fresh villous tissue from 20 spontaneous preterm (cases) and 20 term (controls) human placentas. Bulk NGS RNA sequencing was conducted using MinION Oxford Nanopore Technology. Differential gene expression analysis was conducted to identify significantly differentially expressed genes in the preterm placenta. Gene ontology and signalling pathway analyses were conducted using upregulated and downregulated gene sets separately. A sub-group analysis was also conducted to differentiate gene expression profiles of male and female preterm placentas compared to term placentas, to elucidate the influences of fetal sex differences on preterm labour.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from the villous tissue samples using RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (QIAgen, Cat No- 74134), following the manufacturer’s instruction. Briefly, 35 mg of frozen tissue was lysed in RLT Lysis buffer using a TissueLyser LT homogeniser (Qiagen, Cat No- 85600) for 10 min at 50 Hz. Genomic DNA was removed using a gDNA eliminator spin column. The quality and quantity of the yield RNA was checked using Nanodrop ND1000, Qubit RNA HS Assay Kit (ThermoFisher, Cat No- Q32852) and Agilent 6000 Nano kit (Agilent, Cat No- 5067-1511). All RNA samples were stored in -80 degree C prior to library preparation. Sequencing libraries were prepared using the Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT) PCR-cDNA Barcoding Kit (Oxford Nanopore, Cat No- SQK-PCB109). 50 ng of total RNA from each sample was used for library preparation.

Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular mechanisms of spontaneous preterm birth (PTB) by conducting RNA-seq transcriptomic analysis, qRT-PCR, and ELISA on placental villous tissue from spontaneous preterm and term deliveries, aiming to identify dysregulated genes and signaling pathways involved in PTB pathogenesis.
