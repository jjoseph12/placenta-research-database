Assay description

Uterine mast cells (MCs) were isolated from human term placenta with positive selection using CD117 and IgE antibodies coupled with magnetics beads and HMC-1.2 cell lines were used as controls. For each condition ( MCS and HMC-1), each microarray is a replicate of the same biological sample.
