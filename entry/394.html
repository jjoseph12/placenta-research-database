Methods text

Assay description

Placental small RNA profiles of 8 preeclamptic (PE) and 6 normal pregnant (NP) women and medium/large STB-Evs small RNA profiles of 8 preeclamptic (PE) and 7 normal pregnant (NP) women

Extraction protocol

For Placenta tissue, samples were mechanically homogenised, RNA was harvested using Rneasy mini kit. For STB-EVS, RNA was harvested with the miRCURY RNA isolation kit. Illumina TruSeq-stranded total RNA 20020597 was used with an equal amount of total RNA (depending on the sample type) for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
