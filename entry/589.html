Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We performed allele-specific and RNA strain-specific RNA-seq analysis of total RNA samples isolated from individual, 8.5-9.5 dpc placentas of somite stage-matched embryos carrying different deficiencies of Ehmt2. The genotypes and parental genotypes regarding the Ehmt2 mutant allele varied to allow detecting robust or subtle RNA changes due to the specific deficiencies in EHMT2 function. Maternal mutant, maternal haploinsufficient, paternal haploinsufficient and control placentas were collected at the 6-somite stage. Zygotic homozygous and wild type placentas were collected at the 6-somite and 12-somite stage to allow measuring transcriptomic differences in the progression of development. Reciprocal mouse crosses between the 129S1 and JF1/Ms mouse strains were used to generate the placentas to allow distingushing the parental alleles. Four replicate samples (2 males and 2 females) were collected for RNAseq analysis from each condition above. Total RNA was deep sequenced to allow detecting non-coding RNAs.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from placentas by acid-phenol extraction using RNA-Bee (Tel-TEST) followed by isopropanol precipitation. Genomic DNA was removed with the DNA-free Kit (Ambion). Total RNA ibraries were prepared by the Van Andel Genomics Core from 500 ng of total RNA using the KAPA RNA HyperPrep Kit with RiboseErase (v1.16) (Kapa Biosystems, Wilmington, MA USA) following kit parameters. In short, RNA was sheared for 6 min at 86 oC to produce 300-400 bp fragments and it was converted to cDNA. After repairing the ends the cDNA fragments were ligated to Bioo Scientific NEXTflex Adapters (Bioo Scientific, Austin, TX, USA) and amplified using 11 cycles of PCR. Quality and quantity of the finished libraries were assessed using a combination of Agilent DNA High Sensitivity chip (Agilent Technologies, Inc.), QuantiFluor® dsDNA System (Promega Corp., Madison, WI, USA), and Kapa Illumina Library Quantification qPCR assays (Kapa Biosystems). Individually indexed libraries were pooled, and paired end 75 bp sequencing was performed on an Illumina NextSeq 500 sequencer using a 150 bp HO sequencing kit (v2) (Illumina Inc., San Diego, CA, USA), with all libraries run across 5 flowcells to return a minimum read depth of 40M read pairs per library. Base calling was done by Illumina NextSeq Control Software (NCS) v2.0 and output of NCS was demultiplexed and converted to FastQ format with Illumina Bcl2fastq v1.9.0.
