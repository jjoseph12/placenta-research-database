Main topic
This study investigates the miRNA and mRNA expression profiles in placentas from Tibetan and European women living at high altitudes to understand the genetic mechanisms underlying high-altitude adaptation in Tibetans, focusing on the role of miRNA-mRNA regulatory networks in placental adaptation to high-altitude hypoxia.
