Methods text

Assay description

Directional RNA profiles of amnion membranes were generated by deep sequencing using Illumina HiSeq2500. Twenty-nine human amnion specimens were used: 12 control and 17 preeclampsia exposed.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using TRIzol reagent from amnion membrane and the total RNA was then treated with DNaseI to eliminate residual DNA. The 500 ng of extracted RNA samples were subjected to directional RNA seq library preparation. We eliminated ribozomal-RNA (rRNA) from total RNA with RiboZero kit (Illumina) before reverse transcription with the SuperScript III kit (Invitrogen) using oligo-d(T) and random hexamers. The second strand cDNA was synthesized with dUTP. After the second strand synthesis, the products were fragmented, end-repaired, dA tailed and added Illumina sequencing adapters. Before PCR amplification, we treated the product with Uracil-DNA glycosylase.

Main topic
This study investigates the value of using amnion samples for DNA methylation studies to reveal distinctive patterns between fetuses exposed to proteinuria or hypertension and fetuses from normal pregnancies, suggesting amniotic membranes as a valuable surrogate fetal tissue for epigenome-wide association studies of adverse intrauterine conditions.
