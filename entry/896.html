Methods text

Assay description

m1A-Seq analyses of mRNAs from normoxia and hypoxia trophoblast cells

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from normoxia or hypoxia HTR8 cell lines. Polyadenylated RNA was further enriched from total RNA using a Dynabeads mRNA purification kit (Invitrogen). RNA fragmentation, m1A-IP and library preparation were carried out according to previously published protocols(Dominissini, D. et al. The dynamic N(1)-methyladenosine methylome in eukaryotic messenger RNA. Nature 530, 441-446 (2016).)

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of YTHDF3, an m1A reader protein, in regulating trophoblast activity and its potential impact on pregnancy. The study finds that YTHDF3 selectively binds to m1A-modified RNAs and inhibits trophoblast activity by targeting IGF1R expression, suggesting a role in maintaining pregnancy.
