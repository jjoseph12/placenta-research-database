Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Gene expression of epithelium in normal day4 or pseduopregnant day 4 uteri

Extraction protocol

To isolate the luminal epithelium, frozen sections of pregnant uteri (20 µm) were mounted on polyethylene naphthalate slides (Leica Microsystems). The target regions in the sections were dissected using the Leica LMD7 (Leica Microsystems, Wetzlar, Germany). Total RNA was extracted from collected cells using RNAeasy Micro Kit Qiagen 74004 and eluted with 14 μl of RNase-free water. Total RNAs from uteri were extracted by TRIzol and purified by ploy-A before subjected to RNA-Seq. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper investigates the effect of the blastocyst on uterine differentiation during the window of receptivity by examining global gene expression changes in the uterine lumenal epithelium (Le) of pseudopregnant mice without embryos and pregnant mice with embryos.
