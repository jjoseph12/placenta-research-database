Methods text

Assay description

DNA from 2 cell types from 5 placentas exposed to 3 different conditions of hypoxia (1%,8%,20%) and treated with 3 levels of vitamin D were bisulfite converted and run on the Illumina HumanMethylation450 BeadChip

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was purified from cultured trophoblasts using Wizard® Genomic DNA Purification Kit

Main topic
The study investigates the effect of oxygen tension on villous trophoblast by modulation of gene expression through DNA methylation in human cytotrophoblasts and syncytiotrophoblasts under different oxygen levels.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates how hypoxia (low oxygen tension) alters the DNA methylation profile of primary cultures of human cytotrophoblasts and syncytiotrophoblasts.
Source excerpt
We used the Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChip array to compare the DNA methylation profile of primary cultures of human cytotrophoblasts and syncytiotrophoblasts under < 1%, 8% and 20% oxygen levels.
