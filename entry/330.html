Methods text

Assay description

Examination of gene expression patterns in embryos in the post-implantation stages E5.25, E5.5 and E5.75 along the proximal-distal axis.

Extraction protocol

Samples were harvested by laser capture microdissection (LCM), and then RNA isolation. Low-input RNA-seq was performed using the Smart-seq2 protocol.
