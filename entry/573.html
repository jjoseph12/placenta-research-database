Methods text

Assay description

ICR female mice at 8 weeks of age were used for IVF, after 2-cell stage for the zona removal and cultured until blastocyst. Zona intact (ZI) embryos and zona free (ZF) embryos were cultured in the cWOW system until blastocyst for gene expression examination and immunostaining, then E3.5 early blastocysts were performed the embryo transfer (ET), after ET, the E17.5 placenta of ZI and ZF groups were obtained for RNA seq.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA samples were prepared from two independent experiments (ZI and ZF groups) using ISOGEN II (Nippon Gene, Tokyo, Japan). The sequencing libraries were constructed following the MGI Library Prep Kits Guide using DNBSEQ-G400RS PE150 (MGI, China).
