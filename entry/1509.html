Main topic
This paper examines the DNA methylation differences between primary trophoblast cell populations and derived-cell lines to understand trophoblast function, addressing the challenges in long-term culture and characterization of primary trophoblasts.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study compares genome-wide DNA methylation profiles of primary first-trimester human trophoblast cell populations with various trophoblast-derived cell lines to evaluate their suitability as models for placental research.
Source excerpt
In this study we have revealed the wide-ranging and functionally relevant differences in DNA methylation profile that exist between primary human trophoblasts and derived cell models.
