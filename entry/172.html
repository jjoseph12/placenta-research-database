Main topic
This study investigates embryonic stem (ES) cell fate in the absence of de novo DNA methyltransferases, finding that ES cells lacking DNA methylation enzymes do not differentiate efficiently into cell types of the embryo and are diverted into producing placental cells. This switch in cell fate is driven by the transcription factor Ascl2, which should only be produced in the placenta.
