Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Human placental trophoblasts were dispersed using a trypsin-deoxyribonuclease-dispase/Percoll method, plated in 6-well plates, and maintained in standard culture conditions (O2=20%). After 4 h (defined as time 0), the plates were divided to those in continued standard culture conditions, or to culture in hypoxia (O2=0%). Cells were then harvested at 6 h, 12 h, 24 h, 48 h and 72 h, and processed for mRNA arrays
