Assay description

Differential gene expression was examined by Illumina Mouse WG6 v2.0 Expression Bead Chip microarray analysis. Expression profiling was done at embryonic day 9.5 (E9.5) and E7.5; wild-type placenta versus Grhl2-/- placenta; n=3 biological replicates, respectively.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of grainyhead-like 2 (GRHL2), a transcription factor, in placental development and its potential link to pre-eclampsia and intrauterine growth retardation.
