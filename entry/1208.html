Main topic
This paper investigates the transcriptomic responses of human placental trophoblasts to pathogenic and probiotic Enterococcus faecalis strains, revealing differences in gene expression patterns and signaling pathways.

Reviewed answer A comparative transcriptomic analysis of human placental trophoblasts (BeWo cells) in response to infection by pathogenic and probiotic Enterococcus faecalis strains.

In this study, we first constructed an interaction model between two different E. faecalis strains and human choriocarcinoma BeWo cells in vitro, and DNA microarray was performed to depict the transcriptional responses of BeWo cells.
