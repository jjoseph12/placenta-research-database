Methods text

Assay description

Mouse TSCs, differentiated TSCs, and MEFs confluence (60–70% confluence) were treated with mouse TNFα and IFNγ (20 ng/ml, PeproTech, Rocky Hill, NJ) individual or in combination (TNFα/IFNγ), then total RNA was extracted, followed by RNA-sequencing analysis.

Extraction protocol

RNA isolation from cultured cells was used Trizol reagent. RNA-seq libraries were prepared using the NEBNext RNA Library Prep Kit (New England Biolabs, NEB) .
