Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

For characterization of BMP4-treated hiPSCs, four types of hiPSCs were treated with 50ng/ml BMP4 for 10 days. Subsequently, KRT7-positive cells were purified from differentiated cells using flow cytometry and analyzed using DNA microarray.
