Main topic
This paper investigates the role of G protein-coupled estrogen receptor (GPER) in human trophoblast cells, identifying angiopoietin-like 4 (ANGPTL4) as a target gene of GPER and demonstrating that GPER stimulates trophoblast cell invasion via YAP-mediated ANGPTL4 expression.
