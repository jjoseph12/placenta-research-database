Methods text

Assay description

In vitro decidualized priamry cultures of human endometrial strmal cells transfected with a siRNA againts HOXA10 or a scrambled siRNA. Cells harvested at 24, 48 and 72 h post transfection. Dual channel hybridization with dye swap design in biological replicates

Extraction protocol

The siRNA transfected cells (HOXA10 siRNA and Scrambled siRNA) were harvested with chilled Trizol reagent (Invitrogen Corp, Carlsbad, USA). The volume of Trizol was maintained at 1 ml for every 1 x 106 cells. The cells with Trizol were stored at -80 degree centigrade until used for RNA extraction. The lysates were thawed at room temperature and passed through a 21-gauge needle for 10 times. This ensured that the lysates was uniformly homogenized. Post homogenization, the lysates were allowed to settle for 5 minutes at room temperature. The lysates were then spun at 12000 rpm for 15 minutes in a fixed angle rotor centrifuge at 4 degree centigrade. The upper aqueous phase was then transferred to a new microfuge tube and equal volumes of chilled 70% ethanol were added to the tubes. The ethanol-aqueous phase was mixed gently with a pipette and total RNA was purified using the RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen, Valencia, USA). The lysates were then transferred to a purification column and spun at 10,000 rpm for 10 seconds. The flow-through is discarded and the column is filled with 500μl of RW1 buffer provided by the kit. The columns are spun at 10,000 rpm for 10 seconds and the flow-through is discarded. The columns are then filled with 500μl of RPE buffer and spun at 10,000 rpm for 10 seconds. The RPE buffer spin is repeated once more and a dry spin is performed by centrifugation of the empty column at 13,000 rpm for 2 minutes. The final elution step was performed by adding 32μl of nuclease free water and spinning the column for 5 minutes at 13,000 rpm. The concentration of the RNA is determined using 1μl of RNA with a Nanodrop spectro-photometer (ND-1000,). Amount of RNA per sample observed was 32-36µgs. Label Cy3 Label protocol The total RNA from control Scrambled siRNA transfected cells were labeled with fluorescent dyes (Cy3), whereas total RNA from HOXA10 siRNA transfected cells were labelled with Cy5 using the indirect labeling method. Briefly, twenty micrograms of total RNA from decidual cells were converted to cDNA using modified amino-allyl dUTP. The resultant cDNA was purified using the silica columns provided by the kit and subsequently used for dye conjugation. The purified cDNA was eluted in binding buffer and incubated with the DMSO dissolved Cy3 dye suspension for 3 hours in a thermo mixer at room temperature. Excess dye was quenched using 20mM hydroxylamine and the resultant labeled cDNA was again purified using the silica columns as provided by the kit. The purified and labeled cDNA was eluted using 65μL elution buffer. The exultant was dried under vacuum and resuspended in 65μl of hybridization buffer. The resuspended cDNA was heated at 95 degree centigrade for 5 minutes, flash chilled on ice and microfuged on ice before use in hybridization.
