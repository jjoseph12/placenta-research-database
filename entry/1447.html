Methods text

Assay description

35 IUGR (including 8 technical repeats for batch effect), 19 preeclamptic and 40 control placentas included in this study were collected during the years of 2004-2008 and hybridized in two batches to microarrays. Samples were randomized across arrays to control for array and batch variability.
