Methods text

Assay description

Study Design: We generated a single-cell atlas of gene expression at the site of accreta invasion (AI) versus site of accreta opposite, non-invasion (AO) in PAS placentas versus controls (C). Mechanical and enzymatic dissociation of single cell suspensions of placentas was performed with confirmation of high cell viability and RNA quality prior to library creation. We utilized a high-throughput whole-transcriptome system for single-cell sequencing.

Extraction protocol

Control, PAS-invasion, or PAS-opposite were mounted on the same slide (3 matched pairs on 3 slides) before hybridization with the Cancer Transcriptome Atlas (CTA) panel of probes corresponding to 1,825 genes (NanoString). Following hybridization, the slides were scanned on the GeoMx instrument with the workspace used to select regions of interest (ROI) The NanoString GeoMx Digital Spatial Profiler(DSP) protocol Spatial transcriptomics
