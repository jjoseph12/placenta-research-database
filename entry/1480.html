Methods text

Assay description

Examine allelic expression in E17.5 placenta tissues from two individual samples, one from each of the two reciprocal crosses.

Extraction protocol

The mRNA-seq libraries were made with 5 ug of starting total RNA samples using the mRNA-Seq 8-Sample Prep Kit (Illumina Inc., CA), following the Illumina protocol for mRNA sequencing sample preparation.

Main topic
This paper presents a genome-wide scan for novel imprinted genes by performing mRNA-seq experiments on embryonic day 17.5 (E17.5) mouse placenta cDNA samples from reciprocal cross F1.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Identifying and validating novel imprinted genes in mouse placenta using transcriptome-wide mRNA sequencing of reciprocal F1 crosses.
Source excerpt
As a first-pass scan for novel imprinted genes, we performed mRNA-seq experiments on embryonic day 17.5 (E17.5) mouse placenta cDNA samples from reciprocal cross F 1 progeny of AKR and PWD mouse strains and quantified the allele-specific expression and the degree of parent-of-origin allelic imbalance.

We dissected E17.5 placenta tissues from the F 1 mice from the following crosses: AKR/J female × PWD/PhJ male cross (AKR × PWD for short), PWD/PhJ female × AKR/J male (PWD × AKR for short), C57BL/6 female × CAST/EiJ male (B6 × CAST for short), and CAST/EiJ female × C57BL/6 male (CAST × B6 for short).
