Assay description

Using agilient horse one colour array, gene expression in equine placental chorionic girdle tissue and matched chorion (as a control) at gestation days 27, 30, 31 and 34. 5 replicates of each time pont and each tissue for a total of 40 RNA samples. Two samples were excluded as significant outliers on a PCA visualization.

Main topic
This study identifies genes and signaling pathways active in vivo during equine chorionic girdle trophoblast development by using whole transcriptome profiling to measure and compare gene expression in chorionic girdle trophoblast and adjacent regressing chorion at pregnancy days 27, 30, 31, and 34.
