Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We cultured human endometrial organoids in Matrigel or collagen-based gels (70% type-I collagen and 30% matrigel) for 7 days. Human endometrial organoids were established and maintained in the ECSY medium. The composition of the ECSY medium was as follows: DMEM/F-12 (Life Technologies) supplemented with 1% ITS-X supplement, 0.15% BSA (FUJIFILM Wako), 1% Knockout Serum Replacement (KSR, Thermo Fisher Scientific), 200 mM L-ascorbic acid (FUJIFILM Wako), 50 ng/ml EGF, 2 μM CHIR99021, 2 μM SB202190, and 10 μM Y27632. We used 2 independent organoids from 2 donors.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Mini or Micro Kit (QIAGEN), and genomic DNA was removed by digestion with RNase-free DNase I (QIAGEN). RNA libraries were prepared using the NEBNext UltraII Directional RNA Library Prep Kit (New England Biolabs)
