Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

By using spatial transcriptome of Geo-seq , we carried out transcriptome profiling on embryo locations at a high resolution of 20-40 cells per sample. We then constructed a comprehensive spatio-temporal transcriptome map from the pre-gastrulation to late-gastrulation embryos. Combining single-cell RNA-seq data generated using 10x Genomics Chromium system, we visualized the spatio-temporal map in a 3D embryonic model. Definitions: region AP: region of 1st section of epiblast region EAP: region of 1st section of endoderm region Aregion of anterior epiblast/ectoderm region EP: region of posterior endoderm region P: region of posterior epiblast/ectoderm region EA: region of anterior endoderm region M: region of mesoderm region L: region of left lateral epiblast/ectoderm region R: region of right lateral epiblast/ectoderm region L1: region of left anterior lateral epiblast/ectoderm region L2: region of left posterior lateral epiblast/ectoderm region MA: region of anterior mesoderm region MP: region of posterior mesoderm region R1: region of right anterior lateral epiblast/ectoderm region R2: region of right posterior lateral epiblast/ectoderm region Epi: region of epiblast region VE: region of visceral endoderm region MAR: region of anterior right lateral mesoderm region MPL: region of posterior left lateral mesoderm region MPR: region of posterior right lateral mesoderm region MAL: region of anterior left lateral mesoderm

Extraction protocol

According to Geo-seq (Chen et al., 2017), RNA from LCM samples were extracted by 5M GuSCN (Invitrogen, # 20012-043), then RNA was precipitated by ethanol with the help of RNA carrier. Libraries were constructed using illumina Nextera XT DNA sample preparation kit.

Main topic
This paper constructs a spatio-temporal molecular atlas of lineage trajectories in the gastrulating mouse embryo by mapping single cells to spatial coordinates in the germ layers with reference to positional data in the transcriptome, providing insights into the molecular drivers of lineage diversification and tissue patterning during gastrulation.
