Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Placenta was collected from gestational Day19 (D19) of C57BL/6J mouse either exposed to air pollutant material or sterile PBS during the entire gestation period (from D1 of conception to D18). Three different compartments of placenta (Decidua, Junction and Labyrinth) were separated under the microscope and single cell suspension was prepared to make scRNA sequencing library.

Extraction protocol

For scRNA-seq library prep, single suspension of each placental compartment was prepared separately by enzymatic digestion of the tissue followed by passing the cell suspension through 100μm cell strainer (MACS, Smart Steiner) Single-cell RNA-Seq libraries were prepared using the Chromium Single Cell 3′ Library & Gel Bead Kit (P/N 1000075, 10x Genomics) following manufacturer’s instructions. scRNA sequencing libraries were sequenced by the NovaSeq-6000 (Illumina Inc.; San Diego, CA, USA).
