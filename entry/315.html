Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

GFP expressing JEG-3 spheroids were co-cultured with confluent monolayers of receptive Ishikawa or non-receptive HEC-1-A epithelia. After 48 hours of co-culture, GFP+ (trophoblast JEG-3 spheroid cells) and GFP- cell fractions (receptive Ishikawa or non-receptive HEC-1-A epithelial cells) were isolated by fluorescence-activated flow cytometry (FACS). The specific transcriptional changes of the isolated cell populations were analyzed by RNA-seq profiling. Statistical significance of gene expression differences was set at an absolute log2 fold change (log2FC) ≥1 and an adjusted p-value <0.05.

Extraction protocol

RNA from each sample was isolated using RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen N. V., Netherlands) following manufacturer´s recommendations and eluted in 30 µl nuclease free water. RNA was purified using RNase-Free DNase Set (Qiagen N. V., Netherlands). RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using the kits NEBNext® Poly(A) mRNA Magnetic Isolation Module (Cat# E7490) and NEBNext® Ultra™ II RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (Cat#E7770), following manufacturer´s instructions and 8-11 cycles of amplification.

Main topic
This study investigates the molecular dynamics of trophoblast and epithelial substrate interaction during attachment by establishing an in vitro model to dissect the transcriptional responses of the trophoblast and receptive vs. non-receptive epithelium after co-culture.
