Main topic
This paper elucidates the importance of the STOX1A/STOX1B imbalance in trophoblast function, identifying DNA sequences recognized by STOX1 and demonstrating the limited effect of STOX1 isoform downregulation on trophoblast gene expression, while showing that overexpression of STOX1A or STOX1B leads to dysregulation of pathways vital for placental function.
