Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA was sequenced from placenta, maternal brain and fetal brain from wildtype mice at day 15 pregnancy. Also, total RNA was sequenced from placenta of FOXA2 conditional (uterine) knockout mice for comparsion with gene expression of control wildtype mice at the same day (day 15) of pregnancy.

Main topic
This study investigates the communication between the placenta and the maternal and fetal brain in pregnant mice, using gene expression and network analysis to identify potential mediators of crosstalk.
