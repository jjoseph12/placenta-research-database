Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Placental RNA profiles at 12.5 embryo day old in Control and Oxcodone exposed mice

Extraction protocol

Placenta tissue samples were collected, snap frozen in liquid nitrogen, and total RNA was harvested using Qiagen AllPrep DNA/RNA/miRNA Universal Kit (cat#80224). Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 850 ng of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
