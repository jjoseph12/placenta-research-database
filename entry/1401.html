Main topic
This paper investigates the role of E2F transcriptional activators and repressors in regulating the mammalian endocycle, a variant cell cycle essential for metazoan development that results in highly polyploid cells. The study utilizes mouse models with targeted gene inactivation to evaluate the functions of canonical E2F activators (E2F1-3) and atypical repressors (E2F7/E2F8) in endocycling trophoblast giant cells (TGCs) and hepatocytes.
