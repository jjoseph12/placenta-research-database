Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We performed microarray analysis on highly purified CD56brightCD16-CD3- uNK cells comparing decidual tissue from 1st and 2nd trimester pregnancy terminations. Each group consisted of 6 donors. Total RNA was amplified and labeled with cy5 and co-hybridized with cy3 labeled Stratagene Universal Reference RNA on Agilent human V2 arrays.
