Main topic
This study quantifies BMP, WNT, and NODAL signaling dynamics in an in vitro model of human gastrulation, suggesting that BMP signaling initiates waves of WNT and NODAL signaling activity that move toward the colony center, and that the dynamics of signaling events in the BMP, WNT, and NODAL cascade control fate patterning of human gastruloids.
