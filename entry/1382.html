Main topic
This study investigates fetal DNA-methylation levels in chorionic villi (CVS) and amniotic cell (AC) samples, revealing significant methylation differences between karyotypically normal AC and CVS, as well as identifying genes differentially methylated in CVS with trisomy 21, suggesting epigenetic programming in Down syndrome.
