Main topic
This paper identifies tissue-specific protein-coding and noncoding transcripts across 14 human tissues using RNA-seq data, providing a unique dataset and analysis of expression characteristics and functions to facilitate future investigation of tissue-specific diseases and adverse drug reactions.
