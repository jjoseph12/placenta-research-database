Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

84 samples in total; Embryos/Placenta derived following ART or Natural matings; n=3-6 per group; comparison of treatment (4x or 10x folic acid supplemented) vs. CD for ART samples; comparison of Natural Matings (on CD) vs. ART (on CD); comparisons done within the same sex of embryo/placenta

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted from tissues using DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit (Qiagen) as per manufacturer protocols Reduced representation bisulfite sequencing (RRBS) libraries were prepared according to previously published protocols using gel-free technique (Boyle, P. Genome Biol., 2012). Briefly, DNA was digested with MspI restriction enzyme, followed by size selection, gap filling and 3'-A-tailing. Ligation of NEBNext methylated adaptors was performed, followed by bisulfite conversion and index PCR amplification.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of moderate and high dose maternal folic acid supplementation on developmental and epigenetic outcomes in assisted reproductive technologies (ART) using a mouse model, finding that moderate doses may be optimal.
