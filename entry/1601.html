Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was extracted from paired tumor and normal thyroid tissues from 9 PTC patients. The same set samples were applied to Custom miRNA microarray chips (OSU_CCC version 2.0) and Affymetrix HG-U133 plus 2 chips.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of microRNAs (miRNAs) in papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC), demonstrating that numerous miRNAs are transcriptionally up-regulated in PTC tumors compared to unaffected thyroid tissue and identifying a set of miRNAs that can distinguish between PTC and normal thyroid.
