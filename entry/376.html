Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was isolated and purified using the QIAamp Fast DNA Tissue Kit(Qiagen) Genomic DNA was sonicated to yield 150–600 bp fragments, and adaptors for paired-end sequencing (Illumina) were ligated using the NEBNext Ultra II DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (New England Biolabs). Immunoprecipitations were carried out using 500ng DNA per sample, 1.25μg anti-5mC antibody (Eurogentec Cat# BI-MECY-0100, RRID:AB_2616058) and 10μL Dynabeads coupled with M-280 sheep anti-mouse antibody (Invitrogen). Pulled down DNA was amplified for 12 cycles with adapter-specific indexed primers. Final clean-up and size selection was carried out with AMPure-XP SPRI beads (Beckman Coulter). Whole E10.5 placentas were homogenized using lysing matrix D beads and total RNA was extracted using TRI Reagent according to the manufacturer’s instructions Libraries were prepared using 200 ng of total RNA with the Illumina TruSeq stranded mRNA Library Preparation kit

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of one-carbon metabolism on the placental methylome in a mouse model with a hypomorphic mutation in the methionine synthase reductase gene (Mtrr gt), revealing its role in epigenetic stability in the mouse placenta.
