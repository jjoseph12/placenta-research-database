Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA used for microarray analysis was obtained from 6 placentas with 12 infected and 12 control explants from each placenta. At 4 h after inoculation, total RNA was isolated from the trophoblastic surface of the explants. Three RNA pools from two placentas each were generated prior to cDNA synthesis.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA extracted using Trizol following manufacturer's instructions Label Alexa Fluor reactive dye 647 Label protocol cDNA from infected and control CAM explants was synthesized and labeled using random hexamers and amino allyl dUTP. cDNAs from infected CAM explants were labeled with Alexa Fluor reactive dye 647 (Molecular Probes/Invitrogen) and cDNAs from control CAM explants were labeled with Alexa Fluor reactive dye 555 (Molecular Probes/Invitrogen).
