Methods text

Assay description

Examination of m(6)A modifications in eight fetal tissues.

Extraction protocol

For RIP-seq, the tissues were isolated after a mechanical dissection, and all the samples were serially washed and immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen. Total RNA of tissue or cells was isolated using TRIzol and treated with DNase. RNA were then chemically fragmented, and subjected to immunoprecipitation with anti-m6A antibody. After stringent washing, bound RNA was eluted by competition with N6-methyladenosine, and used for library construction as IP samples. After removal of ribosomal RNA from total RNA, the input RNA samples was subsequently fragmented and subjected to library construction. The libraries were prepared using NEBNext Ultra RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina following manufacturer’s recommendations. For ChIP-seq, nine 10-cm dishes of pCDNA4-SFB-Tre-METTL4 stable transfected cells were treated by doxycycline (1 ng/μl) for 24 hr to trigger the expression of METTL3, then subject to 2-step fixation by 2 mM DSG (AppliChem) for 30 min and 1% formaldehyde for 10 min at room temperature. The following processes were all carried out at 4°C unless otherwise emphasized noted. After quenching with 125 mM glycine, the cells were harvested and lysed by 200 ul RIPA buffer supplemented with 2 mM RVC, 1 mM DTT, 100 μM PMSF and protease inhibitor cocktail (Roche) per dish. The lysate were sonicated using Bioraptor (Diagenode), and subject to streptavidin Sepharose (GE) binding for 2 hr. After washing with by RIPA buffer for 3 times, and the bound protein-DNA complex were eluted by competition with 2 mg/ml biotin (Sigma) for 2 hr. Then the eluate were incubated with S-protein agarose (Millipore) overnight, washed with RIPA for 3 times, and eluted by proteinase K digestion at 56°C for 30 min. The DNA of input and IPed samples were incubated at 65°C overnight to reverse crosslink, and extracted by Phenol/Chloroform followed by ethanol precipitation. The libraries were prepared using NEBNext Ultra DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina.
