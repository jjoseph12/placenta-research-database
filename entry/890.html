Methods text

Assay description

Pregnant mice were treated with either a GPER1 antagonist (G15) or vehicle control and then infected with IAV before collection of lungs and placental tissues for transcriptional profiling

Extraction protocol

Whole lungs or placentas were homogenized in PBS before being extracted via standard Trizol extraction protocol mRNA was prepped for sequencing using the NEBNext Poly(A) mRNA Magnetic Isolation Module (NEB #E7490), NEBNext Ultra II RNA Library Prep Kit (NEB #E7770), and NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina (NEB #E7335, #E7500).

Main topic
This paper reports that activation of the guanine nucleotide–binding protein–coupled estrogen receptor 1 (GPER1) during pregnancy is both necessary and sufficient to suppress IFN signaling and does so disproportionately in reproductive and fetal tissues, preserving fetal health during maternal inflammation.
