Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

This study examined male and female placental tissues across embryogenesis at E9.5, E12.5 and E17.5 (H3K4me3Q5ser ChIP-seq and bulk RNA-seq), compared E12.5 WT vs Sert KO, Tph1 KO, and Oct3 KO placenta tissues (H3K4me3 and H3K4me3Q5ser ChIP-seq), and examined associated offspring brains at E12.5 (bulk RNA-seq)

Extraction protocol

Homogenization of tissue in Trizol Reagent with subsequent clean-up using Rneasy Microcolumns. TruSeq RNA Library Prep Kit v2 (Illumina RS-122-2001)

Main topic
This study investigates the role of serotonin transporter (SERT/SLC6A4)-dependent histone serotonylation in the placenta and its contribution to the neurodevelopmental transcriptome, demonstrating its impact on gene expression and neurodevelopmental gene networks.
