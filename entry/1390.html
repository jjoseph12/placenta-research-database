Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The F1 TSC lines profiled were generated from crosses between CAST/EiJ (Cast) and C57BL/6J (B6) mice. C/B denotes a Cast mother and B6 father, and B/C denotes a B6 mother and Cast father.

Extraction protocol

Strand-specific RNA-Seq libraries were prepared according to (Ingolia NT, et al. Science. 2009; PMID 19213877). ChIP-Seq libraries were prepared according to Illumina instructions. FAIRE-Seq libraries were prepared according to (Giresi PG, Lieb JD. Methods. 2009; PMID 19303047). DNase-Seq libraries were prepared according to (Song L, et al. Genome Res. 2011; PMID 21750106).
