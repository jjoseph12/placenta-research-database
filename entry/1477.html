Main topic
This study uses DNA methylation profiling of human placentas from triploidies to characterize human differentially methylated regions (DMRs) associated with known imprinted genes and identify novel imprinted DMRs.

Reviewed answer Genome-wide mapping and characterization of human imprinted differentially methylated regions (DMRs) by comparing DNA methylation profiles in human placentas from diandric and digynic triploidies.

In this study, we have taken advantage of the unbalanced parental genomic constitutions in triploidies to further characterize human DMRs associated with known imprinted genes and identify novel imprinted DMRs.
