Methods text

Assay description

Healthy women during early pregnancy were recruited, and FACS was performed to isolate and purify the CD4+ T cells from the decidua and peripheral blood. We sequenced the RNA samples on an Illumina HiSeq 2500 platform.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted by using Trizol Reagent, and was purified by RNeasy Micro Kit. Library preparation was performed as previously described (Zhang et al., 2014). We used 200 ng RNA per sample as input material for the RNA sample preparations. cDNA libraries were constructed by using the NEBNext® UltraTM RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (NEB, USA) according to the manufacturer's instruction, and the products were purified by AMPure XP system and library quality was determined on the Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 system.

Main topic
This study compares the transcriptional profile and alternative splicing landscape between paired decidual and peripheral CD4+ T cells from healthy pregnant women during the first trimester, revealing distinct transcriptional signatures and alternative splicing patterns that contribute to our understanding of their characteristic and functionality during early pregnancy.
