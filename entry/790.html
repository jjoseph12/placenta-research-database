Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNAseq of BeWo (WT and TFEB KO) cells undergoing syncitiotrophoblast differentiation, with or wihout transfection of siRNA against TFEB and BeWo TFEB KO cells overexpressing TFEB ORF in the presence of Forskolin or Torin-1.

Extraction protocol

RNA was harvested using Rneasy mini plus kit (Qiagen) and 250 ng of total RNA was used for the construction of sequencing libraries. 3'DGE mRNA-seq research grade sequencing service (Next Generation Diagnostics srl) 3'DGE mRNA-seq

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of TFEB, a transcription factor, in promoting the formation of a functional syncytiotrophoblast in the placenta, demonstrating its involvement in trophoblast fusion and endocrine function, which are crucial for embryonic development.
