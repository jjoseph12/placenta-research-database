Methods text

Assay description

TGCs were micro-dissected from day E9.5 nine implantation sites from C57BL/6J mice. The portion of the TGCs in direct contact with the spongiotrophoblast layer and the labyrinth layer were manually removed to avoid collecting any polyploid cells from the former or multi-nucleated syncytiotrophoblast cells from the latter.

Extraction protocol

Tissues were lysed by homogenization in TRIzol reagent and RNA was prepared as indicated in the manufacture's protocol. Ten micro grams of RNA were processed with an Illumina mRNA kit to generate cDNA libraries for mRNA-sequencing according to manufacture's instructions.Thirty-six base sequencing was performed on an Illumina Genome Analyzer II (GAII) sequencer, according to Illumina’s standard protocols.
