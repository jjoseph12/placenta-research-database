Extraction protocol

About 500-1000 cells were used per library and about 1µg histone H3K4me3 antibody (Cell signaling Technology, 9727S, USA) or histone H3K27me3 antibody (Diagnode, C15410195, Belgium) were used for immunoprecipitation reaction. The sequence libraries were generated by Kapa Hyper Prep Kit (KAPA, KK8504, Switzerland). The prepared cells were transferred to lysis buffer by a mouth pipette. Reverse transcription was performed directly using Oligo (dT) primer in the lysate containg completely cytoplasm. The second-strand cDNAs were synthesised depending on the poly(A) tail attached to the 3′ end of the first-strand cDNAs. Fragment cDNA was prepared using Covaris sonicator (Covaris S220, Woburn, MA, USA) for sequence library construction. Kapa Hyper Prep Kit（KAPA , KK8504, Switzerland）was used to complete the sequence libraries. The prepared cells were transferred to lysis buffer by a mouth pipette. Reverse transcription was performed directly using Oligo (dT) primer in the lysate containg completely cytoplasm. The second-strand cDNAs were synthesised depending on the poly(A) tail attached to template switching by TSO. Fragment cDNA was prepared using Covaris sonicator (Covaris S220, Woburn, MA, USA) for sequence library construction. Kapa Hyper Prep Kit（KAPA , KK8504, Switzerland）was used to complete the sequence libraries. According to previous methods, approximately 10-20 cells were used to construct each library. The prepared cells were transferred to lysis buffer by mouth pipetting. Reverse transcription was performed directly using oligo (dT) primers in lysates containing complete cytoplasm. Second-strand cDNA was synthesized depending on template switching with a TSO. Fragment cDNA was prepared for sequence library construction using a Covaris sonicator. A Kapa Hyper Prep Kit was used to complete the sequence libraries. According to previous studies, approximately 500-1000 cells were used per library and 5% human cells 293T was added as spike in control. Approximately 1 µg of histone H3K4me3 antibody was used for the immunoprecipitation reaction. The sequence libraries were generated with a Kapa Hyper Prep Kit.
