Extraction protocol

Lysates were clarified from sonicated nuclei and histone-DNA complexes were isolated with antibody. ESRRB piPSCs were crosslinking with formaldehyde and chromatin was sonicated to 200-1000 bp, and then the sonicated chromatin was immunoprecipitated with the antibody FLAG. The DNA fragments were agarose electrophoresis quantified using Qubit 2.0 and sequenced on Illumina HiSeq2500 PE150 by SEQHEALTH Technology Corporation (China). Total RNA of the RNA samples was isolated from cell pellets by Trizol reagent (TaKaRa) and RNA-seq library was generated according to the manufacturer’s recommendations. Paired-end 150 bp sequencing was performed on DNBSEQ (Illumina) at BGI Technology Corporation (China).

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of ESRRB in porcine-induced pluripotent stem cells (piPSCs) and demonstrates that ESRRB facilitates the conversion of these cells to trophoblast-like stem cells by directly regulating CDX2 and other trophoblast stem cell-specific markers.
