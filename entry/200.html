Assay description

Conditioned medium containing PlGF (PlGFCM) was obtained from HEK-293 clones overexpressing PlGF2. Mouse splenic CD4+ T cells were stimulated with anti-CD3 antibody and anti-CD28 antibody in the presence of PlGFCM, control conditioned medium (ConCM), or IL6+ConCM for 12 hours.
