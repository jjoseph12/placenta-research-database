Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Tissue was collected from 2nd trimester (14-26 wks) human fetuses with spina bifida (SB, n=22) or anencephaly (AN, n=15) in addition to controls (CON, n=19), to explore DNAm using the Illumina Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChip (450k) platform. Whenever possible, a sample of chorionic villi, kidney, spinal cord, brain and muscle was obtained from each individual. DNAm was compared within a tissue, and spina bifida and anencephaly cases were compared as separate NTD status groups to controls, as it has not been determined if they have a common or distinct etiology.

Extraction protocol

genomic DNA was extracted by standard salt method and purified using Qiagen DNeasy Kit according to standard instructions

Main topic
This study profiles DNA methylation in neural tube defects (NTDs) in a folate-replete population in British Columbia, Canada, finding aberrant array-wide DNA methylation in the kidneys of spina bifida cases but not in other NTD-associated tissues.
