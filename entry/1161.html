Methods text

Assay description

Five scRNA-seq 10X Genomics samples of synthetic embryos, generated with iCdx2 cells, induced for 3 or 10 days, or with established TSC cells. The embryos were developed ex-utero. In addition, six 10X Genomics samples of mouse E8.5 natural embryos, developed in-utero.

Extraction protocol

RNA extracted according to 10x Genomics Chromium v3.1 Dual Index system kit instructions. 10x Genomics Chromium v3.1 Dual Index system (5000 cell target cell recovery).

Main topic
The study describes the creation of advanced synthetic embryos (sEmbryos) from mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) that can self-assemble and develop organ-specific progenitors and extraembryonic compartments ex utero, mimicking post-gastrulation stages of natural mouse embryogenesis.
