Assay description

In the bovine species, attachment of conceptus to the uterine endometrium is initiated on day 19.0~19.5 (day 0 = estrus cycle).To study gene expression associated with implanation event in bovine conceptuses, microarray analysis was initially conducted in RNAs isolated from days 20 (start to attchment) and 22 trophoblast (tight attachment) tissues.
