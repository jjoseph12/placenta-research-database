Methods text

Assay description

RNA sequencing was conducted on the following cells/conditions: two primary hTSC lines from term placenta cultured in TUA medium, TSCM from TUA medium, and TA medium. Two term derived cell lines are T1 (also referred to as CTB21126R+) and T2 (also referred to as CTB22131R+). Total sample #17. Two primary hTSC lines from the first trimester (CT29, CT30) were used as the control group.

Extraction protocol

Purelink RNA Mini Kit (Thermofischer) following manufacturer’s protocol. The RNA samples were measured using the Qubit 2.0 Fluorometer (Life Technologies, Carlsbad, CA, USA) to determine their quantity, and their integrity was assessed using the Agilent TapeStation 4200 (Agilent Technologies, Palo Alto, CA, USA). The RNA sequencing libraries were generated using the NEBNext Ultra II RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina, following the instructions provided by the manufacturer (NEB, Ipswich, MA, USA). In summary, mRNAs were originally concentrated using Oligod(T) beads. The mRNAs were subjected to fragmentation by heating them at a temperature of 94 °C for a duration of 15 minutes. Subsequently, the first strand and second strand cDNA were produced. The cDNA fragments underwent end repair and adenylation at their 3' ends. Universal adapters were then attached to the cDNA fragments. This was followed by the insertion of indexes and library enrichment by PCR with a limited number of cycles. The sequencing library underwent validation using the Agilent TapeStation (Agilent Technologies, Palo Alto, CA, USA) and was quantified using the Qubit 2.0 Fluorometer (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA) and quantitative PCR (KAPA Biosystems, Wilmington, MA, USA). The sequencing libraries were combined and distributed into a flowcell on the Illumina HiSeq 4000 instrument or a comparable device, following the instructions provided by the manufacturer. The samples underwent sequencing using a 2x150bp Paired End (PE) arrangement. The HiSeq Control Software (HCS) was used to do image analysis and base calling. The raw sequence data obtained from Illumina HiSeq, in the form of .bcl files, was transformed into fastq files and then de-multiplexed using the Illumina bcl2fastq 2.20 software. One discrepancy was permitted for the identification of index sequences.

Main topic
This paper reports the derivation and characterization of human trophoblast stem cells (hTSCs) using cytotrophoblasts (CTBs) isolated from term placentas, cultured in a specific trophoblast stem cell medium (TSCM) supplemented with a low concentration of the mitochondrial pyruvate uptake inhibitor UK5099 and AlbuMAX II. The study demonstrates that these hTSCs from term placentas exhibit high transcriptome similarity with hTSCs from first-trimester placentas and can be differentiated into extravillous trophoblast (EVT) and syncytiotrophoblast (STB) in vitro.
