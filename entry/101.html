Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We perform RNA-seq on sow's placenta at day 60 of gestaion (G60), day 90 of gestaion (G90) and farrowing day (L0).RNA-seq was completed on an Illumina HiSeq 2000, paired-end 100bp reads.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from each individual sample using TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen, USA) RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard illumina protocols

Main topic
This paper investigates the expression profile of mRNAs and long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) of placentas at different gestational stages in pregnant swine, revealing dysregulation of bile acids transport and detoxification as pregnancy progresses.
