Assay description

Main topic
The paper proposes an algorithm, CelFiE, to accurately estimate the relative abundances of cell types and tissues contributing to circulating cell-free DNA (cfDNA) from epigenetic cfDNA sequencing, with applications in pregnant women and ALS patients.
