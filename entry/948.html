Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA-Seq was performed on RNA isolated from E7.5 BPH/5 and C57 implantation sites (n=4).

Extraction protocol

Uterine implantation sites from pregnant females at E7.5 were collected, snap frozen, and stored at -80C. Total RNA was purified using Trizol (Thermo Fisher) according to the commercial protocol. RapidOUT DNA Removal kit (Thermo Fisher) was then used to remove any co-purified genomic DNA. RNA-seq libraries were prepared from 1 ug total RNA using the NEBNext Ultra Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (New England Biolabs), with initial polyA+ isolation.
