Main topic
This paper investigates the role of natural selection in altering DNA methylation status and benefiting placental function in a high-altitude environment, focusing on the dysferlin gene and a specific variant (rs10166384) to understand how it affects syncytiotrophoblast turnover and repair.

Reviewed answer The paper investigates the effects of natural selection (balancing selection) induced by high-altitude hypoxia on placental dysferlin (DYSF) DNA methylation and gene expression.
