Methods text

Assay description

At weaning, wild-type C57BL/6 female mice were placed for a month on control diet (CD) or 5xFASD, then mated, and were maintained on the same diets until embryonic day 17.5 (E17.5). Placentas of both sexes from both diets were used for RNA extraction and were conducted gene expression analysis by microarray using Affymetrix Clariom S array_Mouse Chip.
