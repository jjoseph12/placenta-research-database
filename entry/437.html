Methods text

Assay description

The rapid development of gene microarray and next generation sequencing has expedited the understanding of the genetic landscape of PE. While substantial evidence supports differential placental gene expression in PE, few studies were reported to systematically study miRNA and gene expression profiles in PE which should provide new insights into the miRNA-gene regulatory networks during the pathological process of PE. To address this paucity of data, we investigated global placental miRNA and gene expression using high-throughput RNA sequencing and quantitative real-time (qRT)-polymerase chain reaction (PCR) methodologies among 5 EO-PE cases and 5 normotensive preterm control subjects to identify miRNA-gene interaction networks and PE-associated biological pathways.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from the frozen human placentas using TRIZOL reagent following the manufacturer’s protocol. The RNA yield and purity were determined using Agilent 2100 Bio-analyzer (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA, United States) and NanoDrop 1000 Spectrophotometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, United States). All RNA samples passed quality control. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
