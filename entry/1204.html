Main topic
This study investigates the early developing fetal lung for transcriptomic correlates of postconception age (maturity) and sex, and their associations with chronic lung diseases by analyzing whole-lung transcriptome profiles of 61 females and 78 males at 54–127 days postconception (dpc) from nonsmoking mothers.
