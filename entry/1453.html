Main topic
This paper investigates the biological relevance of isomiRs, variants of microRNAs (miRNAs), through ultra-deep miRNA-seq on adult human tissues, revealing that isomiRs and canonical miRNAs are functionally cooperative partners that act coordinately to target pathways of functionally related genes.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study aims to demonstrate the biological relevance and functionality of microRNA variants (isomiRs) across ten adult human tissues using deep sequencing, polysome profiling, and target assays.
Source excerpt
To assess the biological relevance of isomiRs, we have performed ultra-deep miRNA-seq on ten adult human tissues, and created an analysis pipeline called miRNA-MATE to align, annotate, and analyze miRNAs and their isomiRs.
