Extraction protocol

Main topic
This study investigates how maternal genetic background affects the penetrance of growth phenotypes and sex-specific placental adaptation in offspring sired by alcohol-exposed male mice, finding that a CD-1 maternal background mitigates fetal growth restriction but induces sex-specific changes in placental morphology and gene expression.
