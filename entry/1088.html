Extraction protocol

Chromatin was tagmented and DNA extracted (Nextera DNA Library Prep Kit, Illumina) and incubated at 37oC for 30 minutes, (see methods) Transposed DNA was column purified and used for library amplification with custom made adaptor primers (see primer table) using NEBNext High-Fidelity 2x PCR Master Mix (New England Labs) RNA was purified from sorted cells using RNeasy Mini Kit (QIAGEN), according to manufacturer's instructions. Ovation Rna-seq system System v2 (Nugen), followed by KAPA LTP Library Preparation Kit

Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular barriers preventing the self-renewal of pluripotent stem cell-derived hematopoietic stem/progenitor cells (HSPCs) and identifies defective medial HOXA gene activation as a major developmental barrier, with retinoic acid (RA) signaling during endothelial-to-hematopoietic transition being a key inducer of HOXA genes and HSC fate.

Reviewed answer The study identifies defective medial HOXA gene expression as a major barrier to the self-renewal of hESC-derived HSPCs, and demonstrates that activating retinoic acid signaling during the endothelial-to-hematopoietic transition can induce HOXA genes and specify a definitive HSC fate.
