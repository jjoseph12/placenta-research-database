Methods text

Assay description

We have compared gene expression profiling of WA09 hESC cultured in minimal media and treated with BMP4 for 4 days to a panel of placental tissues (Amnion, and Stroma), primary cells (first and third trimester CTB) and placenta-derived cell lines (choriocarcinoma-derived BEWO and JEG3, and immoratalised extravillous trophoblast (EVT) cell line HTR8).
