Methods text

Assay description

Decidual tissues were collected during the procedure of dilation and curettage (D&C) after the ultrasonic diagnosis of fetal stopping developing in the uterus from four recurrent pregnancy loss patients. And decidua tissue from three control women was also collected during the procedure of D&C.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using RNAiso Plus Total RNA extraction reagent (Cat#9109, TAKARA)following the manufacturer’ s instructions and checked for a RIN number to inspect RNA integrity by an Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 (Agilent technologies, Santa Clara, CA, US).Qualified total RNA was further purified by RNAClean XP Kit (Cat A63987, Beckman Coulter,Inc.Kraemer Boulevard Brea, CA,USA)and RNase-Free DNase Set (Cat#79254, QIAGEN, GmBH, Germany). RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols
