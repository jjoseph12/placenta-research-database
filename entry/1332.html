Methods text

Assay description

Arterial and venous endothelial cells were isolated from eight different placentas, four of each sex. A total of ten placentas were used for isolation of cytotrophoblasts and six for syncytiotrophoblasts, with equal numbers from each sex.

Main topic
This paper investigates the influence of fetal sex on gene expression in different placental cell types, specifically trophoblast epithelium and villous vessel endothelium, to understand the molecular mechanisms underlying sexual dimorphism and sex-biased susceptibility to diseases.
