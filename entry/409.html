RNA-seq in CON and ESRRB-piPSCs

Extraction protocol

Total RNA of the RNA samples was isolated from cell pellets by Trizol reagent (TaKaRa) and RNA-seq library was generated according to the manufacturer’s recommendations. Paired-end 150 bp sequencing was performed on DNBSEQ (Illumina) at BGI Technology Corporation (China).

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of ESRRB in porcine-induced pluripotent stem cells (piPSCs) and demonstrates that ESRRB facilitates the conversion of these cells to trophoblast-like stem cells by directly regulating CDX2 and other trophoblast stem cell-specific markers.
