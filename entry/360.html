Methods text

Assay description

Pregnant dams were exposed to 1ppm ozone or filtered air controls on gestational day 10, placental tissue was dissociated and libraries were prepped 8 days post exposure on gestational day 18.

Extraction protocol

Pregnant mice were anesthetized via isoflurane and euthanized via cardiac exsanguination. Placentas were removed (n=7 per mouse), and excess decidual tissue was excised. Placentas were placed in a conical tube containing 10mL warmed RPMI media containing Collagenase B at a concentration of 2.4mg/mL. Tissue was minced via surgical scissors to increase surface area of sample. Tubes were then places on a rocker in an incubator at 37C for 2 hours. Following incubation, 20 mL DPBS was added to the mixture and resuspended through the pipettor to further break down the tissue. Tubes were centrifuged at 1200rpm for 7 minutes and the supernatant was decanted. The tissue pellet was resuspended in 6mL of fresh RPMI with DNase added. This solution was returned to the incubator for 20 minutes. The tube was next centrifuged as described previously and the supernatant decanted. and the pellet was resuspended in 10mL of 1mM EDTA and pipetted through a pipettor to mix. This solution was poured through a 70μm cell strainer into a fresh 50mL conicle tube. This tube was centrifuged as described and the supernatant was decanted and the pellet resuspended in 2mL fresh RPMI media for library preparation. Cell concentrations and viability were determined using the Cell Countess II FL (ThermoFisher) prior to calculating cell counts and loading the suspension into the Next GEM Chip G and Chromium Controller (10x Genomics) per the manufacturer’s protocol for GEM formation. The 10x Chromium Next GEM 3’ protocol was used to create 3’ libraries; briefly, cells were lysed and barcoded within each GEM prior to first strand cDNA synthesis, also within each GEM. After GEM’s are broken, cells are pooled prior to library completion as described in the manufacture’s protocol. Library quality was assess after cDNA synthesis and after completion on the BioAnalyzer using a DNA High Sensitivity Chip (Agilent). Prior to sequencing, Agilent Tape Station 4200 and Invitrogen Qubit 4.0 reagents were used determine final library concentrations prior to dilution, normalization and pooling at 4nM. qPCR was used to determine cluster efficiency.
