Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Lentiviral mediatedy genetic modification of hyaluronan metabolism, specifically targeted to embryonic trophoblast cells followed by embryo transfer to pseudo-pregnant mice

Extraction protocol

NCR1+ cells were sorted into 40µl of lysis/binding buffer, from which, mRNA was captured with 12 ml of Dynabeads oligo(dT) (Dynabeads™ mRNA DIRECT™ Purification Kit, NY, USA), washed, and eluted at 85C with 10 ml of 10 mM Tris-Cl (pH 7.5) and processed according to protocol previously developed for single-cell RNA-seq (Jaitin et al., 2014). A variation of MARS-seq was used as described (Jaitin et al., 2014), developed for single-cell RNA-seq to produce expression libraries.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of hyaluronan in vascular remodeling during early mouse pregnancy, demonstrating its importance in fine-tuning the maternal-embryo vascular barrier and the hypoxic avascular niche.
