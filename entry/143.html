Methods text

Assay description

We performed RNA-seq transcriptomic analysis of NM and IVF embryos at the stage of 2-cell, morula, E3.5 blastocyst (trophectoderm, TE; inner cell mass, ICM), E7.5 embryos (Epiblast, Epi; Extraembryonic ectoderm, ExE)

Extraction protocol

The prepared cells were transferred to lysis buffer by a mouth pipette. Reverse transcription was performed directly using Oligo (dT) primer in the lysate containg completely cytoplasm. The second-strand cDNAs were synthesised depending on the poly(A) tail attached to the 3′ end of the first-strand cDNAs. Fragment cDNA was prepared using Covaris sonicator (Covaris S220, Woburn, MA, USA) for sequence library construction. Kapa Hyper Prep Kit（KAPA , KK8504, Switzerland）was used to complete the sequence libraries.
