Main topic
This study investigates the role of ZFP36L3, a placenta- and yolk sac-specific mRNA-binding protein, in female reproductive physiology and fecundity using mice deficient in ZFP36L3, focusing on its impact on mRNA target accumulation and placental iron metabolism.
