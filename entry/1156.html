Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Analysis of similarities and differences in the transcriptomes among mouse embryos and stem cell derived synthetic embryos, generated with the recently reported protocol (Amadei et al., 2021) and the new protocol established in this study

Extraction protocol

Samples were dissociated to single cells by cutting with needles and then incubation in TrypLE Express dissociation solution for 15 minutes followed by reaction inactivation with media containing 15% fetal bovine serum, filtration and 2 washes in PBST (0.02% Tween-20. inDrop v3

Main topic
The paper describes the creation of a mouse embryo model (ETiX embryoids) from embryonic and extraembryonic stem cells that can develop the brain, neural tube, heart, foregut, somite, allantois, primordial germ cells, and yolk sac structures, closely resembling natural mouse embryo development up to embryonic day 8.5.
