Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Female wild type mice (8-10 weeks old) underwent bone marrow transplantation from GFP-expressing transgenic wild type male donors following 5-fluorouracil-based submyeloablation. After timed mating, uterine implantation sites were collected on E10.5. Implantation sites were dissected to remove embryonic and placental parts. The uterine tissues were finely minced and subjected to enzymatic digestion to obtain single cell suspensions. scRNA-seq was performed using droplet microfluidics (10x Chromium). Libraries were created with Single Cell 3′ Library kit V2 according to the manufacturer’s protocol, and sequenced on Illumina HISeq 4000 instrument.

Main topic
This study investigates the role of maternal CXCR4 in pregnancy using CXCR4-deficient mice, finding that CXCR4 deletion leads to increased pregnancy resorptions and decreased litter size due to immune dysregulation and placental defects.
