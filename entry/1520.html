Main topic
This study investigates the mechanisms underlying pre-eclampsia and unexplained fetal growth restriction (FGR) by analyzing differentially expressed genes in placental tissue from severe pre-eclamptic pregnancies, unexplained FGR pregnancies, and normal pregnancies.
