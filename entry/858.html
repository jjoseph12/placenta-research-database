Methods text

Assay description

siRNA targeting the human Prdx2 or negative control siRNA were transfected into HTR8 cells. Cells were cultured for 48 h after transfection before they were harvested. Three independent experiments were performed for each sample. The Agilent SurePrint G3 Human Gene Expression v3 8x60K Microarray was used to detect gene expression.
