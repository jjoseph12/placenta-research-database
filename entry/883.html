Main topic
This paper investigates the lineage potential of human naive embryonic stem cells, demonstrating their ability to differentiate into trophectoderm, the founder tissue of the placenta, unlike mouse embryonic stem cells. The study also explores the developmental specification coupled to lineage restriction, showing that epiblast plasticity and the potential for blastocyst regeneration are retained until implantation in humans.
