Assay description

20 total BeWo cytotrophoblast samples cultured with dietary fats conjugated to BSA for 72 hours, 5 treated with F12K media supplemented with 0.3% Fatty Acid-Free BSA (BSA control); 5 treated with F12K media supplemented with 100 µM Palmitate (PA; 2:1 conjugation to BSA); 5 treated with F12K media supplemented with 100 µM Oleate (OA; 2:1 conjugation to BSA); 5 treated with F12K media supplemented with 50 µM each PA and OA (P/O; 2:1 conjugation to BSA)

Main topic
This study investigates the impact of non-esterified fatty acids (NEFAs) palmitate (PA) and oleate (OA) on placental villous trophoblast cell metabolism using the BeWo cell line model, integrating transcriptomic, metabolomic, and lipidomic analyses to characterize the underlying effects of these dietary metabolites on placental lipid processing.

Reviewed answer An integrated multi-omics study (transcriptomics, metabolomics, and lipidomics) characterizing the impacts of exogenous palmitate and oleate on BeWo trophoblast cell metabolism and lipid processing.

Using the BeWo cell line model, the current study integrated transcriptomic (mRNA microarray), metabolomic, and lipidomic readouts to characterize the underlying impacts of exogenous PA and OA on placental villous trophoblast cell metabolism.
