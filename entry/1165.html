Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

For this study, the 15 samples are divided into five groups of 3 replicates each. Three of the groups are control groups at gestation date 85, 110, and 133. Two groups are experimental at gestation date 110 and 133.

Extraction protocol

Total cellular RNA was isolated from cotyledon samples using Trizol reagent and purified using a PureLink Mini RNA kit (Invitrogen, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA) according to manufacturer instructions. Library preparation was conducted using the NEB Next Ultra II Library Prep Kit for Illumina according to manufacturer instructions.
