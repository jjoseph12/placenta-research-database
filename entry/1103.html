Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Primary human trophoblasts (PHT) were derived and cultured from three term human placentas obtained by the Obstetrical Specimen Procurement Unit from women after a healthy pregnancy, labor, and delivery at Magee-Womens Hospital of the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center. Isolated PHT lines obtained from three individual (1 female and 2 male) placentas were cultured separately.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA were prepared using miRNeasy Mini Kit following the manufacturer's instructions Libraries were constructed following the Illumina’s protocol with reagents supplied in Illumina’s TruSeq mRNA stranded sample preparation kit.

Main topic
This paper compares syncytiotrophoblast (STB) derived from human embryonic stem cells (ESCs) with cytotrophoblasts isolated from term placentas, finding dissimilarities that suggest the ESC-derived STB represents syncytial tissue encountered at the initiation of placental development and may serve as an in vitro model for studying diseases of placentation.
