Main topic
This study investigates the effects of developmental exposure to bisphenol A (BPA) and bisphenol S (BPS) on the midgestational mouse placenta, revealing similar changes in gene expression, morphological defects, and alterations in neurotransmitter content, suggesting potential impacts on the placental-fetal brain axis.
