Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To characterize the transcriptome profile of parietal trophoblast giant cells and determine how oxycodone exposure affected placenta and decidual cells at embryonic age 9.5 and 12.5, three replicates were tested per group with Parse Biosciences single nuclei RNAseq approach. Thus, a total of 12 samples were tested at embryonic age 9.5 and 12 at embryonic age 12.5. Single nucleotide RNAseq thus represents two embryonic ages (9.5 and 12.5), two types of tissues (placenta and decidual samples), and two treatments (oxycodone vs. control).

Extraction protocol

Nuclei were isolated following a protocol detailed by Rousselle et al. (3) for human kidney biopsies, which we modified to suit mouse placental and decidual samples. Each sample was minced into roughly 1 mm pieces and returned to their original centrifuge tubes. A 7 mm micro stir-bar was placed in each tube, along with 1 ml lysis buffer that was composed of 1× PBS pH, 7.4 10 mM Tris–HCL pH 7.5,0.0125% Triton X-100, 1 mM dithiothreitol, and 0.2 U/mL RNAse inhibitor (Promega, cat. no. PRN2611), and 1.5 l DNAse I, RNase free (1U/l, Thermo Fisher Scientific, cat. no. AM2222). The samples were then placed on ice on a magnetic stir plate and stirred at ~350 RPM for 10 min. The contents were allowed to settle, and the supernatant solution removed by gentle suction with a P1000 pipette. The homogenate was passed through a 100 m filter into a 15 ml collection tube and the filter washed with 6 ml nuclear wash and resuspension buffer (1x PBS, pH 7.4, 2 % bovine serum albumin [Sigma-Aldrich, cat. no. A9418-50G], and 0.2U/ml RNAse inhibitor). The process was repeated twice on the original tissue samples, and the three preparations combined, re-centrifuged, resuspended, and filtered through a 100 m filter. The pelleted nuclei were resuspended in 0.2 ml of ice-cold buffer and pipetted 50 times to minimize nuclear aggregation. Next, the isolated nuclei samples were transferred to a 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube and were purified with Optiprep as detailed in Martelotto’s protocol (4), which is recommended by Parse Biosciences. Isolated nuclei were fixed with Evercode™ Nuclei Fixation v2. Nuclei barcoding, cDNA synthesis, and library preparation were performed with the Parse Evercode™ WT v2 kit. Final library sequencing The size and purity of the final library was determined with the Fragment Analyzer (Agilent Technologies, Inc.), quantified with the Qubit dsDNA HS Assay kit (Invitrogen), and diluted according to Illumina’s standard sequencing protocol. Libraries were pooled and run on an Illumina NovaSeq 6000 sequencer by using a paired end 100 bp read format on a S4 flow cell to generate ~100 million paired reads per sample. snRNA-seq

Placental transcriptome changes due to oxycodone exposure
Main topic
This study uses single-nucleus RNA-seq analysis to determine how exposure to oxycodone affects different mouse placenta cell lineages, focusing on parietal trophoblast giant cells (pTGC) and their role in maternal metabolism and the placenta–brain axis.
