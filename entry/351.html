Assay description

Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data to find the key regulatory molecule

Extraction protocol

Total RNA from mouse placentas was extracted by Trizol regent (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and then treated with DNase I (cat#: AM2222, Thermo Fisher Scientific). RNA sequencing was done in Genesky using TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library Preparation (illumina, CA, USA).

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of gestational arsenic (As) exposure on placental and fetal development in mice, focusing on the role of Cyr61 m6A in fetal growth restriction (FGR). The study also includes a case-control study to verify the association between maternal arsenic exposure and FGR.
