Main topic
This paper investigates the role of maternal SMCHD1 in regulating imprinted Xist expression and X chromosome inactivation in mouse preimplantation embryos, demonstrating that maternal SMCHD1 is essential for the imprinted expression of Xist and proper epigenetic patterning of the zygotic genome.
Source evidence 36 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that maternal SMCHD1 is required for correct imprinted Xist expression and imprinted X chromosome inactivation in mouse preimplantation embryos and the mid-gestation placenta.
Source excerpt
Combining genomics and imaging, we show that maternal SMCHD1 is required not only for the imprinted expression of Xist but also for imprinted X chromosome inactivation.
