Main topic
This paper reports on a class of genes, "disallowed genes," that are expressed in all tissues except one, and proposes that these genes need to be repressed in the specific target tissue to ensure correct tissue function. The study provides a systematic analysis of tissue-specific repression of housekeeping genes.
