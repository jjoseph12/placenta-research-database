Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

HFFs reprogrammed into hiTSC lines by transient ectopic expression of GATA3, OCT4, KLF4, MYC (GOKM) were subjected to RNA-seq, along with samples of HFFs, hbdTSCss, hESCs and hiPSCs

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using the Macherey-Nagel RNA NucleoSpin kit (Ornat) mRNA libraries were prepared using the SENSE mRNA-seq library prep kit V2 (Lexogen)

Main topic
This paper describes a direct approach to produce human induced trophoblast stem cells (hiTSCs) from fibroblasts using GATA3, OCT4, KLF4 and MYC (GOKM) and shows that it produces superior hiTSCs when compared to OSKM-hiTSCs.
Source evidence 30 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that transient ectopic expression of GATA3, OCT4, KLF4, and MYC (GOKM) directly converts human fibroblasts into functional human induced trophoblast stem cells (hiTSCs) independently of acquiring a pluripotent state.
Source excerpt
Here we explore whether the hTSC state can be induced independently of pluripotency, and what are the mechanisms underlying its acquisition. We identify GATA3, OCT4, KLF4 and MYC (GOKM) as a combination of factors that can generate functional hiTSCs from fibroblasts.
