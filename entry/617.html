Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To investigate the impact of Zika virus infection on relative transcript abundances in human choriocarcinoma (JAR) cells, we analyzed total RNA of sample cells infected with Zika virus for 12 hours or 72 hours and compared their transcriptional profiles. Three replicates of 12 hour ZIKV-infected cellular RNA and three replicates of 72 hour ZIKV-infected cells were evaluated for a total of six samples.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA extractions were performed in accordance with recommended protocols (Invitrogen, #15596026). Libraries were constructed from 500 ng of total RNA using the SMARTer™ PCR cDNA Synthesis Kit (Clontech, #634926) and Advantage 2 PCR kit (Clontech, #639207) followed by Tn5-mediated tagmentation (see Methods and Materials for an in-depth protocol).

Main topic
This study demonstrates that sfRNA production by Zika virus represses XRN1 and interacts with a common set of 21 RNA-binding proteins that contribute to the regulation of post-transcriptional processes in the cell, including splicing, RNA stability, and translation. The regulations of cellular mRNA decay and RNA splicing are compromised by Zika virus infection as well as by sfRNA alone.
