Methods text

Assay description

MethylC-seq was performed on the placentas of 23 typical and 24 autism placentas from the MARBLES study. In addition, MethylC-seq was performed on four regions in a normal placenta to assess heterogeneity within the tissue.

Extraction protocol

Samples of the placenta were frozen immediately after birth. DNA was purified using Qiagen's Puregene kit and sonicated to about 200 bp. Methylated adapters were added with the NEBNext DNA library prep kit. Libraries were bisulfite converted with the Zymo EZ DNA Methylation Lightning kit, amplified for 14 cycles with PfuTurbo Cx, and purified with AMPure beads. Samples of the placenta were frozen immediately after birth. DNA was purified using Qiagen's Puregene kit and bisulfite converted with Zymo's EZ DNA Methylation Lightning kit. Library was prepared with Epicentre's EpiGnome Methyl-Seq kit using indexed adapters, amplified for 14 cycles, and purified with AMPure beads.
