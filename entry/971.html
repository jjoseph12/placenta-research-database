Methods text

Assay description

Comparison of DNA methylation patterns in Extraembryonic Ectoderm and cancer

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA for Whole Genome Bisulfite Sequencing was isolated according to Smith, Chan et. al, Nature, 2012 using a lysis buffer containing 300 ug/mL Proteinase K and 50 ng/mL DNAse-free RNAse, followed by phenol:chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation. Libraries were prepared using the Accel-NGS™ Bisulfite DNA library kit (Swift Biosciences) according to the manufacturers protocol. Final libraries were generated from 10-12 PCR cycles.

Main topic
This paper compares the epigenetic landscapes that evolve during early mammalian development by generating whole genome bisulfite sequencing (WGBS) and RNA-seq datasets from mouse precompacted embryos, Inner Cell Mass (ICM) and Trophectoderm from blastocysts, as well as Epiblast and Extraembryonic Ectoderm (E×E) from conceptuses, to understand genome regulation in human cancers.
