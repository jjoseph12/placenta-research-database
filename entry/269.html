Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

1) Rat placentation site compartments were dissected on gd18.5. Dissected junctional zone tissues from wild type, Akt1 null, and Foxo4Xm- fetuses were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80°C until processing for RNA isolation. 2) Foxo4 was knocked down in rat trophoblast stem cells using lentiviral delivery of shRNA. Following selection by puromycin, cells were differentiated for fifteen days. On day fifteen of differentiation, cells treated with either control shRNA or Foxo4-specific shRNA were collected and RNA was isolated.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated from cells and tissues using TRIzol reagent (cat no. 15596018, Thermo Fisher, Waltham, MA). Complementary DNA libraries from total RNA samples were prepared with Illumina TruSeq RNA preparation kits (Illumina, San Diego, CA). Barcoded cDNA libraries were multiplexed and sequenced with the NovaSeq 6000 DNA sequencer (100-bp paired-end reads) using a TruSeq 200-cycle SBS kit (Illumina) at the KUMC Genome Sequencing Facility. Reads from *.fastq files were mapped to the rat reference genome (Ensembl Rnor_5.0.78) using CLC Genomics Workbench 12.0 (Qiagen, Redwood City, CA). mRNA abundance was expressed in transcripts per million (TPM). A P value of 0.05 was used as a cutoff for significant differential expression.
