Assay description

E14.5 wild-type and Syde1-knockout placentas were collected for RNA extraction and hybridization on Affymetrix microarrays. The differentially expressed genes between wild-type and Syde1-knockout were selected and validated with quantitative real-time PCR and immunoblotting.
