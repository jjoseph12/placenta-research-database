Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular mechanisms underlying placenta accreta spectrum (PAS), a condition characterized by overly deep invasion of cytotrophoblasts (CTBs) into the uterus, through global transcriptomic profiling of CTBs from PAS cases compared to gestational age-matched controls, identifying dysregulated genes and pathways involved in cell signaling, migration, and immune functions.
