Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Trophoblast debris were isolated by four individual preeclmaptic and three gestational controlled normotensive human placentae. HMEC-1 cells were co-cultured with trophoblast debris (60ug/ml total debris protein contents) for 21 hours before RNA extraction. Untreated HMEC-1 cells were used as negative controls. In total, 10 samples were analyzed.
