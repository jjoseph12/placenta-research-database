Methods text

Assay description

RNA was isolated from JAR choriocarcinoma cells following six days of culture with 100µM CoCl2 versus control conditions (six replicates each) for expression profiling by high-throughput sequencing

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was isolated using Zymo direct-zol RNA prep. Extracted RNA was treated with NEBNext rRNA Depletion Kit v2. RNA was converted into double stranded cDNA using NEBNext mRNA Second Strand Synthesis Module. cDNA was tagmented and amplified by using Nextera XT DNA Library Preparation Kit.

Main topic
This paper investigates the role of hypoxia-inducible factor 1 (HIF-1) signaling in placental aging and its potential to provoke preterm labor. The study uses transcriptomic analysis of mouse and human placenta to demonstrate that HIF-1 stabilization is a hallmark of advanced gestational timepoints, accompanied by mitochondrial dysregulation and cellular senescence, and provides evidence for the role of placental aging in signaling that promotes the onset of labor.
