Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Liver, intercaruncular endometrium, caruncular endometrium, and placenta were sampled from Holstein dairy cows that were either lactating or non-lactating. RNA was extracted and hybridized on Affymetrix arrays. Samples were collected when the cows were pregnant on days 28, 35, or 42 of pregnancy Please note that Group refers to the months of the year when the samples were collected. Group 1 samples were collected during March to July. Group 2 samples were collected during September to December.
