Methods text

Assay description

Human placenta derived MSCs (HuMSCs) were isolated and cultured for three passages. Then they were cultured as 3D MSCs and 2D MSCs, respectively. Two types of cells were collected and prepared for the RNA-sequencing.

Extraction protocol

Cells were collected and prepared for RNA extractionusing Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 1 ug of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the therapeutic potential of three-dimensional (3D) human placenta-derived mesenchymal stem cells (HPMSCs) for treating spinal cord injury (SCI) by comparing them with conventionally cultured MSCs in vitro and in vivo, finding that 3D-HPMSCs promote neuroprotection and functional recovery in mice with SCI.
