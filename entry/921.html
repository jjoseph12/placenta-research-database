Assay description

Examination of gene expression in pericyte and fibroblast cells.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted and purified using the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) with an on-column DNase treatment. Poly-A, strand-specific RNA library construction was performed by the Yale Center for Genomic Analysis utilizing the TruSeq Library Prep Kit 2.0 per the manufacturers specifications. . Following first-strand synthesis with random primers, second strand synthesis was performed with dUTP for generating strand-specific sequencing libraries.

Main topic
This paper investigates the differential effects of fibroblasts (FBs) and pericytes (PCs) on microvascular network (μVN) formation using human endothelial colony forming cells (ECFCs) in a microfluidic chamber, finding that FBs stimulate μVN formation through paracrine growth factors while PCs directly interact with ECs to modify microvascular morphology.
