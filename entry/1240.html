Methods text

Assay description

We profiled DNA methylation for 17 human placentas, in which 5 were from LGA, 6 from IUGR and 6 from AGA placentas as controls

Extraction protocol

Genomic DNA was extracted from placental tissue using QIAmp DNA mini kit (Qiagen). Genomic DNA was digested with MspI. They were then ligated with Illumina adaptors, and size selected. Fragments from 40 to 220 bases were selected. These fragments were denatured and treated with sodium bisulfite. Libraries were sequenced using the Solexa sequencing technology (Illumna Hiseq 2000 sequencers). The reads were aligned to the reference genome (hg19) using the modified bisulfite aligner, BS Seeker2.

Main topic
This study examines genome-wide DNA methylation variation in placental samples from offspring born IUGR, LGA, and AGA to identify differential methylation of genes important for cardiometabolic disease risk, finding distinct methylation signatures and altered gene expression related to fetal growth patterns and potential cardiometabolic disease risk.

Source excerpt
Our study is a discovery-based investigation of genome-wide epigenetic variation in fetal tissues based on prenatal growth patterns and birthweight. We hypothesized that altered fetal growth, either IUGR or LGA fetuses, may correlate with changes in placental gene expression and DNA methylation in genes conferring risk for cardiometabolic disease.
