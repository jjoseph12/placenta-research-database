Assay description

Illumina MethylationEPIC analysis performed on 5 tissues from 10 animals (5 THC; 5 CON). One heart sample (right ventricle) was not collected at necropsy. One Cerebellum sample was removed due to low signal intensity.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of prenatal edible cannabis (THC) consumption on the fetal and placental epigenome in rhesus macaques, revealing alterations in DNA methylation at genes involved in neurobehavioral development, particularly those associated with autism spectrum disorder.
