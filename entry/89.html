Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

We generated genomic 5hmC-Seal and RNA-seq profiles of 19 human tissues and compared with other reference epigenomic data.

Extraction protocol

DNA was purified using Qiagen's Puregene kit and sonicated to about 200 bp. Fragmented DNA was purified by Zymo DNA Clean and Concentration Kit. Then, the selective 5hmC chemical labeling was performed in glucosylation buffer (50 mM HEPES buffer pH 8.0, 25 mM MgCl2) containing above fragmented DNA, βGT, N3-UDP-Glc, and incubated at 37°C for 2 hr. After DNA purification in ddH2O, DBCO-PEG4-Biotin (Click Chemistry Tools) was added and incubated at 37°C for 2 hr. The biotin labeled DNA was pulled down by C1 Streptavidin beads (Life Technologies) for 15 min at room temperature. Next, the captured DNA fragments were subjected to PCR amplification using Nextera DNA sample preparation kit. The resulting amplified product was purified by 1.0X AMPure XP beads. Input library was made by direct PCR from fragmented DNA directly without labeling and pull-down. The libraries were quantified by a Qubit fluorometer (Life Technologies) and sequenced on NextSeq 500. Total RNA was isolated from tissue samples with TRIZOL reagent. Poly-adenylated RNA was enriched by using FastTrack MAG Maxi mRNA isolation kit (Invitrogen). RNA-seq libraries were constructed using illumina Truseq stranded mRNA kit.

Main topic
This paper describes the development of a 5hmC tissue map by characterizing the genomic distributions of 5hmC in 19 human tissues derived from ten organ systems, revealing that 5hmC is preferentially enriched on tissue-specific gene bodies and enhancers.
