Main topic
This study addresses transcriptional alterations induced by IUGR in a multi-organ perspective, by systematic analysis of 20-days rat fetuses. The study suggests that a combination of tissue-specific mechanisms contributes to bring about tissue-driven modifications of gene cascades.
