Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Three samples from the each implantation site of normal and abortion in the same pregnant uterus.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using RNeasy mini kit (Qiagen) from mouse pregnant uterus at day 14.5 of pregnancy,which were divided the normal implantation sites and the abortion ones including dead embryo and shrunk placenta. Label Cy5 Label protocol cDNA synthesis and cRNA labeling were carried out using Low RNA Fluorescent Linear Amplification kit (Agilent). Cy3- or Cy5-labeled cRNA was purified using RNeasy mini spin columns (Qiagen).
