Assay description

NextGen RNA sequencing of total RNA isolated from bulk placental tissue homogenate by phenol/chloroform extraction. Sequencing performed at UVa Genome Analysis & Technology Core, Office of Research Core Administration. University of Virginia Health System, P.O. Box 800741, Charlottesville, VA 22908-0741

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from homogenized placental tissues using phenol/cholorform (Trizol reagent). Libraries were prepared according to manufacturer instructions with the NEBNext® Ultra™ RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina®
