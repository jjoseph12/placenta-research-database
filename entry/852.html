Methods text

Assay description

Comparative gene expression profiling analysis of RNA-seq data for treated and untreated cytotrophoblasts.

Extraction protocol

Primary cytotrophoblasts were isolated from three different human placentas by using Dispase II digestion and Percoll gradient separation. RNA was harvested using PureLink RNA Mini Kit (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer's protocol. RNA libraries for RNA-seq was prepared by the Genewiz company (Azenta, Chelmsford, MA, USA)
