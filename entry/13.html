Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA was extarcted from day-15 placenta from Cav1-null mice (B6.Cg-Cav1tm1Mls/J). The data was compared with RNA-seq profiles of day-15 placenta from wildtype mice (C57BL6/J) generated from our earlier study (accession # GSE214759).

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from whole placenta using an AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit (Qiagen, Cat No./ID: 80204) following the manufacturer’s instruction. Novogene workflow of RNA-seq library construction

Main topic
This paper investigates the single-cell transcriptional response of the placenta to the ablation of Caveolin-1 in mice, aiming to understand the adaptive regulation of the brain–placental axis. The study compares gene expression in wild type and knockout mice placentas and fetal brains to identify key cell types and genes involved in this regulation.
