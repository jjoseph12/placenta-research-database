Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of gene expression changes in wild type and Dnmt1-null neonal (P7) lung myofibroblasts.

Extraction protocol

Two WT-Pdgfrb-GFP+/- and two CKO Pdgfrb-GFP+/- lungs were harvested from P7 animals. Blood was removed by infusing PBS into the right ventricle. Lungs were then minced and digested with collagenase IV (1mg/ml, Sigma), DNase I (0.16 mg/ml, Sigma) and dispase II (2mb/ml, Roche) for 45 minutes at 37C. Tissue homogenate was filtered through a 70 um filter to obtain single cells suspension. Pdgfrb-GFP+/- positive cells were retrieved via FACS. Dead cells were detected with DAPI. Approximately 300000-350000 cells were purified from each sample. Cell pellet was resuspended in Trizol (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and RNA was extracted from the upper aqueous phase with Qiagen RNeasy Mini Spin Columns. In column DNase treatment was performed according to the Qiagen manual. Quality of the purified RNA was confirmed with Agilent Bioanalyzer System. Kapa low
