Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Examination of the sexual dimorphisim during developmental stages of 8 Cell, Es Cell, Fetal Liver and Adult Liver

Extraction protocol

RNA-seq libraries from embryonic stem cells, fetal and adult liver samples were constructed using the Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Kit, with 1 mg of Total RNA extracted with Qiagen miRNeasy Micro Kit. The eight-cell embryo RNA-seq libraries were generated using a single-cell protocol adapted from: Tang, F., Barbacioru, C., Nordman, E., Li, B., Xu, N., Bashkirov, V.I., Lao, K., and Surani, M.A. (2010). RNA-Seq analysis to capture the transcriptome landscape of a single cell. Nat Protoc 5, 516-535.​ All other RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the sexually dimorphic transcriptome in mice across different developmental stages (pre-implantation embryos, fetal liver, and adult liver) using RNA-seq and ChIP-seq data, revealing distinct phases of sex dimorphism and their implications for understanding sex-specific phenotypes and diseases.
