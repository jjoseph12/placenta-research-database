Assay description

Amplified material from each extra-embryonic tissue was indirectly labelled using "random" hexamers. One independent target per tissue was generated and hybridised onto one array. 10 measurements per reproduction mode were generated.

Main topic
This study investigates the developmental implications of embryonic/extra-embryonic interactions in bovine somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) conceptuses by analyzing the morphological and molecular features of elongating and gastrulating tissues, revealing a high incidence of discordance in embryonic and extra-embryonic patterns associated with SCNT.
