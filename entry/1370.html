Assay description

Six E18.5 Tex19.1-/- placentas (KO: four XX, two XY), four E18.5 Tex19.1+/- littermate control placentas (HET: four XX), and two E18.5 Tex19.1+/+ littermate control placentas (WT: two XY) are included in the analysis.

Main topic
This paper investigates the expression of retrotransposon-suppressing genome-defence genes in the hypomethylated trophectoderm-derived components of the mammalian placenta, focusing on Tex19.1 activation in the mouse placenta and its role in genome defense.
Source evidence 32 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates whether DNA hypomethylation in the mouse placenta regulates the expression of the germline genome-defence gene Tex19.1 to suppress retrotransposon (LINE-1) activity.
Source excerpt
Here, we investigate whether similar mechanisms operate in hypomethylated trophectoderm-derived components of the mammalian placenta to couple expression of genome-defence genes to the potential for retrotransposon activity. We show that the hypomethylated state of the mouse placenta results in activation of only one of the hypomethylation-sensitive germline genome-defence genes: Tex19.1.
