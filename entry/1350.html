Main topic
This study investigates the role of Smchd1, an epigenetic modifier essential for X chromosome inactivation, in the expression of autosomal genes subject to monoallelic expression using genome-wide microarray expression profiling and RNA-seq.
Source evidence 31 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication investigates the role of the epigenetic modifier Smchd1 in regulating autosomal genes subject to monoallelic expression (including imprinted gene clusters and clustered protocadherins), demonstrating its function extends beyond X chromosome inactivation.
Source excerpt
Smchd1 has a role in the expression of several autosomal gene clusters that are subject to monoallelic expression, rather than being restricted to functioning uniquely in X inactivation.

Source excerpt
Smchd1 is an epigenetic modifier essential for X chromosome inactivation: female embryos lacking Smchd1 fail during midgestational development. Male mice are less affected by Smchd1-loss, with some (but not all) surviving to become fertile adults on the FVB/n genetic background. On other genetic backgrounds, all males lacking Smchd1 die perinatally.
