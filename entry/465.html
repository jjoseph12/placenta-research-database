Main topic
This paper studies the role of CBP and HDAC6 in phenotypic switching using trophoblast stem cells, identifying Rel, an NF-kB family member, as transcriptionally coregulated by CBP and HDAC6, and showing that Rel induces mesenchymal-epithelial transition and itself regulates Hdac6 expression and nuclear localization.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication details the coregulation of the transcription factor Rel (an NF-κB family member) by MAP3K4, CBP, and HDAC6, demonstrating its novel role in inducing mesenchymal-epithelial transition (MET) and phenotypic switching in trophoblast stem cells.
Source excerpt
Here we show that CBP and HDAC6 coordinate expression of only 183 genes predicted to be critical regulators of phenotypic switching. The highest-ranking co-regulated gene is the NF-κB family member Rel
