Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To identify gene expression changes that might be driving the invasion defect in EOPE-TB at 20% O2, RNA was collected from CTL-TB (n = 10) and EOPE-TB (n = 14) cultured in both O2 conditions for sequencing.

Extraction protocol

RNAs were extracted with STAT60 (Tel-Test) and 1-bromo-3-chloro-propane (Sigma). Libraries were constructed following the manufacturer’s protocol with reagents supplied in Illumina’s TruSeq mRNA stranded sample preparation kit.

Main topic
This study describes a model for early onset preeclampsia (EOPE) that uses induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) generated from umbilical cords of EOPE and control (CTL) pregnancies, which are then converted to placental trophoblast to recapitulate events of early pregnancy, and the cells are tested for parameters disturbed in EOPE, including invasive potential.
