Main topic
This paper demonstrates that the activation of Wnt signaling downregulated the expression of inhibitory G protein and induced trophoblastic lineage switching to the syncytiotrophoblast progenitor state in macaque trophoblast stem cells. The ability to regulate the direction of macaque trophoblast stem cell differentiation would be advantageous in elucidating the mechanisms underlying placentation in non-human primates.
