Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Mice were randomized to 3 treatment groups on day 1.5 of pregnancy: (1) ad libitum fed (control) (2) 50% food restriction (restricted) (3) 50% food restriction with leptin replacement (1µg/g body weight/day) (leptin). On day 11.5 placentas were collected, and two placentas from each mother were pooled for microarray analysis.

Main topic
This study compares placental development and global placental gene expression profiles in pregnant control mice, mice that were undernourished, and mice that were undernourished but given leptin to understand the physiological mechanisms by which maternal undernutrition or overnutrition during pregnancy can have negative consequences for the health of children.
