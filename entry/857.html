Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA-seq differential gene expression analysis comparing different modes of conception: infertility treatments (IVF and NIFT together) vs spontaneous, NIFT vs spontaneous, IVF vs spontaneous, and IVF vs NIFT. Analysis was adjusted for fetal sex and RNA-seq dataset in the DESeq2 model.

Extraction protocol

Chorionic villi tissue was homogenized in RLT Plus lysis buffer (QIAGEN) containing 1% beta-mercaptoethanol, physically dissociated with single use needles attached to a 1 mL syringe. Total RNA was isolated from the homogenate using the AllPrep DNA/RNA Mini Kit (QIAGEN) following the manufacturer protocol. RNA-seq libraries of >200 nt RNA were constructed from 200 ng total RNA per sample using Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA with Ribo-Zero Gold sample prep kits (Illumina). Libraries were depleted of cytoplasmic and mitochondrial ribosomal RNAs.

Main topic
This study aims to identify differences in the transcriptomic profiles during placentation from pregnancies conceived spontaneously to those with infertility, using non-in vitro fertilization (NIFT) or in vitro fertilization (IVF). It concludes that transcriptomic profiles are similar in pregnancies conceived using NIFT or IVF and spontaneous conceptions.
