Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Expression profiling of three time points in two tissue types of the wallaby placenta and three time points of lactating mammary gland tissue

Extraction protocol

For fresh tissue, total RNA was isolated from placenta using a phenol–chloroform purification with Trizol (Invitrogen) and mRNA was subsequently isolated using Dynabeads Oligo (dT)25. the samples were given a short heat-shearing treatment immediately followed by cDNA synthesis. The resulting cDNA was repaired using 3’A-tailing and ligated to linkers containing unique illumina barcodes for sequencing. An agarose gel was used for size selection and the samples were then PCR amplified for 15(fresh)-17(fixed) cycles and purified using AMPure XP beads. Quality of the libraries was assessed using both Qubit and Bioanlyzer technology and then sequenced Fixed samples were initially subjected to a brief protease digestion to remove nucleic acid cross-linking. Total RNA was then extracted using the ambion RecoverAll Total Nucleic Acid Isolation kit and mRNA was subsequently isolated using Dynabeads Oligo (dT)25. the samples were given a short heat-shearing treatment immediately followed by cDNA synthesis. Main topic
This study investigates the molecular program of the placenta and mammary gland of the tammar wallaby and compares it to eutherian mammals, finding that marsupial and eutherian placentas share similarities and that genes facilitating fetal development and nutrient transport display convergent co-option by placental and mammary gland cell types to optimize offspring success.
