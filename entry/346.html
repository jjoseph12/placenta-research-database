Assay description

Human Placental Chorionic Villi Explants (HPE) were obtained from term placentas (>38 weeks) with an informed consent from heatlhy women in elective cesarean delivery. In a Level II biosafety cabinet, 0,5 cm3 HPE were isolated from the center of the cotyledon, washed with sterile PBS in order to remove excess of blood and later incubated with 105 or 106 parasites/ml (Y Strain Tripomastigotes, the infective form of the parasite) for 2 and 24 hrs.

Main topic
This paper investigates the changes in human placental gene expression induced by Trypanosoma cruzi, the parasite that causes Chagas disease, focusing on the reprogramming of genes involved in the innate immune response.
