Assay description

For each tissue sample, the total RNA obtained from established organoids were used compared to both the initial glandular digests and stromal cells isolated from the same endometrial biopsy. Glands, Organoids and Stroma samples 1 to 7 refer to endometrial derived samples. Organoids 8 to 13 refer to decidua derived organoids.

Main topic
This paper describes a method for establishing and culturing long-term, hormone-responsive organoids of human endometrium in a chemically-defined medium, which can recapitulate endometrial function in vitro.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Development of long-term, genetically stable, hormone-responsive 3D organoid cultures of human endometrium and decidua in a chemically-defined medium.
Source excerpt
Here, we describe a robust chemically-defined method for establishing genetically stable endometrial organoids from human non-pregnant endometrium and decidua that can be cultured long-term and recapitulate the molecular signature of endometrial glands in vivo.
