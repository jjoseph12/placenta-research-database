Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To investigate the function of TFEB in the regulation of trophoblast syncytialization in human and mice, we established BeWo cell lines in which TFEB has been knockout by CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing technology.We then performed gene expression profiling analysis using data obtained from RNA-seq of control or TFEB KO BeWo cell lines treated with DMSO or FSK at 12h or 24h.

Extraction protocol

Total RNAs from BeWo cells with DMSO or FSK treatments were extracted by TRIzol and purified by poly-A before subjected to RNA-Seq. 1 μg of total RNA was used for sequencing libraries. MGIEasy Fast PCR-FREE FS Library Prep Set
