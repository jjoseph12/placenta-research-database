Methods text

Assay description

Primary human trophoblasts from 3 placentas along with fused and non-fused cells from 3 BeWo cultures were infected or not with L. monocytogenes in duplicate

Extraction protocol

Cells were lysed in TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen). PureLink RNA extraction kit with on-column DNase treatment (Invitrogen) was used to isolate RNA. Input RNA integrity number (RIN) were all > 7 and concentration was >100 ng/µl RNA, as determined with the Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA) and Qubit Fluorometer (Thermo Fisher). RNA-seq libraries were generated with NEBNext® Ultra™ II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina (NEB #E7760L) and NEBNext® rRNA Depletion Kit with sample purification beads (NEB #E6350) with an input amount of 200 ng total RNA per sample. Libraries were pooled and sequenced on an Illumina NovaSeq SP flowcell in paired-end 150 bp format (Illumina, San Diego, CA) to a read yield between 70-80 million reads (equivalent to 35- 40 million clusters).

Main topic
This study investigates the interaction between Listeria monocytogenes and human placental trophoblasts, focusing on the resistance of syncytia (fused trophoblasts) to infection compared to mononuclear trophoblasts, and the associated immune responses.
