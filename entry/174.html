Extraction protocol

Freshly collected day 15 placentae from three biological replicates were pooled that was used to preapre ChIP DNA using Zymo-Spin ChIP Kit (Catalog# D5210). The REST antibody from Millipore-sigma (Catalog# 17-641) was validated and used to prepare the ChIP DNA. ChIP-seq approach was adopted for library prepartion, and Illumina Hiseq was used to sequence the libraries. Freshly collected gestation day 15 placenta and fetal brain from littermate wildtype mice were snap frozen in liquid nitrogen. Genomic DNA was isolated from frozen specimen using Genomic DNA was extracted using Gentra Puregene Tissue Kit (catalog #158667, Qiagen) from placenta and fetal brain of day 15 fetuses of both sexes from the wildtype mice (total 4 samples). Equimolar amount of DNA from three biological replicates for these samples. The pooled DNA was used for bisulfite conversion and libraries were prepared by the University of Missouri DNA core using NEBNext® Ultra™ II DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina. Freshly collected day 15 placenta and fetal brain from wildtype mice were snap frozen in liquid nitrogen. Total RNA was isolated from frozen speciment using a standard TRIzol-based protocol (Catalog 15596026, Thermo Fisher). To eliminate genomic DNA contamination, extracted RNA was treated with DNase I and purified using a RNeasy MinElute Cleanup Kit (Qiagen). The quality and concentration of RNA were determined using a Fragment Analyzer (Advanced Analytical Technologies). Libraries were prepared by the University of Missouri DNA core using an TruSeq Small RNA Library Prep Kit (Illumina Inc.) The miRNA-seq strategy of TruSeq small RNA libraries was used. Freshly collected gestation day 15 placenta and fetal brain from littermate wildtype and Foxa2 conditional knockout mice were snap frozen in liquid nitrogen. Total RNA was isolated from frozen speciment using a standard TRIzol-based protocol (Catalog 15596026, Thermo Fisher). To eliminate genomic DNA contamination, extracted RNA was treated with DNase I and purified using a RNeasy MinElute Cleanup Kit (Qiagen). The quality and concentration of RNA were determined using a Fragment Analyzer (Advanced Analytical Technologies). Libraries were prepared by Novogene using an Illumina TruSeq mRNA kit (Illumina Inc.). Freshly collected gestation day 15 placenta and fetal brain from littermate wildtype and Foxa2 conditional knockout mice were snap frozen in liquid nitrogen. Total RNA was isolated from frozen speciment using a standard TRIzol-based protocol (Catalog 15596026, Thermo Fisher). To eliminate genomic DNA contamination, extracted RNA was treated with DNase I and purified using a RNeasy MinElute Cleanup Kit (Qiagen). The quality and concentration of RNA were determined using a Fragment Analyzer (Advanced Analytical Technologies). Libraries were prepared by Novogene using an Illumina TruSeq mRNA kit (Illumina Inc.) and sequenced (paired-end 150; 30 million read pairs) using an Illumina NovaSeq 6000. The strategy was based on lllumina TruSeq mRNA kit.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of ablating uterine Forkhead Box A2 (Foxa2) on fetal brain development, focusing on sexually conflicting transcriptional responses, placental function, and longevity-related genes using a conditional knockout mouse model.
