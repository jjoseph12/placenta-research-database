Methods text

Assay description

HTR-8/SVneo SP cells or NSP cells were isolated using a FACS Vantage fluorescence-activated cell sorter (BD Bioscience, San Jose, California) using dual wavelength analysis (blue, 424 - 444nm; red 675 nm) after excitation with 350 nm UV light. PI-positive dead cells were excluded from the analysis. Sorted SP and NSP cells' RNA had been analyzed by microarray analysis.
