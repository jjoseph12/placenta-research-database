Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The day 15 placenta and brain of male and female fetuses of wild-type (C57BL/6) mice were analyzed by whole-genome bisulfite sequencing (WGBS).

Extraction protocol

Freshly collected gestation day 15 placenta and fetal brain from littermate wildtype mice were snap frozen in liquid nitrogen. Genomic DNA was isolated from frozen specimen using Genomic DNA was extracted using Gentra Puregene Tissue Kit (catalog #158667, Qiagen) from placenta and fetal brain of day 15 fetuses of both sexes from the wildtype mice (total 4 samples). Equimolar amount of DNA from three biological replicates for these samples. The pooled DNA was used for bisulfite conversion and libraries were prepared by the University of Missouri DNA core using NEBNext® Ultra™ II DNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina.
