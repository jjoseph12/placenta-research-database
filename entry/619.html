Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

This study examines gene expression in mouse hippocampal cells (ex vivo) subjected to (i) ACSS2 inhibition and (ii) acetate treatment to simulate the effects of heavy drinking. RNA-seq was performed on cells treated with an ACSS2 inhibitor or DMSO; those cells were in turn split into two groups and treated with acetate or not. Each of the four experimental groups had four replicates each, except for cells treated with ACSS2 inhibitor and no acetate, which had three.

Extraction protocol

Cells were lyzed using QIAzol (Qiagen) and RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen). RNAseq libraries were prepared using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA library prep kit (NEB).
