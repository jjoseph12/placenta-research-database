Methods text

Assay description

ChIP-Seq (H3K27ac) and RNA-Seq data are generated for human trophoblast stem cells with or without interferon-gamma stimulation, respectively.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA for human TSCs was extracted using RNeasy Micro kit (Qiagen, 74004) with on-column DNase digestion. RNA-Seq library is construction by TruSeq stranded mRNA sample preparation kit (Illumina), and then sequenced as 75 bp paired-end reads with HiSeq2500 (Illumina) platform.
