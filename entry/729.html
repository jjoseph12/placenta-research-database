Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

decidua samples from 3 healthy controls and 3 RSA patients were bulk sequenced for gene expression

Extraction protocol

decidua were collected, flash frozen in liquid nitrogen, and RNA was harvested using Trizol reagent. Illumina TruSeq RNA Sample Prep Kit (Cat#FC-122-1001) was used with 1 ug of total RNA for the construction of sequencing libraries. Total RNAs of decidua tissues were extracted using Trizol (Invitrogen, CA, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instruction. Oligo(dT)-attached magnetic beads were used to purified mRNA, which then was fragmented into small pieces with fragment buffer at appropriate temperature to generate final library. The final library was amplified with phi29 to make DNA nanoball (DNB) which has more than 300 copies of one molecular, DNBs were loaded into the patterned nanoarray and single end 50 bases reads were generated on the BGIseq500 platform (BGI-Shenzhen, China).

Main topic
This study investigates the role of γδ T cells in recurrent spontaneous abortion (RSA) by analyzing peripheral blood from RSA patients and healthy women, finding that activated γδ T cells with higher CD107a expression and inflammatory potential are present during early pregnancy in RSA patients.
