Main topic
This paper investigates the role of chromosome 19 microRNA cluster (C19MC) miRNAs in regulating the migration of extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs), which are essential for proper implantation and maternal-fetal exchange during early pregnancy.
Workbook excerpt Source evidence 33 evidence notes linked 0 supplement repair findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study demonstrates that chromosome 19 miRNA cluster (C19MC) miRNAs are expressed at higher levels in villous trophoblasts (VTs) than in extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs), and that ectopic expression of these C19MC miRNAs in an EVT-derived cell line selectively attenuates cell migration without impacting cell proliferation or apoptosis.
Source excerpt
In the work reported here, we found that the expression of C19MC miRNAs is higher in VTs than in EVTs... Together, our data suggest a role for C19MC miRNAs in modulating the migration of EVTs.
