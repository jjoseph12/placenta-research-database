Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Rcho-1 TS cells transduced with control shRNAs were used as controls. Cells transduced with two different shRNAs targeting Satb1 were used as treatment. RNA-seq was performed in TS cells at 4 days (acute) and 10 days (chronic) after transduction.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted from cultured cells using TriReagent according to the manufacturer's recommendations. Libraries were prepared from poly-A containing RNAs according to protocols provided by the TruSeq RNA sample preparation kits (Illumina).
