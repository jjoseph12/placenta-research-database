Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Poly(A) RNA-seq were performed from mESCs with siRNAs against different splicing factors cultured in Serum/LIF medium, TBLCs cultured in SLP medium and mESCs/H1299 upon high concentration treatment. TBLCs cultured in SLP medium and extracted the RNA and DNA for highthrough sequencing.

Extraction protocol

DNA and RNA extracted according to the manufacturer’s instructions. RNA-seq libraries preparation using the TruSeq Stranded mRNA Sample Prep Kits (Illumina). 4sU-seq libraries preparation using TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Sample Prep Kits (Illumina). ATAC-seq Library Preparation is according to insructions of Corces et al. Nature Methods 2017. DNA and RNA extracted according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Libraries of WGBS-seq, single-cell seq and ribo-seq constructed according to the manufacturer's instructions.
