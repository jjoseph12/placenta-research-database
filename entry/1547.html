Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Pregnant Long-Evans rats voluntarily consumed either a 0% or 5% ethanol solution four hours each day throughout gestation. Ethanol consumption produced a mean maternal daily intermittent peak serum ethanol concentration of 84 mg/dL. Placentas were harvested on Gestational Day 20 for gene expression studies.

Main topic
This study investigates the effects of moderate ethanol consumption during pregnancy on placental gene expression in Long-Evans rats, aiming to identify potential biomarkers for maternal drinking and risk indicators for adverse neurobehavioral outcomes in offspring.
