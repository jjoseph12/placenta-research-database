Methods text

Assay description

This study contains 3 groups of monkeys: control, standard HIG-infused, and high-potency HIG-infused. All control animals + 2/3 standard HIG-infused animals had congenitally infected fetuses. Thus, there are 4 independent groups: (1) control + CMV-infected, (2) standard + CMV-infected, (3) standard + not CMV-infected, and (4) high-potency HIG + not CMV-infected. RNA was extracted from sections of fetal placenta for 7 animals in total.
