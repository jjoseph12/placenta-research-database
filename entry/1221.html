11 samples were analyzed: MEF4F, mouse embyronic fibroblasts (MEFs) transduced with Tfap2c, Gata3, Eomes and Ets2, 3 replicates. TS, mouse TS-EGFP trophoblast stem cells, 2 replicates. iTSC4F5, mouse induced trophoblast stem (TS) cells clone A from 4 factor transduced MEFs, 3 replicates. iTSC4F8, mouse induced trophoblast stem (TS) cells clone D from 4 factor transduced MEFs, 3 replicates.
