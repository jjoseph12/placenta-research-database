Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Total RNA was extracted from whole E14.5 brains (3 ZIKV-infected (ZIKV) and 3 Mock-infected (CTRL) litter-matched embryos) following ZIKV-infection at E10.5 for RNAseq comparison.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using PureLink RNA Mini Kit (Invitrogen) combined with TRIZOL purification and treated with Turbo DNA-free Kit (Invitrogen).Whole E14.5 brains were collected for RNA-seq analysis. Three embryos from different litters in which ZIKV copies were > 1010 copies/ug RNA were selected from the ZIKV-injected group, and 3 littermates with mock injection were selected as controls. RNA was extracted as described above. RNA integrity number (RIN) measured by Bioanalyzer (Agilent) for all samples was above 9.0. Libraries were constructed using TruSeq RNA Kit (Illumina).

Main topic
This paper investigates the molecular pathways perturbed by congenital Zika virus (ZIKV) infection in an immunocompetent mouse model, using integrative systems biology, proteomics, and RNA-seq to analyze embryonic brain tissues, revealing a robust immune response and downregulation of critical neurodevelopmental gene programs.
