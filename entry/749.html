Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

To induce Prdm1 gene deletion in the uterus, Prdm1 BEH/+;PRCre/+ studs (Vincent et al., 2005, Soyal et al., 2005) were crossed with female Prdm1CA/CA mice (Shapiro-Shelef et al., 2003) to generate Prdm1 BEH/CA ;PRCre females, hereinafter referred to as Blimp1 mutant. Female littermates (6–10 weeks of age) were used as the wild type. Whole decidua from E5.5 and E6.5 Blimp1 mutant and wild type mice were analysed by RNA-Seq.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted using an RNeasy Mini kit (Qiagen Cat#74104) with on column DNase treatment according manufacturer’s protocol. RNA was normalized to 800ng per sample followed by depletion of cytoplasmic and mitochondrial ribosomal RNA sequences (Ribo-Zero Gold rRNA Removal Kit (H/M/R), Cat: #MRZG12324). Subsequent library preparation was completed using the Illumina TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Library Prep kit (H/M/R) (Cat: #20020597). Followed by sequencing on an Illumina HiSeq4000 (75bp paired end).
