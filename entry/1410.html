DNA methylation
Main topic
This study examines DNA methylation discordance in three neonatal tissues from monozygotic (MZ) and dizygotic (DZ) twin pairs to estimate the relative contribution of genetic and environmental factors to phenotypic variability, finding that nonshared intrauterine environment and stochastic factors have the largest impact on DNA methylation.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study analyzes genome-scale DNA methylation patterns in three neonatal tissues from MZ and DZ twins at birth to investigate genetic, environmental, and stochastic influences on the neonatal epigenome.
Source excerpt
Using DNA methylation profiling of ~20,000 CpG sites as a phenotype, we have examined discordance levels in three neonatal tissues from 22 MZ and 12 DZ twin pairs.
