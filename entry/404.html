Main topic
This study aimed to understand the differences in placental genomic expression profiles comparing IVF-ET and natural pregnancy-derived placentas, and the link between IVF-ET manipulation and placental structure and function. They used next-generation sequencing techniques to analyze of miRNA and mRNA expression profiles in IVF-ET and natural gestational placental tissue.
