Methods text

Assay description

Examination of gene expression differences in mouse placenta tissue bewteen wild-type and Zfp36l3 knockout groups, each with 5 biological replicates

Extraction protocol

RNA was isolated from embryonic day 15.5 placentas using the GE Healthcare RNA isolation kit. RNA was quantified using the Thermo Scientific Nanodrop 2000c. Libraries were prepared according to Illumina's Instructions

Main topic
This study investigates the role of ZFP36L3, a placenta- and yolk sac-specific mRNA-binding protein, in female reproductive physiology and fecundity using mice deficient in ZFP36L3, focusing on its impact on mRNA target accumulation and placental iron metabolism.
