Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Placentas collected from (i) preterm spontaneous delivery (<34 weeks of gestation) and (ii) term spontaneous delivery (38-39 weeks of gestation) were subjected to RNA extraction and hybridization on Affymetrix microarrays. To identify gene expression patterns that are commonly involved in preterm spontaneous labour, we analyzed 5 placentas from each of these 2 groups and tested for any differentially expressed genes by Mann-Whitney Rank Sum Test.

Main topic
This study profiles RNA expression in spontaneous preterm birth (SPB) placentas compared to spontaneous term birth (STB) placentas to identify SPB-associated RNA transcripts detectable in maternal plasma, finding 36 such transcripts and illustrating the potential of IL1RL1 as a marker.
