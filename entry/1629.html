Main topic
This paper focuses on dissecting the molecular make-up of the dermal papilla (DP) and its surrounding cell types in the hair follicle using a novel multicolor labeling approach and transcriptional profiling to understand mesenchymal-epithelial interactions and identify key factors involved in hair development and associated disorders.
