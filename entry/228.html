Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Intact RNA isolated from 6 human decidua tissues was fragmented, end repaired, adapter ligation and PCR amplified following illumina protocol. Libraries were sequencing by illumins Hiseq 2000.

Extraction protocol

RNA was extracted by RNeasy Kits (Qiagen, Hilden, NRW, Germany) and treated with DNase I (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Wilmington, DE, USA) according to standard protocols. RNA-sequencing was done in Genesky using TruSeq Stranded mRNA Library Preparation (Genesky, Shanghai, China). Briefly, intact RNA was fragmented, end repaired, adapter ligation and PCR amplified following illumina protocol. Libraries were sequencing by illumins Hiseq 2000. Main topic
This paper investigates the role of DNA methylation in recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL) by performing genome-wide DNA methylation analysis combined with gene expression analysis in decidua tissue, identifying CREB5 as a potential risk gene.
