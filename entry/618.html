Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

This experiment assesses the gene expression response to EtOH in mouse hippocampus by RNA-seq, in the presence or absence of enzyme ACSS2. EtOH was injected systemically via intraperitoneal injection, as was an ACSS2 shRNA knockdown. Controls for EtOH treatment (two replicates each of ACSS2- or control) were previously published under GEO series GSE90855 and are re-uploaded here. Three and four replicates (dorsal and ventral) of EtOH treated ACSS2+ samples are included here, as are five and four replicates (dorsal and ventral) of EtOH treated ACSS2- samples. An additional total hippocampus ACSS2- EtOH+ sample is also included.

Extraction protocol

Cells were lyzed using QIAzol (Qiagen) and RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen). RNAseq libraries were prepared using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA library prep kit (NEB).
