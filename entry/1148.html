Methods text

Assay description

Placental villous explants were exposed in five independent experiments to 0 or 20 µM DCVC for 12 h in vitro; 10 samples in total. Villous explants used for non-treated and treated groups, respectively within each experiment, were sourced and matched from the same placenta. Gene specific gene expression levels were compared between non-treated samples (controls) and samples treated with 20 µM DCVC for 12 h.

Extraction protocol

Each explant was transferred to a 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube, lysed in QIAzol reagent and homogenized in a TissueLyser LT (Qiagen) for 5 minutes at 50 Hz, using one 3.2 mm stainless steel bead per tube. mRNA was extracted using the miRNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen) following the manufacturer’s recommended protocol. RNA libraries were prepared for sequencing using standard Illumina protocols

Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptional response of first-trimester placental models to the trichloroethylene (TCE) metabolite S-(1,2-dichlorovinyl)-l-cysteine (DCVC), finding that DCVC induces the integrated stress response (ISR) and alters biological processes related to amino acid transport, metabolism, and tissue development.
