Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

RNA m6A modification landscape of placental samples of preeclampsia (n=4) and control patients (n=4), were generated by Illumina Hiseq 4000.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA samples are qualified by agarose gel electrophoresis and quantified using Nanodrop. mRNA was isolated and then fragmented to 100-nucleotide-long fragments. m6A methylated mRNAs were enriched with anti-N6-methyadenosine (m6A) antibody. We use commercial kit for RNA-seq library preparation of m6A mRNA and input samples. Completed libraries were qualified and then sequenced on Hiseq 4000 platform.

Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptome-wide m6A methylome in placentas from preeclampsia (PE) patients and healthy controls, revealing altered m6A methylation and mRNA expression profiles, and identifying potential pathways and genes associated with PE.
