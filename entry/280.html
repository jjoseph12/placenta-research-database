Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted from indicated cell lines at 50% confluency with Trizol reagent. RNA was next utilized for RNA-Seq by ScriptSeq Preparation Kit v2 (Illumina) according to manufacturer’s instruction Cells were trypsinized and counted, 50,000 cells were centrifuged at 500g for 3 min, followed by a wash using 50 μl of cold PBS and centrifugation at 500g for 3 min. Cells were lysed using cold lysis buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl2 and 0.1% IGEPAL CA-630). Immediately after lysis, nuclei were spun at 500g for 10 min using a refrigerated centrifuge. Next, the pellet was resuspended in the transposase reaction mix (25 μl 2× TD buffer, 2.5 μl transposase (Illumina) and 22.5 μl nuclease-free water). The transposition reaction was carried out for 30 min at 37 °C and immediately put on ice. Directly afterwards, the sample was purified using a Qiagen MinElute kit. Following purification, the library fragments were amplified using custom Nextera PCR primers 1 and 2 for a total of 12 cycles. Following PCR amplification, the libraries were purified using a QiagenMinElute Kit and sequenced.

Main topic
This paper reports that primed human PSCs can be directly and robustly converted into TSCs through TGF-β pathway inhibition, and that these primed PSC-derived TSC lines are similar to TSC lines derived from human placenta, blastocysts, or isogenic human naïve PSCs.

Reviewed answer The publication demonstrates that both human primed and naïve pluripotent stem cells can be directly and robustly converted into functional trophoblast stem cells in vitro using TGF-beta pathway inhibition or YAP activation.

Here we report that, in the absence of WNT stimulation, transforming growth factor β (TGF-β) pathway inhibition leads to direct and robust conversion of primed human PSCs into TSCs.
