Assay description

Mouse (Mus musculus) term placentas from 8 term mouse placentas derived from blastocysts after frozen ET (4 female,4 male); 8 from fresh ET (4 female, 4 male) and 8 from unassisted conceptions were obtained for profiling of genome-wide DNA methylation changes.

Main topic
This study examines DNA methylation in human placentas following frozen and fresh embryo transfer (ET) cycles, comparing them to unassisted conceptions to understand the impact of in vitro fertilization (IVF) interventions on pregnancy outcomes.
Source evidence 33 evidence notes, 0 supplement findings
Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The publication investigates the effect of embryo cryopreservation (frozen embryo transfer) on genome-wide DNA methylation in both human and mouse placentas, showing sex-specific epigenetic changes driven primarily by male placentas.
Source excerpt
This study examines DNA methylation in human placentas using the 850K Infinium MethylationEPIC BeadChip array obtained after 65 programmed frozen ET cycles, 82 fresh ET cycles and 45 unassisted conceptions. In parallel, eight mouse placentas from fresh and frozen ET were analyzed using the Infinium Mouse Methylation BeadChip array.
