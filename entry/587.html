Methods text

Assay description

Two weeks before mating, female rats were randomly assigned either to a control diet group (37 mg/kg iron) or an iron-restricted diet group (3 mg/kg iron diet). After 2 weeks, female rats were bred to age-matched males fed a standard rodent chow. Dams in the control group were maintained on the same control diet throughout pregnancy; dams on the iron-restricted diet were transferred to a diet that contained 10 mg/kg iron for the duration of the study. On GD18.5, placentas were isolated, RNA extracted using a TRIzol-RNeasy column hybrid protocol including on-column DNase I digestion, and purified. Each sample represents RNA pooled from 2 placentas from a single dam. Normalization and output file generation was performed with Affymetrix Gene Expression Console (Thermo) using the Robust Multi-Chip Analysis (RMA) algorithm [RMA-Gene-full].
