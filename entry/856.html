Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

Transcriptomic profile of control and GCM1-deficient trophoblast stem cells.

Extraction protocol

RiboZol was used to extract RNA from cells. The aqueous layer was removed, diluted in 70% ethanol, placed on RNeasy columns, treated with DNase I, and RNA purified. RNA libraries were prepared according to manufacturer's instructions using the NEBNEXT® Ultra™ Library Kit for Illumina®

Main topic
This study investigates the role of Glial cells missing-1 (GCM1) in human trophoblast stem cells, demonstrating its importance in both syncytiotrophoblast (ST) and extravillous trophoblast (EVT) development, and suggesting that GCM1 regulates differentiation of human TS cells into EVTs by inducing expression of ASCL2 and NOTUM.
