Methods text

Assay description

Total RNA-sequencing of NWR iPSCs cultured in 3 different conditions: primed mTeSR1 (feeder-free, 4 replicates), naïve N2B27 (on feeder cells, 2 replicates), naïve Rset (on feeder cells, 2 replicates).

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was was isolated using RNeasy mini kit (Qiagen, #74104) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. 3 µg of isolated RNA were treated with TURBO DNase (Life Technologies, #AM2239) followed by clean up using RNeasy MinElute RNA cleanup kit (Qiagen, #74204). RNA purity and quality were assessed with microcapillary electrophoresis on Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer (RNA Pico 6000 kit, Agilent, #5067-1513). Ribosomal RNA was depleted from 1 µg of total RNA using RiboZero Gold (Human/Mouse/Rat) kit (Illumina), followed by purification with RNeasy MinElute RNA cleanup kit. Sequencing libraries were prepared from rRNA-depleted RNA using TruSeq Stranded total RNA LT kit (Illumina, #20020598) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. The final PCR amplification included 11 cycles followed by purification with Ampure XP beads (Beckman-Coulter, #A63881). Quality of final libraries was evaluated by Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer using DNA 1000 kits (Agilent, #5067-1504). DNA concentrations were measured with Qubit (Life Technologies) and dsDNA HS Assay Kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific, #Q32854). Libraries were sequenced using Illumina NextSeq 500 instrument [75-nt single-end reads, NextSeq 500/550 High Output Kit v2.5 (75 Cycles), Illumina, #20024906].

Main topic
This paper focuses on generating induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) from a deceased Northern White Rhinoceros (NWR) female to rescue the functionally extinct species by producing gametes and embryos in vitro, highlighting the similarities between NWR iPSCs and human PSCs and their potential for differentiation and integration into mouse embryos.
