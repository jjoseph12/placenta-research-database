Methods Assay, extraction, and data processing text

Assay description

The RNA-seq experiment assesses the transcriptional changes in wild type (Vector) versus Pigl knockout (KO) or Pigf KO mouse trophoblast stem cells at three time points: under stem cell conditions at 0 days (0d), and after 3 days (3d) and 6 days (6d) of differentiation.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted with Trizol followed by Dnase treatment using TURBO DNA-free kit (Life Technologies AM1907). RNA-seq libraries were generated from 500ng using TruSeq Stranded mRNA library prop (Illumina, 20020594).

Main topic
This study focuses on the significance of two integral components of the GPI pathway, the PIGL and PIGF proteins, in trophoblast biology, demonstrating that GPI pathway mutations impact placental development and impair the differentiation of the syncytiotrophoblast (SynT). It also connects GPI pathway defects with the pathogenesis of preeclampsia.
