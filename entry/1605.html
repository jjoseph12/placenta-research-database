Methods text

Assay description

Tumor tissues were collected under IRB-approved protocols at the Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center, New York, between 1987 and 2003. The tumor samples consist of 34 NSGCT specimens. RNA was isolated, labeled, and hybridized to Affymetrix U133A and U133B microarrays using standard protocols. Data were processed by the RMA method.
