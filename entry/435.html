Methods text

Assay description

Examination of gene expression changes in wild type and Dnmt1 CKO E18.5 lungs. 3 wildtype, 6 mutant (3 with weak phenotype, 3 with strong phenotype) lungs were utilized. Mutant lungs with weak phenotype were only hypoplastic. Mutant lungs with strong phenotype were hypoplastic and displayed widespread hemorrhages within the parenchyma.

Extraction protocol

Embryonic E18.5 lungs were homogenized and dissolved in Trizol (Thermo Fisher Scientific). RNA was extracted from the upper aqueous phase with Qiagen RNeasy Mini Spin Columns. In columns-DNAse treatment was performed according to the Qiagen manual. The quality of the purified RNA was confirmed by Agilent Bioanalyzer System, and reverse cDNA library synthesis and sequencing was performed at the Genomic Core at USC (50 M reads, 75 SR per sample).
