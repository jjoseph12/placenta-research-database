Main topic
This study establishes an in vitro model of complete hydatidiform mole (HM) using patient-specific induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) derived trophoblasts harboring NLRP7 mutations to investigate the role of NLRP7 in HM pathology and trophoblast differentiation, finding that impaired NLRP7 expression results in precocious downregulation of pluripotency factors, activation of trophoblast lineage markers, and promotes maturation of differentiated extraembryonic cell types in a BMP4 signaling-dependent manner.
