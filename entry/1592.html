Main topic
This study presents a comprehensive analysis of imprinted genes in swine using expression and phenotypic profiling of parthenogenetic fetuses, combined with pyrosequencing to confirm the imprinting status of 36 genes in several fetal tissues at Day 30 of gestation.

Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer Comprehensive characterization of genomic imprinting status in swine, evaluating placental and fetal phenotypes of parthenogenetic pregnancies and using reciprocal crosses to confirm parent-of-origin allelic expression.
Source excerpt
To increase our understanding of imprinted genes in swine, we carried out a comprehensive analysis of this gene family using two complementary approaches: expression and phenotypic profiling of parthenogenetic fetuses, and analysis of imprinting by pyrosequencing.
