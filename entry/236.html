Assay description

Hippocampus and Hypothalamus of WT dams carrying E16.5 Phlda2+/+(129) (non-transgenic; 1X), Phlda2-/+(129) (maternal KO; 0X) and Phlda2+/+BACx1(129) (single copy Phlda2 transgene; 2X) placenta(N=4 of each genotype, two independent litters) were used to extract RNA and hybridize to Affymetrix microarrays.

Main topic
This study investigates how the paternally silenced imprinted gene Phlda2 influences maternal care in mice. The findings suggest that the genetic makeup of offspring can influence maternal behavior, with varying doses of Phlda2 affecting nest building, nurturing, and grooming behaviors in dams.
