Methods text

Assay description

Endometrial mid-luteal phase biopsies or timecourse of primary endometrial stromal cells decidualized in culture were recovered and subjected to single-cell analysis using nanoliter droplet barcoding and high throughput sequencing of RNA (Drop-Seq)

Extraction protocol

Endometrial pipelle biopsies were dissociated as described previously (Barros, Brosens and Brighton (2016) Isolation and Primary Culture of Various Cell Types from Whole Human Endometrial Biopsies, Bio-Protocol: 6(22); DOI: 10.21769/BioProtoc.2028) Cells in suspension were captured and lysed singly in nanoliter droplets, as described by Macosko et al (Cell. 2015 May 21;161(5):1202-1214. doi: 10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.002. PMID: 26000488). Libraries were prepared as described by Macosko et al (Cell. 2015 May 21;161(5):1202-1214. doi: 10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.002. PMID: 26000488).

Main topic
This paper reconstructs the decidual pathway at a single-cell level in vitro and demonstrates that stromal cells mount an acute stress response before emerging as decidual cells or senescent decidual cells. It identifies DIO2 and SCARA5 as markers and investigates the association between recurrent pregnancy loss and decidual response.
