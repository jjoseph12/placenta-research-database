Methods text

Assay description

Healthy women at the first trimester of pregnancy were recruited, and FACS was performed to isolate and purify the CD8+ T cells from the decidua and maternal peripheral blood. We sequenced the mRNA samples on an Illumina Hiseq 2500 platform.

Extraction protocol

Total RNA was extracted by using Trizol Reagent, and was purified by RNeasy Micro Kit. Library preparation was performed as previously described (Zhang et al., 2014). We used 200 ng RNA per sample as input material for the RNA sample preparations. cDNA libraries were constructed by using the NEBNext® UltraTM RNA Library Prep Kit for Illumina® (NEB, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instruction, and the products were purified by AMPure XP system and library quality was determined on the Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 system.

Main topic
This study investigates the transcriptional and alternative splicing signatures, as well as the functional features, of paired pCD8 and dCD8 T cells at the first trimester of human healthy pregnancy using high-throughput mRNA-Seq and flow cytometry.
