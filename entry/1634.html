Workbook field Main topic of the publication
Reviewed answer The study investigates the levels of endothelin-1 (ET-1) in maternal plasma and placental tissue among pregnant women with obesity complicated by obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).
Source excerpt
Our data confirm the presence of ET-1 in maternal plasma and placental tissue in high-risk obstetric patients with OSA. In a population with stringent inclusion and exclusion criteria and high prevalence of sleep apnea (39%), our data do not demonstrate any statistically significant difference in the median levels of ET-1 between those with OSA compared with those without.
