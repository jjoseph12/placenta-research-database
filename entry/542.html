Extraction protocol

Frozen tissue was pulverized using a liquid nitrogen-chilled hammer mill, crosslinked with 1% formaldehyde in a volume of 10mL, quenched by addition of 5mL 2.5 M glycine for 5 min at 4°C, washed generously in ice-cold PBS, and homogenized in Farnham Lysis Buffer [5 mM PIPES pH 8, 85 mM KCl, 0.5% NP-40, 1 mM DTT, 1x cOmplete protease inhibitor cocktail (Roche, 11697498001)], as described (Murakami et al., 2017). The nuclei were collected by brief centrifugation and resuspended in SDS Lysis Buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.9, 1% SDS, 10 mM EDTA, 1 mM DTT, 1x cOmplete protease inhibitor cocktail) by pipetting and incubating on ice for 10 min. The chromatin was sheared to ~200 bp DNA fragments by sonication using a Bioruptor sonicator (Diagenode) for 24-28 cycles of 30 sec on and 30 sec off. Fragment size was verified by agarose gel electrophoresis before quantification of protein concentrations using a BCA protein assay kit (Pierce, 23225). 100 µg of soluble chromatin was precleared with Protein A Dynabeads (Invitrogen, 10001D) before incubation overnight with 2.5 g of antibody: H3K4me1 (Abcam, ab8895) or H3K27ac (Abcam, ab4729). ChIP-seq libraries were constructed as described previously (Franco et al., 2015) Frozen tissue was pulverized using a liquid nitrogen-chilled hammer mill, homogenized in ice-cold Swelling Buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8, 2 mM magnesium acetate, 3 mM calcium chloride, 0.25mM DTT, and 1x cOmplete protease inhibitor cocktail (Roche, 11697498001)), and passed through a 40 micron filter. The nuclei were released from the pulverized tissue in Lysis Buffer (Swelling Buffer containing 10 mM NaCl, 300 mM sucrose, and 0.5% NP-40), washed three times in Lysis Buffer , and flash frozen in Nuclei Freezing Buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.3, 5 mM magnesium chloride, 0.1 mM EDTA, 40% glycerol) in aliquots of 5 x 106 nuclei. Nuclear run-on was performed at 37°C for 5 minutes using biotin-labeled CTP. PRO-seq libraries were constructed following the protocol of Mahat and colleagues (Mahat et al., 2016) Frozen tissue was pulverized using a liquid nitrogen-chilled hammer mill and then homogenized in Trizol. Following chloroform-mediated phase separation, the total RNA was purified from the aqueous phase using RNeasy columns (Qiagen, 74104) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. The RNA concentration was quantified using a NanoDrop and the quality was assessed by RNA ScreenTape (Agilent). The total RNA was stored at -80°C until processed. Ten µg of total RNA were used as input for polyA RNA-seq library construction as previously described (Zhong et al., 2011)

Main topic
This paper maps sites of transcriptional enhancer activity and associated changes in gene expression across gestation in human placenta tissue to identify genomic loci and predicted transcription factor activity related to critical placenta functions. It integrates genomic assays and computational pipelines to identify enhancers active at various time points in gestation, correlating their activity with gene expression and adverse pregnancy outcomes.
